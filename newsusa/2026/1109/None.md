# D8TAOPS Brings Governed Knowledge to Amazon Quick, Now Generally Available in AWS Marketplace

(Troy Wyatt, Founder and CEO, and Dr. Navid Kalantari, PhD, Chief AI Officer, D8TAOPS) - Built with Mark Relph's team at AWS, the integration lets Quick customers govern the data and the agents behind every answer, inside their own AWS account.

BEAVERTON, Oregon (November 1, 2026) D8TAOPS today announced the general availability of its integration with Amazon Quick, now available in AWS Marketplace. It gives organizations a way to take Quick from its first teams to the whole company while keeping firm control of their data and their agents.

The integration connects D8:Platform to Amazon Quick through a single MCP connection, managed by D8's Agentic Capital Management™ (ACM). People keep working in Quick the way they already do. Beneath them, D8 prepares, reconciles, and governs the enterprise knowledge their questions and agents depend on, and checks every request against the person's role, the data's classification, and their part of the organization. Everything runs inside the customer's own AWS account.

D8TAOPS built the integration with Mark Relph's team at AWS, which asked the company to prove the connection end to end inside Quick before taking it to customers. Dr. Navid Kalantari, PhD, D8TAOPS's Chief AI Officer, led the engineering.

Two halves of the same rollout

Amazon Quick gives an enterprise one place where people and agents ask questions, build automations, and act on what they find. That reach is what makes a company-wide rollout worth doing. It is also why security, compliance, and data leaders want a say once Quick moves past its first teams. Before they sign off, they want to know whose data an agent can see, whether the answers hold up, and who is accountable when an agent acts.

D8TAOPS was built for that conversation. Quick provides the interface and the agents. D8 governs the knowledge beneath them and keeps account of every agent working on it, so the people who approve an enterprise rollout have what they need to say yes.

"Every company we talk to wants Quick in front of all of its people," said Troy Wyatt, Founder and CEO of D8TAOPS. "What slows them down sits underneath: whose data an agent can see, whether two people asking the same question get the same answer, and who answers for an agent once it acts. We built D8 to settle those questions inside the customer's own environment, so the decision to go company-wide gets easier for everyone who has to make it."

Where it applies

The value looks different depending on who is using Quick and what they answer for. A few examples:

Consider a global insurer preparing to open Quick to every business unit. The security architect's first concern is agents. Employees will build them by the thousands. With D8 connected, an agent can still be built in Quick in minutes, but it reaches no governed data, tool, or API until it is registered in Agentic Capital Management™ with an owner, a purpose, and explicit permissions. Every request is checked against the person's role, the data's classification, and their part of the organization, so only what they are entitled to see ever reaches Quick. The rollout gets approved because the guardrails are in place before the first agent goes live.

A regulatory reporting manager at a regional bank has a different concern: her mornings. She asks Quick once for the overnight exceptions summary she used to assemble by hand. D8 turns the request into a plan she approves and a scheduled pipeline her data team reviews, running in the bank's own AWS account. The summary is waiting every weekday, and when an examiner asks how a number was produced, the record already exists.

Some answers depend on getting the data to agree with itself. An upstream oil and gas operator may track the same well under three different identifiers across its historian, maintenance, and inspection systems. D8 reconciles them in a knowledge graph, so when operations staff ask Quick which wells showed pressure anomalies after a pump replacement, they get one answer they can trace to its sources. At an automotive supplier, the earliest sign of a failure pattern often lives in technicians' free-text service notes. D8 turns those notes into knowledge Quick can reason over, tied to the right part numbers, so the warranty team sees the pattern before it reaches the claim counts.

In healthcare, the stakes sit with protected health information. A denials specialist working through rejected claims can ask Quick why a claim was denied and what the payer's policy says, and get the policy citation in the same answer. D8 applies access policy before anything is retrieved, so protected health information reaches Quick only for people whose role allows it. That is what lets a health system open Quick to its entire revenue cycle team.

"Customers, including Financial Services Institutions, are implementing Amazon Quick for multiple employee types, which include different roles, responsibilities, and varying levels of access to sensitive data," said Jeff Axelrad, Director Governance, Risk, & Compliance, AWS Financial Services. "D8TAOPS enables them a way to enforce that policy inside their own AWS environment."

One connection, eight agents behind it

"To someone working in Quick, this is one connection," said Dr. Navid Kalantari, PhD, Chief AI Officer at D8TAOPS. "Behind it, eight D8 agents bring the data in, clean it, catalog it, and enforce policy under the permissions of the person asking. Then they build the infrastructure that keeps it running on a schedule, in the customer's own account, and every step leaves a record. In testing, a plain-language request in Quick became a governed, cataloged dataset in about 23 minutes, with a person approving each stage."

That reflects the principle D8TAOPS builds on: govern the knowledge, not just the agents. When the knowledge underneath Quick is reconciled, described, and governed, every agent built on top of it starts from solid ground.

Available now

D8:Platform for Amazon Quick is generally available today in AWS Marketplace: https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-hf2wfrtihdqya?sr=0-1&ref_=ucaf&applicationId=AWSMPContessa. It works alongside an existing Amazon Quick subscription, deploys into the customer's AWS account, and Quick users connect to it without changing how they work.

"Our customers chose AWS and Amazon Quick for good reasons, and our role is to make that choice go further," said Jared Cooper, Director of Partnerships and Marketing at D8TAOPS. "Through AWS Marketplace, D8 arrives the way the rest of their AWS investment does: through procurement they already use, on the same AWS bill, and into an account they control. What they have built becomes the foundation for what comes next."
