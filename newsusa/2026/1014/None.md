# Double Duty: RoC Just Dropped Two Game-Changing Serums for Fine Lines and Dark Spots

![](https://newsflow.newsusa.com/sites/default/files/inline-images/ROC-128_LS_TripleSerum_ET_Serum_OnShelf.jpg)

(NewsUSA) - Some mornings, it’s a stubborn fine line catching the light. Other days, a sudden dark spot takes center stage no matter what you try. RoC Skincare—the brand backed by dermatologists and over 65 years of research—is taking direct aim at both skin dilemmas with two new high-performance innovations designed for visibly smoother skin and a radically more radiant, even tone.

Smooth Operator: Turning Back the Clock

![](https://newsflow.newsusa.com/sites/default/files/inline-images/TS%20BUBBLE%20PINK%20%20%282%29.jpg)

Retinol Correxion Line Smoothing Triple Serum

Think of this serum as a visible skin reset in a bottle. RoC packs three heavyweight components into one clever, multi-chambered formula, featuring bubbles of micro-encapsulated clinical retinol that burst upon application to kickstart visible skin renewal. This is paired with Hyaluronic B5 Jelly for plumping hydration, a complex of the viral PDRN to power-pack a youthful-looking, refreshed bounce.

RoC dubs this triple-threat blend “7 years, 7 signs of aging, 1 serum”—and the clinical stats fully back up the claims—no needles or expensive office visit1. In clinical testing, 100% of participants had instantly smoother skin within minutes2. Within just one week, 96% experienced a noticeable reduction across 7 key aging indicators, including fine lines, dullness, and dryness3. By week four, users saw a visible reduction in skin age by up to 7 years, with the look of decreased crow’s feet and under-eye wrinkles4.

Spot Eraser: Rewriting the Story on Discoloration

![](https://newsflow.newsusa.com/sites/default/files/inline-images/EVENTONE-ASSET%204%20%281%29.png)

Even Tone Dark Spot Roll-On Serum

If uneven tone is stealing your glow, this laser-inspired spot treatment acts as a high-precision defense against the appearance of sun spots, age spots, brown patches, and post-blemish marks. Formulated to work up to 10 layers deep in the epidermis, the serum brings together a powerhouse lineup: patented Tranexamic Acid5 to visibly fade persistent dark spots, Niacinamide to balance tone, Resveratrol Exosomes to supercharge luminosity, Prebiotics to help guard against future discoloration, and Lactic Acid to gently exfoliate for fresh surface-level visible skin renewal.

Equipped with a flash-cooling, professional-level steel roller ball tip for instant massage and precision application, the melting milky serum gets to work fast—with visible skin renewal and fading beginning within minutes. It targets all four types of discoloration in just one week6, leaving your complexion looking luminous, refined, and beautifully even-toned by week four.

“What I love about these launches is that they take high-level clinical science and make it incredibly easy for everyday use,” says board-certified dermatologist Dr. Angela J. Lamb. “With the Triple Serum, you’re getting the line-smoothing power of micro-encapsulated retinol paired with deep hydration. Meanwhile, the Even Tone Roll-On targets persistent pigmentation up to 10 layers deep with patented TXA and cooling precision7. They’re practical, targeted solutions that deliver visible, office-level transformations right at home.”

If your daily lineup is due for a high-tech overhaul, consider this your sign. Both high-performance formulas are available for $28.49 at Walmart. Whether you pick a favorite or layer the duo for the ultimate smooth-and-bright power move, visible results are officially within reach.
