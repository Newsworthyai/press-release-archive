(NewsUSA) - If the thought of a chemical peel conjures images of red, flaky downtime, RoC Skincare is here to rewrite the narrative. The brand’s new Line Smoothing 2-Step Peel Pads bring the power of an in-office resurfacing treatment straight to your bathroom vanity for skin age visibly reduced by up to 5 years —sans the scary recovery period or typical four-figure price tag.

Developed with dermatologists, this over-the-counter duo relies on a smart, effortless process designed to visibly smooth texture and boost radiance without irritation:

Step 1, PEEL: The first pad delivers a gentle yet effective chemical exfoliation using a blend of glycolic, lactic, mandelic, and phytic acids plus gluconolactone and hyaluronic acid to visibly smooth rough texture, boost radiance, and reduce the look of lines and wrinkles while sloughing away dead skin.

Step 2, RENEW: The second pad swipes on the Step 2 formula with RoC Patented Clinical Retinol and Ectoin after two minutes to help neutralize the peel step, soothe skin, and visibly smooth signs of aging for younger-looking skin over time with continued use.

The standout technology, however, lies in RoC’s Patented Clinical Retinol —a formula 7x more powerful than traditional retinol. Together, the acids and retinol deliver what the brand calls a "total age peel," tackling the look of lines and wrinkles, dullness, rough texture, and uneven tone. Plus, unlike other exfoliating or toner pads on the market, the entire treatment is fragrance and alcohol-free, making it suitable even for sensitive skin,
