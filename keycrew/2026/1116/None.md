# ZAMINAI and San Diego MLS Partner to Advance AI-powered Property Intelligence Through the GDX Network

Signed at the 4th International MLS Forum in Istanbul, the agreement establishes direct collaboration between an MLS and an AI technology company through the GDX network to develop personalized, transparent and evidence-based property intelligence.

 ISTANBUL, Türkiye — November 16, 2026 — ZAMINAI and San Diego MLS (SDMLS) today signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) at the 4th International MLS Forum at Raffles Istanbul. ZAMINAI is a real estate technology company using advanced AI to make property decisions more transparent, evidence-based and personalized. The agreement establishes direct collaboration between SDMLS and ZAMINAI through the Global Data Exchange (GDX) network, which provides the medium for their work together.

 SDMLS and ZAMINAI will work together to apply ZAMINAI’s advanced AI models, built on standardized MLS data, to help transform the real estate market with intelligence that is personalized to each client’s needs, transparent about how it was reached, and grounded in evidence rather than opinion. The aim is to make value (not only price) and risk clearer, so that buyers, sellers and their agents, as well as lenders, can decide with confidence.

 The initiative represents a step beyond the listing-display model associated with Internet Data Exchange (IDX). Developed roughly 25 years ago, before the current wave of AI, IDX was designed to let brokers display one another’s listings online. This collaboration brings an MLS and a technology company together directly to develop AI applications through the GDX network, extending the focus from displaying listings to interpreting property data and supporting decisions.

 The SDMLS–ZAMINAI collaboration is intended to lay the foundation for similar agreements between MLSs and technology companies. GDX’s role is to connect those organizations through a common network, enabling them to establish direct collaborations around new applications of real estate data.

 The partners see benefits well beyond the individual buyer or seller. More accurate, transparent valuation can help lenders measure risk and, over time, support confidence across the wider real estate finance ecosystem, including secondary-market investors and international buyers.

 “AI gives MLSs an opportunity to create more value for the real estate market by turning trusted real estate data into insights agents and clients can use. I believe the technology ZAMINAI has built has the potential to become a standard for property valuation and property intelligence, for buyers, lenders and ultimately the broader capital markets,” said Saul Klein, CEO of San Diego MLS.

 “The structured, standardized data that MLSs and the GDX provide is an extraordinary foundation for AI that serves the whole market,” said Dr. Alireza Haghighi, Founder and Chair of ZAMINAI. “On that foundation, ZAMINAI takes real estate beyond the number, with intelligence that is personalized for each client, transparent in how it is reached and grounded in evidence. Our ambition is to turn MLS data into decision intelligence: to create new value for MLSs, give agents deeper insight, and help every client make a more informed decision.”

 “GDX provides a network through which MLSs and technology companies can collaborate directly through RESO standards,” said Merri Jo Cowen, Inaugural President of GDX.

 Commenting on the agreement, Sam DeBord, CEO of the Real Estate Standards Organization (RESO), said: “Global collaboration in real estate depends on data that can be understood consistently across markets and borders. RESO standards set the stage by giving MLSs and technology companies a common language for working together. The collaboration between San Diego MLS and ZAMINAI through the GDX network shows how organizations can build on that foundation to develop new applications and create value together.”

 About ZAMINAI

 ZAMINAI is a real estate AI company building Property Decision Intelligence to help people understand what drives a property’s value and make decisions that reflect their priorities. Its platform draws on MLS and other real estate data to show buyers, sellers, agents and lenders the evidence behind a valuation and help them see how it applies to the decision at hand. Learn more at www.zaminai.com

 About San Diego MLS

 San Diego MLS (SDMLS) is the only locally based MLS in San Diego County and a wholly owned subsidiary of the Greater San Diego Association of REALTORS®. SDMLS is a founding partner of the GDX, which it joined at the 2025 International MLS Forum in Toronto. Learn more at sdmls.com.

 About the Global Data Exchange (GDX)

 The Global Data Exchange is a standards-based, federated network that enables cross-border sharing of public real estate listing data while respecting local governance, attribution and market rules. It provides a medium for direct collaboration between MLSs and technology providers.

 About the International MLS Forum

 Now in its fourth year, the International MLS Forum takes place November 16–17, 2026, at Raffles Istanbul. It is organized by RESO, ADRES and RealtyFeed.
