# The AI Solution’s Carrie Soave Emcees International MLS Forum In Istanbul

Carrie Soave, founder and CEO of The AI Solution, which helps real estate agents and teams build artificial intelligence (AI) into their businesses, emceed the 4th International MLS Forum and spoke on one of its panels. The Forum was held November 16-17, 2026, in Istanbul at Raffles Istanbul.

 Her message to the Forum’s international audience was to build organized systems before adding AI, and then use AI to grow revenue. “What I focus on, and what I think all agents should focus on, is using AI to make money,” Soave says.

 “Saving time is wonderful. Everybody wants to save time,” she adds. “But if you’re just using AI to save time, you’re not doing it right.”

 Soave, who is based in Niagara Falls, Canada, originally came to Istanbul for her panel. A week before the event, organizers asked her to emcee as well. “I do a lot of keynotes. I speak all over the world, but that’s one thing I haven’t done before,” she says.

 Through The AI Solution, Soave works mostly with individual agents and teams, and about 90% of her clients are in North America. She says her work has shifted from teaching AI to implementing it directly inside her clients’ businesses.

 Why Soave Says Systems Come Before AI The Forum audience was a different market from the one Soave usually serves. She describes it as more brokerage owners and more leaders from organized real estate: the Multiple Listing Services (MLSs) and associations that set and run industry rules.

 She sees a clear difference in how agents operate. In North America, she says, agents tend to run their businesses on defined systems and processes. Overseas, many agents don’t use customer relationship management (CRM) software to track clients and leads.

 “Not that they’re doing anything wrong at all. They just do things differently,” Soave says. “My intention here is to teach them that systems are good. Systems are their best friend.”

 That foundation matters because AI needs direction, the same way a new hire does. “You have to look at AI almost like you would hiring a human. You’re bringing on an employee, even though it’s digital,” she says.

 “You wouldn’t hire an employee without handing them a playbook, or the systems and processes you follow. You have to do the same thing with AI,” she adds. Her advice is to get those systems organized before using AI agents, software that carries out tasks on its own.

 How The AI Solution Ties AI To Revenue Soave says the industry’s challenge is results, not adoption. By her estimate, about 87% of North American agents have adopted AI, but only 17% are seeing an impact on their business.

 “We don’t have a problem in this industry with adoption,” she says. She traces the gap to messy back-end systems, which is why her work starts with the business rather than the tools.

 Her method begins with the model of an agent who earns $1 million a year in gross commission income (GCI), the total commission earned before brokerage splits and expenses.

 “Let’s take AI completely out of the equation. What needs to happen every single day in order to make that kind of money?” Soave says. “We build the workflows that are needed, the ones humans would usually do at volume. Instead of adding humans, we just add AI.”

 “I think it’s a very simple concept, but I find it’s making the most impact,” she adds.

 How GDX Connects Markets Across Borders The Global Data Exchange (GDX) was a central focus of this year’s Forum. GDX is a federated network, built on Real Estate Standards Organization (RESO) data standards, that lets MLSs, associations, brokerages and technology providers share public listing data across borders.

 It is not a central database or a portal. Each partner keeps ownership of its own listings and decides which partners can display them.

 For agents, that can mean access to listings in other countries for clients who are relocating, buying a second home or investing abroad, along with exposure for local listings to buyers overseas.

 Why Soave Is Looking To Europe Looking ahead, Soave plans to expand further into Europe and to bring more experts into her company, so more people can share the knowledge and take the stage.

 “I’m looking forward to expanding more into Europe. I love spending time overseas,” she says. “I’m looking forward to expanding my company and bringing more experts in, because collaborative knowledge is always best.”

 Agents who want to learn more can find free resources from Soave on Instagram at @carriesoaveai.

 About The International MLS Forum

 The International MLS Forum brings together MLS leaders, real estate organizations and technology providers from around the world to build more connected, transparent property marketplaces. The 4th edition, organized by RESO, ADRES and RealtyFeed, takes place November 16-17, 2026, at Raffles Istanbul.
