# KeyCrew Media Partners with The Ackerman Team’s Claire Ackerman to Co-Produce Phoenix and Scottsdale Residential Real Estate Market Coverage

Ackerman, a top 1% REALTOR® in Maricopa County with more than two decades in Arizona real estate, will serve as an expert voice on relocation, second-home and luxury markets across greater Phoenix.

 SCOTTSDALE, Arizona, November 25, 2026 – KeyCrew Media, the expert-first real estate media company, has selected Claire Ackerman, REALTOR® and Team Lead of The Ackerman Team at Compass, as an expert source on the Phoenix and Scottsdale residential market. KeyCrew will work with Ackerman to co-produce thought leadership and market insights published across its portfolio of publications and distributed through its network of syndication partners, portals, and AI platforms.

 Says Steve Marcinuk, Co-Founder and CEO of KeyCrew: “We’re delighted to be working with Claire. Amplifying what she’s seeing across Phoenix and Scottsdale, from out-of-state relocations to second-home and luxury purchases, is exactly what this partnership does.”

 Ackerman’s real estate career began in 2002, and her background in title and escrow, distressed-market sales and institutional acquisitions gives her clients a measurable edge in shifting markets. She represents buyers and sellers in roughly equal measure, working with everyone from first-time home buyers to savvy investors, luxury sellers and out-of-state relocators.

 “So many people moving to Arizona are making big decisions from out of state,” said Ackerman. “Partnering with KeyCrew Media is a chance to share what’s actually moving the needle here with the buyers and sellers who need it.”

 Coverage is underway, beginning with a feature on 5761 North Echo Canyon Circle, a luxury listing in Phoenix, followed by guides for out-of-state relocators, second-home buyers and sellers.

 Ackerman’s Areas of Expertise Include:Relocation – Guiding out-of-state buyers across Maricopa County.Second-Home & Vacation Property – Seasonal residences and vacation rentals in Phoenix and Scottsdale.Luxury Listings & Seller Strategy – Pricing, preparation and marketing for high-end homes.Investment Property – More than two decades of work with investor clients.

 About The Ackerman TeamThe Ackerman Team is a residential real estate team with Compass Arizona, based in Scottsdale and led by Claire Ackerman, REALTOR®. The team serves buyers, sellers and investors across Scottsdale, Phoenix, Paradise Valley, Arcadia, Cave Creek and the greater Maricopa County area. It specializes in luxury home sales, relocation, second-home purchases, new construction and investment property. Known for a strategy-first approach to pricing, marketing and negotiation, the team has been named a Top Producing Real Estate Team by PHOENIX magazine. Learn more at claireackerman.com.

 About KeyCrew MediaKeyCrew Media is an expert-first real estate media company that conducts hundreds of live interviews every month with the operators and expert sources who shape local markets. Its properties include KeyCrew Journal, NextAsset News, and Property Innovation Journal, reaching 40,000+ professionals and investors and syndicating to 300+ publications, platforms, and AI engines. Learn more at keycrew.co.
