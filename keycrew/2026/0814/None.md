# Outamation Debuts on the 2026 Inc. 5000 at #133, Ranking Among America’s Fastest-Growing Private Companies

DALLAS, Aug. 14, 2026 – Outamation, a leader in automation and digital transformation for mortgage servicing and origination, today announced it has been named to the 2026 Inc. 5000, debuting at #133 with 2,291% three-year revenue growth, placing it in the top 3% of America’s fastest-growing private companies. This is the company’s first appearance on the list.

 The Inc. 5000 ranks privately held U.S. companies by percentage revenue growth over a three-year period, offering a data-driven look at the nation’s most successful independent businesses.

 Outamation has built its growth around one of the mortgage industry’s hardest problems: automating document-heavy, highly regulated workflows across servicing and origination without sacrificing accuracy or compliance. Its AI-powered platform, including Outamate™ Workflow Automation, OutamateMods™, OutamateDS™, OutamateDocs™, OutamateQMS™, and OutamateAI™, has been adopted by servicers and lenders modernizing operations still built on decades-old manual processes.

 “We didn’t grow by chasing easy wins. We went after the parts of mortgage servicing and origination most companies avoid, the document-heavy, heavily regulated work where getting it wrong isn’t an option,” said Sapan Bafna, Founder and CEO of Outamation. “We’re building toward a more integrated, standards-based mortgage industry, because a better-connected system ultimately serves homeowners. This ranking belongs to our team and to the clients who trusted us to fix problems the industry had lived with for years. It’s a checkpoint, not a finish line.”

 Compliance sits at the foundation of that growth. Outamation is the first U.S. mortgage technology company to achieve ISO/IEC 42001 certification, the international standard for responsible AI management systems, backed by ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II attestations that give servicers and lenders independent, third-party validation of how the company governs its AI, security, and data. As active members of MISMO, the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA), and the Property Records Industry Association (PRIA), Outamation is committed to advancing the data standards and interoperability that move the industry toward a more integrated future in the best interest of homeowners.

 “In mortgage, AI doesn’t earn trust by promising ROI. It earns trust by delivering it, on regulated work, with an audit trail behind every decision,” said Devang Kamdar, Chief Technology Officer and Co-Founder of Outamation. “We engineered our platform so that responsible AI and measurable results are the same thing. Every model we put in front of a servicer is governed, traceable, and accountable, which is exactly why our clients are completing modifications two to three times faster, not just reading about efficiency in a slide deck. Growth like this doesn’t come from hype. It comes from putting working technology in clients’ hands and standing behind what it does.”

 The recognition adds to a growing list that includes back-to-back HousingWire Tech100 Awards, the MortgagePoint Tech Excellence Award, and its ISO/IEC 42001 first.

 Outamation plans to continue investing across its product suite and go-to-market teams heading into next year.

 To see how Outamation helps mortgage servicers and lenders modernize their workflows, schedule a demo at outamation.com/booking.
