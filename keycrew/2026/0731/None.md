# KeyCrew Media Recognizes Derrik Carlson as a Verified Expert in Park City Luxury Real Estate

PARK CITY, UT, July, 2026 – KeyCrew Media, a real estate analytics and media network, has recognized Derrik Carlson, Resort Real Estate Advisor and team lead of Real Estate in Park City, the Luxury Division of KW Park City Keller Williams Real Estate, as a KeyCrew Verified Expert in recognition of his expertise in Park City and Deer Valley luxury, ski-access, and resort real estate.

 KeyCrew Verified Experts are recognized for their demonstrated expertise, industry leadership, and ability to provide valuable insight within their fields. This recognition highlights professionals whose experience and market knowledge help inform conversations across the real estate industry.

 Derrik Carlson brings nearly 20 years of real estate experience to Park City, Deer Valley, and the surrounding Wasatch Back, built on an unusually deep finance and development background. He earned business finance and management degrees from Westminster College, financed new construction and resort properties nationwide during college, and managed an 85-unit condo development on the island of Roatán, Honduras after graduation. By age 26, he owned nine personal investment properties before shifting into full-time brokerage, giving him a numbers-driven perspective that now shapes how he negotiates on behalf of his clients.

 Carlson is a top-producing agent in the Luxury Division of KW Park City Keller Williams Real Estate, currently ranked #1 in Utah within the Keller Williams network for 2026. He holds the Certified Negotiation Expert (CNE), Resort and Second Home Specialist (RSPS), Luxury Homes Certification (LHC), and Certified Expert Listing Agent (CELA) designations, and roughly one in five of his transactions closes off-market, reflecting the depth of his network across the region.

 “I built my business around understanding not just the properties but the numbers behind them,” said Derrik Carlson. “Buyers and sellers in this market want someone who can walk them through the real math of a deal, not just show them a house. That’s what nearly twenty years here and a finance background let me do.”

 Carlson’s Areas of Expertise Include:

 * Park City & Deer Valley Luxury Real Estate – Buyer and seller representation across Park City’s resort core, Deer Valley, and the surrounding luxury and ski-access neighborhoods.
* Off-Market & Ultra-Luxury Transactions – Access to off-market opportunities and experience representing buyers and sellers at the highest price points in the market.
* Investment Property & Rental Income Analysis – Underwriting rental purchases and evaluating cash flow and appreciation potential for out-of-state and local investors alike.
* Finance-Driven Negotiation – A background in mortgage lending and development that informs how deals are structured and negotiated on a client’s behalf.
* Older Home vs. New Construction Guidance – Helping buyers weigh the trade-offs between renovated resale properties and new-build homes across Park City’s neighborhoods.
