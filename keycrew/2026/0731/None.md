# Spreadsight Launches Autonomous AI Wholesaling Platform at Spreadsight.ai

Proprietary AI built entirely in-house sources, calls, negotiates, underwrites, and assigns real estate deals — with zero human touches

 LOS ANGELES, CA — July 2026 — Spreadsight, an autonomous wholesaling platform for real estate investors, today announced the launch of its web application at www.spreadsight.ai. The platform is built around artificial intelligence developed entirely in-house, not a stack of third-party point solutions, and is designed to carry a wholesale real estate deal from first contact through a signed assignment without a human needing to pick up the phone.

 Most technology in the wholesaling space stops at a scored lead or a distress signal flagged inside a CRM. From there, a person still has to call the seller, qualify motivation and timeline, negotiate price and terms, underwrite the numbers, search a buyer list by hand, and call a buyer to confirm before a contract gets drafted. Spreadsight was built to close that gap.

 The platform’s AI voice agent calls sellers directly, asks the questions needed to surface motivation, timeline, and property condition, and negotiates price and terms in real time. An underwriting engine sets the offer using live comparable-sales data and repair estimates, while a buyer-matching engine ranks an investor’s entire cash-buyer network against the deal and calls the top match to confirm, before auto-drafting the assignment contract. Every stage runs autonomously, end to end.

 “We didn’t want to build another lead list with a chatbot bolted on top,” said Daniel Kaufman, founder of Spreadsight. “Every piece of this — the calling, the underwriting logic, the buyer-matching — is proprietary AI we built ourselves, purpose-built for wholesaling. That’s the difference between a tool that flags a deal and a system that actually closes it.”

 Spreadsight is available now at www.spreadsight.ai, where wholesalers and real estate investors can request a demo of the platform.

 About SpreadsightSpreadsight is an autonomous wholesaling platform that sources, scores, calls, underwrites, and assigns real estate deals using proprietary AI built in-house. The platform is developed by Daniel Kaufman and Kaufman Real Estate & Consulting LLC. Learn more at www.spreadsight.ai.
