# Tovel AI Launches Recorder That Turns Conversations Into CRM and Calendar Records, Pending User Approval

Milford. Michigan – September 22, 2026 – Tovel AI today launched the Tovel AI Card, a pocket-sized recorder and companion app that captures calls, meetings, and in-person conversations and prepares the resulting follow-up work: CRM records, calendar events, tasks, and contacts for a user to review and approve before any of it is created.

 The launch arrives as much of the artificial intelligence industry moves toward autonomous software agents that act without human confirmation. Tovel is built on the opposite premise. The company has made an approval step mandatory: the app identifies decisions, risks, and action items from a conversation, populates the fields, and displays the destination, record type, and key values, but creates nothing until a person confirms it. Users may later authorize rules for action types they trust.

 “If we remove the human, there’s no accountability,” said John LeRoy, co-founder and chief executive officer of Tovel AI. “Users need to be accountable for their actions. They can’t be oblivious and not pause to think about the consequences.”

 Existing AI recording tools have converged on a common output of transcript and summary, leaving the resulting work to the user. LeRoy said that gap is the reason the company built the product.

 “What’s in the marketplace does not instigate the follow-up actions,” he said. “The user still needs to take the information these recorders provide and do the work behind it. They need to organize it, copy and paste it.”

 LeRoy, who over more than 35 years has founded, scaled and sold companies in health care, consumer products and technology, said the problem that drew him to the category was customer relationship management software, which sales organizations have struggled for decades to get their staff to use. Companies routinely restructure compensation plans to force adoption, because the work of logging notes, opportunities and contacts falls outside the working day.

 Tovel ships with direct connections to more than 30 business applications rather than requiring customers to build their own automations. Supported CRM platforms include Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive, ActiveCampaign, GoHighLevel and the real estate systems Follow Up Boss and BoldTrail. The product also connects to Google, Outlook, and Apple calendars; Zoom, Google Meet, and Calendly; Asana, ClickUp, Jira, Linear, Notion, monday.com, and Google Tasks; and Slack, Microsoft Teams, Gmail, Outlook, and QuickBooks Online. Zapier and Make.com are supported for workflows that fall outside the direct connections.

 Rather than classifying every extracted item as a generic to-do, Tovel sorts each next step by what it should become – a contact, a lead or opportunity with a value and stage, an appointment, an assigned task, a quote or invoice, or a follow-up message – and prepares the corresponding record.

 Tovel uses a physical device rather than a software bot that joins a video call, an approach the company said covers conversations that never touch a screen, including client visits, property showings and site inspections. The app groups recordings into a searchable library spanning both calls and in-person meetings.

 The product includes a consent mode that asks how many participants are present and prompts each one to state their name and consent before the recording workflow continues.

 “It’s not only a sensitive topic about whether or not you’re recording me,” LeRoy said. “It’s also a legal and liability topic. Laws about consent vary state by state, and it’s important to give the user the ability to comply.”

 Recording and consent laws differ by jurisdiction and situation. Tovel said users and organizations remain responsible for compliance with applicable laws and professional requirements.

 Subscriptions are offered in three tiers, activated after the device arrives. A free plan provides 120 transcription minutes a month and basic summaries using an AI provider account the customer already holds. A bring-your-own-AI plan at $9.99 a month adds unlimited transcription, subject to a fair use policy, along with advanced summaries and action-item extraction; customers connect OpenAI, Claude, or Gemini and are billed by that provider for usage. A personal plan at $29.99 a month includes Tovel-hosted AI usage and requires no separate provider account or API key. Both paid tiers include a 30-day free trial.

 The Tovel AI Card is available at a launch price of $99, against a regular price of $159, with two-device and three-device packages at $160 and $210. The first 300 completed orders include one month of subscription at no charge. Every order carries a 30-day return period with prepaid return shipping and a full refund of the device price.

 For businesses, Tovel licenses are assigned per device rather than per user, and a team leader’s read-only management account does not consume a seat. Managers can oversee devices, recordings, and sync status from a single view.

 Tovel AI develops an AI recorder and companion app that captures conversations and prepares approved follow-up actions, including CRM records, calendar events and tasks. The company is focused on client-facing professionals and teams in sales, real estate, legal, consulting, and field services. The Tovel app is available on the App Store and Google Play. More information is available at tovel.ai.
