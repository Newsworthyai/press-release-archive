# KeyCrew Media Partners with Gibson Sotheby’s SVP Chuck Silverston to Co-Produce Greater Boston Real Estate Coverage

The partnership will feature Silverston, who has guided Greater Boston buyers and sellers for over 20 years, as an expert voice across KeyCrew’s editorial network.

 BOSTON, Massachusetts, November 17, 2026 – KeyCrew Media, the expert-first real estate media company, has selected Chuck Silverston, Principal of The Chuck Silverston Team and Senior Vice President at Gibson Sotheby’s International Realty, as an expert source on residential real estate strategy in Greater Boston. KeyCrew will co-produce thought leadership with Silverston, published across its publications, syndication partners, portals, and AI platforms.

 “We’re delighted to be working with Chuck and his team,” said Steve Marcinuk, Co-Founder and CEO of KeyCrew. “Twenty-plus years of referral-built relationships give Chuck a rare view of how Greater Boston buyers and sellers make decisions, and this partnership brings that to an open, non-paywalled audience nationwide.”

 Based in Boston, Silverston’s team works almost entirely by referral and specializes in the “sell-buy,” helping clients sell one home and buy another at the same time. A certified Buffini & Company mentor, Certified Full-Service Professional (CFSP) and recent panelist on AI in real estate, he previously taught Real Estate for the Start-Up Investor at Brookline Adult Education and ran a successful First-Time Home Buyer program.

 “My business is built on relationships and referrals,” said Silverston. “Partnering with KeyCrew Media is a chance to share the strategies we use every day with Greater Boston buyers and sellers, and with the agents nationwide who trust us with their clients.”

 Silverston’s Areas of Expertise Include: Relationships, Results & Strategy – The hallmarks of Silverston’s philosophy, whether buying and selling at the same time or working with buyers, sellers and investors.

 Multiple-Offer Strategy – Competing as a buyer and netting the most as a seller.

 AI in Real Estate Practice – Using AI alongside agent and client, not between them.

 “We educate our clients that the negotiation starts at the front door,” said Silverston.

 About The Chuck Silverston Team

 The Chuck Silverston Team is a Boston-based residential real estate team with Gibson Sotheby’s International Realty. Learn more at chuckteam.com.

 About KeyCrew

 KeyCrew is an expert-first real estate media company. The company conducts hundreds of live interviews every month with the operators and expert sources who shape local markets, and its editorial staff turn those conversations into original reporting published, syndicated, and amplified across hundreds of markets. KeyCrew’s properties include KeyCrew Journal, NextAsset News, and Property Innovation Journal.
