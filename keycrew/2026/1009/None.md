# KeyCrew Media Partners with Eakin Group’s Ed Eakin to Co-Produce Los Cabos Luxury and Golf Community Real Estate Coverage

The partnership will feature Eakin, a Certified International Property Specialist (CIPS) and the second person ever to receive NAR’s CIPS Excellence Award, as an expert voice across KeyCrew’s editorial network, deepening the company’s coverage of luxury and golf course real estate in Los Cabos.

 CABO SAN LUCAS, Mexico, October 7, 2026 – KeyCrew Media, the expert-first real estate media company, has selected Ed Eakin, REALTOR® and leader of the Eakin Group, as an expert source on luxury and golf course developments, buying property in Mexico as an American, and international residential real estate in the Los Cabos market, based on his demonstrated experience and on-the-ground market activity. Eakin joins KeyCrew’s growing roster of expert sources across hundreds of markets worldwide.

 Through the partnership, KeyCrew’s team will work with Eakin to co-produce dedicated thought leadership, development features and market insights on Los Cabos luxury real estate and cross-border buying, published across KeyCrew Media’s portfolio of publications and distributed through its network of syndication partners, portals, and AI platforms.

 Says Steve Marcinuk, Co-Founder and CEO of KeyCrew, “We’re delighted to be working with Ed and the Eakin Group. Buyers coming to Cabo are making significant decisions in a market with its own laws, language and way of doing business, and Ed brings decades of certified, international experience to that conversation. Sharing what he’s seeing on the ground with an open, non-paywalled audience of buyers and investors is exactly what this partnership does.”

 After years of selling real estate in Mexico from Texas, Eakin has moved to Cabo San Lucas full time, where he works directly with high-end developers to connect buyers from California, Texas, the wider United States and Canada with luxury and golf course communities. In a market where many practitioners work as advisors or consultants, he brings the standards of a certified REALTOR® and a deep international track record. He earned his CIPS designation in 2012, now teaches it to agents around the world, and was the second recipient of NAR’s CIPS Excellence Award. He was named International REALTOR® of the Year in Dallas–Fort Worth in 2022, serves as one of NAR’s 30 Global Ambassadors, representing Belize, Barbados, and Trinidad and Tobago, and holds global leadership roles with MetroTex, HAR, ABoR and Texas REALTORS®.

 “I moved to Cabo for the lifestyle – the golf, the water, and a luxury market that’s still moving,” said Ed Eakin, REALTOR® and leader of the Eakin Group. “Buyers here deserve representation from an established professional who knows the developers and has a team in place to make sure everything is done right. Partnering with KeyCrew Media is a chance to share that with buyers before they ever sign.”

 Coverage is underway. KeyCrew’s first articles with Eakin explore Puerto Los Cabos, the 2,000-acre master-planned coastal community just east of San José del Cabo, and what U.S. buyers should understand before buying in Mexico, from the fideicomiso bank trust to the role of the notario and protecting a pre-construction deposit. Ahead: branded residences in Belize, more of Los Cabos’ luxury golf communities, and why Texas buyers are making the move to Cabo.

 Eakin’s Areas of Expertise Include: Los Cabos Luxury & Golf Course Communities – High-end, master-planned developments across the Los Cabos corridor, from Cabo San Lucas to San José del Cabo and the East Cape.

 Buying Property in Mexico as an American – Fideicomiso bank trusts, notarios, third-party escrow and the due diligence buyers need before committing to pre-construction.

 Developer Representation – Working directly with leading developers to bring vetted, high-end inventory to U.S. and Canadian buyers.

 Texas-to-Cabo Buyers – Guiding Texas and other U.S. buyers through second homes, relocation and lifestyle purchases in Los Cabos.

 International & Caribbean Property – CIPS-backed guidance and a global network spanning Belize, the Caribbean, Latin America and beyond.

 About the Eakin GroupEd Eakin is a Cabo San Lucas-based REALTOR® and leader of the Eakin Group, specializing in luxury and golf course developments across Mexico, the Caribbean and Latin America. A Certified International Property Specialist (CIPS) and CIPS instructor, Ed is one of only three people to receive NAR’s CIPS Excellence Award. He was named International REALTOR® of the Year in Dallas–Fort Worth, and he serves as a National Association of REALTORS® Global Ambassador to Belize, Barbados, and Trinidad and Tobago. A member of REALM, Ed works directly with leading developers to connect buyers with exceptional properties in Mexico and beyond. Learn more at eakingroup.com.
