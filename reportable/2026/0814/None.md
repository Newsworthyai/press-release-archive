# UGI System Upgrades Set to Begin in Pittston

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - AUGUST 14, 2026

UGI Utilities is scheduled to begin system upgrades in Pittston on Monday, August 17, 2026. The natural gas main replacement is part of UGI’s commitment to provide safe and reliable service and is an opportunity for some residents and businesses to convert to natural gas.
