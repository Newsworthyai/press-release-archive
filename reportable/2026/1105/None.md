# CoorsTek Wins German Federal Patent Court Ruling in Pink Ceramic Hip Component Trademark Dispute

MUNICH, GERMANY, NOVEMBER 5, 2026

CoorsTek Bioceramics has secured another legal victory in its longstanding dispute with CeramTec GmbH over trademark rights to the color pink for ceramic hip components. In the latest ruling, the German Federal Patent Court (GFPC) upheld cancellation of CeramTec’s trademark in Germany, finding that the application had been filed in bad faith with the intention to extend an expiring patent monopoly and keep competitors out of the market.

Key Takeaways:* The German Federal Patent Court upholds cancellation of CeramTec’s trademark over the color pink for ceramic hip components in Germany, finding the application was filed in bad faith to extend an expiring patent monopoly and block competitors.
* The ruling bolsters CoorsTek Bioceramics’ ability to market its ceramic hip replacement components, including Permallon orthopaedic ceramics, and follows favorable decisions in France and the United States.
* CeramTec appeals the decision, while CoorsTek cites continued court wins and OEM regulatory clearances as building a more competitive orthopaedic ceramics supply chain.
