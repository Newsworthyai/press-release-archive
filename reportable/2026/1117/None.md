# Boldin Introduces Financial Planning Gift Subscription for the 40- to 60-Year-Olds Who “Don't Need Anything” for the Holidays

SAN FRANCISCO, NOV. 17, 2026 –

Boldin, the award-winning financial planning platform with more than 100,000 paid subscribers, has introduced an annual gift subscription for under $200. Holiday shoppers can now give a meaningful financial wellness gift to people who are hard to shop for, don’t need anything, or don’t want anything.

About Boldin

Boldin is a retirement planning platform on a mission to make high-quality planning accessible to everyone. The platform helps people understand their numbers, explore their options, and make informed decisions about retirement, taxes, Social Security, healthcare, housing, investing, and more. Combining powerful planning technology and personalized AI with education, coaching, community, and access to fiduciary advisors, Boldin gives people the tools and guidance to build a plan they can understand, trust, and act on. Today, more than 100,000 subscribers use Boldin to plan their futures, representing over $247 billion in self-reported assets. Learn more at Boldin.com.
