# UGI System Upgrades Now Underway in Catasauqua

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - AUGUST 20, 2026

UGI Utilities began system upgrades this week in Catasauqua. The natural gas main replacement is part of UGI’s commitment to provide safe and reliable service and is an opportunity for some residents and businesses to convert to natural gas.
