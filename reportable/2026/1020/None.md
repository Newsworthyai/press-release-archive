# Niqo Robotics Unveils North America's Widest Hooded AI Weeder Expanding Its RoboWeeder Lineup for Specialty Crops

YAKIMA, WASH., OCTOBER 20, 2026

Niqo Robotics, the physical AI company building the intelligence layer for farm machines, is expanding its U.S. lineup of AI weeding robots built to fit local farms with the RoboWeeder F50, North America's widest hooded AI weeder. This wide-format weeder has a 46-foot working width extendable to 50 feet. U.S. growers can now choose from three models: the 20-foot flagship RoboWeeder, the 24-foot F24, and the wide-format F50.

About Niqo Robotics

Niqo Robotics is a physical AI company building the intelligence layer for farm machines and other equipment that work in changing, real-world conditions. The Niqo RoboWeeder™ robots use Niqo Sense, the company's proprietary AI, to identify crops and weeds plant by plant, handling precision weeding, thinning, and beneficial spraying in one smart pass. Niqo builds its machines "localized to the zip code," fitting them to local crops and farm sizes, from small and midsize farms to large operations, and sells them without a subscription.
