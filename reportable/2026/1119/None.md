# UGI Urges Customers in Elizabethtown Borough to Conserve Natural Gas Due to Pipeline Repair

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - NOVEMBER 19, 2026

UGI Utilities requests that all its customers in Elizabethtown Borough conserve natural gas by lowering thermostats to 65 degrees or lower until further notice.
