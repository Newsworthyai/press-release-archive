# UGI Safety Alert: Take Simple Steps to Prevent Carbon Monoxide Emergencies

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - OCTOBER 22, 2026

This is the time of year when UGI often responds to an increasing number of carbon monoxide (CO) emergencies. There have been several CO incidents in the past few weeks. UGI urges customers to know the symptoms of CO poisoning and take simple steps to prevent exposure.
