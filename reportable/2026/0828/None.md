# UGI System Upgrades Beginning Next Week in the City of Lancaster and Lancaster Township

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - AUGUST 28, 2026

UGI Utilities is scheduled to begin a system upgrade project on Monday, August 31, 2026, that will impact sections of both Lancaster City and Lancaster Township. The natural gas main replacement is part of UGI’s commitment to provide safe and reliable service and is an opportunity for residents and businesses along the project route to convert to natural gas.
