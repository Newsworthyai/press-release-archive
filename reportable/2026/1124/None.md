# UGI Offers Simple Safety Tips to Help Prevent Carbon Monoxide Emergencies

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - NOVEMBER 24, 2026

The upcoming holiday season is traditionally the time of year when carbon monoxide (CO) emergencies surge. UGI is responding to an increasing number of carbon monoxide-related incidents and wants to remind everyone of the symptoms of CO poisoning and how to take simple steps to prevent exposure.
