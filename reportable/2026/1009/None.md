# Woody Lancaster Smashes Topsfield Fair Record With 2,598-Pound Pumpkin

Topsfield, MA October 9, 2026

Topsfield, MA October 9, 2026 -- A new Topsfield Fair record was set at this year’s Giant Pumpkin Weigh-In, as Woody Lancaster of Topsfield, MA brought in a massive 2,598-pound pumpkin. The entry topped a strong field of competitors and now stands as the largest pumpkin ever recorded at the Fair.

Key Takeaways:* Woody Lancaster of Topsfield, MA sets a new Topsfield Fair record with a 2,598-pound giant pumpkin.
* The leaderboard includes second place Larry & Shawna Masters at 2,220 pounds, third place Joe Jutras at 2,197 pounds, and Youth Prize winner Alistair Lehto at 1,815 pounds.
* Fairgoers can view the record-breaking pumpkin and other top entries during the Fair's 11-day run, with admission at $20 per day and free entry for children under 8 with an adult admission ticket.
