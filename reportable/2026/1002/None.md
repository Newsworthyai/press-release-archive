# UGI System Upgrades Beginning Next Week in Manheim Township, Lancaster County

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - OCTOBER 2, 2026

UGI Utilities is scheduled to begin a system upgrade project on Monday, October 5, 2026, on Oregon and Lititz Pikes in Lancaster County. The natural gas main replacement is part of UGI’s commitment to provide safe and reliable service and is an opportunity for residents and businesses along the project route to convert to natural gas.
