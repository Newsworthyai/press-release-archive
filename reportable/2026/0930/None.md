# BioUtah’s 2026 Utah Life Sciences Summit Presented by Mayer Brown Announces Early Bird Discounts

SALT LAKE CITY, UT SEPTEMBER 30, 2026

BioUtah, Utah’s trade association dedicated to the life sciences community, has opened Early Bird registration for all. Be sure to register at a reduced price before early bird sales end on October 11. The 2026 Utah Life Sciences Summit (ULSS) presented by Mayer Brown will be held November 11 at the Hilton, Salt Lake City Center.

To view the full announcement, including downloadable images, bios, and more, click here.

Key Takeaways:* Early bird discounted registration is now open for the 2026 Utah Life Sciences Summit, November 11, Hilton Salt Lake City Center.
* Keynote speaker, FDA’s Dr. Michelle Tarver, Director, CDRH, will host an in-person Town Hall discussion.
* Topic-specific tracks include workforce, regulatory, legal, and AI.

About BioUtah

BioUtah is an independent 501(c)(6) trade association serving Utah’s life sciences industry. Its member companies reflect a broad spectrum of the industry with strengths in medical device manufacturing and services, research and testing, biotechnology, biopharmaceuticals, and diagnostics, amongst others; and are a key driver of Utah’s economy and advancing healthcare.
