# Avatar Medical and Kyber Obtain 3.7 Million Euros to Expand Access to Immersive 3D Imaging in Hospitals

PARIS, October 1, 2026

PARIS, October 1, 2026 – Avatar Medical and Kyber today announced they have secured a grant of up to €3.7 million under the French government's France 2030 program to develop Avatar MediVerse, a project aimed at making immersive, personalized imaging available wherever clinicians and patients need it.

To view the full announcement, including downloadable images, bios, and more, click here.

Key Takeaways:* Immersive 3D beyond a dedicated room. MediVerse aims to make interactive 3D anatomy accessible wherever it can be useful in the surgical journey.
* Two French deeptech expertise combined. Avatar Medical brings its expertise in 3D medical visualization and Kyber its know-how in ultra-low latency XR streaming and remote rendering.
* The same visual information throughout the care pathway. From consultation to surgical planning and into the operating room, the ambition is to enable clinicians and patients to rely on the same personalized 3D representation of the patient's anatomy.

About Kyber

Kyber is a French company specializing in deeptech technologies and a commercial spin-off of KLabs, the company hosting the VideoLAN project, creators of VLC, one of the most widely used open-source media players in the world. The company develops real-time infrastructures for the healthcare, robotics, cloud rendering, and AI-driven video applications sectors. Learn more at https://kyber.tech.

About Avatar Medical

Founded in 2020, Avatar Medical is a pioneer in visual intelligence for surgery. It develops a scalable solution that transforms medical imaging into actionable visual information throughout the surgical care pathway. Its flagship solution, Avatar Medical Vision, instantly and without segmentation transforms CT scans and MRIs into interactive, realistic 3D visualizations, providing clinicians with a more intuitive way to understand each patient's specific anatomy, plan interventions, collaborate, and communicate with patients. From consultation to the operating room, including surgical planning, the company strives to make visual intelligence accessible wherever critical surgical decisions are made. Originating from research conducted at Institut Curie and Institut Pasteur, Avatar Medical has FDA-cleared and CE-marked technology products. It collaborates with more than 20 hospitals in the United States and Europe. Learn more at avatarmedical.ai.
