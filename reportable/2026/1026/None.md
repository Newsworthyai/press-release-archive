# UGI Urges Eligible Customers to Apply for Operation Share Grants to Help with Heating Bills

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - OCTOBER 26, 2026

UGI Utilities, Inc. is increasing its contribution to the Operation Share Energy Fund to $1.5 million. The fund helps low to moderate income customers who are having trouble paying their heating bills. Grants up to $600 are available for eligible customers and do not need to be paid back.
