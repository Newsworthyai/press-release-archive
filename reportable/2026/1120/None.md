# Elizabethtown Borough Residents Can Now Resume Normal Usage of Natural Gas Following Pipeline Repairs

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - NOVEMBER 20, 2026

UGI Utilities completed repairs on a damaged pipeline overnight, and customers in Elizabethtown Borough can now resume normal natural gas usage.
