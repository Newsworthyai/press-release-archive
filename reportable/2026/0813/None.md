# UGI Scheduled to Begin System Upgrade Project Next Week in Berwick

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - AUGUST 13, 2026

UGI Utilities is set to begin system upgrades in Berwick on Monday, August 17, 2026. The natural gas main replacement is part of UGI's commitment to provide safe and reliable service and is an opportunity for some residents and businesses to convert to natural gas.
