# UGI System Upgrades Scheduled to Begin Next Week in Carlisle Area

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - SEPTEMBER 4, 2026

UGI Utilities is scheduled to begin a system upgrade project on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, that will impact the Carlisle area. The natural gas main replacement is part of UGI’s commitment to provide safe and reliable service.
