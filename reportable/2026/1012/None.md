# UGI System Upgrade Project Now Underway in Hellertown

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - OCTOBER 12, 2026

UGI Utilities began a system upgrade project on Monday, October 12, 2026, in Hellertown, Northampton County. The natural gas main replacement is part of UGI’s commitment to provide safe and reliable service and is an opportunity for residents and businesses along the project route to convert to natural gas.
