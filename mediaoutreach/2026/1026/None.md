# Bangkok pledges to put Asian LGBTQIA+ communities at the heart of WorldPride 2030

BANGKOK, THAILAND – [Media OutReach Newswire](https://www.media-outreach.com/) – 26 October 2026 – Bangkok has pledged to make WorldPride 2030 a platform for advancing LGBTQIA+ visibility and solidarity across Asia – ensuring the event's first arrival in the region strengthens communities beyond the host city. As the official applicant and host, Bangkok Pride's bid commits to giving activists and organizations from across Asia a meaningful role in WorldPride while fostering connections with global partners to build support that continues beyond the occasion.    Across Asia, LGBTQIA+ communities operate under widely differing conditions. Bangkok's bid aims to provide a platform where communities can raise their voices, contribute to international discussions and strengthen cross-border cooperation.   "Bringing WorldPride to Asia for the first time would be more than a celebration. It would be a powerful moment for LGBTQ+ Asians to see ourselves represented on a global stage – and for the world to hear the diverse voices, stories and struggles of our communities," said Raymond Phang, co-founder of ShanghaiPRIDE. "From Shanghai to Bangkok, from grassroots organizers to a new generation of LGBTQ+ Asians, our movement has always been about creating space for people to live openly, safely and with dignity. I hope Bangkok 2030 can be a WorldPride that celebrates how far Asia has come, while reminding us of the work still ahead for equality, acceptance and human rights."   The bid includes practical measures to widen participation. The proposed Bangkok WorldPride Activist Fellowship would provide at least 150 fully funded places for frontline activists, with at least 70 percent allocated to participants from the Global South to reduce barriers in accommodation and accreditation.   Regional perspectives would also help shape a week-long Human Rights Forum, which would anchor Bangkok Action 2030 – a proposed framework for cooperation on legal and family recognition, bodily autonomy, protection for human-rights defenders, health equity and digital rights.   Furthermore, Bangkok WorldPride 2030 would establish a Pride City Network Asia community hub, building upon Thailand's existing network of local organizers and authorities to create creating opportunities for Pride organizations across the region.   Building a legacy beyond 2030   Bangkok's bid builds on Thailand's continuing journey towards equality. Marriage equality took effect in January 2025, making Thailand the first country in Southeast Asia to recognize same-sex marriage. By 2030, the country will mark five years since the law took effect – with the global landscape having since shifted.   ​Ann Chumaporn, Core Team Lead of WorldPride 2030, Bangkok Candidate City, added: "At a time when gender equality and LGBTQIA+ rights are facing severe pushback across the globe, holding our ground is no longer enough, and we must move forward with courage and solidarity. Thailand's journey to marriage equality proved that transformative change is possible when community leadership remains at the core. Bringing WorldPride 2030 to Bangkok is about opening a vital gateway for Asia – a sanctuary where frontline activists can breathe, connect, and reclaim their narratives. This bid is our collective promise that Asia will not be silenced, but will rise as a powerful force of hope, resilience, and enduring progress for human rights worldwide."   The bid proposes a legacy that extends beyond the event itself. A share of operational surpluses would establish a permanent Pride City Network Fund, providing micro-grants, capacity-building resources and emergency relocation support for human-rights defenders across Asia. It also proposes a Sexual Orientation, Gender Identity and Expression, and Sex Characteristics (SOGIESC) Museum and Archive to preserve and increase the visibility of Asian queer histories.   Bangkok's ambition is for communities across Asia to leave WorldPride 2030 with stronger partnerships, greater access to international support and resources for advancing equality in the years ahead.
