# Chinese, US Youth Forge Friendships Across Borders

BEIJING, CHINA - [Media OutReach Newswire](https://www.media-outreach.com/) - 16 October 2026 - In response to President Xi Jinping's initiative to invite 100,000 young Americans to China over the next five years, a China-US youth exchange event was held from Oct 12 to 14 in Beijing, bringing together more than 200 students from both countries to engage in cross-cultural dialogues.
