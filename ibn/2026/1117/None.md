# Versus Systems (NASDAQ: VS) Takes Filter Fan Cam Beyond the Stadium Screen with Mobile AR Experience

* Versus Systems, a leading gamification and audience engagement marketing technology developer, is expanding its popular stadium event Filter Fan Cam system with a new mobile version for fan use.
* The mobile enhancement changes the format from a Fan Cam moment displayed on a stadium video board into a branded activity that attendees can personalize and share themselves using their mobile phones.
* The mobile experience is designed to let users scan a QR code, open a camera-based web experience, apply branded filters, and create their own photos.
* Fans can save and share images, extending sponsor exposure beyond the brief moment when someone appears on a stadium screen.
* Brands and venues can distribute campaign QR codes through in-venue displays, signage and digital promotions, creating additional ways for audiences to enter an activation.
* The mobile format is designed to provide campaign-level engagement data, including user consent, photo captures, saves and shares, subject to the platform’s implementation and applicable privacy requirements.
* Integration with Versus’ Winfinite games could connect photo creation with interactive promotions and rewards.

 Versus Systems (NASDAQ: VS), a leading provider of Versus Systems, a leading gamification and audience engagement technology developer, is expanding the role of its Filter Fan Cam augmented-reality platform by bringing the experience directly to fans’ mobile phones. The mobile enhancement changes the format from a moment displayed on a stadium video board into a branded activity that attendees can personalize and share themselves.
