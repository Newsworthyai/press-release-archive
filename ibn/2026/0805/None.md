# InvestorNewsBreaks – BOS Better Online Solutions Ltd. (NASDAQ: BOSC) Wins $1.9 Million Air Defense Supply Orders

BOS (NASDAQ: BOSC) announced that its Supply Chain Division has secured purchase orders totaling approximately $1.9 million for an air defense project. The orders, which are expected to be delivered through 2028, are for electromechanical connectors, the division’s core product line, and were received from Israeli subcontractors of a leading Israeli air defense manufacturer.

 The company said the awards reflect continued replenishment efforts driven by growing demand for air defense munitions. Management said BOS is well positioned to support this demand by providing critical connector components for defense applications, highlighting the company’s established role in aerospace and defense supply chains.
