# CATL Now Commands 40.2% of the Global Market for EV Batteries

Chinese battery maker Contemporary Amperex Technology Co. Limited (CATL) has strengthened its position as the world’s largest electric vehicle (EV) battery manufacturer. The company now holds a 40.2% share of the global EV battery market, showing its growing influence in the fast-changing electric mobility industry.

 As CATL progresses in its efforts to commercialize increasingly superior EV batteries, a time may come when all models from leading firms like Massimo Group (NASDAQ: MAMO) will feature batteries from CATL…
