# Copper Prices Rebound as Demand Recovers

Copper prices recovered at the start of this week, supported by improving market sentiment, a weaker greenback and growing concerns over tightening supplies in China. In late-morning trading in New York, Comex copper for December rose to $6.35 per pound. The red metal remains about 13% higher since the beginning of this year.
