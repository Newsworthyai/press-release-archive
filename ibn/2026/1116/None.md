# Personalized Vaccines are Giving More Patients Hope After Brain Cancer Diagnosis

Personalized vaccines are giving more brain cancer patients reason to hope, and at UCLA, one tested in clinical trials has helped about two dozen people with glioblastoma live far longer than expected. Doctors are now working to extend that benefit to the thousands of brain tumor patients who need it.
