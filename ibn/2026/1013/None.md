# AINewsBreaks – AZIO AI Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: AZIO) Featured in AINewsWire Editorial Highlighting Power-Backed AI Infrastructure

AZIO AI Holdings (NASDAQ: AZIO) announced its placement in an editorial published by AINewsWire (AINW), one of more than 75 brands within the Dynamic Brand Portfolio at InvestorBrandNetwork (IBN). The editorial, titled “Contracted Capacity Becomes Key Metric for AI Infrastructure Investors to Watch,” examines how rising electricity demand from artificial intelligence (AI) data centers is reshaping infrastructure development and increasing the importance of securing reliable power capacity. The article highlights AZIO AI’s Atlas One project in south Texas, where the company recently signed its first hosting agreement with Power Champion Investment Limited for an initial 10 megawatts of capacity, with expansion rights up to 50 megawatts. The agreement establishes Atlas One’s first contracted, paying customer as the company advances its power-backed AI computing infrastructure strategy.
