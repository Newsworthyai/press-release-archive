# InvestorNewsBreaks – Market Street Capital Inc. Connects Rising Power Demand with Strategic Energy Financing

Market Street Capital was featured in a recent article that highlights how rising electricity demand driven by artificial intelligence, electrification and data center expansion is creating new growth opportunities and financing challenges for middle-market energy companies. The capital markets and financial advisory firm helps energy founders evaluate debt, equity and hybrid financing options based on their cash flow, growth plans and risk tolerance. While debt can preserve ownership and may offer a lower after-tax cost of capital, it introduces repayment obligations and potential restrictions; equity offers greater repayment flexibility but dilutes ownership and may introduce new investor oversight. The expanding private credit market has also broadened financing options, including senior debt, unitranche facilities, mezzanine financing and asset-based lending. Through its debt capital markets, specialty lending and private equity practices, Market Street connects clients with potential financing sources to support expansion, acquisitions and new infrastructure projects. As power demand reshapes the energy landscape, the firm emphasizes aligning capital structures with business objectives and investment timelines to help founders pursue growth while managing financial risk.
