# TechMediaBreaks – HWAL Inc. (HWAL) Bridges Music History, Digital Royalties and the Future of Entertainment

HWAL (OTCID: HWAL) is positioning itself at the intersection of music preservation, intellectual property, and emerging digital entertainment, using its Melody Trust subsidiary and Lunar Records joint venture to manage and expand a catalog of master recordings tied to artists including Ray Charles, Aretha Franklin, Elvis Presley, Dolly Parton, James Brown, and the Jackson 5. The company’s strategy goes beyond traditional licensing, emphasizing preservation, royalty management, new artist development, merchandising, and digital distribution, while also exploring blockchain-based structures for music royalties. HWAL’s music catalog and related intellectual property were independently valued at approximately $480 million according to the company’s latest quarterly disclosure. Its partnership with SI Blue Foundation (SpaceBlue) also links the company to the Lunaprise Museum, a lunar archive carrying hundreds of songs and artworks, highlighting HWAL’s broader vision of preserving and monetizing entertainment assets across both traditional and emerging platforms.
