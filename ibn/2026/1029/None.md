# Bitcoin ETFs Bleed $773 Million in Two Days

Bitcoin faced renewed selling pressure late last week as spot exchange-traded funds recorded heavy withdrawals. Data showed that Bitcoin spot ETFs registered combined outflows of about $773 million between October 22 and 23. Over the same two-day period, Bitcoin fell 3.45%, extending its decline as the market moved toward the weekend.
