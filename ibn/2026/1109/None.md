# Barry Ritholtz Joins Howard Lindzon to Open the Stocktoberfest 2026 Programming Day

The programming day of Stocktoberfest presented by Tradr ETFs opened on October 7 at City Winery in New York City with a keynote from Stocktwits Founder and CEO Howard Lindzon, followed by a fireside chat between Lindzon and Barry Ritholtz, Chairman and CIO of Ritholtz Wealth Management.

 Lindzon’s keynote set the tone for the day with a live walkthrough of real historical crowd calls, revealed before the market knew how they would turn out, offering a direct look at the kind of crowd-sourced signal Stocktwits was built around.
