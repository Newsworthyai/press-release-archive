# CATL Considers Making Lithium-Air Batteries for EVs

Chinese battery manufacturer CATL is studying lithium-air batteries as an alternative to traditional lithium-ion batteries in electric cars. Currently the largest energy storage manufacturer on the globe, Contemporary Amperex Technology Co. Limited (CATL) is focused on lithium-air batteries to increase EV battery energy density and potentially open the door to electric cars with longer range.
