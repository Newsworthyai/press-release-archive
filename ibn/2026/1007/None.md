# InvestorNewsBreaks – Streamex Corp. (NASDAQ: STEX) to Present at ThinkEquity Conference on Oct. 15

Streamex (NASDAQ: STEX) a technology company focused on commodity market tokenization, announced that Chief Financial Officer Christine Plummer will present at the ThinkEquity Conference on Oct. 15, 2026, at the Mandarin Oriental Hotel in New York City. The presentation is scheduled for 4 p.m. ET and will be available via live webcast on the company’s investor relations website, with a replay accessible for 90 days. Plummer will also host in-person one-on-one meetings with investors during the conference. Investors interested in attending or scheduling a meeting may contact ThinkEquity at conference@think-equity.com.

 To view the full press release, visit https://ibn.fm/HjiCh

 About Streamex Corp.

 Streamex Corp. (NASDAQ: STEX) is a technology and infrastructure company focused on the tokenization and digitalization of commodity real-world assets. Streamex delivers institutional-grade solutions that bridge traditional finance and blockchain-enabled markets through secure, regulated, and yield-bearing financial instruments.

 For more information, visit www.streamex.com
