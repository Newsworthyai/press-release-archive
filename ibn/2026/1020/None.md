# AINewsBreaks – Nightfood Holdings Inc. (OTCQB: NGTF) Completes 1-for-150 Reverse Stock Split to Support Nasdaq Uplisting

Nightfood Holdings (OTCQB: NGTF), doing business as TechForce Robotics, announced the completion of a 1-for-150 reverse stock split effective Oct. 20, 2026, reducing its outstanding common shares from approximately 543.9 million to 3.6 million. The company’s shares began trading on a split-adjusted basis under the temporary symbol NGTFD on Oct. 20 and will revert to NGTF after 20 trading days. The reverse split applies proportionately to all shareholders, with fractional shares rounded up to the next whole share, and does not change the number of authorized shares.

 The reverse split is intended to increase the per-share trading price and support Nightfood’s efforts to meet the initial listing requirements of the Nasdaq Capital Market. The company has submitted a Nasdaq listing application and is working to satisfy applicable standards related to share price, public float, liquidity, shareholder distribution and corporate governance. CEO Jimmy Chan described the split as another milestone in the company’s uplisting process, which remains subject to Nasdaq review, satisfaction of all applicable requirements and final approval.
