# Health Insurance Has Spiked 78% in the US Since 2011

Surging health insurance costs have compelled even healthcare workers to stop getting coverage not only for themselves but also for their staff at private facilities. Premiums climbed 78% between 2011 and 2024, a JAMA study shows.
