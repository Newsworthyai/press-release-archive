# AINewsBreaks – AZIO AI Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: AZIO) Launches Atlas One to Address Growing AI Power Demand

AZIO AI Holdings (NASDAQ: AZIO) has unveiled Atlas One, a South Texas data center campus designed to address growing AI computing demand by developing dedicated, on-site power infrastructure. Spanning more than 548 acres, the project is engineered for up to 500 megawatts of planned behind-the-meter power capacity, with approximately 11 megawatts of natural gas generation and supporting electrical infrastructure already secured and about 6 megawatts activated for an initial Bitcoin mining workload. The company has also committed approximately $2.4 million to an AT&T agreement for dedicated enterprise fiber connectivity. Atlas One is intended to connect Azio AI’s GPU hardware business with its planned hosting infrastructure, complementing a separate agreement to sell up to 512 NVIDIA HGX B300 GPU systems to Power Champion Investment Limited for an estimated $307 million.

 About AZIO AI Holdings Inc.

 AZIO AI Holdings is a technology infrastructure company focused on developing, owning, and operating artificial intelligence data centers, enterprise GPU compute infrastructure, digital power solutions, and digital asset mining operations. Through this diversified AI infrastructure strategy, the company is positioned to capitalize on the rapidly expanding global demand for AI infrastructure, compute capacity, digital power, and next-generation AI technologies.
