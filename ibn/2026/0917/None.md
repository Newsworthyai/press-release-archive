# NetworkNewsBreaks – Streamex Corp. (NASDAQ: STEX) Announces Sixth GLDY Yield Distribution

Streamex (NASDAQ: STEX), a technology company focused on commodity-market tokenization, announced that the sixth yield payment for GLDY, its yield-bearing gold asset, will be distributed Sept. 17, 2026, covering Aug. 1 through Aug. 31. The company said GLDY currently earns a targeted annualized yield of 3.5% through its institutional gold leasing program. For August, 10.617518 GLDY, representing 10.617518 ounces of gold, will be distributed, resulting in a distribution of 0.003347 ounce of GLDY per token.
