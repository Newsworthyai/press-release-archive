# InvestorNewsBreaks – FingerMotion Inc. (NASDAQ: FNGR) Completes 9.9% Investment in Lyken.AI, Enters Enterprise Compute Market

FingerMotion (NASDAQ: FNGR) completed its previously announced acquisition of a 9.9% interest in Lyken AI Computing Inc., operating as Lyken.AI, from Alset AI Ventures Inc. (TSXV: GPUS), following TSX Venture Exchange approval. FingerMotion acquired 99,000 Lyken common shares in exchange for 1,674,480 restricted FingerMotion common shares, with no cash consideration paid at closing. Alset AI retains a 90.1% interest in Lyken.

 The transaction marks FingerMotion formal entry into the enterprise computing market and the first step in its strategy to build a recurring-revenue enterprise compute business alongside its existing mobile data and telecommunications operations. FingerMotion intends to pursue enterprise compute as a distinct, scalable business line and potentially increase its participation in Lyken and the underlying infrastructure, subject to commercial performance, capital availability and regulatory requirements.

 About FingerMotion, Inc.

 FingerMotion is an evolving technology company with a core competency in mobile payment and recharge platform solutions in China. As the user base of its primary business continues to grow, the Company is developing additional value-added technologies to market to its users. The vision of the Company is to rapidly grow the user base through organic means and have this growth develop into an ecosystem of users with high engagement rates utilizing its innovative applications. Developing a highly engaged ecosystem of users would strategically position the Company to onboard larger customer bases. FingerMotion eventually hopes to serve over 1 billion users in the China market and eventually expand the model to other regional markets.
