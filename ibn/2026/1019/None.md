# The Future of Healthcare: AI-Powered, Coordinated and Proactive Care

AINewsWire Editorial Coverage: American healthcare is moving from treating illness after it strikes to preventing it before it starts. U.S. health spending reached $5.3 trillion in 2024, and Medicare Advantage now covers 35.2 million people, or 55% of eligible beneficiaries. KFF projects Medicare Advantage payments of $574 billion in 2026, giving health plans a powerful reason to keep members healthy. That shift is opening the door for a new kind of player. ECGI Holdings Inc. (OTC: ECGI) (profile) has completed its acquisition of Avanta Group, a developing platform that spans a Medicare Advantage health plan, management services and AI-enabled health technology. The acquisition gives ECGI a direct path into coordinated, proactive care just as the industry is searching for better ways to deliver it.
