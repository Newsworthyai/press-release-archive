# Stocktwits Wraps Stocktoberfest 2026 in New York City with Tradr ETFs as Title Partner

Stocktwits, the leading social platform for investors and traders, wrapped Stocktoberfest presented by Tradr ETFs, its flagship annual event, held in New York City from October 5 to 7. This year’s event brought together more than 350 ticketed guests, including 40 financial creators with a combined audience of more than 11 million followers, for three days of networking, entertainment, and conversations with the public company executives shaping today’s markets.

 Stocktwits is built around unscripted conversation and relationship-building rather than formal presentations, bringing together top traders, public company executives, content creators, and financial professionals.
