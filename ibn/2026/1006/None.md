# Ecommerce is Expected to Record 5% Growth in Europe

Europe’s online shopping market is still growing, but not as fast as before. According to the latest European E-commerce Report 2026, ecommerce sales across the continent are forecast to rise by 5% in 2026, reaching €961 billion ($1.07 trillion). This marks a slowdown from the 7% growth seen in 2025, when online consumer spending hit €911 billion ($1.02 trillion).
