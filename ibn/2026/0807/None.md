# Spanish Patient Highlights Risks of Poorly Cooked Pork

Doctors in Spain recently handled a fascinating yet terrifying case of a patient that developed brain cancer-like symptoms and was treated as such, but additional tests brought to light a scarier cause of his illness: cysts resulting from a pork tapeworm infection. This case highlights the dangers of exposure to these worms, or eating pork that hasn’t been cooked fully.
