# InvestorNewsBreaks – SKYX Platforms Corp. (NASDAQ: SKYX) to Launch Four Additional Turbo Heater Ceiling Fan Models in Q4 2026

SKYX Platforms (NASDAQ: SKYX), announced plans to expand its patented all-in-one Turbo Heater ceiling fan product line with four additional models in 20-inch, 44-inch, 52-inch and 60-inch sizes, complementing its existing 24-inch model. The new models, expected to launch during the fourth quarter of 2026, will be offered in five colors: white, black, brushed nickel, gold and bronze. SKYX expects the expanded lineup to be available through major retailers, including Home Depot, Lowe’s, Target, Walmart and Amazon, as well as its own e-commerce platform. The expansion follows growing sales and seasonal demand for the company’s integrated ceiling fan and heating technology.

 SKYX anticipates the additional models will generate significant winter revenue and support its goal of achieving positive cash flow as it exits 2026. The company said the product expansion also supports its growing presence in builder and retail channels and its recurring-revenue business model. SKYX reported $27.7 million in total cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash at the end of the second quarter of 2026. The company positions its Turbo Heater ceiling fan as an alternative to portable space heaters, combining heating and cooling functionality in a single ceiling-mounted product.
