# Lahontan Gold Corp. (TSX.V: LG) (OTCQB: LGCXF) CEO Notes Key Milestones, 2027 Construction Plans in Featured CEO Podcast

Disseminated on behalf of Lahontan Gold Corp. (TSX.V: LG) (OTCQB: LGCXF) and may include paid advertising.

 * Lahontan CEO walked through the steps that keep the company’s 2027 construction target on track during recent interview.
* Lahontan recently announced a definitive agreement to acquire Emergent Metals, which would give it 100% of West Santa Fe, eliminates approximately $1.73 million in future payments, and ends 1% net smelter return royalties on West Santa Fe and 27 York claims at Santa Fe, and adds adjoining New York Canyon project.
* Permitting work has been under way for the past three and a half years and an updated preliminary economic assessment is due imminently.

 A gold price above $4,100 an ounce does little for a developer that cannot say when it will build, which makes the timeline the real story for companies still short of production. The company is a Nevada-focused mine developer advancing four gold and silver projects in the Walker Lane, led by the planned restart of the past-producing Santa Fe Mine.
