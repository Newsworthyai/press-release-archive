 About Adaptogenics Health Corp.

 Adaptogenics is building a science led company focused on identifying, acquiring, designing and advancing proprietary peptide and small molecule programs. The company is evaluating opportunities in metabolic health, muscle preservation, mitochondrial biology and inflammation, including delivery technologies intended to improve the performance of promising molecules. The company will continue its existing nutraceutical operations while evaluating GLP 1 companion products intended to address challenges associated with medical weight loss, including muscle loss, nutritional deficiencies and reduced physical function.
