# TechMediaBreaks – Shelfie-Tech Ltd. (CSE: SHLF) (OTCQB: SHLFF) Secures Recurring SaaS Agreement with Israeli Fashion Retailer B-Good

This article has been disseminated on behalf of Shelfie-Tech Ltd. and may include paid advertising.

 Shelfie-Tech Ltd. (CSE: SHLF) (OTCQB: SHLFF) announced that its subsidiary, Aspect Information Systems Ltd., has entered into an ongoing software-as-a-service (“SaaS”) agreement with B-Good, an existing customer and Israeli fashion retail chain, for its newly launched artificial intelligence (“AI”)-powered retail software platform. The platform is designed to provide retailers with improved insights into inventory, customer needs and pricing decisions, supporting greater operational efficiency and an enhanced shopping experience.

 The agreement represents an expansion of ShelfieTech’s existing relationship with B-Good and supports its strategy to increase adoption of its AI-powered platform through a scalable recurring SaaS model. The company plans to leverage established customer relationships, pursue new retail clients and develop additional data intelligence capabilities as part of its longer-term strategy to expand its retail data business.

 About ShelfieTech:

 ShelfieTech is a technology company focused on providing shelf inventory management technological solutions for the retail industry, in particular for large grocery stores and supermarkets.

 Using patent-pending technology, ShelfieTech’s technological solution includes a robotic retail shelf monitoring system which uses its self developed proprietary software that utilizes machine learning and image processing algorithms to automatically optimize shelf inventory management.
