# OpenAI Abandons Plans to Release New Model it Considered “Unsafe”

OpenAI has decided against releasing a new artificial intelligence system after internal testing revealed problems with how reliably and safely it could operate. The system, known as GPT-6.1 Astra, was designed to work more independently than a conventional chatbot, including browsing the internet, interacting with software and completing multi-step tasks on behalf of users.
