# InvestorNewsBreaks – Med-X Inc. (Reserved NASDAQ: MXRX) Advances Nature-Cide Natural Pest-Control Platform Ahead of Planned Public-Market Debut

Med-X (Reserved NASDAQ: MXRX), a privately held health and wellness company preparing for a planned Nasdaq listing, is advancing its Nature-Cide brand as the primary growth platform within its portfolio. Nature-Cide develops and commercializes natural pesticide and pest-management products for professional operators and consumers, with applications spanning residential pest control, agriculture, commercial facilities, municipalities and other markets. Med-X also owns Thermal-Aid, a line of reusable heating and cooling pain-relief products, and Malibu Brands, which focuses on essential oil-based wellness solutions.

 Nature-Cide has expanded from its origins in localized pest-management applications into a portfolio of indoor, outdoor, dust and granular products, with Med-X emphasizing formulation science, testing and adoption by professional pest-control operators. The company recently obtained OMRI certification for key products, potentially broadening opportunities in organic food production, livestock operations and other agricultural applications, while management is targeting expanded commercial adoption, production capacity, acquisitions and research and development as Med-X moves toward its anticipated public-market debut.

 About Med-X, Inc.

 Med-X, Inc. (NASDAQ: MXRX) is a leading innovator of biological pest control solutions addressing both consumer and professional markets globally. Nature-Cide – the Company’s flagship product line – is a safer, all-natural alternative to conventional chemical products, formulated to kill or repel a wide variety of pests. Med-X’s comprehensive go-to-market strategy includes leveraging strategic partnerships alongside an integrated e-commerce, brick and mortar and on-site services presence in key markets.
