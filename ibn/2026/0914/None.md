# NetworkNewsBreaks – Knightscope, Inc. (NASDAQ: KSCP) Publishes Fifth Autonomous Security Force Field Brief as Force Debuts at GSX 2026

Knightscope (NASDAQ: KSCP) published “Human Capabilities,” the fifth field brief in its Autonomous Security Force series, as the Force makes its live debut at GSX 2026, the Global Security Exchange, Sept. 14-16 at the Georgia World Congress Center in Atlanta. The brief outlines the human layer of Knightscope’s ASF-7 model, including licensed security agents and the company’s internal Mission Operations team, which provides intelligence, client support, technical reliability and field service. Knightscope said the model places its human security roles within one company rather than distributing responsibility among third-party staffing, monitoring and maintenance providers.

 The company said its approach combines autonomous machines, software and human judgment, with personnel responsible for functions including alert verification, executive protection, armed and unarmed security, de-escalation, response and equipment maintenance. Knightscope said its agents receive equity, full health benefits and paid training beginning on their first day. The company currently serves 434 clients across 42 states, with its machines logging more than 4.4 million autonomous hours in the field.
