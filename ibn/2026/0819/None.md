# MissionIRNewsBreaks – A2Z Cust2Mate Solutions Corp. (NASDAQ: AZ) Receives $11 Million Follow-On Order for 1,050 Smart Carts

A2Z Cust2Mate Solutions Corp. (NASDAQ: AZ), a global leader in smart retail technology, announced a follow-on purchase order from Israeli home goods retail chain HaStock for an additional 1,050 Cust2Mate Smart Carts, increasing HaStock’s total commitment to 3,050 Smart Carts. The additional order, received as deliveries under the original purchase order have commenced, is valued at approximately $11 million over the life of the five-year agreement and is on the same terms as the original agreement. The collaboration includes data, retail media and digital services managed by A2Z Cust2Mate, with the companies sharing in resulting revenue.

 About A2Z Cust2Mate Solutions Corp.

 A2Z Cust2Mate Solutions Corp. The Smart Cart platform helps retailers and brands grow revenue through targeted retail media and real-time shopper engagement at the moment purchase decisions are made. It delivers actionable, real-time data that provides full visibility into in-store shopper behavior and decision-making. With its modular, state-of-the-art technology, Cust2Mate enables retailers to increase revenue, optimize store operations, and mitigate loss across their chains at scale.
