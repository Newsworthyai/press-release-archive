# NetworkNewsBreaks – Powermax Minerals Inc. (CSE: PMAX) (OTCQB: PWMXF) Steps Up Cameron REE Exploration with 2026 Field Program

Disseminated on behalf of Powermax Minerals Inc. (CSE: PMAX) (OTCQB: PWMXF) and may include paid advertising.

 Powermax Minerals (CSE: PMAX) (OTCQB: PWMXF) has commenced its 2026 exploration program at its 100%-owned Cameron Rare Earth Element (“REE”) Project in British Columbia, building on encouraging findings from previous exploration campaigns. The three-week program will combine geological mapping and prospecting with rock, soil and stream sediment sampling, ground radiometric surveys and integrated data analysis to refine REE target corridors, identify potential bedrock sources and prioritize areas for future trenching and first-pass drilling. Earlier Phase 1 and 2 exploration identified enrichment of both light and heavy rare earth elements across multiple geological units, with soil samples returning total rare earth oxide values of up to approximately 2,840 ppm and surface rock samples reaching 740 ppm. Located across 2,984 hectares with access to established transportation infrastructure, the Cameron Project is also prospective for niobium and other mineralization.

 About Powermax Minerals Inc.

 Powermax Minerals is a Canadian mineral exploration company focused on advancing rare earth element projects. The company holds an option to acquire the Cameron REE Property, comprising three mineral claims totaling approximately 2,984 hectares in British Columbia. Powermax also has options to acquire the Atikokan REE Property and Pinard REE Property in NW Ontario. Powermax also owns a 100% interest in the Ogden Bear Lodge Project in Crook County, Wyoming.
