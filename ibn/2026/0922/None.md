# Survey Finds That Insurance Doesn’t Shield Americans from Medical Debt

A nationwide survey conducted by the Commonwealth Fund has found that contrary to a common view that it is the uninsured that incur medical debt, the reality is that a sizeable fraction of adults in the U.S. with health insurance have medical debt.
