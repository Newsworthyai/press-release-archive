# Studio 9 Salon Completes 24 Years Serving Macomb County Clients

Studio 9 Salon Marks 24 Years of Color Services and Precision Cuts in Macomb County

 Sterling Heights, United States - October 25, 2026 / Studio 9 Salon /

 Studio 9 Salon has reached its 24th anniversary as a hair salon in Sterling Heights, MI, marking more than two decades of advanced hair color services, precision cuts, and individualized client work across Macomb County. The milestone is accompanied by over 3,000 five-star Google reviews accumulated across the salon's two Metro Detroit locations.

Two Decades of Color Mastery in Macomb CountySince its founding, Studio 9 Salon has built its identity around the technical depth of its styling team. The salon's advanced hair color specialists have made color services a defining pillar of the business, working with clients who require precision across a range of applications - from dimensional balayage to corrective color treatments. Over 24 years, that specialization has developed a consistent client base throughout Macomb County and the surrounding Metro Detroit region.

The salon operates two locations, extending access to its specialized services across a wider geographic area. That two-location structure has supported the consistent delivery of technical standards the salon has maintained throughout its history.

Precision Cuts and Personalized TransformationsAlongside color work, precision cutting has remained a core component of the Studio 9 Salon service model. Stylists approach each appointment individually, accounting for face shape, hair texture, and the client's longer-term style objectives rather than applying a standardized approach. This focus on individualized outcomes is reflected in the volume and consistency of the salon's five-star reviews, which now exceed 3,000 on Google - a figure that points to repeat visits and sustained client satisfaction over many years.

The advanced hair color specialists at Studio 9 Salon continue to pursue technical development, keeping the methods and products used in the salon aligned with current professional standards. The combination of experienced stylists and a service environment centered on individual client results has defined the salon's approach throughout its 24-year presence in Sterling Heights.

A Sterling Heights Institution Entering Its Next ChapterFor the Sterling Heights, MI community, the anniversary represents more than a calendar marker. Studio 9 Salon has served multiple generations of clients across Macomb County, with some families returning across more than a decade of appointments. That level of continuity is uncommon in the salon industry, where stylist and ownership turnover can disrupt the long-term client relationships that sustain a business over time.

As the salon moves into its 24th year of operation, its two Metro Detroit locations continue to serve clients seeking color services, precision haircuts, and individualized attention from a team with substantial institutional experience. The hair salon Sterling Heights MI residents have visited for over two decades has maintained its standing through consistent reviews and an uninterrupted community presence built on technical skill and client-focused service.

About Studio 9 SalonStudio 9 Salon is a hair salon serving Sterling Heights, MI and the broader Macomb County area, operating two Metro Detroit locations. The salon specializes in advanced hair color services and precision cuts, supported by a team of experienced stylists. Studio 9 Salon has accumulated over 3,300 five-star Google reviews across its locations.
