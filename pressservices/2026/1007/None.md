# Braces Now Adds Digital Scanning to Buford GA Orthodontic Office

Digital Imaging and Full-Spectrum Treatment Options Now Available at Buford GA Orthodontic Office

 Buford, United States - October 7, 2026 / Braces Now /

 Braces Now offers traditional braces and Invisalign clear aligner treatment for children, teens, and adults at its Buford, Georgia location. The practice provides a full range of orthodontic options for patients in the surrounding region seeking treatment supported by modern in-office technology.

Full-Spectrum Orthodontic Treatment for All Ages

The orthodontist in Buford GA at Braces Now evaluates and treats patients across all age groups, from early childhood orthodontic assessments through adult care. Each patient receives an individualized treatment plan developed using current in-office diagnostic technology, enabling the clinical team to evaluate alignment, spacing, and bite characteristics with a level of precision that traditional methods do not consistently provide.

Braces in Buford GA are offered in formats suited to varying clinical needs and patient preferences. Traditional metal braces remain a widely used option for younger patients and more complex cases, providing consistent, controlled tooth movement over the course of treatment. For patients seeking a less visible alternative, the practice also offers clear bracket systems as part of its braces lineup.

Invisalign Clear Aligner Treatment Now Accessible Locally

In addition to conventional braces, Braces Now provides Invisalign in Buford GA, giving local patients access to the clear aligner system that uses a series of custom-fitted, removable trays to progressively shift teeth into proper alignment. Invisalign is available for qualifying teens and adults and is frequently chosen by patients who prefer a less visible appliance or whose daily routines are better suited to a removable treatment option.

The Invisalign process at the Buford location begins with a thorough clinical assessment and digital imaging to map out a projected treatment sequence. Patients receive trays designed to fit their specific tooth structure and advance through the series at intervals set by their treating orthodontist. Because the trays are removable, patients can maintain their regular diet and oral hygiene routines without significant modification.

Comfort-Focused Experience Backed by In-Office Technology

The Buford practice has equipped its office with diagnostic and imaging tools intended to support accuracy in treatment planning while reducing patient discomfort during the process. Digital scanning technology eliminates the need for traditional physical impressions, which many patients find uncomfortable. The same technology allows the clinical team to generate precise models used in fabricating aligners or determining bracket placement for braces.

The practice structures its patient experience around reducing common barriers to orthodontic care, including appointment anxiety and uncertainty about what to expect during treatment. Staff at the Buford location walk patients and their families through each phase in straightforward terms, adjusting communication based on whether the patient is a child, teenager, or adult.

Serving the Buford Community with Individualized Care Plans

Braces Now's Buford office operates with the understanding that orthodontic needs vary considerably depending on a patient's age, dental development, and personal goals. For children, early evaluation can identify developing alignment issues before they require more involved correction. For teens, treatment often aligns with key stages of jaw and tooth development. Adult patients, many of whom may have deferred orthodontic treatment for years, receive a clinical approach that accounts for differences in adult bone density and oral health history.

Both the orthodontist in Buford GA and the broader clinical team evaluate braces and Invisalign options during an initial consultation, providing patients and families with the information needed to select a treatment path suited to their individual circumstances. Treatment timelines and methods are not applied uniformly but are instead calibrated to each patient's clinical profile.

Braces in Buford GA through this practice reflect a patient-specific approach that incorporates current orthodontic standards while allowing for adjustments as treatment progresses. The Buford location operates as part of the broader Braces Now network, drawing on shared clinical protocols and technology infrastructure while serving its local patient population directly.
