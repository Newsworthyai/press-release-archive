# Safari SoftWash Releases Surface-by-Surface Cleaning Prices for Williamsburg

How Much Does Power Washing Cost in Williamsburg, VA?

 Williamsburg, United States - November 19, 2026 / Safari Softwash /

 Safari SoftWash has published a cost guide that lists starting prices for every exterior cleaning service it offers in Williamsburg, a level of detail missing from most local quotes. Homeowners across the Historic Triangle routinely field bids that range from under $200 to more than $500 for the same house with no explanation of the difference. The guide, How Much Does Power Washing Cost in Williamsburg?, walks through what sets the final number and how to compare one company against another.

Why Exterior Cleaning Prices Confuse Williamsburg HomeownersPublished cost figures rarely reflect what a careful job costs in coastal Virginia. National estimates often fall below $300, yet those averages blend small single-surface jobs with full-house work and say nothing about method. The method is where the money goes. The dark streaking common on Williamsburg roofs comes from airborne algae, and the Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association warns that high-pressure washing is likely to damage asphalt shingles and recommends a bleach-and-water solution instead. Doing it by hand carries its own cost, since Consumer Reports found pressure washers sent more than 6,000 people to emergency rooms in a single year. Humidity and heavy tree cover push organic growth back faster here than in drier regions, so the choice of method shapes both the price and how long the result lasts.

What the New Cost Guide CoversThe guide answers the pricing question before it explains anything else. It lists house washing starting at $300, roof cleaning at $400 with a gutter clean-out included, gutter cleaning at $149, and driveway and concrete cleaning at $0.28 per square foot. It then details what moves each price, including siding material, roof pitch and access, how much growth is present, and the exposed aggregate and river rock driveways common in the area that need a pressure pass rather than soft washing alone. A comparison section gives homeowners specific questions to ask any company before accepting a bid.

What Safari SoftWash Says Homeowners Should KnowThe company frames honest pricing as the point of the guide rather than a marketing angle.

"Most people here get three quotes and have no way to tell why one is half the price of another," a Safari SoftWash representative said. "We wrote down our real starting prices and explained what changes them, because a homeowner should be able to compare bids line by line instead of guessing. If a job is not worth doing, we would rather tell someone that up front than take the work."

Where Williamsburg Homeowners Can Learn MoreThe full cost guide is available on the Safari SoftWash website, and updates are posted on the company's Facebook page.

About Safari SoftWashSafari SoftWash is a locally owned exterior cleaning company based in Williamsburg, Virginia, serving the Historic Triangle and the Virginia Peninsula. It uses low-pressure soft washing for homes, roofs, decks, and fences and provides driveway and concrete cleaning, gutter cleaning, window cleaning, and commercial and community property services.
