# Lloyd Baker Expands to Four Offices Across Nevada and Arizona

Lloyd Baker Injury Attorneys Grows to Four Offices with Reno and Tucson Additions

 Las Vegas, United States - July 16, 2026 / Lloyd Baker Injury Attorneys /

 LAS VEGAS, Nev., July 14, 2026 - Lloyd Baker Injury Attorneys, the personal injury firm recognized across Arizona and Nevada for its record of taking on insurance companies and its "Baker Wins!" reputation, is continuing its expansion across both states with the recent opening of new offices in Reno, Nevada and Tucson, Arizona.

Founded by attorney Lloyd Baker in 2001, the firm has recovered hundreds of millions of dollars in verdicts and settlements for clients injured in auto, motorcycle, trucking, pedestrian, and wrongful death cases. That track record forms the foundation of what the firm calls the "Baker Promise" - a guarantee that each client walks away with more than the firm does. That commitment has become central to the firm's identity: clients are treated like family and represented until Baker wins.

"Anyone from Reno to Tucson who needs an injury attorney deserves the best representation that we provide to our Las Vegas and Phoenix clients," said Baker. "Our new offices extend that commitment to even more people across Nevada and Arizona. We fight for bigger and faster settlements for our clients. Get the Baker Promise, which guarantees that our clients always get more than us regardless of the amount of medical bills."

The expansion to four offices reflects growing demand for representation that combines local, in-person accessibility with the resources of a regional practice. Each office is staffed by trial attorneys with extensive experience handling the full range of personal injury matters, including car accidents, 18-wheeler wrecks, and motorcycle accidents - a practice area where Lloyd Baker Injury Attorneys has built particular authority through its "For Riders, By a Rider" advocacy and ongoing engagement with the region's motorcycle community.

The firm offers free consultations and operates on a contingency-fee basis - "No Fee Unless We Win" - for all personal injury and wrongful death matters, backed by the Baker Promise that clients retain more of every recovery than the firm collects in fees. Spanish-language services are available at every location.

About Lloyd Baker Injury Attorneys

Lloyd Baker Injury Attorneys is a personal injury law firm serving accident victims throughout Nevada and Arizona, with offices in Las Vegas and Reno, Nevada, and offices in Phoenix and Tucson, Arizona. Since 2001, the firm has represented people seriously hurt in auto, motorcycle, trucking, and pedestrian accidents, as well as wrongful death cases, going up against insurance companies, corporations, and negligent parties on their behalf. The firm operates on a contingency-fee, no-win-no-fee basis and offers free consultations 24/7. Learn more at lloydbakerinjuryattorneys.com or bakerwins.com.
