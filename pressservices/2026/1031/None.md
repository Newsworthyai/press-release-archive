# Complete Packaging Solutions Adds In-House Tooling to Its Rebrand Rollout

Complete Packaging Solutions Highlights In-House Tooling and Thermoformed Packaging in Its Brand Relaunch

 Brookville, United States - October 31, 2026 / Complete Packaging Solutions /

 BROOKVILLE, Ind. (October 30, 2026) - Complete Packaging Solutions has unveiled a refreshed brand identity and a newly enhanced digital presence. This website relaunch marks a significant step in the company's ongoing evolution as a full-service provider of turnkey packaging solutions.

A Brand Identity Built on Operational DepthThe rebrand is designed to more accurately represent Complete Packaging Solutions' entire service portfolio. The company covers every phase of the packaging lifecycle, positioning it to serve clients who require integrated, end-to-end support rather than managing multiple vendors.

"This updated brand and website represent where we are as a company and where we are headed," said Jeff Franks, CEO of Complete Packaging Solutions. "We have always placed the customer at the center of everything we do by continuously investing in our manufacturing capabilities and operational excellence."

Enhanced Website Streamlines the Path to Custom PackagingThe redesigned website is structured to reduce friction for packaging buyers, engineers, and procurement teams seeking turnkey packaging solutions. Visitors can explore the company's full-service offering, request quotes, and connect directly with engineering staff through more intuitive navigation.

"The new site was built with our partners in mind," said Abigail Knapp, Senior Sales Manager of Complete Packaging Solutions. "Whether someone is exploring custom packaging options for the first time or ready to submit specifications for a quote, the site makes it faster and easier to start a conversation."

Thermoforming and Full-Service Capabilities Under One RoofAmong the company's core offerings is thermoformed packaging, a process used across a wide range of industries to produce custom trays, clamshells, blisters, and protective inserts. Complete Packaging Solutions integrates thermoformed packaging with downstream services including assembly, kitting, warehousing, and fulfillment, allowing clients to consolidate their packaging supply chain with a single partner.

Custom tooling is also developed in-house, giving the company direct control over lead times and design iteration. This capability is a critical factor for clients managing complex packaging programs.

The combination of design, manufacturing, and logistics under one operational structure is a defining characteristic of Complete Packaging Solutions' approach to serving its market. The newly launched brand identity and website are intended to make that structure more visible and accessible to the industries and companies the organization works with.

About Complete Packaging SolutionsComplete Packaging Solutions is a full-service packaging manufacturer specializing in custom thermoforming, retail packaging systems, contract packaging, and fulfillment operations. The leadership team combines 130 years of industry experience across packaging engineering, manufacturing, logistics, and production management. That depth of knowledge positions the company to work alongside manufacturers, engineers, purchasing teams, and operations leaders in addressing complex packaging challenges.
