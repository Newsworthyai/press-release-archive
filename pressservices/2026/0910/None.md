# Summit Cleaning &amp; Restoration Site Launch Timed to Oregon Wet Season

Summit Cleaning & Restoration Times SummitClean.com Launch to Rise in Water Damage Calls

 Stayton, United States - September 10, 2026 / Summit Cleaning & Restoration /

 Summit Cleaning & Restoration, a disaster mitigation and restoration company serving Oregon and Southwest Washington, launched its newly redesigned website, SummitClean.com, on Friday, Aug. 28.

The redesigned site was built to serve as a practical resource for homeowners and insurance professionals navigating the aftermath of fire, wind, mold, and water damage, bringing relevant information, service details, and answers together in one place at the moment people need them most.

SummitClean.com features detailed descriptions of Summit's services, frequently asked questions, service area information, customer accounts, and a Learning Center covering what to expect after property damage occurs. The site is designed to help homeowners and insurance professionals locate useful information and understand the restoration process. All content is freely accessible with no account or form submission required.

"When someone experiences fire or water damage in their home, they need answers quickly and they need someone they can trust," said Matt Davies, Owner and Operator of Summit Cleaning & Restoration. "Our new website was built to provide helpful information and give people a better understanding of what comes next during what can be a very stressful time."

The launch arrives ahead of Oregon's wet season, when heavy rainfall and winter freeze-and-thaw cycles typically drive an increase in water damage calls across the Willamette Valley and the Portland metro area.

Summit Cleaning & Restoration has longstanding roots in the Pacific Northwest. The company traces its origins to 1978, when it operated as a small family-owned business in Stayton serving customers who were also neighbors. It was established under the Summit name in 2005 by Brian and Angie Harper. Today, the company is owned and operated by Matt Davies and serves communities throughout Oregon and Southwest Washington from offices in Portland, Stayton, Redmond, and Junction City.

"We came up in a small town, where the person whose house flooded was somebody you knew," Davies said. "That hasn't changed for us. Putting this information out for free, whether or not anybody ever hires us, is the same idea."

Summit is IICRC certified and licensed in both Oregon and Washington. The company guarantees a two-hour emergency response and assigns a dedicated project manager to every job.

The website launch represents a continued effort by Summit to function as an accessible resource for homeowners and insurance professionals before, during, and after a disaster event.

About Summit Cleaning & Restoration

Summit Cleaning & Restoration is a family-owned disaster mitigation and restoration company serving Oregon and Southwest Washington. With offices in Portland, Stayton, Redmond, and Junction City, Summit specializes in fire and soot, wind, mold, and water damage restoration.
