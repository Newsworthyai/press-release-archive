# Bubba Goodcrumb's Bernadette Valer Launches Gifting Line for Bakers

Bernadette Valer of Bubba Goodcrumb Introduces Plush and Starter Gifting Line

 Las Vegas, United States - September 15, 2026 / Bubba Goodcrumb /

 Bubba Goodcrumb founder Bernadette Valer has officially launched Bubba's Babies, a huggable sourdough boule plush designed to bring the warmth of artisan bread culture into homes across the country. The Bubba's Baby sourdough plush, priced at $34.99, is now available nationwide with shipping to all 50 U.S. states and Washington D.C., marking a new direction for Bubba Goodcrumb in connecting living fermentation traditions with everyday gifting.

A Plush That Looks the Part

The Bubba's Baby sourdough plush was designed with the details that home bakers recognize. Each plush features a golden crust finish, flour freckles, a scored top, and a smiling face - a nod to the real boules that emerge from home ovens across the country. The result is a soft, collectible companion that speaks directly to the sourdough community. At $34.99, the plush is available as a standalone item on the Bubba's Baby product page.

Bernadette Valer has built the Bubba Goodcrumb brand around keeping sourdough fermentation approachable and active in home kitchens. The launch of the Bubba's Baby sourdough plush reflects that same philosophy - making bread baking culture something that can be gifted, shared, and felt, not just practiced.

Sourdough Starter Bundles Pair the Plush with a Living Culture

For those looking to give more than a plush, Bubba Goodcrumb's sourdough starter bundles pair Bubba's Babies with the brand's dehydrated living sourdough starter culture, Bubba's Classic Starter. Two bundle options are available. The Baby Bundle is priced at $49.99 and includes one Bubba's Babies plush alongside the starter culture. The Babies Bundle - three Bubba's Babies plush plus one starter for $99.99 - expands the offering for gifters looking to share the experience with multiple recipients or across a household.

Both sourdough starter bundles are structured around Valer's focus on connecting people to heritage fermentation practices through gifts that feel personal and playful. The living dehydrated starter culture included in each bundle gives recipients what they need to begin their own sourdough journey.

"Bubba's Baby is for every home baker who has ever loved their starter like it was something living - because it is," said Bernadette Valer, founder of Bubba Goodcrumb. "The bundles bring that connection full circle, pairing the plush with a real living culture so the gift can grow right alongside the person who receives it."

Nationwide Availability and Where to Shop

All products ship to all 50 U.S. states and Washington D.C., making the launch accessible to the full sourdough baking community across the country. The standalone Bubba's Baby sourdough plush and both sourdough starter bundles are available through the dedicated Bubba's Babies landing page, with additional product details on the Bubba Goodcrumb product page.

The launch positions Bubba Goodcrumb to reach gift shoppers looking for something that reflects a genuine connection to the craft of sourdough baking rather than generic kitchen accessories. With the plush and the living starter culture now available together, the brand offers a gift rooted in fermentation heritage and the straightforward pleasure of giving something that resonates.

About Bubba Goodcrumb

Bubba Goodcrumb is a sourdough-focused brand founded by Bernadette Valer. The brand offers living dehydrated sourdough starter cultures and related products designed to make heritage fermentation practices accessible to home bakers. Bubba Goodcrumb ships to all 50 U.S. states and Washington D.C.
