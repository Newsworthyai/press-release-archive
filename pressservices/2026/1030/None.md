# TriState Barn Builders Constructs Open-Span Timber Frame Barn Structures

Timber Frame Barn and Horse Arena Construction Available Across the Mid-Atlantic Region

 Newburg, United States - October 29, 2026 / TriState Barn Builders /

 TriState Barn Builders has extended its turnkey design-build services to formally include equestrian construction projects across Pennsylvania, Virginia, Maryland, and West Virginia. The move positions the Newburg, Pennsylvania-based company to address growing demand from horse property owners throughout the Mid-Atlantic region.

The company's service model relies on an in-house crew that oversees every phase of a project - from initial design and permitting through completed construction. That single-source structure applies to custom horse barn builds, horse arena construction, and timber frame barn structures, giving property owners a defined point of accountability at each stage of their project.

Turnkey Services Under One RoofTriState Barn Builders holds a Class A contractor license, which covers the full scope of residential and commercial construction work required for large-scale equestrian facilities. The design-build structure means clients work with the same team from the first site visit through the final walkthrough, rather than coordinating between separate architecture, permitting, and construction firms.

The in-house crew handles structural planning, local permitting submissions, and on-site construction directly. For horse property owners navigating municipal requirements across multiple states, having a single contractor manage the entire process reduces coordination time and helps keep project timelines on track.

Custom Horse Barn and Arena Projects Across the Mid-AtlanticThe company builds across four states - Pennsylvania, Virginia, Maryland, and West Virginia - with its base of operations in Newburg, Pennsylvania. Projects range from individual stall configurations in a custom horse barn to full-scale horse arena construction, including covered and indoor riding arenas that require precise structural engineering to span open interior spaces without supporting columns.

Timber frame barn construction represents a distinct segment of the company's work, combining traditional joinery techniques with current engineering standards. Timber frame structures are frequently selected for equestrian applications because of their open-span capability, structural longevity, and flexibility for multi-use layouts that incorporate stall areas, tack rooms, wash bays, and hay storage.

Owner-Led Process for Each BuildTriState Barn Builders operates on an owner-led model, meaning the company principal remains directly involved in project oversight rather than delegating management to subcontractors or project managers outside the core team. This structure aligns with the company's design-build approach, where the same group handles both design decisions and construction execution throughout the project.

For equestrian property owners in the Mid-Atlantic region, the multi-state service area means the company can take on projects in rural and semi-rural settings that require contractors familiar with state-specific permitting requirements, site access considerations, and agricultural construction standards. The company's geographic footprint across Pennsylvania, Virginia, Maryland, and West Virginia positions it to serve a region where equestrian land use is common and demand for purpose-built horse facilities reflects long-standing agricultural and recreational traditions.

About TriState Barn BuildersTriState Barn Builders is a Newburg, Pennsylvania-based design-build contractor specializing in custom horse barns, horse arenas, and timber frame barn construction. The company serves clients across Pennsylvania, Virginia, Maryland, and West Virginia through a Class A-licensed, owner-led build process that manages design, permitting, and construction in-house.
