# David S. Fence Reaches Greenville, SC After a Decade in Charlotte

After 10-Plus Years in Charlotte, David S. Fence Launches Greenville, SC Services

 Charlotte, United States - August 4, 2026 / David S. Fence Company /

 CHARLOTTE, N.C. - August 3, 2026 - David S. Fence Company has extended its residential and commercial fence installation services into Greenville, South Carolina, while maintaining its established presence across the Charlotte, North Carolina market. The move brings the company's wood, vinyl, aluminum, chain-link and commercial fencing options to homeowners, businesses, property managers and contractors throughout both regions.

With more than a decade of experience planning and installing fences for residential and commercial properties, David S. Fence offers free, no-obligation estimates and provides guidance on materials, style, privacy, security, maintenance considerations and project budgets.

"Greenville is a natural extension of the work our team has been doing throughout the Charlotte area," said Felix Martinez, owner of David S. Fence Company. "This expansion allows us to serve more property owners while maintaining a consistent process for planning, material selection and installation."

Fence Options for Homes and BusinessesDavid S. Fence installs pressure-treated wood fencing, vinyl fencing, aluminum fencing, chain-link fencing and custom gates. Wood options include privacy, picket and other traditional designs, while vinyl presents a lower-maintenance alternative that does not require regular painting, sanding or staining.

Aluminum fencing serves as an ornamental option for yards, landscaped areas and certain pool applications. Chain-link fencing is available in multiple heights and finishes for properties where visibility, boundary definition or security is a priority.

The company's commercial fencing services include chain-link, vinyl, ornamental and industrial fencing options. Commercial projects may incorporate gates and access-control features based on the property's layout and specific security requirements.

Serving Charlotte and Greenville Property OwnersDavid S. Fence continues to provide fence installation in Charlotte, NC, and surrounding communities. The Greenville expansion carries those same residential and commercial services to property owners throughout the Greenville area.

Each project begins with an on-site evaluation and a review of the customer's intended use for the fence. The company assesses property layout, material preferences, maintenance expectations, appearance goals, privacy needs, security considerations and budget before presenting a project estimate.

Property owners seeking fence services in Greenville, SC, or the Charlotte area may contact David S. Fence to request additional information or schedule a free estimate. Financing information is available for qualifying projects.

About David S. Fence CompanyDavid S. Fence Company is a residential and commercial fence contractor serving Charlotte, North Carolina; Greenville, South Carolina; and nearby communities. The company installs wood, vinyl, aluminum, chain-link and commercial fencing, along with custom gates and selected access-control options. David S. Fence provides free estimates and works with homeowners, businesses, property managers and contractors.
