# Handmade Apple Wood Pencils Crafted From Pruned Winesap Branches

Artist H.M. Peavy Transforms Winter Apple Tree Prunings Into No. 2 Twig Pencils

 Palm Springs, United States - October 19, 2026 / Re-psychles Vintage Shop & Art Gallery /

 Artist H.M. Peavy has unveiled a collection of handcrafted No. 2 graphite and colored drawing pencils made entirely from seasonal winter prunings harvested from mature Winesap apple trees -- bringing a zero-waste approach to fine art tools and sustainable gifting.

From Orchard Pruning to Drawing Tool

Rather than allowing winter prunings to be discarded or composted, H.M. Peavy rescues the freshly cut branches before they leave the orchard. Each apple wood pencil is shaped by hand from a single Winesap branch, retaining its natural bark casing as the exterior shell. No synthetic coatings, no plastic sleeves, and no living tree is felled in the process. The result is a fully functional pencil -- available in No. 2 graphite and colored drawing formulations -- that is both biodegradable and traceable to a specific tree species and seasonal harvest.

The collection is now available through Apple Pencil at the twig pencils collection.

A Zero-Waste Philosophy Rooted in the Orchard

The practice of sourcing from prunings -- rather than milling timber -- is central to how H.M. Peavy defines the work. Every winter, mature Winesap apple trees are pruned as part of standard orchard maintenance. Those cuttings, which would otherwise serve no further purpose, become the raw material for each handmade pencil in the collection.

"The tree gives what it no longer needs, and the branch becomes something a person can create with for years," said H.M. Peavy, artist of Apple Pencil. "Nothing is wasted. That is the whole point."

The natural bark of the Winesap branch is preserved as the pencil's outer casing, giving each piece a distinct grain, color variation, and tactile quality that manufactured pencils cannot replicate. Because no two branches are identical, no two pencils are either.

Plastic-Free and Built for Fine Art Use

The pencils are constructed without any plastic components, adhesives, or lacquers that would interfere with their biodegradability. The twig pencil format -- drawing directly on the natural branch shape rather than cutting it into a uniform hexagonal rod -- also reduces material waste during the crafting process itself.

Each pencil functions as a standard drawing tool, compatible with conventional sharpeners and suited for sketching, illustration, and fine art work. The colored drawing versions expand the collection's utility beyond graphite, giving artists access to a range of pigments within the same bark-cased format.

Sustainable Gifting With Functional Intent

The collection has drawn attention as an option for sustainable gifting, particularly among buyers seeking alternatives to plastic-wrapped or conventionally manufactured art supplies. Because each handmade pencil originates from a documented species -- the Winesap apple tree -- and is made without industrial inputs, the product carries a verifiable material story from orchard to finished tool.

"When someone uses one of these pencils, they are holding a branch that was alive last winter," said H.M. Peavy. "That connection is not incidental -- it is what the work is about."

The apple wood pencil collection reflects a broader practice of finding creative potential in materials that conventional production chains would overlook. Each seasonal harvest determines the volume of pencils available, meaning the collection is inherently limited by what the orchard yields rather than by manufacturing capacity.

About Apple Pencil

Apple Pencil is a studio practice founded by artist H.M. Peavy, focused on handcrafting writing and drawing tools from pruned Winesap apple tree branches. The collection includes No. 2 graphite and colored drawing pencils, each made with a natural bark casing, no plastic components, and no materials sourced from felled trees.
