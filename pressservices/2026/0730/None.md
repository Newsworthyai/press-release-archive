# Ray Wakley's Car Care &amp; RV Center Expands RV Rentals and Support in PA

RV Dealers in Northeast PA Expand 2026 Selection of Campers for Sale and New RV Options

 North East, United States - July 30, 2026 / Ray Wakley's Car Care & RV Center /

 NORTH EAST, PENNSYLVANIA, July 30, 2026 — Ray Wakley’s Car Care & RV Center reports an updated 2026 RV inventory in North East, Pennsylvania, including new and used travel trailers, Class C motorhomes, Class B camper vans, and rental-ready units. The update supports growing demand across Pennsylvania for flexible RV travel options.

The dealership continues to serve buyers searching for campers for sale in northeast PA, along with RV rental options and full-service support.

![2025 EAST TO WEST Alita 23TK-seat](https://storage.googleapis.com/signal-genesys-press-releases-prod/images/AwBFoqeIJom33uzAlCmEemwYMtFAz6FePEU1xYwi.jpg)

Updated 2026 RV Inventory Across Multiple ClassesThe company has expanded its 2026 lineup with new arrivals from Coachmen, Forest River, East to West, and Winnebago, including travel trailers, camper units, and motorized RVs.

Travel Trailer and Towable UnitsThe updated selection includes bunkhouse layouts, rear bath floorplans, and outdoor kitchen options. These RVs support families and groups that need flexible sleeping space and storage, with lightweight models suited for easier towing.

Motorhome OfferingsClass C and Class B motorhomes remain available, offering built-in living and driving setups. Options range from compact couple-friendly layouts to larger family configurations with added sleeping capacity.

Interest in RV dealers in Northeast, PA continues to grow as buyers compare towing limits, layouts, and efficiency.

RV Rentals for Seasonal and Short-Term TravelThe company offers RV rentals for customers seeking short-term travel options without ownership. Rental units include Class C motorhomes designed for vacations, weekend trips, and seasonal use.

Rental Fleet AvailabilityRental units come equipped with sleeping areas, kitchen space, bathroom facilities, and climate systems. Each unit is maintained for road readiness and prepared for use during peak travel periods.

Travel Flexibility and UsageThe rental program allows customers to experience RV travel without purchasing a unit. It also provides an opportunity to explore different layouts and sizes before making a purchase decision.

Customers who are searching for RV rentals in Northeast, PA can view the availability, specifications, and booking details through the dealership’s rental department.

RV Sales, Service, Financing, and Support OperationsThe company operates as a full-service RV dealership offering sales, financing, maintenance, parts, and collision repair services. The dealership supports both new and used RV buyers with trade-in evaluations and financing assistance through lender partnerships.

Sales and Financing OptionsCustomers can access a wide range of RV types, including travel trailers, fifth wheels, and motorhomes. Financing programs are available for qualified buyers seeking structured payment plans for RV ownership.

Service and Maintenance DepartmentThe service department provides RV inspections, plumbing repairs, electrical diagnostics, winterization, and de-winterization services. These services help maintain RV performance across multiple travel seasons.

Collision and Parts SupportAn on-site collision center handles RV body repairs, paint restoration, and fiberglass work. The parts department supplies OEM components for major RV brands, supporting long-term maintenance and repair needs.

"Since 1978, Ray Wakley’s Car Care & RV Center has proudly served the Northwestern Pennsylvania region with award-winning RV sales, service, and customer care," said Ray Wakley, Owner of Ray Wakley’s Car Care & RV Center.

About Ray Wakley’s Car Care & RV CenterRay Wakley’s Car Care & RV Center has served in North East, Pennsylvania since 1978 as a family-owned RV dealership focused on sales, service, rentals, and customer support. The company operates from North East, Pennsylvania, and serves customers across Pennsylvania, Ohio, and New York travel corridors.

The dealership provides new and used RV inventory, RV rentals, financing support, OEM parts, and collision repair services. Its inventory includes travel trailers, Class A, Class B, and Class C motorhomes from leading manufacturers including Coachmen, Forest River, East to West, and Winnebago.
