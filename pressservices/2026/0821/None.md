# Registered NDIS Provider Closes Service Gaps in NSW and ACT

Behavior Support Practitioners Join Ability to Achieve's Expanded NSW and ACT NDIS Teams

 Sydney, Australia - August 19, 2026 / Ability to Achieve Pty Limited /

 Ability to Achieve Pty Limited has extended its ndis community participation and independent living support services across Sydney and Canberra, now reaching over 180 participants across both regions. The registered NDIS provider confirmed the expansion represents a deliberate effort to address service gaps for individuals with disability who require structured, person-centred support in their daily lives.

The broader service reach brings dedicated behavior support practitioner capacity and community access teams to more participants across New South Wales and the Australian Capital Territory. The growth responds to an increasing caseload and reflects the provider's stated focus on delivering goal-directed support that keeps participants connected to their local communities.

Expanding Access to Community Participation and Independent LivingA central focus of the expansion is strengthening ndis community participation pathways, which allow individuals with disability to engage in social, recreational, and civic activities alongside their peers. Ability to Achieve Pty Limited has structured its services to align with NDIS funding categories that support participants in building capacity and maintaining meaningful routines outside the home.

The provider's independent living support offering is designed to assist participants in developing practical skills required to live with greater autonomy. This includes support with daily tasks, decision-making, and navigating community environments - areas where consistent, skilled assistance can produce measurable outcomes over time.

Community access support forms another core component of the expanded model, connecting participants with local services, group activities, and social networks in their respective regions. Both the Sydney and Canberra teams have been structured to provide continuity of support, ensuring participants work with consistent practitioners who are familiar with their individual goals and preferences.

Qualified Behavior Support Practitioners at the Centre of CareA distinguishing element of Ability to Achieve's expanded model is its integration of a qualified behavior support practitioner within each multidisciplinary support team. These practitioners work directly with participants to assess needs, develop positive behavior support plans, and implement strategies that reduce restrictive practices in line with NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission requirements.

The inclusion of behavior support at this level reflects a broader shift within the disability sector toward evidence-informed, rights-based approaches to care. Participants whose support plans include complex behavior considerations can now access this specialised input alongside their broader community and independent living support programs, without needing to engage a separate provider.

Person-Centred Framework Guides Service DeliveryAbility to Achieve Pty Limited has built its expanded service model around the principle that each participant's support plan should reflect their own goals, preferences, and circumstances. Across Sydney and Canberra, support workers and practitioners are guided by individualised plans developed in collaboration with participants, their families, and relevant allied health professionals.

The provider's community access support teams in both cities are equipped to work across a range of settings, including public transport, community venues, educational environments, and social programs. This flexibility is designed to ensure participants can pursue genuine community integration rather than a narrow set of pre-defined activities.

The expansion also positions Ability to Achieve to accommodate participants at different stages of their NDIS journey - from those newly approved for funding to individuals seeking to change providers or increase the scope of their existing supports. With over 180 participants now receiving services across the two regions, the provider continues to accept new referrals from participants, plan managers, and support coordinators in both Sydney and Canberra.

About Ability to Achieve Pty LimitedAbility to Achieve Pty Limited is a registered NDIS provider delivering community participation, independent living support, behavior support, and community access services to participants across Sydney and Canberra. The organisation operates with a person-centred approach, supporting individuals with disability to pursue their goals and engage actively in their communities.
