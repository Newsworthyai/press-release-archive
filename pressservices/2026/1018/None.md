# Auburn Orthodontist Offers Free Evaluations for Kids Around Age 7

O.M.I Orthodontics in Auburn, IN Provides Free Early Orthodontic Evaluations for Children

 Auburn, United States - October 17, 2026 / OMI Orthodontics /

 O-M-I Orthodontics, located on West Seventh Street in Auburn, Indiana, offers DeKalb County parents free early orthodontic evaluations for children around age 7. The practice, led by Dr. Joel Johnson, offers these no-cost assessments to help families understand where their child's jaw development, bite, and tooth spacing stand -- before problems become more complex to address.

What the Evaluation Covers

The free evaluation at O-M-I Orthodontics is designed to give parents a clear picture of their child's oral development at a critical stage. At approximately age 7, the mouth contains a mix of baby teeth and permanent teeth, which allows Dr. Johnson to assess jaw width, bite alignment, and whether enough space exists for incoming permanent teeth. This window offers a practical opportunity to identify concerns that may benefit from early intervention -- or to confirm that no treatment is needed at this time.

Dr. Joel Johnson, who sees patients at the Auburn office located on West Seventh Street, conducts these evaluations with a straightforward approach: many children who come in are simply placed on a monitoring schedule rather than started on any treatment. The goal is accurate information for parents, not a push toward unnecessary procedures.

When Early Treatment Is and Is Not Recommended

A significant portion of children evaluated at O-M-I Orthodontics leave with no immediate treatment plan. Instead, Dr. Johnson and his team track the child's development over time, bringing them back periodically to reassess. This monitoring approach means families are not committed to treatment until there is a clear clinical reason to act.

In cases where a concern is identified, a limited first phase of treatment may be recommended to address a single, specific issue. One example is the use of a palatal expander, a removable or fixed appliance that gradually widens a narrow upper jaw or corrects a crossbite. This kind of targeted intervention is focused on resolving one problem early, which can simplify or reduce the scope of any treatment that may be needed later in adolescence.

Parents searching for an orthodontist in Auburn, IN will find that the practice's approach to early evaluation centers on clinical need rather than routine intervention. Not every child who comes in at age 7 will require a palatal expander or any appliance at all. The evaluation itself provides clarity, which many parents find useful regardless of the outcome.

For parents who are uncertain whether their child needs to be seen this early, the American Association of Orthodontists has long recommended an initial orthodontic screening around age 7. Dr. Johnson's evaluations align with that guidance, offering families in the Auburn area a local option to follow through on that recommendation without a financial barrier at the first visit.

The practice also sees older children and adolescents considering braces in Auburn, IN, but the free early evaluation program is specifically structured around the age-7 developmental checkpoint, where the combination of mixed dentition and active jaw growth makes assessment most informative.
