# Riptide Offers South Jersey Homeowners a Free Holiday Window Cleaning Deal

Riptide Pressure Washing Unveils Holiday Lighting Special With Complimentary Window Cleaning

 Swedesboro, United States - November 23, 2026 / Riptide Pressure Washing /

 Riptide Pressure Washing is adding a free window cleaning to its holiday light installations, giving South Jersey homeowners a way to hand off two-ladder jobs in the same season. The offer comes as households across Gloucester, Camden, and Salem counties enter the late-November and post-Thanksgiving weeks when most outdoor displays go up. Service details and booking information are posted on the company's holiday light installation page.

Why South Jersey Homeowners Face Ladder Risks Each Holiday SeasonPutting lights along a roofline usually means extension ladders, gutter edges, and cold hands. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission estimates about 160 decorating-related injuries a day during the holiday season, and its holiday decorating injury data shows nearly half of those injuries involve falls. Weather narrows the margin in this part of the state. Rutgers University's New Jersey climate overview notes that snow can fall in the southern counties from about mid-November, the same stretch when many families start decorating. South Jersey's humid seasons also leave organic growth and grime on local homes by late fall, so the windows framed by a new display often need attention at the same time. For many households, that adds up to two jobs that both start on a ladder.

What South Jersey Homeowners Can Expect From the PackageUnder the new offer, both jobs move to Riptide Pressure Washing's crew. The company supplies professional-grade C9 and C7 lights, installs them along roof lines and gutter lines, and returns during the season if a bulb or strand stops working. Roof line and gutter line installation runs $7 to $10 per linear foot, and that price includes January takedown and storage, with lights labeled by strand and location so the same set goes back up the following year. Bushes, wreaths, and standing displays are priced separately. The free window cleaning uses a water-fed pole and purified water, which dries without streaks and needs no wiping.

Riptide Pressure Washing on Keeping South Jersey Displays LitThe company said in-season repair visits are part of every installation.

"If a bulb goes out or something stops working, call us and we'll come back and fix it. We supply professional-grade lights on every job so the installation looks the part from the first bulb to the last. Adding the window cleaning means nobody in the house has to climb a ladder for either one this year," said Shayden, one of the owners at Riptide Pressure Washing.

Where South Jersey Homeowners Can Learn MoreHomeowners in Gloucester, Camden, and Salem counties can find more information about the offer on the Riptide Pressure Washing website and follow company updates on Riptide Pressure Washing's Facebook page.

About Riptide Pressure WashingRiptide Pressure Washing is an exterior cleaning company founded in 2022 and based in Swedesboro, N.J. The company serves homes and commercial properties across Gloucester, Camden, and Salem counties in South Jersey, offering house washing, roof cleaning, driveway and concrete cleaning, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, paver sealing, and holiday light installation.
