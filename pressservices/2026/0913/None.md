# Rucker Orthodontics Offers Braces for All Ages in Temecula

Braces for Kids, Teens and Adults Available at Rucker Orthodontics in Temecula and Murrieta

 Temecula, United States - September 13, 2026 / Rucker Orthodontics /

 Rucker Orthodontics has announced the formal availability of its full braces program for children, teenagers, and adults across its two Southern California offices, with orthodontist-directed care provided by brothers Dr. Jon Rucker and Dr. Ryan Rucker at locations serving Temecula and Murrieta. The announcement consolidates the practice's treatment offerings under a single, clearly communicated program designed to address orthodontic needs at every stage of life.

Orthodontist-Led Care at Two Locations

Rucker Orthodontics operates out of offices in Temecula and Murrieta, California, giving residents in both communities direct access to specialist orthodontic care without traveling outside the region. Dr. Jon Rucker and Dr. Ryan Rucker each hold orthodontic specialization, meaning treatment decisions are made by orthodontic specialists. For patients researching braces in Temecula CA, the practice offers in-office consultations at no charge, allowing families and individuals to understand their options before committing to a treatment plan.

The dual-location model means patients from neighboring communities also have a nearby option. Those seeking braces in Murrieta CA can access the same scope of services, the same treatment technology, and the same orthodontist-directed approach at the Murrieta office as at the Temecula location.

Braces Options for Kids, Teens, and Adults

The practice provides several types of orthodontic appliances, with the Damon System braces among the featured options. The Damon System uses a self-ligating bracket design, which differs from conventional braces that rely on elastic ties to hold the archwire in place. This system is available for qualifying patients across age groups.

Clear aligner therapy, including Invisalign, is also offered for patients who prefer a removable, less visible alternative to fixed braces. Invisalign treatment at Rucker Orthodontics follows the same orthodontist-led supervision model as bracket-based treatment.

For adults specifically, the practice addresses a segment of patients who may have delayed orthodontic treatment or are seeking correction for the first time as adults. Braces for adults in Temecula CA are available through the same consultation and treatment process used for younger patients, with treatment plans tailored to individual clinical needs rather than a one-size approach.

Digital Treatment Planning

Rucker Orthodontics incorporates digital treatment planning tools into its workflow. Digital planning allows the clinical team to map out tooth movement in detail before treatment begins, giving patients a clearer picture of expected outcomes and enabling the orthodontists to refine the treatment sequence with greater precision. The practice describes this digital workflow most fully for its Invisalign clear aligner treatment, where 3D scans and aligner-planning software are used to map tooth movement.

"We work with patients of all ages and backgrounds, and having both a Temecula and a Murrieta location means more families in this part of Southern California can access orthodontic care from an actual orthodontic specialist," said Dr. Jon Rucker, orthodontist at Rucker Orthodontics.

Free Consultations Available

Rucker Orthodontics offers complimentary initial consultations for new patients at both offices. During the consultation, one of the Rucker brothers evaluates the patient's current dental and jaw alignment, discusses which treatment options are clinically appropriate, and outlines what a treatment timeline could look like. The no-cost consultation is available to children, teenagers, and adults alike, including those exploring braces for adults in Temecula CA for the first time.

Families with children who may be approaching the age when orthodontic evaluation is recommended can schedule through either office. The American Association of Orthodontists generally recommends an initial orthodontic evaluation around age seven, though treatment does not necessarily begin at that point.

About Rucker Orthodontics

Rucker Orthodontics is a specialty orthodontic practice led by brothers Dr. Jon Rucker and Dr. Ryan Rucker, with offices in Temecula and Murrieta, California. The practice provides orthodontic treatment for children, teenagers, and adults, including Damon System braces, clear aligners, Invisalign, and digital treatment planning. All patient care is directed by the practice's orthodontic specialists, and complimentary consultations are available at both locations.
