# Nirvanai Agents Scan Supplier Invoices to Recover Owed Funds

Nirvanai AI Agents Detect Invoice Overcharges and Missed Rebates Owed to Enterprises

 Los Angeles, United States - November 22, 2026 / Terzo /

 LOS ANGELES, November 19, 2026 - Companies are under growing pressure to control expenses, but some of their largest savings opportunities may be concealed within the supplier contracts and invoices they already hold.

According to recent research from Grant Thornton, 49% of finance leaders are targeting vendor and supplier costs for reductions, up from 29% in the previous quarter. The findings point to a persistent challenge across corporate America: protecting profit margins while continuing to invest in technology, innovation and growth.

Identifying where money is being lost, however, remains difficult. Large enterprises manage thousands of supplier relationships across disconnected financial systems, making it hard to detect overpayments, missed rebates, unfavorable pricing and opportunities to renegotiate contracts.

Terzo Technologies Inc. is addressing this problem through the relaunch of its Nirvanai Platform, which introduces a new generation of AI-powered Savings Agents designed to surface and act on financial opportunities embedded in supplier spending data.

Terzo reports more than $1 billion in realized customer savings, including $325 million in 2026 for a single Fortune 50 company, illustrating the financial impact that AI analysis of enterprise supplier spending can produce.

"Enterprises don't have a data problem. They have an intelligence problem," said Brandon Card, CEO and founder of Terzo. "For the last several years, we've used AI to create trustworthy, structured spend data for our customers. But we gave them so much data, they couldn't act on all of it. So we built agentic workflows that run across all of that financial data, so people know exactly where to focus their time."

The Hidden Cost of Corporate SpendingFor many organizations, controlling expenses is no longer simply a matter of reducing budgets. It requires understanding where money is already being wasted.

Supplier contracts, invoices and spending records frequently reside in disconnected systems, leaving finance and procurement teams without a complete view of what their organizations agreed to pay, what they are actually paying and where potential savings exist.

Traditional manual reviews and periodic audits can leave significant financial opportunities undiscovered, particularly when companies are managing thousands of contracts and supplier relationships simultaneously.

Nirvanai addresses this gap by connecting and analyzing contracts, invoices and spending data, helping organizations identify potential savings and translate financial insights into concrete action.

AI Agents That Find, Negotiate and Recover MoneyThe relaunched Nirvanai platform introduces expanded Savings Agents built to continuously analyze supplier relationships and identify opportunities across three areas:

Reduce costs: Identify supplier consolidation opportunities, pricing discrepancies and unnecessary expenses that might otherwise go unnoticed.

Negotiate smarter: Analyze existing agreements, spending patterns and renewal opportunities to help procurement teams strengthen their negotiating positions and secure more favorable terms.

Recover money: Detect potential overcharges, missed credits, rebates and warranty-related opportunities to help companies recover funds they may be owed.

Unlike traditional financial dashboards that primarily present information, Nirvanai's agents are designed to prioritize opportunities based on financial impact and provide supporting evidence drawn directly from the underlying contracts, invoices and calculations.

"Nirvanai Savings Agents are finding opportunities that would take humans years to uncover," said Card. "Companies hire consultants to find this money. It's expensive, it's slow, and when they leave, you're left with a deck. Our agents do that work in a matter of minutes, and they keep working long after the consultants are gone."

Turning Financial Intelligence Into Measurable ResultsIdentifying potential savings is only the first step. Capturing them requires coordination, informed decisions and follow-through.

The expanded Nirvanai platform introduces several collaborative capabilities to help finance and procurement teams move from analysis to execution.

Insights ranks financial opportunities and recommends next steps. Chat Assistant answers questions about suppliers, contracts and spending, with supporting source documents. Projects enables teams to collaborate with AI agents on negotiations and savings initiatives, while Artifacts converts findings into briefs, financial analyses and leadership reports.

The platform also tracks savings outcomes, giving organizations a way to measure the financial impact of their decisions over time.

"Procurement teams shouldn't have to spend their days playing data detective," said Spencer Ross, director of global sales at Terzo. "Nirvanai shows where money is being lost, the evidence behind it and a clear path to action."

As enterprises increasingly demand measurable returns from AI investments, Terzo is focused on a direct financial application: helping companies identify and capture savings from spending already underway.

"The companies that use AI to spend smarter will be the ones that win," said Card. "We're turning AI into a money-finding machine for the enterprise."
