# Keystone RV Center Showcases Wolf Camper Options in Greencastle, PA

Keystone RV Center Offers Travel Trailers and Columbus Fifth Wheels

 Greencastle, United States - August 28, 2026 / Keystone RV Center /

 GREENCASTLE, PENNSYLVANIA, August 28, 2026 — Keystone RV Center is highlighting its current recreational vehicle inventory in Greencastle, Pennsylvania, with a selection of new and used RV models that includes Wolf camper options. The dealership’s inventory update provides RV buyers with access to different RV categories, including travel trailers, fifth wheels, toy haulers, and motorhomes.

The dealer continues to serve customers looking for recreational vehicles with layouts and features suited for family camping, weekend travel, and longer road trips. They maintain a broad inventory from multiple manufacturers while providing RV sales, service, parts, and financing support.

Wolf Camper Options for Family CampingThe company’s inventory includes Forest River Cherokee models, offering customers access to several camper options. These campers are available in different floorplans designed around varying travel preferences, including family camping, outdoor recreation, and flexible campsite use.

Wolf Camper Models Include Practical Travel FeaturesWolf Camper options available through the dealership include Cherokee Grey Wolf, Cherokee Alpha Wolf, Wolf Den, Wolf Pup, and Wolf Pack models. Depending on the specific unit, features may include:

* Bunkhouse layouts for families needing additional sleeping areas
* Outdoor kitchens for campsite meal preparation
* Slide-outs that create additional interior space
* Storage solutions for camping equipment
* Floorplans designed for different group sizes

These camper options allow RV buyers to compare different layouts based on towing preferences, travel plans, and desired amenities.

Columbus Fifth Wheel For Larger RV Living OptionsThe dealership is also highlighting its available Columbus fifth wheel inventory for customers interested in larger recreational vehicles. Fifth wheels are commonly selected by travelers who need more interior space, residential-style layouts, and additional features for extended camping trips.

Spacious Floor Plans Supports Extended TravelThe dealership offers fifth wheel models, including the Forest River RV Columbus 388FKH and Palomino Columbus 376DS. These units feature spacious layouts designed to provide added living space, comfort, and flexibility for extended trips and family travel.

Features available may include:

* Front kitchen designs
* Multiple slide-out rooms
* Kitchen islands
* Loft areas in select floorplans
* Bath and half layouts
* Larger sleeping areas

The Columbus lineup provides another option for RV buyers comparing fifth wheels for family travel, seasonal camping, or longer stays.

Overview of RV InventoryThe company continues to maintain a wide selection of recreational vehicles at its dealership in Greencastle, Pennsylvania. Its inventory includes new and used models across various categories, such as travel trailers, fifth wheels, toy haulers, and Class A, Class B, and Class C motorhomes.

The dealership carries brands including Forest River, Jayco, Keystone RV, Palomino, Coachmen, and Thor Motor Coach, giving customers searching for RVs in Greencastle, PA, options the ability to compare RV styles based on size, features, and travel needs.

Support Beyond RV SalesIn addition to RV inventory, the company provides services that support customers throughout RV ownership. The dealership operates RV service and repair operations along with a parts department for maintenance needs and accessories.

Available dealership services include:

* New and used RV sales
* RV service and repair
* RV parts and accessories
* Financing options

Located near Interstate 81, the dealership serves RV buyers throughout Pennsylvania and surrounding areas, including Maryland, West Virginia, Virginia, New Jersey, Delaware, Ohio, and New York.
