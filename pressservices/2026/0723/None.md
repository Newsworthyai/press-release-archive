# California Tightens Security Deposit Rules: Move-Out Cleaning Is Best Defense

New California Deposit Rules Are in Effect. Here Is What Los Angeles Renters Should Do Before They Move Out

 Redondo Beach, United States - July 23, 2026 / Citrus Fresh Housekeeping /

 With deposits capped at one month's rent and landlords now required to photograph units before and after cleaning, an itemized move-out clean has become one of the clearest ways for Los Angeles tenants to protect their money.

A series of changes to California's security deposit law has shifted the ground under renters and landlords alike, and many tenants still do not know the rules exist.

Deposits are now capped at one month's rent for most renters, and landlords who deduct for cleaning must photograph a unit before and after the work.

Under Assembly Bill 12, which took effect July 1, 2024, most security deposits are limited to a single month's rent, down from the two months many landlords previously collected. Assembly Bill 2801 added a second layer, phasing in a requirement through 2025 that a landlord photograph a unit before and after any cleaning or repairs charged against a deposit. Separately, California Civil Code 1950.5 gives landlords 21 days to return a deposit or send an itemized statement, and requires copies of receipts for any deduction over $125.

Taken together, the rules cut both ways. A smaller deposit means a single cleaning charge now consumes a much larger share of what a tenant has at stake. At the same time, the new photo requirement means a landlord has to document any cleaning claim, which gives a prepared renter more leverage than before.

Renters and landlords are now focusing on hiring professional inspection-grade move-out cleaners to prove the work was done properly. Unlike standard cleanings, a move-out clean is performed in an empty home, so it reaches the interiors an inspection actually checks: inside cabinets and drawers, ovens, refrigerators and pantry shelving. Citrus Fresh Housekeeping, a South Bay los angeles house cleaning company says they finish every job with an itemized receipt on company letterhead, which a tenant can hand a landlord as proof the unit was professionally cleaned. The company recently contributed to a detailed move-out cleaning guide that walks renters through the full checklist and the current deposit rules.

“Renters call us every week asking whether the clean will pass inspection and whether they will get a receipt for their landlord,” said Lucy, customer service representative at Citrus Fresh Housekeeping. “With deposits now capped at a single month of rent, that receipt matters more than ever. A move-out clean that actually opens the cabinets and the oven, paired with an itemized invoice on our letterhead, gives a tenant something concrete to show when the deposit is on the line.”

The company notes that the same documentation helps landlords and property managers, who can use the itemized receipt to show an outgoing tenant exactly what was cleaned during a turnover. It also cautions that no cleaning company can promise a deposit will be returned, since that decision rests with the landlord. A professional clean removes cleanliness as a reason to withhold a deposit, and the receipt provides evidence if a charge appears anyway.

Citrus Fresh Housekeeping offers move-in and move-out cleaning across the South Bay and West Los Angeles, and encourages renters to book the clean after movers have cleared furniture and big items out of the unit and before the final walkthrough, so the work is complete when the unit is inspected.
