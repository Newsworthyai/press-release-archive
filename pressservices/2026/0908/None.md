# Midwest B2B Agency Marks 25 Years of Marketing

The Deciding Factor Brings 25 Years of B2B Digital Marketing Expertise for Complex Industries

 Mason, United States - September 1, 2026 / The Deciding Factor /

 MASON, OH, USA — The Deciding Factor, a Midwest-based digital marketing agency, is marking nearly 25 years of continuous operation, a milestone that reflects the firm's sustained focus on helping companies in complex industries connect with the buyers who matter most.

The anniversary arrives at a moment when B2B marketers are navigating a rapidly shifting technology landscape, and The Deciding Factor is using the occasion to reaffirm its commitment to a people-first approach in an era increasingly shaped by artificial intelligence.

Built for ComplexityFrom its roots in the Midwest, The Deciding Factor has concentrated on a specific and demanding corner of the marketing world: industries where the product is difficult to explain, the sales cycle is long, and the stakes for buyers are high. Medtech, manufacturing, SAAS and professional services have formed the core of the agency's client work, sectors where generic marketing frameworks rarely hold up against the weight of technical detail and buyer scrutiny.

The agency's philosophy, captured in its tagline Marketing That Makes Up Minds, reflects this orientation. Rather than generating awareness for its own sake, The Deciding Factor builds customer journeys designed to move people through genuine decision-making processes. That means aligning technology, content, and design into a single, coherent experience rather than treating them as separate disciplines.

People First in an AI-Driven EnvironmentThe agency's 25-year milestone comes as artificial intelligence tools are reshaping how marketing work gets done. The Deciding Factor has integrated AI into its practice, but uses technology as an amplifier rather than a replacement for human judgment and creativity.

According to President Karen Meyers Holzer, “Our position is straightforward: business ultimately occurs between people and organizations, and no automated system changes that fundamental reality.”

This perspective shapes how the agency deploys AI, using it to accelerate research, sharpen content, and improve targeting, while keeping strategic and creative decisions anchored in an understanding of how real buyers think and behave. For clients selling complex products or services, where trust and credibility are central to the purchase decision, that distinction carries practical weight.

A Holistic Model Refined Over DecadesWhat distinguishes The Deciding Factor's approach is the integration of capabilities that many agencies treat as separate offerings. Technology, content, and design work together within the agency's model, built around the goal of serving specific business objectives rather than delivering outputs in isolation. This structure has evolved over nearly 25 years of working with clients whose buyers require education, reassurance, and clear reasoning before committing to a purchase.

According to COO Karen Vance, “Our continued evolution reflects an ongoing investment in staying relevant as both technology and buyer behavior change.” She noted, “We have adapted our tools and methods across multiple cycles of digital transformation without abandoning our core belief that effective marketing must account for the human being at the center of every business decision.”

About The Deciding FactorThe Deciding Factor is a Midwest-based B2B digital marketing agency with nearly 25 years of experience working with clients in medtech, manufacturing, and professional services. The firm specializes in complex products, services, and sales cycles, building people-first customer journeys through an integrated approach to technology, content, and design.
