# Buddy Replaces Flowhub Iframe Menus With Indexed Domain Pages

How Buddy Turns a Flowhub Menu Embed Into a Search-Indexed Ecommerce Site

 Troy, United States - August 23, 2026 / Buddy /

 TROY, Mich. - Buddy, the ecommerce and brand platform built for cannabis, has announced expanded availability of its native Flowhub ecommerce integration, giving dispensaries that run Flowhub point of sale a fully owned online store that goes live in approximately 24 hours.

Most dispensaries using Flowhub sell online through embedded or hosted menus. Those menus render inside an iframe, which means the dispensary's entire product catalog is invisible to search engines, and customer data lives on a third-party domain. Buddy connects directly to Flowhub's API and converts live inventory into server-rendered product, category, and brand pages on the dispensary's own website.

The integration syncs products, pricing, stock levels, and discounts from Flowhub in real time. Orders placed on the Buddy-built storefront flow directly back into Flowhub for fulfillment and compliance, allowing retailers to maintain the point-of-sale workflow their teams already use while replacing only the ecommerce layer.

"Flowhub is a strong point of sale, but dispensaries have been settling for menu embeds that Google simply cannot read," said a Buddy spokesperson. "With Buddy, a Flowhub store gets a real ecommerce website on its own domain, with every product indexed, in about a day. Retailers should not have to choose between the POS they trust and an online store that can actually rank."

Every Buddy storefront includes search engine optimization built into its foundation: server-side rendering on every page, automatically generated product schema with live pricing and availability, per-location metadata and LocalBusiness schema, editable sitemaps and robots.txt, and landing page tools designed for high-intent searches. Dispensaries can also extend their Flowhub catalog to white-label iOS and Android mobile apps with push notifications and in-app checkout.

Buddy currently powers more than 300 dispensary and brand websites across the United States, with customers rating the platform 4.9 out of 5. Alongside Flowhub, the platform integrates with Dutchie, Treez, Jane, and Alpine IQ, and delivers new features to every site on the platform on a weekly basis.

Dispensaries running Flowhub can view a working version of their own store before making a commitment. Most launches are completed in approximately 24 hours from the point of connecting the POS.

Learn more about the Flowhub ecommerce integration or book a demo at hellobuddy.app.
