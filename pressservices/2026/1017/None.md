About Kiowa Casino Operations Authority

The Kiowa Casino Operations Authority (KCOA) is the tribal gaming enterprise of the Kiowa Tribe of Oklahoma, established to own and operate the Tribe's gaming businesses. KCOA operates three properties across Southwest Oklahoma: Kiowa Casino & Hotel (Kiowa Casino Red River) in Devol - the flagship property, open 24/7 with roughly 64,000 square feet of gaming space, 700+ slot machines, table games, a hotel of approximately 60 rooms, the Morning Star Steakhouse, a cafe, and a bar; Kiowa Casino Carnegie in Carnegie, a locally oriented venue with more than 110 slot machines and a cafe offering casual dining and beer service; and Elk Creek Kiowa Casino in Hobart, which held its grand opening in July 2025 as the first casino in Kiowa County, featuring more than 250 slot machines, table games, a bar, and the Boomarang Diner. Together, the properties generate jobs, training opportunities, and economic development for the Kiowa Tribe and surrounding Southwest Oklahoma communities.
