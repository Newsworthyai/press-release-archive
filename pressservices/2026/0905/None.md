# Oklahoma City RV Shoppers Gain Access to 200+ New and Used RVs

Wade’s RV Expands New and Used Inventory Across Oklahoma

 Glenpool, United States - August 31, 2026 / Wade's RV /

 GLENPOOL, OKLAHOMA, August 31, 2026 — Wade’s RV is expanding its current selection of new and used recreational vehicles across its Oklahoma and Missouri locations, with more than 200 RVs available for shoppers comparing options. The updated inventory includes motorhomes, travel trailers, fifth wheels, toy haulers, truck campers, and other RV types.

The inventory update gives Oklahoma City-area buyers additional choices when comparing RVs by size, floorplan, sleeping capacity, weight, price, and intended use.

![Forest River Salem FSX](https://storage.googleapis.com/signal-genesys-press-releases-prod/images/mck89ePGnGPUdS7S05LLHh8Ce1fpCsxWtc7DDXfS.jpg)

More RV Options Near Oklahoma CityThe company’s current inventory includes new and pre-owned units from a range of established RV manufacturers. Inventory changes as units are sold and new models arrive, giving shoppers a regularly updated selection.

Available RV categories include:

* Travel trailers for weekend trips and family camping
* Fifth wheels with larger living and sleeping areas
* Toy haulers for carrying recreational equipment
* Class A, B, and C motorhomes
* Truck campers designed to fit compatible pickup trucks
* Destination trailers for longer stays

For shoppers researching RV sales in Oklahoma City, the Glenpool location provides access to a range of RV types without requiring buyers to limit their search to one style of recreational vehicle.

Campers Oklahoma City Buyers Can CompareThe dealership also maintains camper inventory for buyers looking for different sizes, layouts, and travel setups. Current travel trailer inventory includes models from Forest River, Prime Time RV, and other manufacturers.

Available models vary in length, weight, sleeping capacity, and floorplan. Some smaller travel trailers are designed for lighter towing, while larger models provide additional sleeping space, slide-outs, bunkhouses, and separate living areas.

Truck Campers Add Another RV OptionThe company also carries truck campers at its Joplin location, with current inventory including models from Lance, Northern Lite, and Palomino.

Truck campers sit in the bed of a compatible pickup truck, giving owners an RV setup without towing a separate trailer. Current models vary in length, weight, sleeping capacity, and equipment, including options with solar systems and other features.

This category gives buyers searching for campers for sale in Oklahoma City, another format to consider when comparing compact RV options.

New and Used RV Inventory Continues to ChangeThe company offers both new and used RVs, allowing shoppers to compare different model years, conditions, features, and price points.

Current inventory includes examples such as:

* New Forest River Wildwood FSX travel trailers
* New Forest River Avenger models
* New Prime Time RV Tracer travel trailers
* Used Jayco travel trailers
* Used Coachmen motorhomes
* Used Thor Motor Coach models

Because inventory is updated as vehicles are sold and received, buyers looking for an RV in OKC or nearby should check current availability when comparing specific models.

Services Extend Beyond RV SalesThe company also provides services for customers who need support before and after an RV purchase. Its full-service repair department has 14 service bays and two certified paint booths.

The service department handles:

* Air conditioning and heating
* Electrical and plumbing work
* Appliance repairs
* Roof repairs and replacement
* Flooring repair and replacement
* Structural and frame repairs
* Hitch installation
* Interior reconstruction and remodeling
* Exterior body work and paint
* Warranty and insurance repairs

Trade, Consignment and Financing AvailableThe company supports customers who want to replace an existing RV or sell one they no longer need. The dealership accepts trades and operates a cash offer and consignment program for motorhomes, travel trailers, fifth wheels, toy haulers, and other RV types.

The consignment program includes no fees or commissions, with the dealership handling marketing, buyer inquiries, paperwork, and other parts of the transaction.

Financing is also available, with customers able to pre-qualify before shopping. The dealership works with multiple lenders to provide financing options based on individual buyer circumstances.

“Wade’s RV continues to update its inventory so Oklahoma and Missouri buyers can compare new and used RVs across multiple categories in one place,” said Wade Reeves, Owner of Wade’s RV.

About Wade’s RVWade’s RV is a RV dealership based in Glenpool, Oklahoma, with additional locations in Goldsby and Joplin, Missouri. The dealership carries more than 200 new and used RVs, including travel trailers, fifth wheels, toy haulers, motorhomes, truck campers, and other recreational vehicles. The team also provides full-service RV repair, parts and accessories, financing, trade-ins, consignment, and indoor RV and boat storage.
