# Robert B. Payne Heating &amp; Air: Fredericksburg Averages 18.9% of HDD Pre-December

Only 18.9% of normal heating degree days fall before December at the weather station nearest Fredericksburg

 Fredericksburg, United States - October 22, 2026 / Robert B. Payne Heating & Air /

 Across Virginia's 125 normals stations, the share of normal HDD that falls after Christmas Day (Dec 26-Jun 30) ranges from 63.2% to 70.3%.

Only 18.9% of a normal season's heating degree days (HDD) accumulate before December (Jul 1-Nov 30) at Fredericksburg's nearest-station proxy, Fredericksburg Sewage (USC00443204). At the Fredericksburg proxy station, 66.4% of normal HDD falls after Christmas Day (Dec 26-Jun 30), according to Robert B. Payne Heating & Air's analysis of the 1991-2020 U.S. Climate Normals.

A heating degree day (HDD) is a temperature-based index of heating need, using a base of 65°F. The analysis uses the 1991-2020 daily normals for 125 Virginia stations with complete daily HDD curves. These 30-year normals describe a climatological average season and do not forecast this winter. HDD does not measure equipment run time, fuel use, household energy consumption or bills.

Key findings from the 125-station analysis:

* Fredericksburg proxy: 16.8% of the station's normal HDD accrues by Thanksgiving, measured from Jul 1-Nov 26 inclusive. Nov 26 is Thanksgiving 2026.
* Before December: Across Virginia's 125 normals stations, the Jul 1-Nov 30 share of normal HDD ranges from 15.4% at Eastville to 23.8% at Burkes Garden. The unweighted station median is 19.9%.
* By Thanksgiving: Across Virginia's 125 normals stations, the Jul 1-Nov 26 share of normal HDD ranges from 13.4% at Eastville to 21.9% at Burkes Garden. The unweighted station median is 17.8%.
* After Christmas Day: Across Virginia's 125 normals stations, the Dec 26-Jun 30 share of normal HDD ranges from 63.2% at Burkes Garden to 70.3% at Eastville. The unweighted station median is 65.6%.
* Halfway dates: Across Virginia's 125 normals stations, the dates for reaching half of cumulative normal HDD from Jul 1 range from Jan 16 to Jan 24. The unweighted median station date is Jan 18.

At Eastville station, 70.3% of normal HDD falls in Dec 26-Jun 30, totaling 2,543.5 HDD. At Burkes Garden station, 63.2% falls in the same inclusive window, totaling 3,771.6 HDD. A larger share of a station's annual normal does not necessarily mean a larger HDD total for that window.

Big Meadows station has the largest annual normal-HDD total in the 125-station analysis, at 6,580.2 HDD. Its normal-HDD curve reaches halfway on Jan 21.

Dulles and Reagan National stations share a Jan 19 normal-HDD halfway date. For Dec 26-Jun 30, Dulles has a 66.0% normal-HDD share and Reagan National has a 67.8% share.

At the Shenandoah Valley's Staunton WTP station, 65.5% of normal HDD falls in Dec 26-Jun 30. At Winchester station, the share for the same inclusive window is 65.7%. Both stations reach halfway through cumulative normal HDD on Jan 19. These are separate station results.

The earliest normal-HDD halfway date in the analysis is a tie: Ashland, Buchanan, Brookneal, Danville Rgnl AP and Louisa all reach it on Jan 16.

How the analysis was done:

* Data: Calculations use the U.S. Climate Normals 1991-2020 daily HDD normals, base 65°F, for 125 Virginia stations with complete daily HDD curves. Each station's annual normal is summed over Jul 1-Jun 30 on the 365-day normal calendar, with Feb 29 excluded. Each share divides the window's normal-HDD sum by that station's own annual normal-HDD total.
* Windows: All windows are inclusive: before December is Jul 1-Nov 30; through Thanksgiving is Jul 1-Nov 26, using Thanksgiving 2026; after Christmas Day is Dec 26-Jun 30. Dec 1-25 sits between the principal windows. The through-Nov-30 and Dec-26-onward shares are not complements. Medians are unweighted station medians. Halfway is the first date when cumulative normal HDD from Jul 1 reaches at least half of the station's annual normal total.
* Fredericksburg proxy: Fredericksburg has no first-order airport station. The nearest-station proxy is Fredericksburg Sewage (station USC00443204), about a mile from an analyst-selected downtown Fredericksburg reference point. It does not measure Stafford, Spotsylvania, Caroline or King George counties, or any individual home.
* Coverage and flags: Station coverage is uneven; no population-weighted, area-weighted or regional-average figure was calculated. The normals' completeness flags S, R and E were retained, including R normals built from shorter records and E normals that are estimated. The Fredericksburg proxy is R-flagged. Excluding E-flagged stations leaves the range endpoints unchanged; uncertainty associated with shorter R records remains.
* Precision: Rankings are descriptive at displayed precision, ties are grouped, and no statistical significance is implied. Changing the 65°F base would change the shares and could shift milestone dates. Monthly normals, which derive from the daily normals, provided an internal consistency check.
* Caveat: Milestone dates are points on the 1991-2020 averaged normal curve. The normals are not a forecast for this winter, and HDD does not measure equipment run time or household energy consumption.
