# Pollen Shapes Spring Hill Pressure Washing Timing Says 15cent Pressure Washing

New 15cent Pressure Washing Guide Recommends Late April to May House Washes After Tree Pollen Peaks

 Columbia, United States - November 5, 2026 / Pressure Washing Marketing Pros /

 Spring Hill homeowners who wash their houses during the spring tree pollen peak can find pollen back on the siding within days, according to a new guide from 15cent Pressure Washing that times house washing around pollen season. The guide arrives as fall leaf drop opens a second washing window for homes on shaded lots. Its schedule, published as How Often Should You Pressure Wash Your Home in Spring Hill, TN?, covers siding, roofs, driveways, gutters and windows.

Why Pollen Timing Matters for Spring Hill HomesMiddle Tennessee's tree pollen season begins in February, and WSMV's Middle Tennessee pollen forecast places the peak from mid-March into the fourth week of April. Many Spring Hill homes sit under shade from hackberry and other trees, which keeps north-facing walls damp and feeds mold and mildew. Fiber cement siding, one of the most common sidings in the area, needs regular care, and James Hardie's siding maintenance guidance recommends washing it every six to 12 months with low pressure. Most general advice stops at a once-a-year rule and says little about when in the year to schedule it, leaving homeowners to guess.

What the 15cent Pressure Washing Guide RecommendsThe guide answers that question with a calendar built around pollen. It recommends the main house wash from late April through May, after tree pollen has finished falling, so a home starts summer clean instead of collecting a new coat right away. Homes on shaded or wooded lots, or with north-facing walls that stay damp, get a second wash in fall after the leaves drop. Most Spring Hill homes need a wash about once a year, and the company advises against going longer than two years between washes. The guide also explains why the company soft washes houses with low pressure and a cleaning solution rather than pressure washing them.

15cent Pressure Washing on What Spring Hill Homeowners Should KnowThe company said the timing of a spring wash affects how long the results last.

"If we wash a house in the middle of March, the pollen is back on the siding in a few days. We'd rather come out in late April or May, after the trees are done, so the house starts summer clean. And we soft wash, so the plants along the house come through fine," said Micheal Hood, Owner, 15cent Pressure Washing.

Where Spring Hill Homeowners Can Learn MoreSpring Hill homeowners can read the full guide and find more information on the 15cent Pressure Washing website, and the company posts cleaning tips and project videos on the 15cent Pressure Washing YouTube channel.

About 15cent Pressure Washing15cent Pressure Washing is an exterior cleaning company based in Columbia, Tenn., with a second location in Spring Hill. The company serves homes and businesses in Maury and Williamson counties and nearby Middle Tennessee communities, offering soft wash house washing, roof cleaning, driveway and concrete cleaning, window cleaning, gutter cleaning and holiday lighting installation.
