# MidWest SoftWash Publishes Power Washing Cost Guide for St. Charles County

St. Charles County Power Washing Prices: What Homeowners Should Expect to Pay

 Wentzville, United States - November 5, 2026 / MidWest SoftWash /

 MidWest SoftWash has published a local pricing guide that gives St. Charles County homeowners real cost ranges for exterior cleaning and explains why competing quotes for the same home can differ by hundreds of dollars. Homeowners searching for prices in the area have largely had to choose between national averages that ignore local conditions and service pages that list no numbers at all. The company's power washing cost guide addresses that gap with ranges by surface and a plain explanation of what a quote actually includes.

Why Power Washing Quotes Vary So Widely in St. Charles CountyExterior cleaning prices in the county swing widely because the right method depends on the surface, and not every company uses it. The region's hot, humid summers, tracked by the National Weather Service office in St. Charles, drive the algae, lichen, and moss that collect on roofs and shaded, north-facing walls and make regular cleaning necessary. Those organisms return quickly when a surface is only rinsed rather than treated, which is part of why a low number can cost more over time. The method gap matters most on roofs, where the Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association guidance warns that high-pressure washing is likely to damage asphalt shingles and should never be used on them, recommending a low-pressure cleaning instead. A quote built around high pressure on a roof or delicate siding is not the same service as a true soft wash, even when the dollar figure looks similar.

What the New Pricing Guide CoversThe guide publishes typical ranges for the services homeowners ask about most, including house washing at roughly $500 to $800 for a standard home and soft-wash roof cleaning starting at a $750 minimum and commonly running $1,500 to $2,000. It also breaks pricing down by major cities across the county, lists the factors that move a quote up or down, and marks services such as driveway and concrete cleaning as custom quotes where a published average would mislead. A homeowner can read the full breakdown before ever requesting an estimate.

MidWest SoftWash on What St. Charles County Homeowners Should KnowA company co-owner said the guide reflects how the team talks with homeowners during an estimate. "The question we hear most is why one quote is a fraction of another, and the honest answer is that they are rarely the same job," the co-owner said. "A true soft wash protects the roof and the siding, and the cheapest number often means high pressure on a surface that should never see it. We would rather a homeowner understand what they are paying for before anyone sets foot on the property."

Where St. Charles County Homeowners Can Learn MoreHomeowners can read the complete guide or learn more about the company and its services on their website.

About MidWest SoftWashMidWest SoftWash is a soft-washing company based in O'Fallon, Missouri, serving homeowners and commercial properties across St. Charles County and the greater St. Louis metro. The company provides low-pressure house washing, roof cleaning, deck and concrete cleaning, solar panel cleaning, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, and holiday lighting.
