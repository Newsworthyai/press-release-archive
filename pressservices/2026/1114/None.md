# Carolina Braces' Wine &amp; Align Event to Support Families Together

Carolina Braces Hosts Wine & Align on Nov 18 with $2,000 Invisalign Savings

 Raleigh, United States - November 14, 2026 / Carolina Braces /

 Carolina Braces will host Wine & Align on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, from 5 to 7 p.m., with support for Families Together at the center of the evening. The PLM organization helps Wake County families with children move from homelessness to stable housing.

For every Invisalign® treatment start that evening, Carolina Braces will donate $200 to Families Together, and Families Together's sponsors will match the donation. Every start at the event doubles the support going to local families working toward stable housing.

Families Together connects families experiencing homelessness with housing and resources that support their path forward. Its services include short-term housing, access to safe and affordable housing, temporary rental assistance, professional mentoring, connections to community resources and life-skills workshops.

Wine & Align will take place at Carolina Braces, 100 Sawmill Road, Suite 200, Raleigh, NC 27615, and will be hosted by Dr. Robert Kent and Dr. Samantha Tagerman. The evening features wine and beer, food and music, giving community members a chance to gather while supporting Families Together's work. Guests choose a 30-minute visit that includes a complimentary clear aligner consultation and a digital smile scan. New and current patients are welcome.

For one night only, attendees can receive $2,000 off full comprehensive Invisalign® treatment. To receive the offer, patients must attend the event and sign their treatment contract that evening. The offer cannot be combined with any other benefits or promotions.

Space is limited and an RSVP is required. Community members can RSVP for Wine & Align online or call Carolina Braces at (984) 357-1963 for more information.

About Carolina Braces Carolina Braces is an orthodontic practice in Raleigh, North Carolina, providing care for children, teens and adults. Its doctors include Dr. Robert Kent and Dr. Samantha Tagerman. The practice offers braces and clear-aligner treatment at its Sawmill office. Learn more at https://carolinabraces.com/.

About Families Together Families Together is a PLM organization serving Wake County families with children experiencing homelessness and helping them move into stable housing. Its work combines housing support with resources to help families build self-sufficiency. Learn more at https://familiestogethernc.org/.
