# StreetKing Brings Lift Kits and Leveling Kits to Houma, Lafayette, Gulfport

StreetKing Now Installs Lift Kits, Leveling Kits, and Rock Lighting at Three Gulf South Stores

 Houma, United States - November 2, 2026 / StreetKing Custom Wheels and Tires /

 StreetKing Custom Wheels and Tires has announced an expansion of its product and installation services across three of its retail locations in Louisiana and Mississippi. The Houma, Lafayette, and Gulfport stores are now stocking and installing Lift Kits, Leveling Kits, and a range of truck and SUV accessories alongside the brand's established Custom Wheels and tire offerings.

The move extends the company's vehicle customization focus beyond its core wheel and tire foundation. Products now available for purchase and professional installation at these three locations include bed covers, running boards, power steps, and rock lighting -- categories that complement the suspension and wheel work the brand has built its regional presence around.

Three Locations Now Offering Full Installation ServicesThe Lift Kits and Leveling Kits installation services are currently active at StreetKing's Houma and Lafayette stores in Louisiana, as well as its Gulfport location in Mississippi. Customers visiting other StreetKing locations in search of these services are directed to the Lafayette or Gulfport store, both of which are positioned to serve the broader regional customer base.

The Houma, Lafayette, and Gulfport stores are now equipped to handle the full expanded product lineup in a single visit, giving truck and SUV owners in the region one destination for suspension modifications, exterior accessories, and Custom Wheels.

Accessories Now Installed Alongside Suspension UpgradesIn addition to suspension work, the three participating locations are installing a variety of exterior and functional accessories. Bed covers -- available in both hard and soft configurations depending on application -- are among the additions, along with running boards and power steps that modify vehicle entry and exit for lifted or standard-height trucks.

Rock lighting, which positions LED units beneath the vehicle frame to illuminate the ground around tires and rocker panels, is also part of the expanded lineup. The combination of suspension, lighting, and exterior upgrades allows the three locations to serve customers looking to complete multiple categories of vehicle modification in a single appointment.

Expansion Builds on the Company's Wheel and Tire BaseStreetKing Custom Wheels and Tires established its presence in the Gulf South region through custom wheel fitment and tire services. The addition of Leveling Kits and full suspension lift options extends that vehicle customization scope into territory many existing customers already pursue -- one that has historically required a separate trip to a different shop.

By bringing lift and leveling installation in-house alongside bed covers, running boards, power steps, and rock lighting, the company is consolidating services that truck and SUV owners in Louisiana and Mississippi have typically sourced from multiple vendors. The Houma, Lafayette, and Gulfport stores serve as the primary hubs for the expanded product line, while other StreetKing locations will direct customers to these three stores for installation until the expanded offerings can be launched locally.

About StreetKing Custom Wheels and TiresStreetKing Custom Wheels and Tires operates multiple retail locations across Louisiana and Mississippi, specializing in custom wheel fitment, tire services, and vehicle accessories with flexible payment options. The Houma, Lafayette, and Gulfport stores now offer installation services for lift kits, leveling kits, bed covers, running boards, power steps, and rock lighting in addition to the brand's core wheel and tire offerings.
