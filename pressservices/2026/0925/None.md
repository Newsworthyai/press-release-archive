# Free Dental Day in Warner Robins Serves 66 Patients

Warner Robins Free Dental Day Delivers $20,648 in Care to 66 Patients

 Warner Robins, United States - September 25, 2026 / Hayslip Center For Family and Cosmetic Dentistry /

 On September 18, 2026, Hayslip Center for Family and Cosmetic Dentistry hosted a Free Dental Day in Warner Robins, GA, bringing together 22 volunteers to provide dental care at no cost to members of the community. The one-day event, led by Dr. Curtis Hayslip, served 66 patients and delivered $20,648 in free dental care.

A Community Effort Spanning Volunteers and SponsorsOf the 22 volunteers who took part, 11 came from the Hayslip Center team. Local businesses and dental suppliers helped make the day possible. Woodlands Coffee, Your Pie Pizza, and Melissa G. provided meals for the volunteers. Henry Schein and Darby Dental supplied clinical materials. Livi Kate Boutique offered volunteers a discount on their event shirts.

Rooted in a Personal Philosophy of ServiceFor Dr. Hayslip, the reason behind the Free Dental Day is personal. Speaking to 13WMAZ, which covered the event in a segment published September 21, 2026, he put it simply: "My mom and dad taught me to help everybody," said Dr. Curtis Hayslip of Hayslip Center for Family and Cosmetic Dentistry.

Local Media CoverageMiddle Georgia news station 13WMAZ reported on the event, summarizing it this way: "Hayslip Family Dentistry's Free Dental Day offered crucial care to 60 patients, showcasing community service and the transformative power of a smile." The practice's final count for the day was 66 patients.

For residents of Warner Robins and the surrounding Middle Georgia area, the coverage highlighted a local dentist in Warner Robins, GA giving back to its community. Together, the volunteers, the Hayslip Center team, and the event's sponsors delivered $20,648 in free dental care in a single day.

About Hayslip Center for Family and Cosmetic DentistryHayslip Center for Family and Cosmetic Dentistry is a family and cosmetic dental practice located at 1295 Russell Parkway in Warner Robins, GA.
