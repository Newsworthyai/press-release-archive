# Cucci Chiropractic Opens Intake Forms in English and Spanish

Cucci Chiropractic Offers Adult and Pediatric Intake Forms Including a Spanish-Language Option

 New York, United States - July 26, 2026 / Cucci Chiropractic /

 NEW YORK, July 25, 2026 - Cucci Chiropractic is marking more than 25 years of delivering chiropractic care to patients across New York. Under the direction of founder Dr. Joseph Cucci, the practice serves adults, children, professionals, commuters, and families from its Manhattan office at 131 East 61st Street on the Upper East Side, where Dr. Cucci has devoted over two and a half decades to chiropractic services.

The anniversary reflects the practice's sustained commitment to individualized patient care. Each new patient undergoes a consultation and evaluation designed to give the practice a thorough understanding of the individual's health history, current concerns, movement patterns, posture, daily demands, and personal goals. Care recommendations are then discussed based on examination findings and clinical suitability.

"New York patients often balance demanding work schedules, long commutes, extended periods at a desk, and physically active lifestyles," said Dr. Joseph Cucci, founder of Cucci Chiropractic. "Our goal is to listen carefully, explain what we find, and develop an approach that fits the individual instead of applying the same plan to everyone."

The New York office offers a broad range of chiropractic and supportive services. Depending on the outcome of each individual evaluation, care may include chiropractic manipulative therapy, the Activator Method, upper cervical techniques, manual therapy, myofascial release, trigger point therapy, instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization, kinesiology taping, rehabilitation exercises, neuro re-education, postural evaluations, scoliosis screenings, and digital X-rays where clinically appropriate.

Cucci Chiropractic also provides equipment-based and non-surgical supportive options that may be incorporated into a broader care plan. These include spinal traction, flexion-distraction, shockwave therapy, deep tissue laser therapy, and performance rehabilitation exercises. Such services are not recommended as a matter of course and may not be appropriate for every patient. Decisions are guided by the individual's history, symptoms, examination findings, goals, and overall health status.

The Manhattan location is positioned to serve residents, professionals, and commuters seeking a chiropractic office near the Upper East Side. The office accepts patient appointments on Monday from 10:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Wednesday from 10:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Friday from 10:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Patients are encouraged to confirm current hours and appointment availability directly with the practice.

New patients can access adult and pediatric intake forms for the New York office through the practice website. A Spanish-language adult intake form is also available, allowing patients to prepare relevant information ahead of their scheduled visit.

Cucci Chiropractic notes that a consultation and examination are used to determine whether chiropractic or supportive care may be appropriate for a given patient. When a patient's presentation indicates the need for a different type of evaluation or treatment, coordination with or referral to another qualified healthcare professional may be recommended.

New York patients interested in learning more about the practice or scheduling an appointment can visit Cucci Chiropractic or contact the Manhattan office by phone at 212-980-9332.

About Cucci ChiropracticCucci Chiropractic is a family chiropractic practice led by Dr. Joseph Cucci, who brings more than 25 years of experience to his work. The New York office provides individualized chiropractic techniques, soft tissue care, rehabilitation, evaluation, and supportive services from its location at 131 East 61st Street in Manhattan. Care recommendations are based on each patient's consultation, examination findings, clinical needs, and personal goals.
