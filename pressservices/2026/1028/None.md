# Richard Harris Law Firm's Secret Santa Covers Trees, Meals, and Gifts

Richard Harris Law Firm Covers Full Christmas for Selected Nevada Families in 2026

 Las Vegas, United States - October 28, 2026 / Richard Harris Law Firm /

 LAS VEGAS, NEVADA - The Richard Harris Law Firm, Nevada's largest personal injury law firm, has announced its 2026 Operation Secret Santa Project, an initiative designed to provide families in need across the Las Vegas and Reno communities with a complete holiday experience.

Through Operation Secret Santa, selected Nevada families will receive a Christmas tree and decorations, a holiday meal, and personalized, wrapped gifts chosen directly from each family's wish list.

"Any time our firm has the opportunity to help people in our community, it's a reason to celebrate," said Richard Harris, Founder of the Richard Harris Law Firm. "Giving back has always been an important part of who we are. It brings me great joy to help families create special holiday memories. Christmas is about family, generosity, and the spirit of giving, and Operation Secret Santa is a meaningful way for us to share that spirit with families throughout Las Vegas and Reno."

The Richard Harris Law Firm is inviting community members to nominate a family for consideration. Families must be Nevada residents to qualify for the program.

IMPORTANT DATES

* Family submissions are now open.
* Submission deadline: Wednesday, November 4, 2026
* Selected families notified by: Friday, November 13, 2026

SELECTED FAMILIES WILL RECEIVE

* A Christmas tree and holiday decorations delivered before Thanksgiving.
* A Christmas dinner delivered during the holiday season.
* Personalized, wrapped gifts selected from their wish lists.
* Holiday deliveries scheduled for December 21-22, 2026.

For complete details and to nominate a family, learn more about Operation Secret Santa.

Updates about Operation Secret Santa and the firm's community involvement are available through the Richard Harris Law Firm's social media channels.
