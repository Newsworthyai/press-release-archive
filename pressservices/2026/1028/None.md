# Freeman The Treeman Handles Tree, Excavation, and Snow Work Year-Round

One Local Crew Covers Professional Tree Service and Lawncare Plus Excavation in Northeast PA

 Moscow, United States - October 28, 2026 / Freeman The Treeman /

 Freeman The Treeman, a family-owned outdoor services company based in Moscow, PA, has surpassed 16 years of continuous operation providing tree service and excavation in Northeast PA.

Sixteen years in business represents a significant milestone for any locally owned company, and for Freeman The Treeman, it reflects a steady pattern of returning customers, word-of-mouth referrals, and a service area that has expanded consistently across Northeast Pennsylvania. The company handles projects ranging from routine lawn care maintenance to excavation and land clearing, with a crew that applies the same level of attention to each job regardless of scale. The company has also launched a redesigned website intended to make it easier for property owners to explore available services and submit an estimate request.

Full-Service Outdoor Work Under One Local CrewFreeman The Treeman operates as a local, family-owned company focused on keeping neighboring properties safe and well-maintained. The company offers tree removal, excavation, tree trimming and pruning, stump grinding, landscaping, lawn care, and seasonal snow removal.

Because the crew delivers professional tree service and lawncare alongside excavation work, property owners do not need to coordinate between multiple contractors when a project spans more than one trade. A job that starts as a tree removal, for example, may also call for grading or site cleanup - work the same team can handle without bringing in outside help.

Each job is quoted with itemized pricing and no hidden fees, giving property owners a clear picture of costs before work begins.

Rooted in Northeast PennsylvaniaMoscow, PA serves as the home base for Freeman The Treeman, positioning the crew within reach of communities throughout Northeast Pennsylvania. Local ownership means the people making decisions live and work in the same region as the customers they serve, and response times reflect that geographic proximity.

The company's longevity in the area points to a consistent operational approach: arrive on schedule, complete the work correctly, and maintain direct communication with the property owner at every stage. That track record spans well over a decade of projects across the Northeast PA region.

Snow Removal Rounds Out Year-Round CoverageBeyond warm-weather services, Freeman The Treeman provides snow removal for commercial customers, giving business owners a single point of contact for outdoor property maintenance across all four seasons. Snow removal services cover parking areas, sidewalks, and entryways, coordinated through the same direct communication the company uses for its tree and lawn work.

The seasonal offering completes a full calendar of outdoor services. For property owners who depend on professional tree service and lawncare or who need tree service and excavation in Northeast PA, working with one established local crew across multiple service categories simplifies scheduling and removes the uncertainty that comes with hiring unfamiliar contractors for each new project.

New Website Makes It Easier to Get StartedFreeman The Treeman has launched a new website design that gives each service category - tree service, excavation, landscaping, lawn care, and snow removal - its own dedicated page. The site walks visitors through the company's four-step process from the initial call to the completed job. Property owners can read customer reviews, find answers to common questions about pricing, estimates, and tree health, view the towns the company serves, and submit a free estimate request online.

The updated design follows the same approach the crew applies in the field: clear information, defined next steps, and no guesswork for the property owner.

About Freeman The TreemanFreeman The Treeman is a family-owned and operated outdoor services company based in Moscow, PA. The company provides tree removal, tree trimming, stump grinding, excavation, landscaping, and lawn maintenance to residential and commercial customers, and snow removal to commercial customers. Services extend throughout Northeast Pennsylvania. With more than 16 years of operation, the crew brings direct communication and consistent on-site workmanship to every project.
