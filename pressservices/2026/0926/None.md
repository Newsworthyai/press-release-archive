# Efros Orthodontics Expands with Bennington, VT Office

Efros Orthodontics Brings Braces and Invisalign Services to Bennington, VT

 Hudson, United States - September 26, 2026 / Efros Orthodontics /

 Efros Orthodontics has announced the opening of a new office in Bennington, Vermont, expanding its orthodontic services to residents throughout the region. The practice offers a range of treatment options, including traditional braces in Bennington, VT, and Invisalign, providing patients with multiple pathways to address misalignment and bite concerns.

The Bennington location was selected to improve access for patients across the surrounding area. The office is equipped with digital imaging and 3D scanning technology, which supports precise diagnostics and detailed treatment planning. These tools allow the orthodontist in Bennington, VT to develop individualized plans based on each patient's specific clinical needs.

Traditional braces remain a well-established method for correcting misaligned teeth and bite irregularities. Using a system of brackets and wires, this approach applies consistent, gradual pressure to reposition teeth over time. The Bennington office is outfitted with current equipment to support efficient delivery of this treatment.

Invisalign in Bennington, VT is also available at the new location. This treatment uses a series of clear, removable aligners fabricated to fit each patient's dental profile. The aligners can be taken out for meals and oral hygiene routines, which many patients find practical. The clinical team has received training in current Invisalign protocols to ensure consistent application of the treatment.

The practice places emphasis on patient education as part of the treatment process. Staff members take time to walk patients through their options, address questions, and provide guidance on maintaining oral hygiene throughout orthodontic care. This approach is intended to help patients understand what their treatment involves and what is expected of them during the process.

The Bennington office also reflects the practice's interest in participating in the local community. Efros Orthodontics has indicated plans to engage with local events and initiatives as the office becomes established in the area.

The new location represents a continued effort by Efros Orthodontics to extend its services to additional communities in Vermont. Patients seeking consultations with an orthodontist in Bennington, VT, whether for braces or clear aligner treatment, can contact the office to schedule an appointment.
