# Oneonta Office Offers Metal and Clear Braces for Teens

Hometown Orthodontics Outlines Teen Braces Options and Consultation Process at Oneonta Office

 Pinson, United States - September 26, 2026 / Hometown Orthodontics /

 Hometown Orthodontics has outlined the braces options and consultation process available to teenage patients at its Oneonta, Alabama office, where metal bracket and clear bracket systems are both offered as treatment paths for adolescents undergoing orthodontic correction.

The announcement comes as families in the Oneonta area prepare for back-to-school seasons and annual dental check-ups that often prompt referrals to orthodontic care. Hometown Orthodontics serves patients across Oneonta, Pinson, and Trussville, Alabama, with the Oneonta location positioned as the access point for teens in that community.

Metal and Clear Bracket Options for Adolescent Patients

At the Oneonta office, teenagers and their families can choose between two primary bracket systems. Traditional metal brackets remain a common selection for adolescent orthodontic treatment, offering consistent performance across a range of bite and alignment cases. Clear brackets are also available for patients who prefer a less visible appliance while still undergoing fixed-bracket orthodontic treatment.

Both systems function by applying controlled pressure to teeth over time, gradually moving them into the positions prescribed by the treating orthodontist. The choice between metal and clear brackets is typically discussed during the initial consultation, where the clinical team reviews what each option involves and how it applies to the individual patient's case.

Families looking into braces for teens in Oneonta AL can contact the office at (205) 749-1242 to schedule a first appointment.

Digital Scanning and Treatment Timeline Mapping

Hometown Orthodontics uses digital scanning technology at the Oneonta office to assess a patient's teeth and jaw alignment before treatment begins. Digital scans replace traditional physical impressions in many cases, producing a three-dimensional image of the mouth that the orthodontic team uses to map out a treatment plan.

The scan data informs the estimated treatment timeline, which varies depending on the complexity of the case. Factors such as the degree of crowding, spacing, or bite misalignment influence how long a course of treatment is likely to run. The clinical team uses this information to give patients and parents a clearer picture of what to expect before committing to a treatment path.

What the First Consultation Covers

The initial consultation at the Oneonta office is structured to give families the information they need to make a decision about orthodontic care. During this appointment, the orthodontic team reviews the results of the digital scan, explains which bracket options are clinically appropriate for the patient, and provides an estimated treatment duration based on the case findings for braces in Oneonta AL.

The consultation also covers next steps, including what the start of treatment involves and how appointments are typically scheduled throughout the course of care. No treatment begins at the first visit without the patient and family having a clear understanding of the proposed plan.
