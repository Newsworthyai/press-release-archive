# bluebonnetbartending.com Extends Coverage to Austin and Fredericksburg

San Antonio Mobile Bartending Company Expands Territory and Upgrades Digital Booking Experience

 San Antonio, United States - July 26, 2026 / Bluebonnet Bartenders /

 bluebonnetbartending.com, a San Antonio-based mobile bartending company founded by Nancy Tkac, has launched an upgraded digital platform aimed at streamlining event booking and improving the overall client experience for hosts throughout San Antonio, Austin, Kerrville, Fredericksburg, and the Texas Hill Country.

The update coincides with the company's continued regional growth, extending its full-service, turnkey event bartending service to a broader range of private and corporate events. The refreshed platform introduces automated email and text reminders that keep clients informed at each stage of the planning process, addressing the coordination challenges that commonly arise when managing multiple event vendors.

A Founder Rooted in Hospitality

Nancy Tkac's vision for bluebonnetbartending.com was never simply to operate a bartending business. Her goal was to bring a complete bar experience directly to clients, regardless of where they were gathering or what they were celebrating. Drawing on more than 16 years of hospitality experience, she built the company around the idea that quality service should be portable.

That guiding principle is reflected throughout the operation. When a client books through bluebonnetbartending.com, the team arrives fully equipped to run a complete bar: setup, tools, ice, mixers, garnishes, and glassware are all included. Clients are only responsible for supplying the alcohol. Every other detail is managed by the team, allowing hosts to remain present with their guests rather than absorbed in logistics.

"I built bluebonnetbartending.com because I wanted people to stop stressing about their bar and start enjoying their own events," said Nancy Tkac, Founder of bluebonnetbartending.com. "With our turnkey setup, clients hand us the details and we take care of the rest -- from the first pour to the last guest."

Safety and Certification as a Foundation

A defining aspect of bluebonnetbartending.com is its emphasis on responsible alcohol service. Every member of the bartending team holds TABC certification, a credential issued by the Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission that covers alcohol service regulations, identification verification, and intoxication prevention. For event hosts, having TABC certified bartenders present provides a meaningful layer of protection for both guests and the host.

That commitment to safety works alongside the company's personalized approach to each event. Rather than relying on a fixed menu, bluebonnetbartending.com collaborates with clients to develop custom cocktail menus and signature drinks suited to the specific occasion. The needs of a Hill Country wedding differ from those of a corporate reception in downtown San Antonio, and the team tailors its offerings accordingly.

Expanding Reach Across the Hill Country

The platform improvements accompany an expansion of the company's service area beyond its mobile bartending San Antonio origins. bluebonnetbartending.com now serves clients in Austin, Kerrville, Fredericksburg, and the surrounding Texas Hill Country - a region where outdoor weddings, vineyard events, and private ranch gatherings are common, and where traditional venue bar service is frequently unavailable or impractical.

The technology-supported booking process reflects Tkac's awareness that clients are often managing several vendors and competing deadlines simultaneously. Automated reminders are designed to prevent details from slipping between the initial booking and the event date. The result is a more consistent communication flow from the first inquiry through final execution.

Hosts interested in booking an event bartending service for an upcoming gathering can visit bluebonnetbartending.com or reach Nancy Tkac directly at +1 (210) 993-0058.

About bluebonnetbartending.com

bluebonnetbartending.com is a San Antonio-based mobile bartending company founded by Nancy Tkac. The company provides full-service, turnkey bar experiences for private and corporate events across San Antonio, Austin, Kerrville, Fredericksburg, and the Texas Hill Country. Services include bar setup, tools, ice, mixers, garnishes, glassware, and custom cocktail menu development. All bartenders are TABC certified. Clients supply the alcohol; bluebonnetbartending.com handles the rest.
