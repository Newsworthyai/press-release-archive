# As New Again Pressure Washing Quotes $100 to Over $1000 in Jacksonville

New As New Again Guide Lists Real Costs by Service After Local Quotes Swing Three to Four Times Over

 Jacksonville, United States - November 17, 2026 / Pressure Washing Marketing Pros /

 As New Again Pressure Washing has published a guide that lists what it charges for pressure washing in Jacksonville, service by service, after homeowners reported quotes that varied three to four times over for the same house. Jacksonville's humidity and heavy summer rain keep mold and algae growing on exteriors year-round, so most homeowners are pricing a repeat service rather than a one-time job. The company's pressure washing cost guide for Jacksonville breaks the numbers down by surface and explains what changes each price.

Why Jacksonville Pressure Washing Quotes Vary So WidelyHomeowners searching for a straight answer on cost find little agreement. Published online ranges for a single-family house wash in the area swing from a couple hundred dollars to well over a thousand, with no explanation of what sets them apart. According to HomeAdvisor's Jacksonville cost data, the local average for power washing runs about $256, with a typical range of $182 to $358, yet many quotes land far outside that band. The confusion carries a real cost, because exterior appearance affects resale: research from the University of Texas at Arlington found that curb appeal can account for up to 7 percent of a home's sale price. Without clear pricing, Jacksonville homeowners struggle to tell a fair quote from an inflated one or a low bid that signals a rushed job.

What the New Pricing Guide CoversThe guide answers the cost question with published figures instead of a range nobody can check. House washing is priced at about $100 to $120 per 1,000 square feet, which puts a typical 2,000-square-foot home near the local average. A standard driveway runs about $120 and includes the city sidewalk in front of the home and the curb. The guide also lists prices for roof cleaning, decks, fences, gutters, windows, and commercial properties, along with a section on what a cleaning cannot fix, such as oil stains, battery and fertilizer rust, worn roof shingles, and fogged window glass. Each price is paired with what moves it up or down.

What As New Again Says Jacksonville Homeowners Should KnowThe company framed the guide around setting expectations before a crew ever arrives. "We soft wash, we don't blast, and we tell people up front what will come clean and what won't," a company representative said. "Homeowners here have been quoted all over the map, so we decided to put our real prices in writing. The goal is for the property to look new again and for the price to match the result." The company added that it treats a starter home and a large property the same way, with the same crew and the same attention to detail.

Where Jacksonville Homeowners Can Learn MoreJacksonville homeowners and property managers can find the pricing guide and full service list on the As New Again Pressure Washing website or follow As New Again Pressure Washing on Facebook for updates and before-and-after project photos.

About As New Again Pressure WashingAs New Again Pressure Washing is a soft-wash exterior cleaning company serving Jacksonville and Northeast Florida. The company provides house washing, roof cleaning, driveway and concrete cleaning, window and gutter cleaning, paver and concrete sealing, and commercial pressure washing for homeowners, property managers, and businesses across the Jacksonville metro area.
