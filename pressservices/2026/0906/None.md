# Irvine Practice Offers Airway Assessment and Pediatric Ortho

Pediatric Airway Orthodontics and Airway Assessment Available at Irvine CA Office

 Irvine, United States - September 6, 2026 / Kind Smiles Orthodontics and Pediatric Dentistry /

 Kind Smiles Orthodontics and Pediatric Dentistry has formally expanded its clinical service offerings to include dedicated pediatric airway orthodontics and structured airway assessment at its Irvine, California office, making these services available to children and teens throughout Orange County.

Airway-Focused Care Joins the Practice's Clinical Menu

The addition of airway assessment Irvine families can access reflects a shift in how the practice approaches early intervention for younger patients. Rather than limiting evaluations to tooth alignment and bite correction alone, the office now conducts structured assessments of airway development as part of its diagnostic process. These evaluations are designed to identify obstructions or developmental patterns in the airway that may affect a child's breathing, sleep, and overall oral health.

Airway concerns in children are frequently connected to jaw development and the positioning of the teeth and palate. By evaluating these structural factors during orthodontic consultations, the practice aims to address contributing issues at a stage when intervention can have a meaningful effect on growth outcomes. This approach is particularly relevant during the earlier phases of childhood development, when the jaw and facial bones are still actively forming.

Services Offered Across Two Treatment Phases

Kind Smiles Orthodontics and Pediatric Dentistry provides both Phase 1 and Phase 2 orthopedic treatment for pediatric patients, allowing clinicians to begin early guidance of jaw growth before all permanent teeth have emerged. Phase 1 treatment typically begins between the ages of six and ten and may involve functional appliances or palatal expanders aimed at creating adequate space and improving airway dimensions. Phase 2 treatment follows once most permanent teeth are present and focuses on aligning the full dentition.

As an orthodontist in Irvine CA, the practice also offers Invisalign for eligible patients, digital scanning technology in place of traditional impressions, and dentofacial orthopedics for cases involving skeletal discrepancies. For patients with dental anxiety or those who require a gentler clinical environment, the office offers anesthesia-free treatment options where clinically appropriate.

Serving Orange County Pediatric Patients

The Irvine office draws patients from across Orange County, including families in surrounding cities who are seeking pediatric-specific orthodontic care that incorporates airway evaluation. The practice works with both children and teenagers, with treatment planning tailored to each patient's developmental stage.

The integration of airway assessment alongside conventional orthodontic and pedodontic services reflects a broader clinical trend toward examining how structural development of the jaw and palate interacts with breathing function. Practitioners in this area of dentistry increasingly look at whether crowding, narrow arches, or jaw positioning may be associated with mouth breathing, sleep-disordered breathing, or related conditions in pediatric populations.

About Kind Smiles Orthodontics and Pediatric Dentistry

Kind Smiles Orthodontics and Pediatric Dentistry is an orthodontic and pediatric dental practice located in Irvine, California. The practice provides services for children and teens, including Invisalign, Phase 1 and Phase 2 orthodontics, dentofacial orthopedics, digital scanning, pediatric airway orthodontics, and airway assessment. The office serves families throughout Orange County.
