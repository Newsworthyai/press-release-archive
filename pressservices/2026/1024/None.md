# Computyne Launches Free 5-Hour Pilot for eCommerce Virtual Assistant Services

Computyne Lets eCommerce Sellers Trial Virtual Assistant Work With a Free 5-Hour Pilot

 Gandhinagar, India - October 23, 2026 / Computyne /

 Online sellers face a steady stream of back-office tasks, from listing updates and order tracking to customer messages and marketplace checks. Computyne, a business process outsourcing company, is highlighting how dedicated virtual assistants are being deployed to support eCommerce teams looking to manage this workload without expanding in-house headcount.

Computyne provides managed virtual assistants across administrative, research, customer support, bookkeeping, social media, real estate, and eCommerce functions. For online retailers, the eCommerce virtual assistant service addresses the daily operational work that scales alongside a store as it adds products, sales channels, and customers.

Why eCommerce Operations Create Constant Back-Office WorkRunning an online store involves considerably more than selling products. Each new SKU, sales channel, or seasonal promotion generates additional work behind the scenes. Demand spikes, rapid catalog growth, and shifting marketplace requirements can quickly stretch a small team beyond its capacity.

Routine tasks such as email management, data entry, and research often consume two to six hours per week each, according to Computyne. Across a small team, that volume can account for 20 to 30 percent of the combined working week - time spent on work that does not require specialist skills.

Where a Virtual Assistant Fits in eCommerceComputyne's eCommerce virtual assistants operate inside a seller's existing tools and follow the seller's established processes. Typical responsibilities include:

* Product listing creation, optimization, and category management
* Order processing, tracking, and returns handling support
* Customer inquiry management across email, chat, and marketplace portals
* Competitor price monitoring and marketplace compliance checks
* Support across Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, WooCommerce, and Magento

Sellers can also pair virtual assistants with Computyne's broader eCommerce services, including eCommerce catalog management, eCommerce product data entry, and design and image editing support for product visuals and banners.

Flexible Engagement Models for Online SellersOrder volume in eCommerce rarely remains constant, so support requirements often shift throughout the year. Computyne offers several engagement models for virtual assistant support:

* Hourly or flexible: $5 to $8 per hour for one-off tasks and overflow support, with a 10-hour monthly minimum
* Part-time: 20 hours per week, priced at $320 to $480 per month
* Full-time: 40 hours per week, priced at $600 to $900 per month
* Team package: multiple virtual assistants across functions, with volume pricing under an SLA-based contract

All models include a dedicated virtual assistant, tool access, SLA-driven delivery, weekly reporting, and ISO 27001 information security protocols.

Onboarding and Trial OptionsAccording to Computyne, a dedicated virtual assistant can be onboarded and operational within three to five business days following a project brief, tool access setup, and SOP review. Businesses can also begin with a free five-hour pilot to evaluate the quality and communication of the work before making a longer-term commitment.

Working hours can be aligned to the client's time zone, including UK, US East, and US West schedules, allowing online stores to keep customer messages and order updates moving outside standard business hours.

Supporting eCommerce Growth Without Adding HeadcountAs more online sellers look to manage costs while expanding across marketplaces, outsourced operational support has become a practical option. By combining dedicated virtual assistants with structured workflows and reporting, Computyne provides eCommerce teams with capacity to focus on pricing, marketing, and channel expansion while daily tasks remain on schedule.

Businesses interested in virtual assistant support for their online store can learn more through the Computyne eCommerce page or request a consultation through the company's website.

About ComputyneComputyne is a business process outsourcing company based in Denver, USA. The company provides data collection, data management, document processing, data entry, and data annotation services, along with dedicated virtual assistants and other hire-on-demand resources for businesses in eCommerce, real estate, logistics, finance, healthcare, and other industries. Computyne holds ISO 27001 certification and works with clients in the United States, the United Kingdom, and other regions.
