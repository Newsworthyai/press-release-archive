# What Fishers Families Should Know About Braces

OMI Orthodontics in Fishers Explains Metal, Ceramic Braces and Retainer Basics

 Auburn, United States - November 13, 2026 / OMI Orthodontics /

 O·M·I Orthodontics in Fishers, Indiana, where Dr. Joel Johnson plans and supervises treatment, is answering the questions Hamilton County parents and adults most often ask about braces: how metal and ceramic braces differ, what placement and adjustment visits involve, and why retainer wear begins the day braces come off.

Metal and Ceramic Braces: Same Mechanics, Different Appearance

At O·M·I Orthodontics, metal and ceramic braces operate on the same mechanical principles. Both types use brackets bonded to the teeth and connected by an archwire that applies steady, controlled pressure to move teeth into proper alignment over time. The primary differences lie in appearance and durability. Metal brackets are more visible but tend to withstand daily wear more consistently. Ceramic brackets are designed to blend with the natural color of teeth, making them a more discreet option. For families considering braces in Fishers, IN - whether for a child, a teenager, or an adult - understanding this distinction helps in selecting the option that fits individual preferences and lifestyle.

Families often weigh these two options carefully. Ceramic brackets are more brittle than steel, so hard foods and contact sports call for more caution, and a mouthguard is essential with either material. Older teens who want a more discreet look often ask about ceramic brackets. The recommendation follows the case: Dr. Johnson explains which option suits a patient's bite at the consultation, so families researching braces for teens in Fishers know what to expect.

What Happens at Placement and Adjustment Visits

The treatment process begins with a placement appointment during which brackets are bonded to the teeth and the archwire is set. Following placement, patients return to the office every 6 to 10 weeks for adjustment visits. At these appointments, the archwire is changed or tightened to continue guiding tooth movement. These visits are a consistent part of the treatment timeline regardless of whether a patient has chosen metal or ceramic brackets.

The practice also explains the use of elastics, which are small rubber bands that connect upper and lower brackets. Elastics are used in certain cases to correct bite alignment and are worn according to the specific instructions provided during treatment. Not all patients require them, but for those who do, consistent use as directed plays a direct role in the progression of treatment.

For families exploring braces for kids in Fishers, IN, knowing what routine visits involve helps reduce uncertainty for younger patients before they arrive at the office. The practice offers early appointments starting at 7:30 a.m. Monday through Thursday, which accommodates school and work schedules for Hamilton County families.

Retainers Begin the Day Braces Come Off

Retention begins when active treatment ends: retainer wear starts the day braces are removed. This is not a secondary or optional phase - it is an integral part of maintaining the tooth positions achieved during treatment. Teeth have a natural tendency to shift back toward their original positions, and consistent retainer use works against that tendency.
