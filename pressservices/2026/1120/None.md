# Half Moon Plumbing &amp; Electric MVP Plan Offers 24-Hour Enrollment Access

Half Moon Plumbing & Electric MVP Plan Lets Tulsa Homeowners Enroll Around the Clock

 Owasso, United States - November 20, 2026 / Half Moon Plumbing & Electric /

 Half Moon Plumbing & Electric has introduced the MVP Plan, a home maintenance plan Tulsa homeowners can use to keep plumbing and electrical systems in check without waiting for something to break down. The MVP Plan replaces the company's previous VIP program and consolidates plumbing and electrical care into a single membership.

What Members Get* A yearly plumbing and electrical health check. A technician inspects the home's systems on a scheduled basis, before a failure occurs.
* One standard drain cabling per year to address the slow or blocked drains common in most households.
* Member savings on repairs and replacements.
* Extended warranties on covered work.

Catching Problems EarlyMany homeowners are unaware of developing issues in their plumbing or electrical systems until those issues result in an emergency. The annual inspection built into this plumbing membership Tulsa residents can access is designed to surface problems while they are still straightforward to address, rather than after they have escalated into costly repairs.

How to JoinHomeowners can enroll through the MVP Plan page on the Half Moon Plumbing & Electric website or by contacting the office directly. The company operates phone lines around the clock, allowing homeowners to call at any hour with questions. Any Tulsa plumber on the Half Moon team serves members across Tulsa, Owasso, Broken Arrow, Bixby, Jenks, Claremore, and Bartlesville.

About Half Moon Plumbing & ElectricHalf Moon Plumbing & Electric is an owner-operated company that has served Tulsa-area homeowners since 2007, providing residential plumbing, electrical, septic, and water treatment services across Tulsa, Owasso, Broken Arrow, Bixby, Jenks, Claremore, and Bartlesville.

Learn more about the MVP Plan and membership enrollment on the Half Moon Plumbing & Electric website.
