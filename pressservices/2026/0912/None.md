# White Brown Smiles Offers Braces and Invisalign in Columbia SC

White Brown Smiles Brings Braces and Invisalign to Oneil Court in Columbia SC

 Columbia, United States - September 11, 2026 / White Brown Smiles /

 White Brown Smiles operates as an orthodontic practice in the Oneil Court neighborhood of Columbia, SC, and has provided treatment to more than 18,000 patients in the surrounding community. The practice offers both traditional braces and the Invisalign system, addressing a range of orthodontic needs for children, teens, and adults.

Each patient at White Brown Smiles receives a personalized treatment plan developed around their specific dental profile. Staff members guide patients through each stage of care, from the initial visit through the completion of treatment, with the goal of making the process straightforward and comfortable.

For those exploring braces in Columbia SC, the practice offers several options, including metal, ceramic, and lingual braces. The orthodontic team conducts a thorough consultation at the outset, assessing each patient's dental structure and developing a customized plan. This process includes digital imaging and X-rays to identify existing issues and determine the most effective course of treatment.

White Brown Smiles also provides Invisalign in Columbia SC as an alternative to fixed appliances. Invisalign aligners are fabricated from clear plastic material, making them less visible during wear. This option is frequently selected by adults and teenagers who prefer a less noticeable treatment method. The orthodontists at the practice work with patients throughout the Invisalign process to monitor progress and adjust treatment as needed.

The practice incorporates digital scanning technology in place of traditional impressions, reducing discomfort during the data-collection phase of treatment. Three-dimensional imaging supports precise planning, allowing the team to map out each patient's treatment with a higher degree of accuracy before any appliances are placed.

Beyond cosmetic outcomes, the team at White Brown Smiles addresses the functional aspects of orthodontic care. Misaligned teeth can contribute to tooth decay, gum disease, and jaw complications. Correcting alignment issues through orthodontic treatment can reduce the risk of these conditions and support long-term oral health.

Patient education is a consistent part of the practice's approach. The orthodontists explain each phase of treatment in detail, keeping patients informed about what to expect and answering questions as they arise throughout the process.

White Brown Smiles participates in local events and supports charitable organizations within the Columbia area, reflecting the practice's involvement in the broader community.

For families evaluating the cost of orthodontic care, the practice offers financing options and works with patients to identify payment plans and insurance coverage that fit their circumstances. These details are reviewed during the initial consultation so that financial considerations can be addressed before treatment begins.

The orthodontists at White Brown Smiles engage in ongoing continuing education to remain current with developments in orthodontic techniques and technology. This professional development informs the standard of care delivered to patients at the practice.

Patients who have completed treatment at White Brown Smiles have documented outcomes that include improvements in both oral health and confidence. As an established orthodontist in Columbia SC, the practice continues to serve individuals seeking structured, evidence-based orthodontic care.
