# Tybee Island Wedding Chapel Logs 14 Years of Continuous Operation

Georgia Wedding Venue at Tybee Island Offers Single-Location Ceremonies for 220 Guests

 Tybee Island, United States - October 8, 2026 / The Chapel by the Sea /

 The Chapel by the Sea on Tybee Island, operated through tybeeweddingchapel.com, has announced the availability of its full-service coastal wedding venue to couples planning ceremonies and receptions in the Savannah, Georgia area. With over 7,000 square feet of indoor and outdoor event space and a capacity of up to 220 guests, the property stands among the larger dedicated wedding venues serving the Georgia coast.

A Complete Wedding Experience on Georgia's CoastThe Chapel by the Sea has been welcoming couples since April 2011, offering a single-location setting that covers the full scope of a wedding day - from the ceremony itself to the reception that follows. Located on Tybee Island, Georgia, this Savannah wedding venue sits near historic downtown Savannah, giving guests and wedding parties access to one of the South's most visited coastal destinations without the need to coordinate between multiple sites.

The property managed through tybeeweddingchapel.com includes a Grand Ballroom alongside multiple curated event spaces, allowing wedding coordinators and couples to tailor the layout and atmosphere to fit different styles and guest counts. The combination of indoor and outdoor areas within the 7,000-square-foot footprint gives planners flexibility regardless of seasonal weather conditions or the couple's preferred aesthetic.

On-Site Coordination Designed to Simplify PlanningOne of the practical advantages tybeeweddingchapel.com highlights for prospective couples is the availability of on-site coordination services. Rather than sourcing a separate venue coordinator from outside vendors, couples working with this Georgia wedding venue have access to event coordination built directly into the venue experience. This structure is intended to reduce the logistical complexity that often accompanies large-scale weddings, particularly for families traveling to the Tybee Island area from outside the state.

The venue's proximity to downtown Savannah adds a draw for destination weddings. Tybee Island sits approximately 18 miles east of Savannah's historic district, making it accessible for guests arriving by air through Savannah/Hilton Head International Airport while still offering the distinct character of a barrier island setting along the Georgia coast.

Venue Details and Guest CapacityThe Chapel by the Sea accommodates events ranging from intimate gatherings to larger celebrations reaching 220 guests. The Grand Ballroom serves as the centerpiece for receptions, while the additional event spaces on the property provide options for pre-ceremony gatherings, cocktail hours, or smaller accompanying events on the same day.

tybeeweddingchapel.com serves couples across a range of planning timelines, with the venue's established infrastructure supporting both longer-lead bookings and events organized on shorter schedules. As a dedicated wedding chapel on the Georgia coast, the property offers a natural coastal backdrop that distinguishes it from inland banquet-style venues, while enclosed indoor spaces ensure that event logistics are not entirely dependent on outdoor weather conditions.

The availability of a single property that covers ceremony space, reception space, and coordination services reflects a practical model for couples who prefer to minimize the number of vendors and location changes involved in their wedding day. For those drawn to a Tybee Island wedding and the cultural richness of the Savannah region, The Chapel by the Sea has maintained continuous operation for well over a decade.

About tybeeweddingchapel.comtybeeweddingchapel.com is home to The Chapel by the Sea, a full-service wedding and events venue located on Tybee Island, Georgia. Open since April 2011, the property features more than 7,000 square feet of indoor and outdoor event space, including a Grand Ballroom and multiple curated event areas, with on-site coordination services available for ceremonies and receptions accommodating up to 220 guests. The venue is situated near historic downtown Savannah, making it accessible for both local and destination weddings along the Georgia coast.
