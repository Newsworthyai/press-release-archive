# Shay Smith, FNP-C Leads Morpheus8 Treatments in Burley, Idaho

Morpheus8 Microneedling for Skin Tightening Now Provider-Led at Burley, Idaho Clinic

 Burley, United States - August 16, 2026 / Velaris Med Spa /

 Velaris Med Spa has announced the availability of Morpheus8 RF microneedling at its Burley, Idaho location, broadening access to radiofrequency-based skin tightening for patients throughout the Magic Valley region. The service is provider-led by Shay Smith, FNP-C, and targets collagen remodeling and skin laxity through a combination of microneedling and radiofrequency energy delivered beneath the skin's surface.

What Morpheus8 Microneedling DeliversMorpheus8 functions by combining microneedling with radiofrequency (RF) energy to reach the deeper layers of the skin and underlying tissue. The device uses an array of fine needles to create micro-channels while simultaneously delivering controlled RF heat to stimulate the body's natural collagen production process. The result is a gradual remodeling of the skin's structural framework, which can lead to firmer, smoother skin over time.

Morpheus8 microneedling is commonly used to address concerns such as skin laxity along the jawline, neck, and face, as well as texture irregularities and mild contour changes associated with aging or weight fluctuation. Because the treatment targets the subdermal layer, it works on collagen and elastin at a foundational level rather than affecting only the skin's surface.

At Velaris Med Spa, each session is conducted by a qualified provider rather than delegated to support staff. Shay Smith, FNP-C, oversees patient care directly, which the practice cites as central to its approach to medical aesthetics.

Patient Comfort and the Treatment ProcessA defining aspect of the Morpheus8 Burley Idaho offering at Velaris Med Spa is the use of medical-grade topical numbing applied before the procedure begins. This preparation period allows the numbing agent to take full effect prior to treatment, with the goal of minimizing discomfort during the procedure.

Patients can expect a structured experience: a consultation to assess candidacy and skin concerns, a numbing phase, the Morpheus8 treatment itself, and post-procedure guidance from the provider. The practice notes that results develop gradually as collagen remodeling takes place over several weeks following each session.

"At Velaris Med Spa, we focus on delivering a comfortable, results-focused experience through provider-led care and proven RF microneedling technology," said Shay Smith, FNP-C of Velaris Med Spa. "Morpheus8 allows us to address skin tightening at a structural level, and having a qualified provider present throughout the treatment is something we consider non-negotiable."

Skin Tightening in Burley, IdahoAccess to skin tightening Burley Idaho services through advanced RF technology has historically required patients to travel to larger metropolitan areas. The addition of Morpheus8 at Velaris Med Spa provides a local option for residents seeking collagen-stimulating treatments without the need to visit a distant clinic.

The treatment is positioned as a non-surgical approach for patients who want to address visible signs of skin laxity without the recovery time associated with surgical procedures. While individual outcomes vary and no specific results can be guaranteed, the technology is designed to support the body's own regenerative processes over a multi-week cycle following treatment.

Velaris Med Spa accepts appointment bookings online and by phone, allowing prospective patients to schedule a consultation with Shay Smith, FNP-C to determine whether Morpheus8 is appropriate for their skin concerns and goals. Those seeking Morpheus8 Burley Idaho services can contact the practice directly to begin the process.
