# Bobby Combs RV Centers Expands RV Sales in Mesa, AZ, With OPUS Campers

Used Truck Campers and Folding Pop-Up Campers Offer Flexible Outdoor Travel Options

 Mesa, United States - July 29, 2026 / Bobby Combs RV Centers - Mesa /

 MESA, ARIZONA, July 29, 2026 — Bobby Combs RV Centers - Mesa has expanded its recreational vehicle inventory with new 2026 OPUS camper models, providing RV shoppers with additional options for lightweight travel and off-grid camping. The updated inventory includes OPUS OP4, OP13, OP15, OP17, OP4 LE, and OP2 models available through the dealership’s locations, including Mesa, Arizona.

The lineup gives customers searching for an OPUS camper for sale access to compact and adventure-focused RV options designed for outdoor travel. With multiple layouts, sizes, and sleeping capacities, the updated selection supports travelers looking for flexible camping solutions.

Expanded OPUS Camper SelectionThe company continues to update its RV inventory with models from recognized manufacturers, including OPUS, Forest River, Keystone RV, Coachmen, and Palomino. The addition of new OPUS campers allows customers to explore folding campers and travel trailers designed for different travel needs.

New OPUS Models Available for Outdoor TravelThe 2026 OPUS OP4 is available as a folding pop-up camper with sleeping space for up to six travelers, a 20-foot length, and an approximate dry weight of 3,100 pounds. The model offers a compact option for campers seeking a lightweight RV for outdoor destinations.

The 2026 OPUS OP13 provides an adventure-focused travel trailer option with a 20-foot-5-inch length, sleeping space for two, and an approximate dry weight of 4,800 pounds. This model is designed for travelers who prefer a smaller trailer built for exploring remote locations.

Key details of the updated OPUS camper inventory include:

* Multiple 2026 OPUS models: The lineup includes OP4, OP13, OP15, OP17, OP4 LE, and OP2 options.
* Different camping layouts: Available models provide choices for couples, families, and outdoor travelers with varying space needs.
* Lightweight travel options: Several models feature compact designs that support easier towing and flexible trip planning.
* Adventure-focused designs: OPUS campers are built for customers interested in outdoor destinations and off-grid travel experiences.

Supporting RV Sales in Mesa and Surrounding MarketsWith the expanded inventory, the company continues serving customers searching for RV sales in Mesa, AZ and nearby areas. The dealership provides access to new and pre-owned travel trailers, fifth wheels, motorhomes, toy haulers, truck campers, and folding campers.

Multi-Location Inventory Access for RV ShoppersThe company operates locations across Arizona, Idaho, and Oregon, allowing customers to explore a wide range of RV options. The dealership’s locations in Mesa, Yuma, Caldwell, Hayden, Coeur D’Alene, and Coburg provide additional inventory choices for customers comparing RV models across different markets.

Expanded Truck Campers and Folding Pop-Up CampersThe dealership offers additional compact RV options, including new and used truck campers and folding pop-up campers. They carry models from brands such as Lance, Palomino, Forest River, and Northern Lite to support different travel needs.

Available options include lightweight truck campers like the 2026 Lance 1172, Northern Lite Limited Edition models, and Palomino Backpack Edition campers. With a range of RV categories available across its locations, the company helps customers compare options for outdoor travel, weekend trips, and extended adventures.

Supporting RV Ownership Beyond SalesThe dealership supports customers beyond RV purchases through service, parts, and maintenance resources.

* RV Service Support: Authorized service assistance for travel trailers, fifth wheels, motorhomes, toy haulers, and other RV types.
* Parts Availability: Access to RV parts and resources to help owners maintain their vehicles.
* Warranty Assistance: Support with eligible warranty and insurance-related service needs.

“We focus on providing customers with quality RV options, knowledgeable support, and reliable service throughout their ownership journey,” said Bobby Combs, Owner of the company.
