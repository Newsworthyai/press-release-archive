# Gabler Group AG Invites to Earnings Call on Unaudited Q3 2026 Results

LUBECK, GERMANY - November 10, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Gabler Group AG (ISIN: DE000A421RZ9 / Ticker: XK4, the “Company” or “Gabler”), an established developer and manufacturer of mission-critical subsea technologies in the business areas of Submarine Systems, Subsea Communications & Data and Subsea Power, invites investors and analysts to an earnings call with the Management Board on 11 November 2026, at 14:00 CET. During the call, CEO David Schirm and CSO Ole Johannsen will discuss the unaudited Q3 2026 results and provide an update on the current business development.

 The publication of the unaudited Q3 2026 results will take place on 11 November 2026, before market open.

 Registration for the earnings call is available via the following link:Earnings Call – Q3 2026.INVESTOR RELATIONS CONTACT:

 Patrick JacobsVP Investor RelationsE-Mail: IR@gablergroup.comABOUT GABLER GROUP

 Founded in 1962, the Gabler Group, headquartered in Lubeck, Germany, is an established developer and manufacturer of mission-critical subsea solutions generating the vast majority of its net sales from defense and defense-related solutions. Gabler is the leading European and one of the world’s largest suppliers of mission-critical hoistable masts and associated control systems in terms of volume to conventional submarines in the Submarine Systems business area. Gabler Group is a trusted partner to over 250 worldwide customers including 25 Navies and employs approximately 290 people.
