# Silver Crown Royalties Reports Q3 Results and Record Quarterly Earnings

TORONTO, ON - October 30, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Silver Crown Royalties Inc. (Cboe: SCRI, OTCQX: SLCRF, BF: QS0) (“Silver Crown”, “SCRi”, the “Corporation”, or the “Company”) announces the release of its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The company has filed its unaudited interim condensed consolidated financial statements, and management’s discussion & analysis, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 on SEDAR+ (www.sedarplus.ca) and will be uploading these filings to its website today. All amounts are in Canadian dollars, unless otherwise indicated.

 THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL AND CORPORATE HIGHLIGHTS:

 - Strong YoY Revenue Growth: Generated $1,609,779 in royalty revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026, a 541% increase over the $251,212 generated in Q3 2025. Revenue was 14% lower than the $1,867,056 generated in Q2 2026 due to a decrease in the average silver price in Q3 2026.

 - Silver Deliveries: Attributable silver deliveries totaled 18,063 ounces for the quarter, a 288% increase from 4,651 ounces in Q3 2025 and unchanged from Q2 2026.

 - Record Quarterly Net Income: Net income of $379,110 ($0.07 per basic share) for the quarter, compared to a net loss of $468,172 in Q3 2025, representing the Company’s second consecutive profitable quarter.

 - Strengthened Balance Sheet: Cash and term deposits of $7,720,517 and working capital of $15,485,929 as at September 30, 2026, with $13,837,779 received from the exercise of warrants in the first nine months of 2026.

 - Expanded Madre Sierra Royalty Position: Effective September 15, 2026, the Company completed its acquisition of a third existing privately held 1% net smelter return royalty on Titiminas Silver Inc.’s Madre Sierra deposit in Jauja, Peru. This adds to the two 1% net smelter return royalties acquired effective June 30, 2026. Consideration for the third royalty was:

 -- US$2,000,000 in cash paid at closing;

 -- US$1,000,000 paid through the issuance of 38,401 units of the Company, each comprising one common share and one-half of one warrant exercisable at 150% of the issue price for 24 months;

 -- up to US$1,000,000 in additional units, payable once the first royalty payment is received after at least 30 days of continuous production at a rate of at least 70 tonnes per day.

 ABOUT SILVER CROWN ROYALTIES INC.

 Founded by seasoned industry professionals, Silver Crown Royalties (Cboe: SCRI | OTCQX: SLCRF | BF: QS0) is a publicly traded silver royalty company dedicated to generating free cash flow. Silver Crown currently holds eight silver royalties across six projects. Its business model offers investors exposure to precious metals, providing a natural hedge against currency devaluation while mitigating the adverse effects of production-related cost inflation. Silver Crown strives to minimize the economic burden on mining projects while simultaneously maximizing shareholder returns. For further information, please contact:
