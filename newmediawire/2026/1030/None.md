# Securing Autonomy: Why Cybersecurity Is Becoming Essential Infrastructure for Military Drones and Robotics

* Autonomous drones depend on more than flight capabilities. Secure communications, reliable control systems and trustworthy data are also essential to maintaining mission success.
* As unmanned systems operate in increasingly contested environments, cybersecurity can help protect against threats ranging from unauthorized system changes to disrupted or manipulated communications.
* Mobilicom Ltd. addresses these risks through its Secured Autonomy (TM) framework, which spans platform security, communications and electronic warfare (“EW”) protection, and fleet-level security.

 LOS ANGELES, CA - October 30, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Autonomous drones and other unmanned systems are designed to carry out missions with varying degrees of human oversight. But their capabilities depend on more than their ability to fly or navigate. They also depend on communications links connecting platforms with operators, onboard control systems that execute mission instructions, and reliable data that supports decision-making.
