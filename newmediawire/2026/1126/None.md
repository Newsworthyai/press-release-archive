# Inverite Launches Portfolio IQ, Expanding Financial Intelligence Across the Lending Lifecycle

VANCOUVER, BRITISH COLUMBIA - November 26, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Inverite Insights Inc. ("Inverite" or the "Company") (CSE: INVR) (OTC: INVRF) (FSE: 2V0), a Canadian risk infrastructure company that transforms permissioned financial data into trusted financial intelligence that powers identity, risk and financial decision-making across financial institutions and enterprise organizations, today announced the launch of Portfolio IQ, a portfolio intelligence solution designed to help lenders connect historical and ongoing loan performance with the financial data and risk intelligence available at origination.

 ![](http://newmediawire.s3.amazonaws.com/6b07390fda846f0bfdc8262f_1)

 New portfolio intelligence solution gives lenders the evidence to validate, monitor and refine risk strategies over the life of the loan.

 Portfolio IQ extends Inverite’s risk intelligence capabilities beyond the initial credit decision by tying what was known about a borrower at origination with what happened over the life of the loan.

 Built on Inverite’s existing financial data and risk intelligence infrastructure, Portfolio IQ expands the Company’s intelligence capabilities across the lending lifecycle, creating additional opportunities to deepen customer relationships and deliver ongoing value through portfolio analysis and risk intelligence services.

 Through Inverite’s new Loan Performance API, lenders can provide structured data on historical and ongoing loan repayment outcomes that can be linked back to the original financial data, Default IQ score and lending decision. This creates a continuous feedback loop that gives lenders and Inverite’s risk team the data needed to evaluate portfolio performance over time.

 Portfolio IQ can support retrospective model validation, score-band and cut-off analysis, portfolio monitoring, strategy review and bespoke risk model development.

 “Every lending decision is an opportunity to learn from the outcome,” said Karim Nanji, CEO of Inverite. “Portfolio IQ connects our predictive financial intelligence with actual loan performance, giving lenders a clearer understanding of how their risk strategies perform over time. It also expands the value of Inverite’s intelligence infrastructure by supporting our customers beyond the initial lending decision.”

 Start with the portfolio you already have.

 Lenders do not need to be existing Default IQ users to get started with Portfolio IQ.

 Historical loan performance can be matched with the financial and borrower data available at origination to retrospectively evaluate how Inverite’s Default IQ Core or Default IQ Flex models would have performed against an existing portfolio.

 This analysis can help lenders evaluate how observed outcomes differ across score bands, explore potential score cut-offs and determine whether one of Inverite’s models aligns with their portfolio. Historical performance data can also provide the outcome data needed to support development of a bespoke risk model with Inverite’s data science team.

 Once a lender implements a score or risk strategy, the same Loan Performance API can continue receiving new outcomes. Periodic analysis with Inverite can then help lenders assess whether portfolio performance remains consistent with what was observed during validation and whether further review of a model, cut-off or strategy is warranted.

 This continuous cycle of validation, implementation, monitoring and refinement creates opportunities for ongoing engagement between Inverite and its lending customers, extending the value of Inverite’s financial intelligence beyond the initial lending decision.

 Closing the loop with the Loan Performance API.

 At the centre of Portfolio IQ is Inverite’s new Loan Performance API, a structured way for lenders to provide loan and repayment outcomes to Inverite.

 Depending on the data available, lenders can provide information including loan and verification identifiers, origination date and amount, product and channel, payments and missed payments, non-sufficient funds events, delinquency and days past due, defaults, write-offs and recoveries.

 By pairing these outcomes with origination data, Portfolio IQ creates a dataset that Inverite and its clients can use to analyze how lending decisions perform over time.

 "Lenders are increasingly looking for ways to understand how their credit decisions translate into actual portfolio performance. Portfolio IQ brings together the financial intelligence available at origination with real loan outcomes, giving lenders additional insight into their risk strategies and how those strategies perform over time. We see meaningful value in making these capabilities available to the lending market," said Valérie Robillard, Co-Founder and Co-President of CreditBook.

 From predicting risk to understanding outcomes.

 Portfolio IQ complements Inverite’s existing financial data and risk intelligence products by extending the risk lifecycle beyond origination.

 Together, Default IQ and Portfolio IQ connect predictive risk intelligence with actual loan outcomes, supporting a more complete understanding of portfolio performance throughout the lending lifecycle.

 This lets lenders review outcomes such as delinquency, defaults, losses, and recoveries across dimensions including score bands, vintages, products, channels, borrower segments, funded volume, and loan size.

 Portfolio IQ can also support analysis of the trade-offs between risk tolerance, origination objectives and available capital. For example, a lender looking to expand originations may evaluate the historical risk associated with broadening its approval population, while a lender operating with constrained capital may assess the implications of more selective score cut-offs.

 Portfolio IQ provides the evidence for this analysis; lending, risk and capital decisions remain with the lender.

 Portfolio IQ and the Loan Performance API are now available to lenders working with Inverite.

 For more information about Portfolio IQ, visit https://inveriteinsights.com/portfolio-iq/
