# Insurance Canopy Expands Small Business Coverage With Florist and Plumbing Insurance Programs

New Offerings Help More Small Business Owners Access Same-day Coverage and Instant Certificates of Insurance Through Insurance Canopy's Online Platform

 PLEASANT GROVE, UTAH - September 10, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Insurance Canopy, a national leader in small business and vendor insurance solutions, today announced the launch of insurance programs for florists and plumbing contractors. The launch broadens the company’s specialty portfolio, giving more small business owners access to fast, online coverage.

 Florists and plumbing professionals can purchase coverage online, receive same-day protection, and access an instant certificate of insurance. This helps them quickly meet venue, contract, and jobsite requirements - without lengthy application processes.

 “Every small business deserves an insurance experience that is fast, straightforward, and built around the way they work,” said Daryle Stafford, CEO of Veracity Insurance, parent company of Insurance Canopy. "Whether they're fulfilling customer orders, providing services on-site, or managing projects in the field, today's business owners need insurance that can keep up with the demands of their daily operations. By bringing these programs onto our platform, we're making it easy to purchase coverage online, access policy documents and certificates of insurance from anywhere, and get the proof of coverage they need within minutes so they can stay focused on growing their business."

 Insurance Canopy developed the new programs through its relationship with Coterie Insurance, expanding coverage options while maintaining the streamlined digital experience customers rely on. The launch also lays the groundwork for additional business classes expected to join the platform in the near future.

 “Expanding into florist and plumbing coverage is part of a broader effort to make specialty business insurance easier to access,” said Chris Van Leeuwen, Vice President of Professional Development, Insurance Canopy. “We're continuing to add programs that serve the unique needs of different industries without changing the experience customers have come to expect. Business owners should be able to get the coverage they need quickly, so they can stay focused on running their businesses instead of navigating a complicated insurance process."

 Insurance Canopy provides industry-specific insurance for thousands of small businesses nationwide, combining an online purchasing experience with same-day coverage, instant certificates of insurance, and self-service policy management.

 The new florist insurance and plumbing insurance programs are now available through Insurance Canopy. To learn more, please visit https://www.insurancecanopy.com/.

 About Insurance Canopy

 Insurance Canopy provides customized liability insurance solutions designed for the needs of small and midsize businesses. From instant coverage for solopreneurs and mobile businesses to comprehensive policies for brick-and-mortar operations, Insurance Canopy serves business owners across a wide range of industries.

 Founded in 2016, Insurance Canopy combines the expertise of a traditional brokerage, an in-house agency, and the convenience of a digital insurance provider. Through its user-friendly online platform, business owners can access fast, personalized quotes and purchase coverage directly. At its core, Insurance Canopy is committed to making business insurance more accessible and helping entrepreneurs protect what they’ve built, so they can move their businesses forward with confidence.
