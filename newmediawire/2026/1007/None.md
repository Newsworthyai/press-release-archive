# CleanGo Innovations Inc. Appoints Marine Bunkering Veteran Larry Messina to Advisory Board to Spearhead Global Launch of CG-M100

VANCOUVER, B.C. and HOUSTON, TEXAS - October 7, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - CleanGo Innovations Inc. (CSE: CGII) (OTC: CLGOF) (FRA: APO2) (“CleanGo” or the “Company”)CleanGo Innovations Inc., a leader in green chemical solutions and industrial remediation, is pleased to announce the appointment of international bunker industry veteran Larry Messina to its Advisory Board. In his new role, Messina will provide strategic guidance to accelerate the global commercial launch of CleanGo Marine’s flagship product, CG-M100.

 Larry brings decades of executive leadership, ownership, and market expansion expertise to CleanGo Innovations. A prominent figure in the global marine fuel sector, Larry was a co-owner and long-standing executive at Glander International Bunkering, where he helped steer the company's growth across North American, South American, and Caribbean shipping markets following the relocation of its core operations to Florida in 1991. His extensive career includes navigating complex maritime regulatory frameworks, building robust international networks, and establishing key commercial channels across the global shipping industry.

 The appointment comes as CleanGo Innovations accelerates its maritime sector expansion, anchored by the introduction of CG-M100 - a certified green, water-based, safe, and highly effective solution designed to meet stringent international maritime environmental standards.

 "Larry Messina’s deep-rooted expertise in the global marine industry is an invaluable asset to CleanGo Innovations," said Anthony Sarvucci, CEO of CleanGo Innovations. "Navigating the complexities of maritime compliance and supply chains requires seasoned leadership. With Larry guiding our strategy, we are exceptionally well-positioned to scale the deployment of CG-M100 worldwide, providing the shipping industry with a powerful, certified environmentally friendly alternative that does not compromise on performance."

 "CleanGo Innovations has developed a green certified solution with CG-M100 at a time when the maritime industry is actively seeking verifiable, eco-friendly chemical alternatives," said Larry Messina. "I look forward to working alongside Anthony and the CleanGo team to leverage my experience across the marine sector, opening doors to key global shipping corridors and helping commercial fleets meet their environmental mandates efficiently."

 With Larry’s guidance, CleanGo Innovations will target key international shipping hubs to introduce CG-M100 to vessel operators, port authorities, and maritime service providers worldwide.
