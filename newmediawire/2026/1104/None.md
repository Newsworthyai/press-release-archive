# Lion E-Mobility Announces Planned Board Transition

ZUG, SWITZERLAND - November 4, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - LION E-Mobility AG (LION; ISIN: CH0560888270), a leading manufacturer of battery packs for electric mobility and energy storage solutions, announces that Alessio Basteri will step down from the Board of Directors.Alessio Basteri, Member of the Board of Directors of LION E-Mobility AG: “I am grateful to my colleagues at LION, and especially to my fellow Board members, for their trust and collaboration. It has been an amazing journey. I will continue to do what I can to support LION’s Italian BESS opportunity.”Ian Mukherjee, Chairman of the Board of Directors of LION E-Mobility AG: “Alessio has served LION as Chairman for many years, and his work and commitment have been greatly valued. On behalf of the Board, I would like to express my sincere thanks for his contribution to the Company and for the time and dedication he has given to the role. We also appreciate his willingness to continue supporting LION’s Italian BESS opportunity, and we wish him every success in the future.”The Company will immediately begin the process of identifying a new Board member resident in Switzerland.About LION E-Mobility AG:LION E-Mobility AG is a manufacturer of lithium-ion battery packs. The company offers customized plug-and-play solutions for electric vehicles as well as for stationary and industrial applications. The company operates highly automated module assembly lines at its own production facility in Germany. LION's battery packs offer the highest standards in terms of safety, quality, and reliability. Founded in 2011, LION E-Mobility AG (ISIN: CH0560888270, WKN: A2QH 97) is listed on the stock exchanges in Munich, Frankfurt, and Hamburg.
