# Angkor Resources' Enercam Submits Drilling Contractor Tender to the Ministry of Mines and Energy

GRANDE PRAIRIE, ALBERTA - November 4, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - ANGKOR RESOURCES CORP. (TSXV: ANK OTCQB: ANKOF) (“ANGKOR” OR “THE COMPANY”) announces that its Cambodian energy subsidiary, EnerCam Resources Co., Ltd. (“EnerCam”), has sent the terms of its comprehensive drilling contractor tender to Cambodia’s Ministry of Mines and Energy (“MME”) for review. Once reviewed and approved by MME, the tender will be public so that drilling companies can bid to drill the exploratory wells on Block VIII.

 ![](http://newmediawire.s3.amazonaws.com/6aea57a4da846f196f288ee7_1)

 The tender sets out the full scope of work the successful drilling contractor will be required to deliver, from bringing the rig and equipment into Cambodia through to testing each well. It follows the Ministry of Environment’s approval of EnerCam’s Environmental Impact Assessment (“EIA”) to drill on all four sub-basins, announced on September 30, 2026.

 Mike Weeks, President of EnerCam, commented: “This tender puts on paper everything the first wells in Cambodia need to deliver, and that includes the equipment, the drilling, the testing and how we protect the communities around each site. We have already done our due diligence on the contractors, so once the Ministry has reviewed the terms we can move quickly to selecting the company that will drill.”

 HIGHLIGHTS OF THE 89-PAGE TENDER INCLUDE:

 * Mobilization: import and mobilization of the drilling rig, pumps, drilling muds, casing, wellheads and necessary consumables into Cambodia and to each drill site.
* Drilling sequence: one exploration well in each of the four sub-basins - South Bokor, Central Bokor, North Bokor and Kirirom - drilled in the order specified by EnerCam’s technical team, with a fifth well to be located based on the results of the first four wells.
* Depth and targets: drilling to depths of up to 3,300 meters, targeting a minimum of two potential pay zones at the three Bokor sub-basins, including fractured Permian carbonates below the Indosinian 1 unconformity.
* Completion, logging and testing: logging of each well, and completion and production testing of each well bore that indicates hydrocarbons. Each well is designed to be completed in a full production configuration, flow-tested for four weeks, and then shut in for a 12-week pressure build-up, so that testing gives a definitive answer on the commerciality of the reservoir.
* Social, environmental and governance compliance: adherence to the conditions of the approved EIA within the target areas, including protection of local communities, water and land, the handling of drilling fluids, and restoration of each site if a well is not productive.

 SELECTING THE RIGHT CONTRACTOR

 EnerCam has already completed due diligence on multiple potential drilling contractors, so the bid process can move quickly once the tender is public. During 2026, the team made two visits to China to meet three potential drilling service providers, and hosted site visits to Block VIII by two potential drilling contractors to assess the roads, bridges and drill sites against the equipment required. The focus has been on each contractor’s capacity to deliver the complete service - rigs, crews, logging and testing equipment - to international standards.

 Drilling contractors are selected through a tendered bid that is first approved by MME and then published in various media outlets. EnerCam is targeting bid selection in early 2027. Mobilizing the drilling equipment to the sites takes approximately three months once a drilling contractor is selected.

 SETTING THE STANDARD FOR CAMBODIA’S ONSHORE SECTOR

 As Cambodia’s first onshore oil and gas wells, the Block VIII wells will provide the first direct well data from the onshore basins targeted by EnerCam. Getting the most out of every well - the logs, the flow tests and the pressure data - is essential to building an onshore energy sector for the nation. The program also sets a precedent for how onshore wells are permitted, contracted and drilled in Cambodia as the sector develops.

 Members of EnerCam’s drilling team, including drilling manager John Koch, have designed and built oil and gas drill programs and drill sites in over 60 countries. That experience brings current global practice and technology to Block VIII, so that Cambodia can move directly to modern standards rather than repeating decades of past practice seen elsewhere in the world.

 EnerCam has completed 350 line-kilometers of 2D seismic and defined drill targets across four sub-basins. Block VIII covers approximately 4,095 km² in the southwest quadrant of Cambodia under a 30-year Production Sharing Contract with the Royal Government of Cambodia.

 As Cambodia imports 100% of its hydrocarbon-based energy products, discovering a national domestic supply of oil and gas remains a high priority.

 The Company will continue providing further updates on the drilling tender, drilling contractors and well-planning progress at regular intervals.

 ABOUT ANGKOR RESOURCES CORP.
