# Charbone Announces Preliminary Q3 2026 Revenue Growth

VARENNES, QUEBEC - October 28, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - CHARBONE CORPORATION (TSXV: CH; OTCQB: CHHYF; FSE: K47) (“CHARBONE” or the “Company”), a vertically integrated industrial gases company focused on production, distribution and storage of clean ultra-high purity (“UHP”) hydrogen and other strategic industrial gases, today announced that preliminary gas income for the three-month period ending September 30, 2026 is expected to increase 21% to $0.55 million from gas income of $0.46 million for the prior three-month period ending June 30, 2026. Gas income relates to revenues across CHARBONE’s hydrogen production and regional supply hubs for all industrial gases. The Company anticipates releasing full financial and operational results for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026, on Thursday, November 26, 2026, after markets close at the latest. CHARBONE will also host a webinar on Tuesday, December 1, 2026 at 11:00 AM ET. Registration details are provided below.

 Company to host Q3 2026 results webinar on December 1, 2026

 In Q3 2026, CHARBONE anticipates sales growth across multiple industrial gases in both the U.S. and Canadian markets, including clean UHP hydrogen produced at the Company’s Sorel-Tracy Phase 1A plant, as well as UHP helium and UHP oxygen sourced through partners. Demand remained strong throughout Q3 2026 and is expected to result in increased recurring revenues and new customers. CHARBONE continues to expand its Industrial Gas Platform, including distribution equipment, to support growing demand and expected future sales growth from new and existing customers.

 PRELIMINARY Q3 2026 GAS INCOME HIGHLIGHTS:

 * Gas income is expected to increase 21% to $0.55 million in Q3 2026, compared to $0.46 million in Q2 2026.
* For the nine-month period ending September 30, 2026, gas income is expected to total $1.2 million, compared to $nil for the same period in 2025.
* Operating expenses in Q3 2026 are expected to remain comparable to Q2 2026, as the Company continues to internalize key steps, including cylinder filling at Sorel-Tracy, which are expected to support margin improvement.

 CORPORATE GROWTH METRICS (as of September 30, 2026, compared to Q1 2026):

 * Active customers: 28, up from 3
* Trailers (all gases): 11, up from 2
* Multi-year supply agreements: 4, up from 2
* Customer deliveries: approximately 12 per month, up from approximately 2
* Regional supply hubs: 4 (Quebec, Ontario, Nova Scotia and New York State), up from 2
* Employees: 15, up from 9

 OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS:

 * Sorel-Tracy, Phase 1A: UHP hydrogen production operational capacity 24/7, with on-site quality control chromatographs operational and a dedicated lab technician hired. Moisture is consistently maintained below 2 ppm.
* Sorel-Tracy, Phase 1B: Electrolyzer installation is underway, civil work is complete, and equipment connections are advancing. Phase 1B remains on track for a Fall 2026 launch.
* Wolf River, Wisconsin: The generator is being repaired in Lévis, Quebec, with reinstallation targeted for December 2026. A reservoir refill plan has been submitted to the U.S. Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) and stakeholders.
* Hydrogen and Helium Cylinder Filling Unit: Cylinder filling has been brought in-house at Sorel-Tracy, supporting margin improvement and enhanced control over product quality and delivery timelines. Construction of the unit’s dedicated building is advancing.

 “We have delivered the strongest quarter to date with an incredible team, which underscores our unwavering focus on long-term value creation and growth.” said Benoit Veilleux, Chief Financial Officer and Corporate Secretary of CHARBONE.

 About CHARBONE CORPORATION

 CHARBONE is a vertically integrated industrial gases company focused on developing and operating a network of supply hubs for the production, storage, and distribution of Ultra-High Purity (UHP) strategic industrial gases. The Company serves customers across sectors including semiconductors, artificial intelligence and data centers, advanced pharmaceuticals, and aerospace and defense technologies, where UHP gases are critical for high-precision manufacturing processes and operational performance. CHARBONE is advancing a network of clean UHP hydrogen production plants across North America and selected international markets. The Company’s modular, decentralized, and demand-driven approach, combined with its integrated storage and distribution platform for all UHP gases, supports scalable growth, enhances operational flexibility, and enables more stable and diversified revenue generation. This model allows CHARBONE to efficiently serve mid-tier industrial gas customers with a reliable supply of UHP gases, including hydrogen, helium, oxygen, and any other high-demand gases that are often difficult to source reliably at the regional level. The Company is committed to supporting the global transition to a lower-carbon economy by providing accessible, decentralized clean hydrogen and specialty gases, while addressing supply gaps for underserved industrial customers and accelerating the shift towards localized clean energy. CHARBONE is listed on the TSX Venture Exchange (TSXV: CH), the OTC Markets (OTCQB: CHHYF), and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange (FSE: K47). For more information, please visit: www.charbone.com.
