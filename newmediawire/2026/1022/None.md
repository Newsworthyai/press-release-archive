# Mobilicom (MOB) Targets Growing Drone and Robotics Market With Cybersecure Technology Portfolio

* Mobilicom provides integrated cybersecure hardware and software solutions for defense and commercial drones, robotics and autonomous systems.
* Its technologies are designed to support mission-critical communications, reliable connectivity and protection against electronic interference and cyber threats.
* The recent launch of the SwarmiX family expands Mobilicom’s portfolio with high-throughput mesh communications designed for coordinated drones and robotic fleets.

 LOS ANGELES, CA - October 22, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - As defense organizations and commercial operators expand their use of unmanned systems, the technologies that keep these platforms connected, secure and operational are becoming increasingly important. Mobilicom (NASDAQ: MOB) addresses these needs through an integrated portfolio of cybersecurity, software and hardware designed for drones, robotics and other autonomous platforms operating in demanding environments.
