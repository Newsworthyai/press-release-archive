# Piranha Profits Announced As The Benzinga Featured Brand In Financial Education &amp; Technology At The 2026 Benzinga Awards

DETROIT, MICHIGAN - November 21, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Benzinga today announced Piranha Profits as the Benzinga Featured Brand in Financial Education & Technology in the 2026 Benzinga Awards, recognizing excellence and innovation across financial services and fintech.

 ![](http://newmediawire.s3.amazonaws.com/6b00c813da846f52982a9fc1_1)

 The Benzinga Awards honor companies, products and industry leaders helping shape the future of finance. Now in its 12th year, the program recognizes achievement across a range of categories spanning financial technology, investing, data, platforms, services and innovation.

 "We’re proud of this recognition as Piranha Profits marks its 10th year of empowering retail investors and traders with the mindset, skills and judgment to profit for life,” Adam Khoo, Founder of Piranha Profits, said. “In our next chapter, we’ll further harness AI and new technologies to give our global community smarter tools to navigate the markets with confidence.”

 The 2026 Benzinga Awards were evaluated in collaboration with DALBAR, bringing independent research, analytical rigor and industry expertise to the judging process.

 Winners across the 2026 program are being recognized on November 19, 2026, as Benzinga brings together award recipients, sponsors and industry leaders to celebrate companies and individuals advancing the financial industry.

 For more information about the Benzinga Awards and this year’s winners, visit https://www.benzinga.com/fintech-awards.

 Methodology

 DALBAR evaluated submissions and nominees based on criteria tailored to each award category, assessing the quality, relevance, value and effectiveness of the products, services and customer experiences offered. The judging process considered both quantitative and qualitative factors, including product and service features, ease of use, customer support, cost relative to benefits, transparency, security and customer protections.

 When possible, DALBAR conducted hands-on reviews to assess the customer experience, including how easily customers could access information, navigate services and complete important tasks. Evaluators also considered whether new features or improvements delivered meaningful benefits relevant to the award category, rather than simply representing something new.

 Evaluation criteria were adapted to the specific award, with finalists and winners determined by how well each submission met the relevant standards and demonstrated value to customers.
