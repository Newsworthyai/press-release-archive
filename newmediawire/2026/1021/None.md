# TruGolf Holdings (TRUG) Completes Polymath Acquisition, Offering Expansion Into Institutional Tokenization

* The acquisition adds regulated digital-asset infrastructure to TruGolf’s existing golf simulation technology business.
* Polymath developed Polymesh, a Layer-1 blockchain designed for regulated securities and tokenized real-world assets.
* As of December 31, 2025, Polymath had issued more than $132 million in tokenized assets for over 65 active issuers.
* The combined company plans to pursue two complementary businesses: golf simulation technology and institutional tokenization infrastructure.
* TruGolf Links and Polymath are developing tokenized financing for equipment leasing and fractional franchise ownership, with initiatives targeted for the first quarter of 2027.
* TruGolf has also appointed Natalie Hirsch as Group CFO and COO, while a warrant exercise generated $2.95 million in net proceeds.

 LOS ANGELES, CA - October 21, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - TruGolf Holdings (NASDAQ: TRUG), a producer of advanced golf simulation systems for home or entertainment venues, has completed its acquisition of Polymath Research Inc., bringing a blockchain platform designed for regulated financial assets into the publicly traded golf technology company’s business.
