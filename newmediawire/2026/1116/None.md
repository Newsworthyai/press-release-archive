# Silver Crown Royalties Upsizes Non-Brokered Private Placement to C$11 Million Due to Strong Demand

TORONTO, ON - November 16, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Silver Crown Royalties Inc. (Cboe: SCRI, OTCQX: SLCRF, BF: QS0) ("Silver Crown", "SCRi", the "Corporation", or the "Company") is pleased to announce that, as a result of strong investor demand, it has increased the size of its non-brokered private placement of common shares of the Corporation ("Common Shares") announced on November 12, 2026 (the "Offering"). The Offering will now consist of up to 314,286 Common Shares at a price of C$35.00 per Common Share (the "Offering Price") for gross proceeds of up to approximately C$11,000,000, increased from up to approximately C$5,000,000. All other terms of the Offering remain as previously announced.

 The Company intends to use the net proceeds of the Offering for working capital and corporate development purposes, including the funding of royalty acquisitions. The Offering is expected to close on or before November 30, 2026 and may be completed in one or more tranches. Closing is subject to customary closing conditions, including the acceptance of Cboe Canada. Common Shares issued under the Offering will be subject to a statutory hold period of four months and one day from the date of issuance in accordance with applicable Canadian securities laws.

 Insiders of the Company, including Peter Bures and Michael Gentile, are expected to participate in the Offering as described in the Company’s news release of November 12, 2026, and the Company continues to rely on the exemptions from the formal valuation and minority shareholder approval requirements of Multilateral Instrument 61-101 – Protection of Minority Security Holders in Special Transactions set out in that release.

 Peter Bures, CEO, commented: “following the closing of the private placement, Silver Crown Royalties will have over C$18.9 million in cash with a further 30,000 ounces of silver positioning us to complete the Soma El Bagre Silver royalty and a number of smaller projects.”

 ABOUT SILVER CROWN ROYALTIES INC.

 Founded by seasoned industry professionals, Silver Crown Royalties (Cboe: SCRI | OTCQX: SLCRF | BF: QS0) is a publicly traded silver royalty company dedicated to generating free cash flow. Silver Crown currently holds eight silver royalties across six projects and has agreed to acquire a ninth royalty, on Soma Gold Corp.’s El Bagre Gold Mining Complex in Colombia, which is expected to close on or before November 30, 2026. Its business model offers investors exposure to precious metals, providing a natural hedge against currency devaluation while mitigating the adverse effects of production-related cost inflation. Silver Crown strives to minimize the economic burden on mining projects while simultaneously maximizing shareholder returns. For further information, please contact:
