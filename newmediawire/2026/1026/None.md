# Benzinga Extends 2026 Benzinga Awards Application Deadline To October 28

DETROIT, MICHIGAN - October 26, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Benzinga today announced that the application deadline for the Benzinga Awards 2026 has been extended to October 28, 2026, at 11:59 PM Eastern, giving companies additional time to submit for consideration in this year's program.

 Now in its 12th year, the Benzinga Awards is a digital awards program recognizing leading companies, innovators and professionals across financial services and fintech.

 The 2026 program, which is judged in collaboration with DALBAR, will highlight organizations helping shape the future of financial technology, investing and financial services across a range of award categories.

 The digital awards program will culminate in an in-person celebration on November 19, 2026 for an evening recognizing this year's honorees and the companies shaping the future of finance.

 Interested applicants can apply here: https://www.benzinga.com/fintech-awards#apply

 The awards submission portal will remain open through October 28, 2026, at 11:59 PM Eastern. Applications cost $250 for a single award submission or $500 for unlimited award submissions per company.

 Winners will be announced on November 19th, 2026.

 To apply to the Benzinga Awards 2026 program, click here.

 For more information about the awards or sponsorship opportunities, please visit https://www.benzinga.com/fintech-awards or reach out to partnerships@benzinga.com.
