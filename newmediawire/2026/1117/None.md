# Wrap Technologies (WRAP) Reports Q3 Revenue of $1.5M, Nine-Month Revenue Growth of 43%

LOS ANGELES, CA - November 17, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Wrap Technologies (NASDAQ: WRAP) reported financial and operating results for the third quarter ended Sept. 30, 2026, with revenue of $1.5 million, consistent with the prior-year period. Product sales increased 20% to $1.3 million, while gross profit declined to $0.8 million from $0.9 million and gross margin narrowed to approximately 55% from 59%. Net loss widened to $3.9 million from $2.8 million, primarily reflecting higher noncash share-based compensation. For the first nine months of 2026, revenue increased 43% to $4.7 million, driven by a 174% increase in product sales to $3.9 million. Nine-month gross profit rose 56% to $3.1 million, while gross margin expanded to approximately 66% from 60%.

 Wrap ended the quarter with $12.1 million in cash and cash equivalents, up 249% from $3.5 million at year-end 2025, following a registered direct offering that generated approximately $12 million in gross proceeds. During the quarter, the U.S. Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (“ATF”) classified the BolaWrap 150 as an instrument of restraint, and the company made a strategic investment in Frenel Imaging Ltd. to support the detection component of its WrapShield platform. Net cash used in operating activities for the first nine months improved 30% to $5.3 million from $7.6 million in the comparable 2025 period.

 About Wrap Technologies, Inc.

 Wrap Technologies, Inc. is a public safety technology company focused on non-lethal response solutions, training, and integrated technologies designed to support law enforcement and security professionals. Through its BolaWrap family of remote restraint devices, training programs, and expanding technology portfolio, WRAP seeks to provide officers and agencies with additional options to manage encounters, support de-escalation, and improve public safety outcomes.
