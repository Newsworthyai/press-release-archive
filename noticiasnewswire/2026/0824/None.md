# Businessman Mario Murgado to Receive The ‘Automotive Entrepreneurship &amp; Business Leadership Award’ in Miami

MIAMI, FL — August 24, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Negocios Magazine announced that Mario Murgado, President & CEO of Murgado Automotive Group, will be among the business leaders recognized during the 2026 edition of the Top Entrepreneur of USA Awards, which will take place on Thursday, October 8, at the EB Hotel Miami.

 Murgado will receive the special “Automotive Entrepreneurship & Business Leadership Award,” recognizing his entrepreneurial career, leadership in the automotive industry, and the growth and expansion that have transformed Murgado Automotive Group into an organization with operations across multiple U.S. markets.

 Headquartered in Miami, Murgado Automotive Group currently operates 29 locations across five states, with a presence in markets throughout Florida, Illinois, New Jersey, Virginia, and California.

 From Miami to a Nationwide Presence

 Murgado’s entrepreneurial journey has deep roots in Miami. The group highlights that, under his leadership, Buick, GMC, and Honda operations near Downtown Miami improved their performance after years of declining results under previous ownership, subsequently becoming standout operations for General Motors and Honda.

 Today, the group’s operations represent brands including Honda, Toyota, Ford, Lincoln, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Cadillac, Audi, Volkswagen, Acura, Infiniti, Mazda, Buick, GMC, Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, and Ram, as well as luxury and high-performance brands such as Ferrari, Bentley, Maserati, and Alfa Romeo.

 “For Negocios Magazine, it is a true honor to recognize Mario Murgado, an entrepreneur with deep ties to Miami who has successfully transformed a local automotive operation into a group with a presence across different regions of the United States,” said Eduardo Hapke, Publisher of Negocios Magazine.

 An evening dedicated to business leadership

 The Top Entrepreneur of USA Awards 2026 will bring together approximately 120 entrepreneurs, CEOs, executives, investors, and professionals for an awards ceremony and dinner organized by Negocios Magazine.

 The event will take place on Thursday, October 8, 2026, in the Ballroom of the EB Hotel Miami.

 About Negocios Magazine Negocios Magazine was founded in 2013 by journalist Eduardo Hapke and attorney Karen Blanco with the goal of developing a communications platform focused on entrepreneurs, executives, business owners, and business leaders in South Florida and the U.S. Hispanic market.

 The magazine is published in both print and digital formats and also operates NegociosMagazine.com, a platform featuring news, interviews, business profiles, and content related to business, finance, technology, investments, entrepreneurship, and leadership.

 Negocios Magazine further expands the distribution of its content through international digital platforms such as PressReader, as well as through its social media presence and business events.

 The publication is part of Acta Diurna Urbis Group Corp., a Miami, Florida-based media group that develops publications and platforms targeting different market segments, including Ejecutiva Magazine, Welcome to America, Salud & Buen Vivir Florida, Hispanic Time, and Electric Cars Plus.
