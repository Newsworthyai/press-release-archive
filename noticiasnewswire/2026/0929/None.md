# Bruno Stretti: Global Vision, Business, and a Passion for High-Performance tennis in Miami

MIAMI, FL — September 29, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Hispanic Target Magazine is proud to officially announce the release of its exclusive new cover edition, featuring renowned international coach Bruno Stretti. Entitled “Bruno Stretti: Global Vision, Business, and a Passion for High-Performance Tennis in Miami”, this special issue deeply examines the transformative impact of a strategist who is redefining the standards of elite competition in South Florida and throughout the North American market.

 An elite leader in the South Florida ecosystem

 Armed with elite international certifications and a proven track record on the world’s most demanding sporting stages, the Argentine strategist firmly assumes technical leadership as head coach of the Smatt Tennis Academy (All Florida Tennis LLC), strategically located at the prestigious Golden Isles Tennis Center in Hallandale Beach (600 Blue Heron Drive). His appointment brings undisputed value, energizing the local tennis industry and attracting world-class talent to the Floridian community.

 In South Florida’s competitive ecosystem—where luxury, high-stakes competition, and business opportunities intersect—few trajectories reflect international merit with such force as Stretti’s. His work goes far beyond technical instruction: he embodies a comprehensive development model combining discipline, resilience, and an entrepreneurial vision centered on excellence and regional economic growth.

 From Patagonia to the World Circuit: A Story of Perseverance and Success

 Stretti’s path to global success began thousands of miles away on the demanding hard courts of Trelew, in Argentine Patagonia. Far from being an obstacle, geographic isolation forged an unbreakable character and a unique strategic vision. After competing for more than six years in junior and professional tournaments sanctioned by the Argentine Tennis Association (AAT), COSAT circuits, ITF tournaments, and the Swiss professional circuit representing Lausanne Sports, he realized that talent alone is not enough.

 His early move to Buenos Aires at age 14 broadened his horizons, prompting him to later return to his home region to radically transform the local landscape. Beginning in 2019, as a fundamental pillar of the Trelew Tennis Club’s high-performance program, he coached top prospects such as Estanislao Lassaga—who claimed his first ATP doubles point and an ITF title under his tutelage—and was the main architect behind bringing international competitions like

 the M25 and W15 professional tournaments to Patagonia. This milestone demonstrated his exceptional capacity to generate economic, institutional, and sporting impact on a global scale.

 In the Elite of International Tennis: Grand Slams and ATP/WTA Stars

 Stretti’s international recognition was cemented through his technical leadership of top-tier figures across the planet’s most demanding circuits:

 * Paula Ormaechea: Serving as her head coach since 2024, guiding the standout Argentine tennis player—former world number 59, top-ranked national player, and absolute singles win-record holder in the Billie Jean King Cup—at high-stakes events like Roland Garros and the US Open, alongside multiple WTA and ITF tournaments.
* Tomás Martín Etcheverry: Working on the professional circuit with Argentina’s top men’s ATP figure, who reached world number 25, supporting him through rigorous European and South American clay-court tours, in addition to collaborating with elite athletes like Santiago Rodríguez Taverna.

 His official Grand Slam debut as a coach took place at Roland Garros (2024), followed by his consolidation at the US Open (2025). These achievements place him in an elite group of professionals whose direct experience across the world’s four major tournaments guarantees a standard of undeniable distinction.

 Academic excellence and business vision in the United States

 Beyond his successful experience on the planet’s most prestigious courts, Stretti brings a rigorous academic and methodological foundation: he is a certified Physical Education Teacher, holds the coveted Level B certification from the Global Professional Tennis Coach Association (GPTCA), and holds specializations in applied performance. His versatility also includes his prominent role as physical trainer for Argentina’s national lacrosse team at the 2018 World Championship in Israel.

 His arrival at the Smatt Tennis Academy—a premier facility featuring four clay courts, six hard courts, and recreational areas—directly drives the sports and economic development of Hallandale Beach. Stretti leads a model that shapes athletes capable of competing at the highest level, creating jobs and energizing Florida’s sports market.

 A legacy that inspires the Hispanic community

 “Being a coach is not just about teaching tennis,” Stretti maintains. “It is about helping shape individuals, building confidence, and supporting processes that often go far beyond athletic results.”

 From the Patagonian courts to the Grand Slam stadiums of Paris and New York, and now consolidated in the vibrant market of Hallandale Beach, Bruno Stretti personifies the leadership, excellence, and exceptional contribution that distinguish top figures of the Hispanic community in the United States.
