# reVolver Podcasts’ Erazno y La Chokolata and Código Vital Win Silver at The 2026 Signal Awards

DALLAS, TX — October 22, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — reVolver Podcasts, a leading multicultural podcast network, today announced that two of its Spanish-language programs, Erazno y La Chokolata and Código Vital, have won Silver honors at the 5th Annual Signal Awards, which recognize excellence, creativity and innovation across the podcast industry.

 Erazno y La Chokolata received the Silver Signal Award in the Genre – Comedy category, while Código Vital earned Silver in the Genre – Health & Wellness category. Both programs were recognized in the Shows division, highlighting the range and quality of Spanish-language programming reaching podcast audiences across the United States.

 “Winning two Silver Signal Awards is an important achievement for reVolver Podcasts and a reflection of the talent and dedication behind these programs,” said Jack Hobbs, president of reVolver Podcasts. “Erazno y La Chokolata has built a remarkable connection with audiences through comedy and culturally relevant entertainment, while Código Vital is helping Spanish-speaking listeners navigate important health topics with credible, accessible information. These awards reinforce our commitment to delivering quality content that informs, entertains and connects with Latino communities.”

 Created and hosted by Oswaldo Díaz, Erazno y La Chokolata has entertained Spanish-speaking audiences for more than two decades through comedy, original characters, celebrity interviews, parodies, sports and pop-culture commentary. Díaz’s vocal versatility and ability to transform everyday experiences into comedy have made the program one of the most recognizable Spanish-language entertainment shows in the United States.

 Hosted by veteran journalist and communicator Daniela Ganoza, Código Vital makes complex health information easier for Spanish-speaking audiences to understand. Through conversations with physicians, researchers and specialists, the program explores functional medicine, prevention, longevity, metabolism, inflammation, hormones, cardiovascular health, biomarkers and responsible biohacking through a science-based and accessible approach.

 The two Silver awards underscore the breadth of reVolver Podcasts’ programming and its ability to connect with Latino audiences across different interests and generations. From comedy rooted in shared cultural experiences to health and wellness content that encourages informed decisions, both programs demonstrate the relevance of Spanish-language podcasting in today’s evolving audio landscape.

 The Signal Awards recognize podcasts that help shape the future of audio storytelling through creativity, innovation and meaningful audience connections. Winners are selected by the Signal Awards Judging Academy, a collective of leaders and professionals from across the podcasting, media and entertainment industries. The 2026 competition also included the Listener’s Choice Awards, which attracted more than 260,000 votes from 94,000 listeners.

 “Podcasting is no longer defined by a single format, platform or approach to storytelling, and this year’s submissions make that evolution impossible to ignore,” said Jemma Brown, GM of The Signal Awards. “Charts and download counts can only tell us what’s popular. At Signal, our role is to notice the creative risks that are quietly changing what everyone else makes next. This year’s Winners offer a first look at podcasting’s next chapter.”

 As audiences continue to embrace on-demand audio, reVolver Podcasts has established itself as one of the leading destinations for multicultural podcast content, offering a broad mix of entertainment, news, sports, business, lifestyle, health, and culture programming. The company recently expanded its distribution through a partnership with Claro USA, bringing its podcast catalog to Claro Música Pulso, the newly enhanced entertainment platform that serves more than 500,000 subscribers with music, podcasts, radio, video, and other multimedia experiences. reVolver content is available free to listeners across major platforms, including Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Pandora, Deezer, the iHeartRadio app, Amazon Music, the reVolver Podcasts App via the Samsung Galaxy Store, Roku streaming devices, and at www.revolverpodcasts.com.
