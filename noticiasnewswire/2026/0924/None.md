# HPRA Announces Finalists for 2026 ¡BRAVO! Awards

MIAMI, FL — September 24, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — The Hispanic Public Relations Association (HPRA) today announced the finalists for the 2026 HPRA ¡BRAVO! Awards, recognizing outstanding campaigns and agencies advancing excellence in Hispanic multicultural communications across the country.

 Winners will be announced on Nov. 19, 2026, at the LA River Center and Gardens in Los Angeles, marking HPRA’s return to the city where the organization was founded more than four decades ago.

 This year’s theme, “Legacy in Motion,” reflects both that history and the ways it continues to evolve through each generation of communicators. The 2026 program will also honor the people and ideas that helped create greater opportunity and representation in the industry while celebrating the campaigns, agencies, brands, and emerging talent carrying that work forward in new ways.

 “Legacy is not something we simply inherit or leave behind. It is something we put into motion through the choices we make, the stories we tell and the opportunities we create for others,” said HPRA National President Sonia V. Diaz. “Forty-two years ago, a group of Latino communications professionals came together in Los Angeles because they understood that representation would not happen on its own. As we return to Los Angeles this year, we are honoring what they started while celebrating a new generation that continues to challenge our industry, expand our stories and move us closer to the representation and parity our community deserves.”

 The finalist campaigns similarly demonstrate how the industry continues to evolve, with work spanning 11 categories and addressing audiences across industries, communities and platforms.

 Competing categories this year include: Digital or Media Platform PR Program, Film, Music & Entertainment, Healthcare & Nutrition, Influencer Engagement, Integrated Marketing Communications Campaigns, Internal Communications Programs, Lifestyle, New Product/Service Launch, Non-Profit Campaigns, Social Equity, and Sports. Campaign of the Year will be awarded to the highest-scoring submission.

 Alongside this year’s campaign finalists, ¡BRAVO! will recognize a group of leaders whose careers reflect that same continuum of progress. Spanning communications, media, entertainment and culture, the 2026 honorees have built careers that expand opportunity, challenge traditional paths and use influence to create space for others. Their stories reflect different expressions of Legacy in Motion: what we carry from where we began, how we transform it through our work, and what we make possible for those who follow. HPRA will announce the 2026 individual honorees separately in the coming weeks.

 Methodology

 Campaigns launched between January 1, 2025, and March 31, 2026, were eligible for consideration.

 Finalists are selected from the highest-scoring submissions in their respective categories following evaluation by an independent panel of senior communications professionals representing agencies, brands, nonprofit organizations, academia and other sectors of the industry. Each submission was scored by three judges on a 1-5 scale across ten evaluation pillars, including goals and objectives, strategy, resources, originality/creativity, and outcomes.

 About the HPRA ¡BRAVO! Awards Since 2014, the HPRA ¡BRAVO! Awards have recognized excellence in multicultural communications. The annual program celebrates campaigns, organizations and leaders demonstrating the creativity, discipline and cultural fluency that move the communications industry forward.

 About HPRA The Hispanic Public Relations Association (HPRA), founded in 1984, is the premier organization for Hispanic public relations professionals in the United States. HPRA advances the profession through professional development, national scholarships, chapter programming and industry recognition while supporting professionals working across culture, storytelling and strategy.
