# Gevorg Shahbazyan, Founder &amp; Ceo of Starlife Group, To Receive The Distinction As One of The ‘2026 Top Entrepreneur of The USA’

MIAMI, FL — July 30, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Negocios y Ejecutiva Magazine, leaders in information and interviews with executives in South Florida, will hold the “Top Entrepreneur of the USA Awards 2026” on October 8, 2026, as part of Hispanic Heritage Month, at the EB Hotel in Miami.

 One of the honorees will be Gevorg Shahbazyan, founder & CEO of Starlife Group, who will receive the distinction as one of the “Top Entrepreneurs of the USA”, within the framework of the Hispanic Heritage Month celebration.

 “Behind every great company is a leader who never stopped believing in their vision. Our founder and CEO, Gevorg, will be recognized as one of the “2026 Top Entrepreneur of the USA” for his impact as an entrepreneur, his contribution to business growth, and the value he brings to every project he develops,” the company highlighted in a social media post.

 “My vision was simple yet ambitious: to create a single company that controls all stages of the real estate lifecycle: development, construction, financing, mortgages, brokerage, insurance, property management, and asset protection, all under one roof. From an investor’s perspective, this means working with a single responsible operator who controls the entire value chain. There are no mutual accusations between suppliers. There are no information gaps between departments. There is one team, one standard, one outcome,” Gevorg Shahbazyan, CEO of Starlife Group, told Miami Negocios magazine.

 Top Entrepreneur of the USA

 The “Top Entrepreneur of the USA” awards are a prestigious recognition dedicated to honoring entrepreneurs and business leaders who have demonstrated exceptional performance and leadership in their respective fields. These awards highlight the dedication, innovation, and positive impact these entrepreneurs have had in their communities and beyond. Being recognized as one of the top entrepreneurs not only celebrates professional success but also inspires others who aspire to excel in their own paths.

 The cocktail reception and dinner will be held at the EB Hotel in Miami on October 8, 2026. The evening will feature a cocktail reception followed by a formal dinner, creating an ideal environment for networking and celebrating achievements. During the event, the entrepreneurs will officially receive their awards, recognizing their contributions and commitment to excellence in their industries.

 Attendees will have the opportunity to connect with other entrepreneurs and community leaders in a welcoming atmosphere. The “Top Entrepreneur of USA” ceremony is not only a platform to honor success, but also an opportunity to foster new alliances and collaborations. This event promises to be a memorable celebration of achievement, innovation, and leadership in the United States’ entrepreneurial community.

 The “Top Entrepreneur of USA Awards” are held annually, recognizing businesswomen and men who excel in their businesses or activities. The event is organized by Negocios and Ejecutiva magazines.

 Negocios and Ejecutiva, business leaders in South Florida

 Journalist Eduardo Hapke joined forces with attorney Karen Blanco, and in 2013 they created Negocios Magazine and Ejecutiva Magazine, the only Spanish-language business magazines in South Florida focused on entrepreneurs, published in both print and digital formats. The magazines are published monthly in South Florida.

 The magazines also have their websites, www.negociosmagazine.com and www.ejecutivamagazine.com, which features daily news updates and extensive content distribution on social media.

 Negocios Magazine is also distributed through PressReader, a digital platform with over 7,000 publications worldwide. Hotels, businesses, airlines, and cruise lines have access to this news platform.

 Both Negocios and Ejecutiva magazines are also published on the issuu.com platform, where the complete publication can be viewed online.

 Negocios Magazine is part of the Acta Diurna Urbis Group Corp. media group, headquartered in Miami, Florida, which also publishes Ejecutiva magazine, Welcome a América, Salud & Buen Vivir Florida, Hispanic Time, and Hispanic Time: Electric Cars Plus.
