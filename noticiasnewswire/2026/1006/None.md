# Dr. Elena Ortega-Tauler Officially Takes Office on The City of Doral Special Needs Advisory Board

DORAL, FL — October 6, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Renowned attorney and South Florida legal community leader, Dr. Elena Ortega-Tauler, Esq., has officially assumed her duties as a board member of the City of Doral Special Needs Advisory Board.

 The appointment was formally sealed following the official swearing-in ceremony conducted before City Clerk Connie Diaz, where the official Oath of Office certificate was awarded. During the ceremony, Councilwoman Maureen Porras formally presented the certification accrediting her as a board member. Through her signature and oath, the attorney committed to support, protect, and defend the Constitution and Government of the United States, the State of Florida, and the Charter along with the Ordinances of the City of Doral.

 For Dr. Ortega-Tauler, a graduate of the University of Miami and founder of Legacy Trust Counsel, P.A., stepping into this role represents immense pride and a deep dedication to public service. With over 35 years of experience, she will bring her expertise in estate planning, Special Needs Trusts, and Elder Law. Her mission on the board will be to empower and equip families with essential legal tools to ensure the guardianship and financial protection of the city’s most vulnerable residents against any eventuality.

 “I take immense pride in joining this advisory board. This oath is a promise to every parent and caregiver in Doral. I am placing all my experience at the service of our families to give them the peace of mind of knowing that their loved ones are protected,” stated Dr. Elena Ortega-Tauler.
