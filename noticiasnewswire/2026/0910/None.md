# From Cine de Oro to Secretos de Villanas: Canela.TV’s ‘Latino Proud’ Celebrates Every Era of Latino Entertainment, Free

NEW YORK, NY — September 10, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — From the icons who made history in Mexican cinema to the artists redefining Latino entertainment today, Canela.TV, the free streaming platform with its fully integrated rewards program, Club Canela, celebrates Hispanic Heritage Month with “Latino Proud,” a special selection of movies, series, reality shows and original content honoring the talent, stories and voices that have inspired generations and continue to make the community proud.

 To start your mornings celebrating the pride and strength of the Latino community, Café con Canela, Canela.TV’s exclusive daily show, brings together Natalia Valenzuela, Suly Castillo and Héctor Navarro to amplify the voices, stories and perspectives that reflect the richness of Latino identity. Ídolos takes center stage on Canela Music, spotlighting the extraordinary journey of Nicaraguan-born singer, songwriter and musician Luis Enrique, whose pioneering contributions to salsa romántica, enduring catalog of hits such as “Yo No Sé Mañana,” and acclaimed career have helped expand the sound and reach of Latin music across generations.

 Home to one of the most extensive collections from the Golden Age of Mexican Cinema, Canela.TV’s Cine de Oro collection celebrates the legends who defined generations with classics such as La Presidenta Municipal, starring La India María; La Selva de Fuego, featuring Dolores del Río; and Si Me Han de Matar Mañana, with Pedro Infante.

 This Hispanic Heritage Month, Latino Proud expands that celebration with iconic films, including ¡Viva Quien Sabe Querer!, featuring Miguel Aceves Mejía, and ¡Tacos, Joven!, featuring Bárbara Gil, among many other classics. With more than 100 titles, the collection brings the timeless legacy of Mexican cinema to new generations while keeping alive the stories and stars that left an unforgettable mark on Latino culture.

 The hit Secretos franchise continues to captivate audiences season after season, and Canela.TV offers every installment so fans can relive its most memorable moments, unexpected confessions and unforgettable drama. Secretos de Villanas, Secretos de Las Indomables and Secretos de Parejas feature some of the biggest names in Latino entertainment, including Gaby Spanic, Aylín Mujica, Alicia Machado, Amara La Negra, Bárbara de Regil and Julián Gil, celebrating the diversity, personality and talent of Latinos both in front of and behind the camera.

 Canela Originals also shine with powerful stories and stellar casts. Reinas de la Noche, starring Catherine Siachoque, delivers a gripping story of ambition, rivalry and the high stakes behind the spotlight, while Doble Traición, starring Litzy, raises the tension with a story driven by deception, divided loyalties and unexpected turns. Together, these international co-productions showcase the range of Latino talent through stories that blend drama, passion and emotion for audiences around the world.

 For music lovers, Canela Music sets the rhythm with Lo Que Suena, a video podcast that celebrates the sounds, artists and roots that unite Hispanic audiences. On Canela Kids, the whole family can enjoy SuperEllas, the award-winning animated series that inspires through the stories of iconic women like Shakira and Frida Kahlo. And Canela Deportes celebrates the passion for fútbol with Galaxia Mundialista, spotlighting a special episode featuring Mexican soccer legend and former national team player Ricardo Peláez, who shares insights from his celebrated career and his perspective on the sport’s evolution.

 Club Canela, Canela.TV’s free loyalty program where the more you watch, the more you earn, is launching its official merch collection featuring iconic trending phrases that reflect how Latino audiences express themselves and connect with stories they love, like “No es Drama, Es Character Development” and many more. Registered users can take their passion for entertainment beyond the screen and get these exclusive items at no cost by redeeming the points they earn while enjoying their favorite content on Canela.TV.

 “Latino Proud isn’t a shelf we build in September and take down in October,” said Isabel Rafferty Zavala, Co-Founder & CEO of Canela Media. “Pedro Infante and Dolores del Río live on the same platform as Gabriela Spanic and Amara La Negra, because that’s how our audience actually watches. One culture, every generation, all year long. Hispanic Heritage Month is simply when we turn up the volume.”

 Latino Proud streams free on Canela.TV from September 15 through October 15, available across all major connected TV, web and mobile devices. No subscription is required. Viewers can start watching at www.Canela.TV.
