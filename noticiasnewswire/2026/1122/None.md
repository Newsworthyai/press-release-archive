# HPRA Announces Winners of the 2026 ¡BRAVO! Awards

LOS ANGELES, CA — November 22, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — The Hispanic Public Relations Association (HPRA) announced the winners of its 2026 ¡BRAVO! Awards Thursday evening, with the Smithsonian’s National Museum of the American Latino emerging as the night’s biggest campaign winner. Its Make History Together campaign earned three awards, including the coveted Campaign of the Year honor, along with wins in Digital PR and Nonprofit.

 H+M Communications also had a landmark evening, taking home three awards, including Agency of the Year for the fifth time—more than any other agency in the category’s history. The agency earned additional honors in Lifestyle and Influencer Engagement for its GRAN MALO campaigns.

 Presented at the Los Angeles River Center & Gardens, the 12th annual HPRA ¡BRAVO! Awards recognized excellence across 13 categories, celebrating campaigns that demonstrate the power of cultural understanding, creativity and strategic communications to connect with audiences, elevate important stories and drive meaningful impact.

 Themed Legacy in Motion, this year’s celebration also marked a homecoming for HPRA, returning to Los Angeles, where the organization was founded in 1984.

 “The work we recognized this year is a powerful reminder that cultural competency can’t be just a consideration in effective communications – it is what makes the work resonate,” said HPRA National President Sonia V. Diaz. “The Smithsonian’s Make History Together campaign is especially meaningful as the National Museum of the American Latino celebrates securing its permanent home after decades of advocacy. It reminds us that the stories we tell, and the ones we fight to preserve, shape how our communities are seen and understood.”

 Smithsonian and H+M Communications lead the night The Smithsonian’s National Museum of the American Latino earned three awards for Make History Together, a campaign recognized for its work in elevating Latino history and representation. Its wins in Campaign of the Year, Digital PR and Nonprofit communications highlighted the role of strategic storytelling in engaging audiences and strengthening awareness of the contributions Latino communities have made to the nation’s history.

 The recognition comes at a particularly meaningful moment for the museum. After more than two decades of advocacy and congressional authorization in 2020, the Smithsonian designated the historic Arts and Industries Building on the National Mall as the museum’s permanent home in August 2026. The milestone represents a major step toward establishing a national institution dedicated to preserving and sharing Latino history, culture and contributions.

 H+M Communications was another standout winner, earning its fifth Agency of the Year title for The Business Case for Culture: How H+M Went from Downturn to Dominance. The achievement makes H+M the most decorated agency in the history of the category.

 The agency also received two awards for its GRAN MALO work: Lifestyle for GRAN MALO: The Spark That Ignites the Party and Influencer Engagement for GRAN MALO: How Creators Made It the Spark That Ignites the Party. The wins demonstrated the value of culturally relevant creative strategies and authentic creator partnerships in building connections between brands and consumers.

 Honoring leaders who use their platforms to advocate for and empower communities Beyond the campaign awards, HPRA recognized four individuals whose work demonstrates how influence can be used to challenge inequities, advocate for others, protect communities and expand opportunities.

 Snow Tha Product, President’s Award: The Mexican American rapper, songwriter and entrepreneur was recognized for using her music and platform to defend immigrant communities, challenge discrimination and confront uncomfortable truths, including divisions within the Latino community. Through her independent career and outspoken advocacy, she has demonstrated the courage required to speak up for others, even when doing so carries personal or professional risks.

 Jesus “Malverde” Gonzalez, Pioneer of the Year: The Vice President, Creative, Latin at BMI was honored for his contributions to Latin music and his commitment to supporting songwriters, composers, and creators. A son of braceros from Coachella, he has dedicated his career to paying it forward, expanding opportunities for Latino artists, and strengthening the creative community whose work shapes culture.

 Etienne Hernandez-Medina, DEI Champion of the Year: The founder and CEO of H+M Communications was recognized for his leadership in advancing diversity, equity and inclusion and helping create opportunities for underrepresented professionals. His work reflects a commitment to making the communications industry more representative and accessible.

 Gustavo Arellano, Journalist of the Year: The Los Angeles Times columnist was honored for journalism that examines immigration, identity, politics and the experiences of Latino communities. Through his reporting and commentary, Arellano has challenged stereotypes, elevated overlooked stories and held institutions accountable.

 Together, the honorees reflected the evening’s central message: that influence carries responsibility, and that lasting impact comes from using one’s voice and platform to advocate for others.

 A homecoming celebrating 42 years of latino leadership The 2026 ¡BRAVO! Awards returned to Los Angeles, the city where HPRA was founded in 1984, bringing together communications leaders, journalists, creators and emerging professionals to celebrate the organization’s legacy and the industry’s future.

 The evening opened with a performance by BMI Latin artist and Grammy Award-winning Mariachi Divas de Cindy Shea. Guests also remembered civil rights leader Dolores Huerta (1930–2026), recipient of HPRA’s 2025 President’s Award.

 HPRA recognized its founders—John Echeveste, Myrna Gutierrez and David Garcia—whose vision established a professional home for Latino communicators more than four decades ago.

 “Bringing ¡BRAVO! back to Los Angeles, our founding city, meant grounding every detail of the night in this community,” said Jennyfer Grundler, 2026 ¡BRAVO! Awards Chair. “Every one of our vendors was a Latino-owned, LA-based business, our honorees have deep roots here and our volunteers came from the local community. Uplifting our own means creating opportunities and making sure our community has a seat in the room.”

 Investing in the next generation The ¡BRAVO! Awards serve as HPRA’s primary fundraiser for its National Scholarship Program, which provides financial assistance, mentorship and professional development opportunities to aspiring communications professionals.

 During a scholarship presentation sponsored by Amazon, HPRA recognized six 2026 scholarship recipients:

 – Camila Garcia Chirino, University of Florida

 – Isabelle Flores, The University of Texas at Austin

 – Luis David Latozefsky, Stetson University

 – Melanie Herrera, Florida International University, recipient of the Roshana Gossoff Memorial Scholarship

 – Nasaiah Algarin, American University

 – Xavier Ramirez, University of Southern California

 Aflac and H+M Communications served as presenting sponsors of the 2026 scholarships, helping fund opportunities for the next generation of communications professionals.

 HPRA extends its gratitude to the partners and sponsors who made the celebration possible, including presenting sponsors Aflac and H+M Communications, as well as BMI, Rojas Public Affairs, Amazon, Zaid Communications, the University of Florida, Society 18, RVMP Studio, Plus3, Diversity Action Alliance and Frogtown Brewery.

 For more information, visit https://bravo.hprausa.org/.

 About the HPRA ¡BRAVO! Awards Since 2014, the HPRA ¡BRAVO! Awards have recognized excellence in multicultural communications, honoring professionals, organizations and campaigns that demonstrate creativity, leadership and cultural understanding. The annual celebration also supports HPRA’s commitment to expanding access and opportunity for emerging communications professionals through its National Scholarship Program.

 About HPRA The Hispanic Public Relations Association (HPRA), founded in 1984, is a professional organization dedicated to advancing Hispanic and multicultural communications professionals. Through professional development, networking, mentorship, scholarships and industry events such as the ¡BRAVO! Awards, HPRA supports and celebrates the diverse voices building the future of communications.
