# Medicare Open Enrollment Ends Dec. 7: Give Your Plan a Check-up

NEW YORK, NY — November 5, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Medicare Open Enrollment runs now through Dec. 7. Use this time to make sure your plan still fits your needs. Medicare.gov is your official source for Open Enrollment. Compare every Medicare health and drug plan available in your area. You can easily compare Medicare plan costs, side-by-side, by simply entering the prescription drugs you take. If you prefer to talk with someone, call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227), available 24/7, to review your options and get your questions answered.
