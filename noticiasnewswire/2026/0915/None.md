# Erazno y La Chokolata Brings Its Signature Comedy to The Screen with ‘Erazno y La Chokolata En Video’

DALLAS, TX — September 14, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Erazno y La Chokolata, one of the most popular and enduring comedy programs serving U.S. Latino audiences, is bringing its unmistakable humor to a new format with the launch of “Erazno y La Chokolata En Video.”

 The new video podcast, distributed by reVolver Podcasts, will feature original content three times a week, giving fans a new way to experience the comedy, characters and cultural commentary that have made Erazno y La Chokolata a favorite among Spanish-speaking audiences.

 “Erazno y La Chokolata En Video” is an expansion of the Erazno y La Chokolata universe and will complement—not replace—the program’s daily radio show and audio podcast. The daily program will continue its regular schedule, delivering its familiar combination of comedy, celebrity interviews, parodies, sports, pop culture and popular segments such as El Chokolatazo.

 “Oswaldo has spent more than two decades creating characters and moments that feel like part of the family for millions of Latinos,” said Jack Hobbs, president of reVolver Podcasts. “The video gives audiences an entirely new way to experience his extraordinary talent. ‘Erazno y Chokolata En Video’ preserves everything fans love about the daily show while allowing Oswaldo and his team to be even more creative, visual and spontaneous. We are excited to help bring this next chapter to audiences everywhere.”

 Created and hosted by Oswaldo Díaz, Erazno y La Chokolata is known for its varied cast of original characters—all brought to life through Díaz’s imagination, vocal versatility and talent for impersonation. Among them is Erazno, the show’s masked lucha libre champion and central character. Díaz has also become known for his ability to create spontaneous comedic moments, connect with celebrities and artists, and turn the experiences of everyday Latino life into memorable entertainment.

 Humor has always played an important role in Hispanic culture. It brings families and friends together, helps communities navigate shared challenges and allows people to recognize themselves in familiar stories and experiences. Through characters, satire and candid conversations, Erazno y La Chokolata has built that kind of connection with its audience—using laughter not only to entertain, but also to create a lasting sense of familiarity and community.

 With “Erazno y Chokolata En Video,” audiences will now be able to see the personalities, expressions and behind-the-scenes energy that previously lived primarily through audio. The new format will also give the creative team greater freedom to develop visual sketches, original features, interviews and other video-first content designed specifically for digital audiences.

 New episodes of “Erazno y Chokolata En Video” will be released three times each week. Fans can continue listening to the daily Erazno y La Chokolata radio program on participating stations and access the audio podcast through reVolver Podcasts and major podcast platforms.

 Created and hosted by Oswaldo Díaz, Erazno y La Chokolata is a nationally recognized Spanish-language comedy and entertainment program featuring an original cast of characters voiced by Díaz. For more than two decades, the show has entertained Latino audiences through comedy, celebrity interviews, parodies, sports, pop culture and audience-favorite segments. Its daily radio program and podcast continue to connect with listeners across the United States.

 As audiences continue to embrace on-demand audio, reVolver Podcasts has established itself as one of the leading destinations for multicultural podcast content, offering a broad mix of entertainment, news, sports, business, lifestyle, health, and culture programming.
