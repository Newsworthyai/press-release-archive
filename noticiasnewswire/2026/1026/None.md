# Natalia Montaño, Founder of Aguamarina Schools, Stars on the Cover of Ejecutiva Magazine

MIAMI, FL — October 26, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Ejecutiva Magazine features Natalia Montaño, Colombian businesswoman and founder of Aguamarina Schools, on its cover. Her innovative vision has made Miami the starting point of an educational project with a presence in the United States, Mexico, and Colombia.

 The publication highlights her business leadership, her commitment to transforming education, and her ability to develop an academic model that combines excellence, emotional well-being, critical thinking, and the formation of future leaders.

 The choice of Montaño as the protagonist of this edition coincides with another important recognition: on October 8, she was honored during the Top Entrepreneurs of USA Awards 2026, a ceremony organized by Negocios Magazine and Ejecutiva Magazine at the EB Hotel Miami.

 From Miami to the world: a new vision of education

 Under Natalia Montaño's leadership, Aguamarina Schools has developed the educational philosophy “Kind & Cool”, which integrates academic learning with values, creativity, autonomy, and social responsibility.

 Currently, Aguamarina has three campuses in Miami, located in Brickell, South Miami, and Downtown Miami, as well as a presence in Mexico City and Bogotá.

 “At Aguamarina, we believe that children should learn in environments where they feel safe, heard, and capable of leading with purpose. Education is not just content: it is experience, community, and vision,” says Natalia Montaño.

 Aguamarina's growth also represents a family legacy. Her husband, architect Roberto Salazar, co-founder of Aguamarina Elementary, and her daughter Luisa Salazar, co-founder and director of the school, actively participate in the development of the project.

 Luisa is also the founder of Planet Patrol Foundation, an initiative that promotes environmental education and children's leadership through conservation activities and community participation.

 A cover that celebrates female leadership

 Natalia Montaño's story reflects the spirit of Ejecutiva Magazine: highlighting women who transform industries, create opportunities, and generate a positive impact on society.

 Her career demonstrates how an educational vision can become a business initiative with international reach, driven from South Florida.

 With this cover, Ejecutiva Magazine recognizes not only an outstanding businesswoman but also a woman who is helping to redefine education for new generations.
