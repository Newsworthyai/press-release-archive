# reVolver Podcasts Expands Spanish-Language News Programming with Politics en Español US

DALLAS, TX — November 12, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — reVolver Podcasts, a leading multicultural podcast network, today announced the addition of Politics en Español US, a Spanish-language news and political commentary program hosted by award-winning journalist and broadcaster Victor Martinez. The program expands reVolver’s news and current affairs lineup, offering audiences an independent perspective on the political, economic and social developments shaping the United States and Latino communities.

 Politics en Español US examines U.S. politics, public policy, elections, the economy and major national and international developments, with particular attention to how decisions made in Washington and across the country affect Hispanic communities. Through interviews, informed commentary and straightforward explanations, Martinez helps listeners understand complex issues, challenges public officials and encourages greater civic awareness.

 “Latino audiences are not a monolithic group, and they deserve access to a broad range of perspectives on the issues affecting their lives,” said Jack Hobbs, president of reVolver Podcasts. “Victor Martinez brings years of journalism experience, a strong understanding of the political landscape and a commitment to informed discussion. Adding Politics en Español US strengthens our news programming and gives audiences another voice to help them navigate an increasingly complex political environment.”

 As Hispanic voters and communities continue to play an important role in American political and economic life, demand for accessible, Spanish-language news and analysis remains significant. Political decisions involving immigration, employment, education, healthcare and the economy have direct implications for millions of Latino families, making informed discussion and diverse viewpoints an important part of the media landscape.

 Politics en Español US approaches these conversations from an independent, left-leaning perspective, combining analysis and commentary with interviews and discussions about the issues dominating the news cycle. Rather than simply repeating headlines, Martinez seeks to provide context, examine competing arguments and encourage listeners to become more engaged in civic life.

 The program also maintains a presence across traditional media, airing on three Spanish-language radio stations in Pennsylvania and on television in Puerto Rico. Its addition to reVolver Podcasts extends the show’s availability through digital audio, complementing its existing broadcast distribution and giving audiences greater flexibility in how they access its content.

 “Podcasting has created new opportunities for Latino journalists and commentators to connect directly with audiences and explore important issues beyond the limitations of traditional broadcast formats,” Hobbs added. “Our goal is to provide a platform for compelling voices and programming that reflects the diversity of experiences, interests and opinions within the Hispanic community.”

 The addition of Politics en Español US reflects reVolver Podcasts’ continued investment in Spanish-language programming across news, entertainment, sports, business, lifestyle, health and culture. Through its expanding network, reVolver connects established personalities and emerging voices with audiences seeking relevant, engaging and accessible content.

 reVolver Podcasts recently expanded its distribution through a strategic partnership with Claro USA, bringing its podcast catalog to Claro Música Pulso, an entertainment platform serving more than 500,000 subscribers with music, podcasts, radio, video and other multimedia experiences.

 reVolver content is available free to listeners across major platforms, including Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Pandora, Deezer, the iHeartRadio app, Amazon Music, the reVolver Podcasts App via the Samsung Galaxy Store, Roku streaming devices and at www.revolverpodcasts.com.

 Politics en Español US is a Spanish-language news, analysis and political commentary program hosted by award-winning journalist and broadcaster Victor Martinez. Covering U.S. politics, public policy, elections, the economy and national and international affairs, the program provides an independent, left-leaning perspective with a focus on issues affecting Latino communities. Through interviews and informed commentary, Martinez encourages audiences to stay informed, question political decisions and participate in civic life.
