# Pure Flix Familia Brings Christmas Joy to Miami with Festive Holiday Lodge for Families at Christmas Wonderland Theme Park, Nov. 12 – Jan. 3

MIAMI, FL — November 13, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Pure Flix Familia, Great American Media’s streaming video service for Latino families, is bringing holiday fun, photos, and activities to Miami’s Christmas Wonderland at Tropical Park, November 12, 2026, through January 3, 2027.

 Just steps from the main entrance in Town Square, the Pure Flix Familia Lodge invites guests to trade the dazzling lights outside for a cozy holiday cabin, complete with festive décor, comfortable seating and a perfect fireside photo spot made for this year’s family Christmas card. On screen, families can preview holiday favorites including Alquilé un Novio para Navidad, Un Regalo… Navideño and Three Generations, One Christmas, heartwarming stories about love, family, and the moments that bring everyone home for the holidays.

 All attendees will not only get a first-look at Pure Flix Familia’s wholesome offering but will be able to enjoy a 50% discount by using the code WONDERLAND50 thru December 31, 2026. The service is normally $4.99 per month, or $50/year.

 To join, visit pureflixfamilia.com or scan the QR codes at the Lodge. Founding members who sign up at the Lodge can take home Spanish-language holiday merch, including T-shirts, sweatshirts, caps and beanies.

 Launched with Spanish-language programming and expanding into English-language and bilingual storytelling, Pure Flix Familia is made for every generation at the table, from abuelos to the grandkids. The Lodge brings that mission to life, inviting families to discover entertainment that reflects their lives, values and traditions.

 Event Details

 What: Pure Flix Familia at Christmas Wonderland When: November 12, 2026 – January 3, 2027 Where: Tropical Park, 7900 SW 40th St., Miami, FL 33155 Pure Flix Familia Lodge: Town Square, near the main entrance Tickets and Information: miamiwonderland.com

 About Pure Flix Familia Pure Flix Familia is Great American Media’s Latino-focused streaming destination for culturally relevant entertainment centered on family and faith. Created around a Total Latino Market strategy, the platform is designed to serve Spanish-dominant, bilingual and English-dominant audiences through movies, series, original microdramas and curated programming that families can confidently enjoy together.

 About Great American Media Great American Media is the leader in faith and family entertainment, and one of the fastest-growing entertainment networks on television. Great American Media is home to a portfolio of entertainment brands that celebrate faith, family, and country, including Great American Family, the flagship linear network featuring original Christmas movies, rom-coms, and beloved series; Great American Pure Flix, the leading faith and family streaming service; Great American Faith & Living, the unscripted companion network; and GFam+, an app that lets viewers watch anytime, anywhere. Great American Media, established in June 2021 by Bill Abbott and a group of US-based family offices, is available via cable, streaming on Pure Flix and YouTube TV, and on the GFam+ app.
