# ‘El Show de Piolín En Video’ Brings More Interviews, Comedy and Family Entertainment to Viewers Each Week

DALLAS, TX — November 10, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — reVolver Podcasts today announced a renewed weekly schedule for “El Show de Piolín En Video,” giving audiences more opportunities to watch Eduardo “Piolín” Sotelo’s distinctive combination of celebrity interviews, comedy, current events and useful information for the Latino community.

 “El Show de Piolín En Video” takes viewers inside the conversations and spontaneous moments that make the nationally syndicated radio program unique. Its video content includes interviews with recording artists and entertainment personalities, along with conversations featuring immigration specialists, health experts and community voices. Recent content featuring international music star Chayanne reflects Piolín’s ability to move beyond a traditional celebrity interview and create warm, personal and inspiring conversations.

 The video podcast also brings the show’s jokes, pranks, playful debates and interactions with the entire team to the screen. Viewers can see the reactions, expressions and chemistry that cannot always be captured through audio alone, creating a more personal connection between Piolín and the audience that has followed him throughout his career.

 “Piolín has an extraordinary ability to make every guest feel comfortable and every audience member feel included in the conversation,” said Jack Hobbs, president of reVolver Podcasts. “Whether he is interviewing a major celebrity, discussing an issue affecting Hispanic families or simply making people laugh, video gives audiences another way to experience his authenticity, energy and connection with the community.”

 Hosted by Radio Hall of Fame inductee Eduardo “Piolín” Sotelo, El Show de Piolín has built its reputation by combining entertainment with information audiences can use in their everyday lives. The program covers current events and issues affecting Hispanic families while maintaining the humor, optimism and family-friendly spirit that have become central to the show.

 New episodes of “El Show de Piolín En Video” will be published three times a week, complementing the daily El Show de Piolín radio program and audio podcast. The weekly schedule will allow fans to enjoy original video interviews, memorable conversations and some of the show’s most entertaining moments whenever and wherever they choose.

 Watch and subscribe to “El Show de Piolín En Video” at: https://revolverpodcasts.com/shows/el-show-de-piolin-video/

 About El Show de Piolín El Show de Piolín is a nationally syndicated Spanish-language radio program hosted by Eduardo “Piolín” Sotelo. The program delivers daily current events, celebrity interviews, immigration information, conversations with health experts, comedy, advice and wholesome entertainment that families can enjoy together. Piolín was inducted into the Radio Hall of Fame in 2013 and is recognized as one of the most influential personalities in Spanish-language radio in the United States.
