# Miami Celebrated The Top entrepreneurs of USA Awards 2026 and Recognized Business Leaders for Their Careers, Innovation, and Leadership

MIAMI, FL — October 12, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Miami was the setting for a celebration of business talent with the presentation of the Top Entrepreneurs of USA Awards 2026, a ceremony that recognized 28 entrepreneurs, executives, and professionals for their contributions to economic development, innovation, leadership, and the growth of their respective industries.

 The event was held on Thursday, October 8, at the EB Hotel Miami, with the participation of representatives from companies and institutions associated with various sectors of the economy of South Florida, the United States, and international markets.

 Organized by Negocios & Ejecutiva Magazine, the Top Entrepreneurs of USA Awards aim to highlight the careers of leaders who, through their business vision, capacity for innovation, and professional commitment, contribute to the creation of opportunities, the strengthening of businesses, and the development of their communities.

 The ceremony was hosted by Sabina Covo, who was responsible for introducing the award recipients during an evening that combined business recognitions and musical performances by singer David Gómez.

 The presentation of the 28 awards took place in the hotel’s ballroom, where the 120 invited businesspeople enjoyed dinner and the awards ceremony.

 The honorees of the Top entrepreneurs of USA Awards 2026 were the following:

 Rafael Olloqui, Founder and CEO of BTS, received the “International Business Expansion Award” for his leadership in international business expansion.

 Erwin Mosqueda, Founder and CEO of Top Gun M.O.T. Design Services, was honored with the “Excellence in Traffic Engineering, Roadway Safety & Entrepreneurial Leadership Award” for his career in traffic engineering, roadway safety, and entrepreneurial leadership.

 Manuel Vidal, Founder and CEO of Premium Finance Group, received the “Distinguished Career in Premium Finance & Wealth Preservation Award” in recognition of his career in premium financing and wealth preservation.

 Gevorg Shahbazyan, Founder and CEO of STARLIFE GROUP, was recognized with the “Excellence in Real Estate Development & Business Leadership Award” for his leadership in real estate development and business.

 Mario Murgado, Founder and CEO of Murgado Automotive Group, received the “Automotive Entrepreneurship & Business Leadership Award” for his distinguished career in the automotive industry.

 Mónica Vázquez, Executive Vice President and General Manager of ABANCA USA, was honored with the “International Banking & Executive Leadership Award” for her leadership in international banking.

 Andrea Padilla, Founder and CEO of Square Root, received the “Excellence in Marketing, Communications & Business Leadership Award” for her contributions to marketing, communications, and business leadership.

 Ace Cruz, Founder and CEO of Ojalá Wines, was recognized with the “Visionary Entrepreneurship & Business Leadership Award” for his entrepreneurial vision and business leadership.

 Marcello Serrato, Founder and CEO of Prestige Auto, received the “Distinguished Career in Automotive Industry & Business Leadership Award” for his career in the automotive industry.

 Maurici Lladó, Managing Director of Banco Sabadell Miami Branch, was honored with the “Distinguished Career in Banking & International Financial Leadership Award” for his banking career and international financial leadership.

 Susanna Antequera, Founder and General Manager of Antequera de Jáuregui Abogados, received the “Excellence in Legal Leadership & International Family Law Award” for her legal leadership and experience in international family law.

 Fernando Arellano-Geddes, CEO of PowerHouse Automotive Group, was recognized with the “Automotive Leadership & Business Excellence Award” for his leadership and business excellence in the automotive sector.

 James Michael Burkett, President of U.S. Operations at Northbridge University, received the “Excellence in Higher Education Leadership & Community Impact Award” for his contributions to higher education and his community impact.

 José E. Cueto, CEO of Grove Bank & Trust, was honored with the “Distinguished Career in Banking & Financial Leadership Award” in recognition of his career in the banking industry and his financial leadership.

 German Morales, Partner at Shutts & Bowen LLP, received the “Business Law & Legal Leadership Award” for his career in business law and legal leadership.

 Marianna Montanaro, Founder and CEO of Chef to Go, was honored with the “Distinguished Career in Culinary Entrepreneurship & Business Leadership Award” for her career in culinary entrepreneurship.

 Alonso Martinez, Founder and President of Crece Financial Strategies, LLC, received the “Financial Strategy & Wealth Protection Leadership Award” for his leadership in financial strategies and wealth protection.

 María Fernández de Córdoba Martos, CEO of Grupo EULEN USA, was recognized with the “Visionary Executive Leadership & Business Innovation Award” for her executive leadership and innovative vision.

 Natalia Montaño, Founder and CEO of Aguamarina Preschools and Aguamarina Elementary School, received the “Excellence in Education Innovation & Leadership Award” for her contributions to educational innovation.

 A Celebration of Business Leadership in Miami

 The ceremony highlighted the diversity of sectors that contribute to the economic growth of South Florida and its connections with national and international markets.

 As part of the experience, two special vehicles were exhibited: an all-electric 2027 Mercedes-Benz CLA, presented by Prestige Auto, and a classic 1967 Porsche 911, presented by The Barn Miami.

 The Top Entrepreneurs of USA Awards 2026 reaffirmed the importance of recognizing those who drive new economic opportunities and contribute, from their respective industries, to the development of an increasingly diverse and international business community.
