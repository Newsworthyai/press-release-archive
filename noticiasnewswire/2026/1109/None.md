# Negocios Magazine to Recognize Brandon Ramirez of Hyundai Motor North America as One of the ‘Visionaries of 2027’

MIAMI, FL — November 9, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Negocios Magazine announced that Brandon Ramirez, Director of Corporate Social Responsibility at Hyundai Motor North America, will be honored with the “2027 Visionary Leadership & Community Impact Award”, in recognition of his business leadership, social commitment, and contribution to the development of initiatives that generate a positive impact on communities.

 The award will be presented on Thursday, December 10, 2026, during the “Visionaries of 2027 Leadership Luncheon”, an event organized by Negocios Magazine at EB Hotel Miami, which will bring together approximately 40 special guests, including executives from major companies, business owners, media representatives, community leaders, and members of the Southern Automotive Media Association (SAMA).

 Ramirez has carried out outstanding work in the field of corporate social responsibility, promoting initiatives related to children’s health and safety, education, sustainability, STEM programs — science, technology, engineering, and mathematics — and the strengthening of communities.

 His work reflects a vision of business leadership that goes beyond financial results and recognizes the importance of building lasting relationships between companies and the communities they serve.

 “Brandon Ramirez represents a new generation of corporate leaders who understand that true business success is also measured by the positive impact we leave on society. His commitment to education, children’s health, and community development is an example of how major companies can contribute to building a better future. For Negocios Magazine, it is an honor to recognize his professional journey and his vision of leadership,” said Eduardo Hapke, Publisher of Negocios Magazine.

 A holiday initiative with purpose

 As a special part of the celebration, Negocios Magazine will promote a charitable initiative inspired by Ramirez’s commitment to the well-being of children and families.

 “Thank you so much for this special recognition. I also sincerely appreciate your valuable commitment to supporting Nicklaus Children’s Hospital through the collection of new toys for children receiving medical care during the holiday season,” said Brandon Ramirez.

 Attendees will be invited to bring new, unwrapped toys to be donated to Nicklaus Children’s Hospital, with the intention of helping children receiving medical care during the holiday season enjoy a moment of joy.

 The event is part of Negocios Magazine’s initiatives to recognize those who are driving positive transformations in the business world and to strengthen connections among leaders from different industries.

 About Negocios Magazine Negocios Magazine is a publication specializing in business, leadership, economics, and entrepreneurship, with a presence in South Florida and an audience of business owners, executives, and professionals from the business community.

 Through its editorial platforms and special events, it promotes the exchange of ideas, the visibility of business leaders, and the development of new opportunities for connection and growth.
