# Fast Company Names Visory Health to Its 2026 Best Workplaces for Innovators List

ESTERO, FL — September 9, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Visory Health, a top-three prescription discount card platform that helps Veterans, families, caregivers and underserved individuals afford their medications, has been named to Fast Company’s 2026 Best Workplaces for Innovators list. The annual ranking honors companies that demonstrate innovation at every level and create a workplace where all employees feel empowered to invent new ways of doing business.

 At Visory Health, a good idea doesn’t need permission to travel because of its open, supportive and equal workplace structure. The health tech company doesn’t run on a rigid hierarchy of command. Instead, employees have direct access to leadership and to each other. Ideas are encouraged, and quickly judged on merit regardless of where they originated.

 “As soon as someone on our team sees a better way to help a patient save money, we want to hear about it; that’s the kind of company we’ve built. It’s so critical for all Americans to save on their prescriptions,” said Susan Lang, founder and CEO of Visory Health.

 The culture shows up in the results patients can feel. Millions of Americans use Visory Health to find the best price on their medication in seconds, with discounts of up to 80% at more than 37,000 locations nationwide. Team members who talk with patients provide real feedback to the product and leadership teams, so the platform keeps getting easier for patients to use.

 Continuous innovation isn’t only given to Visory’s users. It’s a core pillar for the workplace, too. Company-wide meetings keep team members close to the impact they’re having on patients using the platform, while quarterly workshops cover everything from financial literacy to new tools the team adopts. Visory Health also invests deeply in its people, supporting continued growth in their professional skills through courses and workshops.

 “Prescription affordability isn’t solved, so we can’t afford a culture where good ideas get stuck,” said Alexandra Robertson, Chief Growth Officer at Visory Health. “Being named to Fast Company’s list tells us we’ve built a team that keeps pushing on a problem that affects too many households.”

 The honor marks Visory Health’s second recognition from Fast Company, following its inclusion on the 2024 Brands That Matter list. Earlier this year, the company won bronze at the 2026 Digital Health Awards for its mobile app, and earned a spot on the 2025 Inc. Best in Business list in the “Best Social Good” category. Visory has also been named a Modern Healthcare Best in Business honoree and is a four-time Inc. 5000 company.

 For information about career opportunities at Visory Health, please visit www.visoryhealth.com/about-us.

 About Visory Health Visory Health is a top-three, patient-first health tech platform transforming how everyone, including Veterans, families, caregivers and underserved individuals, has access to healthcare. Visory Health’s model puts customers’ needs at the core of how it operates. It has saved millions of customers’ money on prescription medications, creating healthier families and communities. With a network of over 38,000 pharmacy partners nationwide, including Walgreens, Kroger, Publix, Stop & Shop, and more, Visory Health delivers affordable prescription prices nationwide and is free to use.
