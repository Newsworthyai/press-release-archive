# Puerto Rico Financial Services Forum Will Bring Together Key Figures from Banking, Insurance, and Regulation in San Juan

SAN JUAN, PR — October 8, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — The Puerto Rico Financial Services Forum will return to the Sheraton Puerto Rico Resort & Casino on November 2 and 3, 2026, to bring together executives, regulators, and specialists from Puerto Rico, the United States, Latin America, and the Caribbean around the changes that are redefining the financial industry.

 In its sixth edition, the meeting will put on the table some of the most impactful issues for banking and insurance, including artificial intelligence, compliance, financial crime, fintech, correspondent banking, captive insurance, AML and sanctions, digital assets, and regulation, among others.

 The agenda will feature high-profile figures from the public and private sectors. Among them are Mónica Rodríguez Villa, Esq., commissioner of Financial Institutions of Puerto Rico; Suzette Del Valle Lecároz, Esq., commissioner of Insurance of Puerto Rico; Zachary Kessler, from the U.S. Department of the Treasury; Angelo Colombo, CEO of AIG for Latin America and the Caribbean; and Jeff Radke, CEO and co-founder of Accelerant, among other industry leaders.

 The Forum will also feature the participation of investigative journalist Ignacio Gomez, recognized for his work in investigations on corruption and financial crimes.

 In addition to the content sessions, the event aims to strengthen connections among leaders in banking, insurance, technology, law firms, consulting, compliance, and regulation, creating a space for the exchange of ideas, business development, and new collaboration opportunities.

 “More than a conference, the Puerto Rico Financial Services Forum has established itself as a high-level space where decision-makers and industry leaders connect, exchange ideas, and explore new opportunities for collaboration and growth,” expressed Hugo Cordova, president of the Puerto Rico International Insurers Association (PRIIA).

 “The Puerto Rico Financial Services Forum continues to strengthen Puerto Rico's position as a center for international banks and insurers, while creating a platform to discuss innovation, regulation, and the future of the financial industry,” said Eduardo Colón, president of the Puerto Rico International Banks Association (PRIBA).

 The event will also offer visibility and networking opportunities for sponsors and organizations interested in connecting with a specialized audience in the financial sector.

 For information, agenda, and registration, visit  www.financialservicesforumpr.com.

 About the Puerto Rico Financial Services Forum The Puerto Rico Financial Services Forum is organized by the Puerto Rico International Insurers Association PRIIA, and the Puerto Rico International Banks Association PRIBA. In its sixth edition, it continues to consolidate itself as a meeting point for leaders in international banking, insurance, and financial services in Puerto Rico.
