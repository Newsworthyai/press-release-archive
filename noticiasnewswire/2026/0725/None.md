# Dr. Gina Goldfeder Introduces the Revolutionary LiberaSueña Method in the U.S.: A Clinical and Spiritual Roadmap for Personal Transformation

MIAMI, FL — July 24, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Renowned clinical psychotherapist, author, and international speaker, Dr. Gina Goldfeder, announces the launch of her latest literary and scientific work, En un sueño cabe tu vida: El método LiberaSueña para la transformación personal (Your Life Fits Inside a Dream: The LiberaSueña Method for Personal Transformation), published by Bienestur / Hachette Livre. Backed by more than three decades of clinical practice working with individuals and couples, Dr. Goldfeder offers a profound and innovative blueprint to step off “autopilot” and turn the world of dreams into a compass for inner healing.

 Holding a Bachelor’s degree in Clinical Psychology from Universidad Anáhuac, a Master’s and Doctorate in Individual and Couples Therapy from the Instituto Mexicano de la Pareja (AMETEP), and a Master’s in Creative Writing from the University of Salamanca, Dr. Goldfeder seamlessly combines the analytical rigor of Jungian psychology with modern self-observation tools.

 Dr. Goldfeder’s published works reflect a prolific career in human development. Key titles and literary contributions include:

 * En un sueño cabe tu vida: El método LiberaSueña para la transformación personal (2026, Bienestur)
* Releasing the Emotional Wound (2026, International Edition)
* Diario de los sueños (Dream Journal, 2025)
* Soltar la herida: Una oportunidad de vida (Healing the Wound: An Opportunity for Life; 2023, Ediciones Urano)
* Al unísono (In Unison – Collective Work, 2024)
* Mujer: técnica mixta (Woman: Mixed Media – Literary Anthology)

 Her latest work introduces the LiberaSueña Method, a structured six-step methodology that integrates dream symbol analysis, active imagination, intuition, and therapeutic writing to decode emotional wounds and transform them into scars of wisdom.

 “Your dreams already know the way; this book teaches you how to walk it. In an era dominated by rush and instant answers, stepping into dream territory means unlocking the soul’s most intimate language,” states Dr. Goldfeder, who has collaborated with global leaders in health and consciousness such as Joe Dispenza, Bruce Lipton, Brené Brown, Brian Weiss, and Marco Antonio Regil.

 Ahead of her upcoming participation at the Miami Book Fair (November 15–22), Dr. Goldfeder will launch a U.S. Spanish-language media tour focusing on high-impact public topics: resilience, mental health, stress management, and practical tools for building healthy relationships.

 About Dr. Gina Goldfeder Dr. Gina Goldfeder is a clinical psychotherapist, speaker, author, and human development specialist with over 30 years of experience in emotional well-being. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Clinical Psychology from Universidad Anáhuac, a Master’s and Ph.D. in Individual and Couples Therapy from the Instituto Mexicano de la Pareja (AMETEP), and a Master’s degree in Creative Writing from the University of Salamanca, Spain. Her multidisciplinary therapeutic approach stands out for its Jungian orientation, integrating subconscious exploration, analytical psychology, therapeutic writing, and symbolic dream interpretation as pathways to deep self-reflection and healing.

 She is the creator of the LiberaSueña Method and author of prominent titles on emotional growth, including En un sueño cabe tu vida (Bienestur / Hachette), Soltar la herida: Una oportunidad de vida (Ediciones Urano), Releasing the Emotional Wound (International Edition), Diario de los sueños, as well as contributions to collective works such as Al unísono and the anthology Mujer: técnica mixta. Throughout her career, she has collaborated with key global figures in personal transformation, including Joe Dispenza, Bruce Lipton, Brené Brown, Brian Weiss, and Marco Antonio Regil. Currently, Dr. Goldfeder conducts keynotes, workshops, and specialized consulting on emotional awareness, modern relationships, and personal narrative reconstruction.
