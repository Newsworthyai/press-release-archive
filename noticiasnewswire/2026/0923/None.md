# 5 Easy Sheet Pan Meals with a Grape Upgrade

NEW YORK, NY — September 23, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Sheet pan cooking makes weeknight meals wonderfully easy – and fresh grapes give them an unexpected upgrade. Roasted alongside chicken, vegetables, gnocchi and more, grapes become warm and juicy, adding a pop of sweetness that complements savory flavors and makes simple dishes feel a little more special.

 From satisfying dinners and sides to a crowd-pleasing dessert, these recipes bring something unexpected to the sheet pan without adding extra fuss. Find more family recipe ideas by visiting GrapesFromCalifornia.com.

 – California Grapes and Sage Chicken – This simple sheet pan dinner pairs savory sage-seasoned chicken with sweet California grapes, butternut squash, red onion and garlic. As everything roasts together, the grapes become warm and juicy while apple cider vinegar adds a bright, tangy counterpoint. It’s an easy, colorful meal that delivers plenty of flavor with minimal cleanup.

 – Roasted California Grapes and Sweet Potatoes – Sweet potatoes and California grapes make a delicious pairing in this colorful, easy-to-prepare side dish. Roasting grapes brings out their natural sweetness, while balsamic vinegar, onion and fresh basil add savory, tangy notes for balance. Serve it alongside your favorite protein or add it to a grain bowl for a simple way to bring more fruits and vegetables to the plate.

 – Roasted California Grapes and Gnocchi – Tender gnocchi, juicy roasted California grapes, broccolini and red onion come together in this satisfying one-pan meal. Fresh thyme, feta and lemon zest balance the grapes’ sweetness with savory, tangy and bright flavors. With just 25 minutes of roasting time, it’s an easy way to turn everyday ingredients into a flavorful meatless dinner.

 – Chicken and Roasted California Grapes with Carrots and Parsnips – Warm, spiced cumin and turmeric give this hearty chicken sheet pan dinner plenty of flavor, while carrots, parsnips, onions and California grapes round out the meal. The grapes add juicy bursts of sweetness that complement the earthy root vegetables and savory spices. Everything cooks together on one pan for a colorful, satisfying dinner with easy cleanup.

 – California Grape and Apple Sheet Pan Pie – Tart apples and juicy black California grapes offer a delicious twist on classic apple pie in this crowd-friendly sheet pan dessert. The fruits bake beneath a golden lattice crust with the grapes adding extra color, sweetness and juicy texture to every slice. Designed to serve 16, it’s an inviting dessert for holidays, parties and other gatherings.
