# reVolver Podcasts Introduces Dinero Consciente, a New Spanish-Language Podcast Exploring The Psychology of Money

DALLAS, TX — September 3, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — reVolver Podcasts, a leading multicultural audio-on-demand content creator and distributor, today announced Dinero Consciente, a new Spanish-language podcast exploring the relationship between money, human behavior and everyday life.

 Hosted by Brazilian financial executive Darla Sierra, Dinero Consciente moves beyond traditional conversations about investments and personal finance to examine a fundamental question: Why do people make the financial decisions they make?

 Through practical and accessible discussions, Sierra explores how emotions, habits, family experiences and personal beliefs influence the way people earn, spend, save and plan for the future. New episodes are released twice a week.

 “Financial information is widely available, but information alone does not always change behavior,” said Jack Hobbs, President of reVolver Podcasts. “What distinguishes Dinero Consciente is Darla’s ability to connect financial concepts with the emotions and experiences that shape everyday decisions. She gives Spanish-speaking listeners an approachable way to understand their relationship with money and make choices that support the lives they want to build.”

 With nearly 20 years of experience in financial markets and training in Behavioral Finance from the University of Chicago, Sierra brings professional expertise and a human-centered perspective to the program. Her approach recognizes that money is rarely only about numbers. Financial decisions can also be connected to identity, culture, security, ambition, fear and personal definitions of success.

 Understanding the Behavior Behind Financial Decisions

 People may know they should save more, control unnecessary expenses or prepare for emergencies, yet still struggle to act consistently. Others avoid looking at their finances because the subject creates stress, guilt or uncertainty.

 Dinero Consciente examines the gap between knowing what to do and putting that knowledge into practice.

 Episodes translate financial and behavioral concepts into relatable conversations. The objective is not to prescribe a universal formula for success, but to help listeners identify the patterns influencing their choices and become more intentional about what they do next.

 The program also explores how financial behaviors develop over time. Lessons absorbed during childhood, periods of instability and cultural messages about money can influence adult attitudes toward spending, saving, debt, professional opportunities and financial risk.

 “Money is present in almost every important area of our lives, but many of us were never taught to understand the emotions and beliefs shaping our financial behavior,” said Sierra. “With Dinero Consciente, I want to make these conversations practical, natural and accessible. When we understand what drives our decisions, we can begin making choices with greater clarity, confidence and purpose.”

 More Than an Investment Podcast

 Although saving, investing and financial planning are part of the broader conversation, Dinero Consciente is not built exclusively around technical strategies or market performance. The podcast begins with the person making the decision.

 Episodes may examine why people spend differently when stressed, how financial conversations create tension within relationships, why social comparison affects purchasing decisions or why an immediate reward can feel more compelling than a long-term objective.

 Topics explored through the program include:

 * Emotional influences on spending and saving
* Family experiences and financial beliefs
* Money, identity and personal confidence
* Behavioral biases affecting financial decisions
* Fear, uncertainty and long-term planning
* Financial communication in families and relationships
* Aligning financial choices with personal priorities
* Developing healthier and more consistent money habits

 Sierra presents these subjects in clear Spanish, making potentially complex financial concepts easier to understand without removing their practical meaning. The program creates room for listeners to learn, question familiar habits and consider different approaches without judgment.

 This perspective is particularly relevant for audiences navigating financial decisions across different countries, cultures and generations. Even within one household, people may hold very different beliefs about money. One person may associate saving with security, while another may prioritize enjoying the present or supporting extended family. Dinero Consciente examines the experiences behind those perspectives.

 Building Awareness and Freedom of Choice

 At the center of the podcast is the idea that greater awareness can create greater freedom.

 Financial freedom does not mean the same thing to everyone. It may involve building an emergency reserve, changing careers, reducing financial stress, starting a business, supporting relatives or preparing for retirement. Instead of imposing one definition, Dinero Consciente encourages listeners to identify what financial confidence and freedom of choice mean in their own lives.

 Understanding behavioral patterns can help individuals pause before reacting, evaluate alternatives and make decisions aligned with their priorities. The goal is not to remove emotion from money decisions, but to recognize its influence and respond more consciously.

 “Darla understands that a meaningful conversation about money must include the person behind the numbers,” Hobbs added. “Her professional experience and knowledge of behavioral finance allow her to explain not only how financial decisions work, but why they can be difficult.”

 Dinero Consciente supports reVolver Podcasts’ commitment to delivering relevant Spanish-language content across business, career and education. With two episodes each week, the program gives listeners an ongoing opportunity to reconsider the role money plays in their lives and build a relationship with their finances based on greater understanding and intention.

 Dinero Consciente is available now on Spotify and other major podcast platforms.
