# Manny Rodriguez of Gilead Sciences to be Recognized as One of the Visionaries of 2027 in Miami

MIAMI, FL — November 19, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Manny Rodriguez, Director of HIV Public Affairs Communications in the United States at Gilead Sciences, will be recognized as one of the Visionaries of 2027 during the business leadership luncheon organized by Negocios Magazine and Ejecutiva Magazine, which will take place on December 10, 2026, from 12:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m., at the EB Hotel Miami.

 The recognition will highlight his leadership in developing communication strategies aimed at promoting education, HIV prevention, and access to culturally relevant health information, especially among Latino communities in the United States.

 From his position at Gilead Sciences, Rodriguez has helped raise awareness of initiatives that seek to reduce the stigma associated with HIV and encourage open conversations about the importance of testing, prevention, and sexual health care.

 Among these initiatives is “Ahorita Mismo” (Right Now!), a campaign launched in October 2026 as part of Care for the Culture, Gilead’s platform dedicated to promoting more open and inclusive conversations about sexual health.

 Leadership Committed to the Latino Community

 In a recent interview with Negocios Magazine, Rodriguez emphasized that Latino communities continue to face significant disparities in HIV prevention and care.

 According to AIDSVu data cited by the executive, in 2024, Latino individuals accounted for 34% of new HIV diagnoses in the United States, despite representing approximately 19% of the population.

 Rodriguez explained that these disparities are related to systemic barriers, such as stigma, language difficulties, a lack of culturally appropriate information, and limited access to healthcare services.

 “The most meaningful progress happens when companies listen to communities, collaborate with trusted organizations, and support efforts that make prevention, testing, and care more accessible,” Rodriguez said during the interview.

 The executive also highlighted Gilead Sciences’ ongoing commitment to Latino community organizations. Since 2020, the company has invested nearly $29 million in support of 40 organizations led by Latino individuals or serving Latino communities in the United States.

 Visionaries of 2027: Recognizing Leadership with Impact

 The Visionaries of 2027 luncheon will bring together approximately 40 business owners, executives, and representatives of organizations from South Florida, at an event designed to recognize leaders whose professional careers and social commitment represent a significant contribution to business and community development.

 In this edition, Negocios Magazine and Ejecutiva Magazine will recognize a select group of professionals and business leaders who stand out for their vision for the future, leadership abilities, and positive impact in their respective sectors.

 The recognition of Manny Rodriguez will particularly highlight the importance of strategic communication as a tool for raising awareness, promoting inclusion, and helping improve community health.

 “Recognizing Manny Rodriguez means highlighting a professional who understands that business leadership also involves making a commitment to communities. His work demonstrates how communication can become a powerful tool to educate, reduce barriers, and generate a positive impact on society,” said Eduardo Hapke, Publisher of Negocios Magazine.

 The event will also provide an opportunity to strengthen relationships among business leaders, professionals, and organizations that contribute to the growth and development of South Florida.
