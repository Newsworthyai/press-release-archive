 The latest iPhone models

 iPhone Duo is the first foldable iPhone, with an innovative design that is beautiful, versatile, and durable. iPhone Duo opens to reveal a large 7.6-inch internal display for viewing content, gaming, and multitasking, and closes to provide a compact 5.4-inch external display that fits in your pocket. Built to last with an innovative hardware design and a precision hinge, iPhone Duo is powered by the A20 Pro chip to deliver pro performance and impressive battery life, plus an advanced camera system, where its foldable design opens up fun new ways to use the camera that aren't possible on any other iPhone. The iOS 27 operating system has also been reinvented for the versatile ways users can interact with iPhone Duo.

 The iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max offer the new 48MP Fusion main camera with variable aperture, the most advanced camera Apple has ever created, and new Pro controls allow users to customize their experience. Powered by the A20 Pro chip, iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max achieve major breakthroughs in battery life and performance. The iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro models feature Apple Intelligence1 and Siri AI2, a completely new version of Siri, launching in beta in English with iOS 27, to combine artificial intelligence capabilities with the user's personal context, becoming a smart personal hub with privacy and security as a cornerstone. iPhone Duo is available in two refined colors: star white and night sky. iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max are available in four elegant finishes: black, silver, glacier, and an all-new wine color.

 * The iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro models feature eSIM3, offering greater flexibility, better security, seamless connectivity, and more battery life on eSIM-only models compared to traditional physical SIM cards. iPhone Duo features an eSIM-only design that saves internal space to maximize battery capacity, while the eSIM-only models of iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max leverage the space previously occupied by the physical SIM card to incorporate a larger battery that provides two additional hours of video playback.

 The new Apple Watch smartwatch models

 The Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 offer the most accurate heart rate sensor in a wearable device4. Both new models have been designed with the new Health Sensing System and the S11 chip to deliver an enhanced set of health and fitness features including a new readiness score, higher-frequency heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV) measurements, plus longer battery life during workouts and faster charging. Apple Watch Series 12 is available in 42mm and 46mm sizes in a variety of beautiful finishes, including dark bronze, black, light gold, and space gray aluminum; radiant gold and natural titanium; and a stunning ceramic material in pearl white and night blue. Apple Watch Ultra 4 is available in natural and black titanium.

 * With watchOS 27, Apple Intelligence comes to your wrist, bringing Siri AI to Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 with personal context understanding and broad world knowledge5. Later this year, new Audio Intelligence features will launch in beta in English to help you stay aware of the world around you, catch up on what you missed, and remember key moments—with your control, privacy, security, and accessibility at the center of its design6—along with a redesigned Health app on iPhone and iPad, starting in U.S. English, that includes a new Longevity tab with a Health Age feature that shows your metrics' progress relative to your age7.

 Introducing AirPods 5

 AirPods 5 offer the industry's best Active Noise Cancellation8 in an open-ear design, powered by a new multiport acoustic architecture and next-generation Adaptive EQ for even more immersive sound. Its innovative open-ear Active Noise Cancellation removes up to 50 percent more external noise compared to the previous generation, along with a more natural Transparency mode. Combined with Siri AI and iPhone, AirPods let users leverage their personal context and get answers with broad world knowledge, entirely hands-free1. Users can also respond to Siri with head gestures or use Live Translation to connect regardless of language. For users who want even more, AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case add longer battery life and volume control on the case9.
