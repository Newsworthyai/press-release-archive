# Dr. Gina Goldfeder Graces The Cover of Hispanic Target Magazine in an Exclusive Interview with Renowned Journalist Nancy Clara

MIAMI, FL — August 4, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Hispanic Target Magazine features Dr. Gina Goldfeder, one of the most influential voices in analytical psychology, human development, and personal transformation across Latin America, on the cover of its latest edition. The lead feature includes an in-depth interview conducted by renowned international journalist Nancy Clara, exploring the specialist’s career and the impact of her new book, “En un sueño cabe tu vida: El método LiberaSueña para la transformación personal” (Your Life Fits Inside a Dream: The LiberaSueña Method for Personal Transformation), published by Bienestur / Hachette Livre.

 In a conceptual and inspiring dialogue, the feature written by Nancy Clara delves into Dr. Goldfeder’s approach to stepping off daily “autopilot” and utilizing the dream world as a clinical, emotional, and spiritual tool for self-discovery. Backed by more than 30 years of private clinical practice working with individuals and couples, the author explains how the LiberaSueña Method combines Jungian-oriented psychology, therapeutic writing, and active imagination to heal past emotional wounds and reconnect with one’s life purpose.

 “Your dreams already know the way; this book teaches you how to walk it. In an era dominated by rush and instant answers, stepping into dream territory means unlocking the soul’s most intimate language,” highlighted Dr. Goldfeder during her interview with Hispanic Target Magazine.

 This cover story and feature article mark the official launch of the author’s public relations and communications campaign leading up to her highly anticipated appearance at the Miami Book Fair, taking place from November 15 to 22, 2026.

 The complete interview written by Nancy Clara is now available to readers across the U.S. Hispanic market and Latin America on the publication’s official portal: https://hispanictarget.com/dra-gina-goldfeder/ .

 About Dr. Gina Goldfeder Dr. Gina Goldfeder is a clinical psychotherapist, speaker, author, and emotional well-being specialist with over 30 years of experience. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Clinical Psychology from Universidad Anáhuac, a Master’s and Ph.D. in Individual and Couples Therapy from the Instituto Mexicano de la Pareja (AMETEP), and a Master’s degree in Creative Writing from the University of Salamanca, Spain. She is the creator of the LiberaSueña Method and the author of prominent works such as En un sueño cabe tu vida (Bienestur) and Soltar la herida: Una oportunidad de vida (Ediciones Urano). Throughout her career, she has collaborated with key global figures in health and consciousness, including Joe Dispenza, Bruce Lipton, Brené Brown, and Brian Weiss.
