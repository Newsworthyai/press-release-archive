# Cosmetique Aesthetics Relocates to New State-of-the-Art Facility in Culver City and Launches Enhanced Digital Experience

CULVER CITY, Calif., October 16, 2026 /Noticias Newswire/— Cosmetique Aesthetics, a premier medical spa specializing in advanced aesthetic treatments, proudly announces its relocation to a new, state-of-the-art facility located at 10744 Washington Blvd, Culver City, CA 90232 (view directions). This expansion reflects the brand’s continued commitment to innovation, elevated patient care, and cutting-edge aesthetic solutions.

 The newly opened location has been thoughtfully designed to provide a modern, sophisticated, and comfortable environment for both existing and new patients. Equipped with advanced technology and refined interiors, the space enhances the overall patient experience while supporting the delivery of high-quality, results-driven treatments.

 In addition to its new physical location, Cosmetique Aesthetics has also unveiled a fully redesigned website aimed at improving accessibility and user experience. The updated platform offers streamlined navigation, detailed treatment information, and an easier way for patients to explore services and schedule appointments.

 We are incredibly proud to welcome our patients to this new, state-of-the-art facility,” said Dr. Ramin Sarshad, Medical Director. “Our mission has always been to combine medical expertise with innovation and a luxury experience. This new space allows us to take that commitment even further.”

 Rose Flores-Gomez, Aesthetic Consultant, Surgical Coordinator, and Medical Aesthetician, added: “This relocation represents more than just a new address—it’s a reflection of our growth and dedication to delivering personalized, high-quality care. We are excited to continue serving our community in this upgraded environment.”

 Besides their core offerings within skin rejuvenation and body contouring procedures, Cosmetique Aesthetics is also preparing to introduce an exclusive VIP peptide campaign, designed to provide select patients with access to innovative treatments and curated benefits. Additional details regarding this highly anticipated launch will be announced soon.

 Patients and interested individuals are encouraged to stay connected for upcoming announcements and exclusive opportunities.

 For more information about Cosmetique Aesthetics, its services, or to schedule an appointment, visit the newly redesigned website or contact the office directly. For Hispanic patients, you can also visit the Spanish-language site: https://es.cosmetiqueaesthetics.com/

 About Cosmetique Aesthetics Cosmetique Aesthetics is a leading medical spa based in Culver City, California, offering a wide range of advanced aesthetic treatments, including skin rejuvenation, injectables, and body contouring procedures. The practice is dedicated to combining medical expertise with innovative technologies to deliver natural-looking, confidence-boosting results in a safe and welcoming environment.
