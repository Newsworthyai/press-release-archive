# Canela.TV Debuts Vertical Micro-Series Featuring Fantasy, Sci-Fi and Anime Romance

NEW YORK, NY — November 12, 2026 — (NOTICIAS NEWSWIRE) — Forbidden fantasy romance, futuristic love stories and anime adventures where time stands still are coming to Canela.TV. The free streaming platform is launching a new slate of original vertical micro-series in Spanish and English, created by the award-winning Canela Studios. Made for mobile viewing, the bite-sized episodes deliver captivating stories that fit into any moment on the go. The new originals debut alongside the Turkish hit Kuma (La Otra Esposa) and restored Mexican Cine de Oro classics, both reimagined in vertical video for a new generation of binge-watchers who scroll and stream from their phones.

 The new originals include:

 – Stolen by the Fae King: A Celtic scholar is dragged into a dangerous fae realm to repay her family’s blood debt, only to fall for the ruthless king who holds her contract. But he harbors a secret that could change everything.

 – The Android Who Chose Her: In a world where androids are programmed to obey their owners, the most advanced android ever created defies the rules to serve a waitress he’s met only once, sparking an unexpected romance.

 – The Midnight Loop: A young woman relives the same rainy night, racing to save the boy she’s falling for from a supernatural catastrophe. But breaking the cycle could mean sacrificing the one she loves.

 The lineup also brings the addictive twists of international television to the micro-series format with Kuma (La Otra Esposa). The Turkish hit follows a young woman falsely accused of murder and forced into an impossible decision. Packed with betrayal, suspense and unexpected twists, the live-action drama delivers a cliffhanger at every turn, making it perfect for binge-watching.

 Canela.TV is also giving iconic Mexican films a new way to shine. Restored in high definition, available in English for the first time and adapted into short-form episodes, beloved titles from the Diana International Films library are finding new audiences while preserving their cultural legacy.

 Featured films include Hasta que Perdió Jalisco, starring Jorge Negrete and Gloria Marín; La Mujer sin Alma, featuring María Félix and Fernando Soler; and Muchachas que Trabajan, starring Ariadne Welter, Rosita Arenas and Angélica María.

 “The way people watch is evolving, and Canela.TV is shaping what that looks like for Latino and bilingual audiences. These originals bring new genres and new voices to Canela.TV in Spanish and English, right next to the classics and novelas our viewers love,” said Isabel Rafferty Zavala, Co-Founder and CEO of Canela Media.

 From fantasy worlds and sci-fi romance to international dramas and timeless classics, Canela.TV’s new micro-series are made to enjoy anywhere. Stream them, along with an extensive library of classic movies, original series and novelas from around the world, for free at https://www.canela.tv/ or on the Canela.TV app for phones and connected TVs, with no subscription required.

 About Canela Media Canela Media is an innovative media company created to connect Latino audiences with the content they love, free and in Spanish. Through its streaming platform, Canela.TV, viewers can enjoy a wide selection of live and on-demand programming, including original content, music, sports, kids programming, novelas, and classic Golden Age Mexican films.

 Canela.TV is the only streaming app with a fully integrated rewards program. Through Club Canela, users earn points and prizes for watching and enjoying their favorite content, creating a more interactive and rewarding viewing experience.

 Available across all major streaming devices for TV, web, and mobile, Canela.TV has become a leading destination for free entertainment for Latino audiences in the United States and Latin America, combining innovation, culturally relevant content, and an experience designed for today’s viewers.

 For more information, visit www.Canela.TV.
