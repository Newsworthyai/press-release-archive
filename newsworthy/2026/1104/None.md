# Glen Rose Invites Visitors to Experience an Action-Packed November

Glen Rose, Texas - the official "Dinosaur Capital of Texas" - is rolling out a packed calendar this November, giving visitors plenty of reasons to make the scenic drive and stay the weekend.

 "With so much happening, one day is never enough. Glen Rose has rolled out the November Calendar with details on all of the upcoming events this month. Glen Rose is the perfect weekend getaway destination," said Traci Niedziela, Tourism Director, Glen Rose

 November 5: Open Mic Night at The Palace Public House

 Kick off the month’s festivities on Thursday, November 5, at The Palace Public House. From 7:00-9:00 p.m., local talent takes the stage for an evening of music and community in the heart of town.

 November 6: First Friday Art Walk + Art Class at Noblemen Wine

 Start the weekend on a creative note on Friday, November 6, with the First Friday Art Walk. Pair local artistry with a visit to Noblemen Wine for an evening of culture and craft.

 November 6-7: Cinch Breakaway at The Expo

 The action ramps up Thursday and Friday evenings at The Expo Center with the Cinch Breakaway, drawing top competitors for high-energy rodeo action. Gates open at 7:00 p.m. both evenings.

 November 6-8: Chili Cook-Off at Dinosaur Valley RV Park

 For a taste of Texas tradition, head to the Chili Cook-Off at Dinosaur Valley RV Park. Running from November 6 through November 8, this event promises delicious competition and a true Hill Country atmosphere.

 November 6-7: Live Music at Loco Coyote Grill

 Just down County Road 1004, Loco Coyote Grill keeps the weekend humming with back-to-back nights of live music. A Chick and a Dude take the stage Friday, November 6, from 7:00-10:00 p.m. followed by Big Joe Walker on Saturday, from 7:00-10:00 p.m.

 November 7: 2nd Annual Dino-Bility Day at Dinosaur Valley State Park

 From 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. on Saturday, Dinosaur Valley State Park hosts the 2nd Annual Dino-Bility Day - a special inclusive event at the park's Blue Hole designed so visitors of all abilities can experience Glen Rose's world-famous dinosaur tracks and natural beauty. ASL interpreters will be onsite.

 November 7: Bluegrass & Spirits at Sledge Distillery's 10-Year Anniversary

 Sledge Distillery marks a milestone with its Hootenanny: 10-Year Anniversary Party, featuring the Beatlegras Band and Social Riot Hat Co. The festivities run from 2:00-10:00 p.m. with craft spirits and live bluegrass.

 November 7: MG Car Show on the Downtown Square

 Classic-car enthusiasts should arrive early Saturday morning when the historic Downtown Square fills with gleaming MGs and other vintage vehicles. Stroll the square, browse the charming shops, and grab lunch at one of Glen Rose's locally owned restaurants.

 November 14-15: Let's Give Thanks - Performance Horses Event at The Expo Center

 The following weekend, The Expo Center welcomes elite equestrians for the Let's Give Thanks - Performance Horses Event, a two-day showcase of cutting, reining, and ranch-horse versatility.

 November 14: Jack Mason & The Full Band at Loco Coyote Grill

 Cap off the evening with Jack Mason & The Full Band live at Loco Coyote Grill from 7:00-10:00 p.m. With a full-band country sound, it’s the perfect end to a Glen Rose Saturday.

 Game Days at The Palace Public House

 Catch all the big games on the big screen at The Palace Public House

 Catch the best of college football at The Palace Public House on the Downtown Square. Doors open at 11:00 a.m. for College Game Day, featuring big screens, cold drinks, and a vibrant atmosphere. Great food, cold drinks, and game-day energy every weekend.

 Make It a Weekend: Explore Glen Rose's Signature Experiences

 Visitors are encouraged to download the Dinosaur Pass on the free Bandwango app and embark on a scavenger hunt to find all 98 unique dinosaur sculptures scattered across town. Complete the hunt and earn a complimentary entry to Fossil Rim Wildlife Center, an acclaimed drive-through safari park home to more than 1,000 exotic and endangered animals.

 Start the adventure at the Glen Rose Visitors Center, where you'll meet Rexanne, the life-size animatronic T. rex, and scan the QR code to begin the Dinosaur Pass. Then lace up your boots for a stroll along the Paluxy River Walk, a scenic trail that winds through the heart of town.

 For a full details on all events, lodging options and trip-planning resources, visit Glen Rose Calendar of Events

 About Glen Rose, Texas

 Nestled along the banks of the Paluxy River just 90 minutes southwest of Dallas-Fort Worth, Glen Rose is the official "Dinosaur Capital of Texas" and the county seat of Somervell County. Home to world-famous dinosaur tracks at Dinosaur Valley State Park, the acclaimed Fossil Rim Wildlife Center, and 98 unique dinosaur sculptures dotting the town, Glen Rose blends prehistoric wonder with small-town Texas charm. Visitors can explore the historic Downtown Square, stroll the scenic Paluxy River Walk, and experience a growing live-music and events scene at venues like the newly remodeled Texas Amphitheater, Loco Coyote Grill and The Palace Public House. Whether it's a weekend of rodeo action at The Expo Center, craft spirits at Sledge Distillery, or a family scavenger hunt on the Dinosaur Pass, Glen Rose offers an overnight destination unlike anywhere else in the Lone Star State.
