# ORGANA International Brings Grab-and-Go Mineral Nutrition to September Retail Meetings

The functional-shots category is expanding as consumers increasingly look for convenient, portable ways to incorporate nutrition and wellness products into busy lifestyles. ORGANA International is bringing its plant-derived mineral focus into that growing category with new ORGANA Mineral Shots, a caffeine-free, grab-and-go format the company will present during upcoming September retail meetings.

 According to Grand View Research, the global functional-shots market was valued at $5.7 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach $19 billion by 2033, representing a compound annual growth rate of 16.2 percent. Single-dose shots accounted for 85.8 percent of category revenue in 2025, reflecting strong consumer interest in convenient, ready-to-use wellness formats.

 The trend aligns closely with ORGANA's new Mineral Shots, which bring the company's long-standing focus on mineral nutrition into an individual format designed for consumers at work, while traveling, at the gym, or simply looking for a convenient addition to their daily wellness routine.

 "Consumers want wellness products that fit the way they live today," said Larry Mills, CEO of ORGANA International. "The growth of the shot category shows how important convenience and portability have become. Mineral Shots give us an opportunity to bring ORGANA's long-standing focus on plant-derived mineral nutrition into a format that makes sense for today's consumer and today's retailer."

 Why Wellness Shots Are Gaining Attention Convenience is playing an increasingly important role in consumer wellness. Rather than carrying larger containers or incorporating complicated steps into their routines, consumers are showing interest in compact, single-use formats that can easily travel with them.

 Industry research points to several factors helping drive the functional-shots category, including growing interest in convenient wellness products, plant-based formulations, natural ingredients, and products designed to fit increasingly busy lifestyles.

 ORGANA Mineral Shots are all-natural and caffeine-free, providing a different approach within a category often associated with traditional energy shots.

 The individual bottles are also packaged in a colorful point-of-purchase display designed to give retailers a highly visible, ready-to-merchandise format while making it easy for consumers to discover and try the product.

 "We wanted Mineral Shots to be easy to understand, easy to try, and easy for retailers to merchandise," Mills said. "It's a new format for ORGANA, but it remains consistent with the natural wellness philosophy we've followed for nearly three decades."

 Bringing a Growing Wellness Format to Major Retail Buyers ORGANA will bring its new Mineral Shots to the ECRM Vitamin, Weight Management & Sports Nutrition Session, taking place Sept. 14-17 in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida.

 Mills will participate in scheduled meetings with buyers representing major health, specialty, food, drug, and mass retail channels, providing an opportunity to introduce Mineral Shots while discussing ORGANA's broader wellness portfolio and plans for continued U.S. growth.

 "ECRM gives us the opportunity to put this new concept directly in front of retail decision-makers and hear what they're looking for," Mills added. "We're excited about Mineral Shots, but we're equally excited about introducing more retailers to the ORGANA brand and the portfolio we've built over the years."

 Founded in 1997, ORGANA International has developed a portfolio centered on plant-derived minerals and natural wellness formulations, including its Fulvic Acid Trace Mineral Drink, Liquid Oxygen, Essiac Tea, Mineral Shots, magnesium creams, and other wellness products.

 ORGANA Products Available on Amazon and OneLavi Consumers can explore and purchase ORGANA products through the company's Amazon storefront and on OneLavi.com. To learn more about the company's wellness philosophy visit organa.net.

 The company's growing e-commerce presence complements its retail expansion strategy and gives consumers convenient access to ORGANA products while the brand works to expand into additional U.S. retail channels.

 About [ORGANA International](https://organa.net/) Founded in 1997, ORGANA International is a wellness company committed to delivering natural, bioavailable products that help people live healthier lives. From trace minerals and liquid oxygen to magnesium creams and detoxification teas, ORGANA’s product line is designed to restore vital elements often missing in modern lifestyles. Headquartered in Springville, Utah, ORGANA is a customer-first company with a 5-star BBB rating and nearly three decades of dedication to natural wellness.
