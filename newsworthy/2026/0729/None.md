# TNT Announces U.S. Expansion With Game Changer

TNT (True Nutrition Technology), a German performance nutrition company known for its science-driven formulations and premium manufacturing standards, today announced plans to bring TNT Game Changer to the United States, further expanding its growing portfolio of German-developed sports nutrition products for American athletes and active consumers.

 TNT Game Changer is an established product in Germany that combines carbohydrates from maltodextrin and dextrose with a mineral complex containing calcium, magnesium, and potassium. Mixed with water, the 100% vegan formula creates an isotonic-style carbohydrate and electrolyte drink designed for use before, during, or midway through demanding physical activity.

 “Game Changer has become an important part of our German product portfolio because it supports athletes during longer training sessions and competition,” said Markus Himmelstoss, Managing Director of True Nutrition Technology. “We are excited to introduce this product to the U.S. market as we continue expanding the selection of premium TNT products available to American athletes.”

 TNT Game Changer was developed for team athletes, endurance athletes, and active consumers participating in prolonged physical activity. Potential uses include hockey, football, basketball, soccer, handball, cycling, running, swimming, hiking, Hyrox and CrossFit-style training, and other activities where athletes may benefit from replenishing carbohydrates and minerals during exertion like hiking trips.

 The formula includes potassium, which contributes to normal muscle function and the normal functioning of the nervous system. Calcium contributes to normal energy-yielding metabolism and normal muscle function, while magnesium contributes to electrolyte balance, normal muscle function, and the reduction of tiredness and fatigue.

 TNT Game Changer can be consumed before exercise, during a training session, or at a natural midpoint such as halftime. The product is intended to provide athletes with a practical way to replenish carbohydrates and important minerals during longer periods of activity.

 “The name Game Changer reflects the role the product is intended to play during demanding activity,” Himmelstoss said. “It is not a shortcut or an instant transformation. It is a convenient way for athletes to replenish resources during training or competition so they can remain focused on the second half of their performance.”

 The planned U.S. introduction of TNT Game Changer reflects TNT’s continued commitment to expanding its American product portfolio. The company is actively working to bring additional established products from its German lineup to the United States as part of its long-term retail growth strategy.

 American consumers can already purchase TNT Creapure® Creatine Monohydrate, and TNT Synapsensause “Wild Berry” Pre-Workout Booster, through Amazon and OneLavi.

 * TNT Synapsensause "Wild Berry" Pre-Workout Booster on Amazon
* TNT Synapsensause "Wild Berry" Pre-Workout Booster on OneLavi
* TNT Creapure® Creatine Monohydrate Powder on Amazon
* TNT Creapure® Creatine Monohydrate Powder on OneLavi
* TNT Clear Whey Protein Isolate - Peach Iced Tea on OneLavi

 More than 500,000 customers have chosen TNT products. The company continues to combine German manufacturing standards, carefully selected ingredients, and performance-focused formulations as it broadens its presence across the United States.

 TNT Game Changer is not yet available in the United States. Additional information about its U.S. launch, package sizes, and availability will be announced as the introduction progresses.
