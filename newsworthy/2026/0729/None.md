# HR.com Honors Excellence in Managing and Leading Performance with 2026 Performance Management Awards

HR.com is proud to announce the recipient of the 2026 Performance Management Award, recognizing an organization that is transforming the way it manages and leads performance. This award-winning program demonstrates how effective performance strategies can strengthen employee engagement, foster growth, build accountability, and drive organizational success.

 This award celebrates HR initiatives that go beyond traditional performance processes to create meaningful employee experiences. The recipient is recognized for developing an innovative approach that helps employees understand expectations, receive ongoing feedback, build new capabilities, and contribute to business outcomes.

 “Today’s leading organizations are moving beyond traditional performance reviews to create cultures of growth, feedback, and recognition. Metrolinx exemplifies how thoughtful performance strategies can empower employees and drive success,” said Debbie McGrath, CEO of HR.com.

 Most Effective Performance Culture: Metrolinx

 At Metrolinx, a high-performance culture starts with people. Guided by clear strategic priorities, empowered leaders, and people-centered programs, we foster an environment where employees feel supported, recognized, and inspired to grow. Together, we're building an inclusive, purpose-driven workplace that enables our people to deliver the future of transit for Ontario.
