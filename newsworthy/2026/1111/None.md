# ORGANA Highlights the Value of Balance During the Holiday Season

As the holiday season approaches, ORGANA International is highlighting the importance of maintaining balance during one of the busiest times of the year, when celebrations, travel, family gatherings, and changing schedules can make everyday wellness routines more difficult to maintain.

 The Utah-based natural wellness company, founded in 1997, encourages consumers to approach the holidays with a focus on consistency rather than perfection, recognizing that good nutrition, adequate rest, physical activity, and time spent with family and friends all contribute to a balanced lifestyle.

 "The holidays are meant to be enjoyed, but they can also become one of the busiest times of the year," said Larry Mills, CEO of ORGANA International. "We believe wellness is about balance, not perfection. It's about maintaining good habits while making room for the traditions, celebrations, and experiences that make this season special."

 Finding Balance During a Busy Season The weeks between Thanksgiving and New Year's often bring changes to familiar routines. Meals may be less predictable, travel can interrupt schedules, and holiday commitments can compete with time normally reserved for exercise, relaxation, and sleep.

 Rather than approaching these changes with restrictive goals or unrealistic expectations, ORGANA encourages consumers to focus on practical habits that can be maintained throughout the season.

 These include eating a varied, balanced diet, staying physically active, getting adequate sleep, and making time to relax.

 Small adjustments, such as taking a walk after a holiday meal, maintaining regular sleep schedules when possible, or making time for a nutritious breakfast, can help people maintain a sense of consistency during busy periods.

 "One of the challenges with wellness is that people sometimes think they need to do everything perfectly," Mills said. "But everyday choices matter. You can enjoy holiday traditions and still make time for the habits that are important to you."

 Keeping Nutrition in Perspective Holiday meals and celebrations often bring greater attention to food choices, but ORGANA believes the conversation around nutrition should extend beyond individual meals or seasonal indulgences.

 A balanced approach includes understanding the role of essential nutrients, choosing a variety of foods, and recognizing that long-term dietary patterns are more meaningful than any single day of eating.

 ORGANA has explored these themes through its ongoing educational efforts, including discussions about nutrient density, trace minerals, plant-derived ingredients, and the relationship between nutrition and everyday wellness.

 The company's product portfolio includes Fulvic Acid Trace Mineral Drink, Liquid Oxygen, Essiac Tea, magnesium creams, and Mineral Shots, reflecting its longstanding focus on natural wellness ingredients and consumer choice.

 

 "Nutrition is an important part of wellness, but it shouldn't become a source of unnecessary pressure during the holidays," Mills said. "We encourage people to enjoy the season, understand the ingredients they choose, and think about wellness as something they practice throughout the year."

 Making Time for Rest and Connection Beyond nutrition, the holiday season provides opportunities to reconnect with family, friends, and community.

 Social connections, relaxation, and adequate sleep are important components of overall well-being, particularly during periods when schedules become more demanding.

 ORGANA encourages consumers to make room for these priorities alongside holiday activities, recognizing that maintaining balance can mean knowing when to slow down.

 For the company, this perspective reflects a broader philosophy developed over nearly three decades in the natural wellness marketplace: wellness is not defined by a single product, trend, or seasonal resolution.

 "The holidays remind us that wellness is about more than what we eat or the products we use," Mills said. "It's also about spending time with people we care about, getting enough rest, and finding ways to enjoy life while taking care of ourselves."

 A Year-Round Approach to Natural Wellness Founded in 1997, ORGANA International has spent nearly 30 years developing natural wellness products and educating consumers about plant-derived ingredients and everyday nutrition.

 As the company continues expanding its U.S. retail presence, ORGANA remains focused on helping consumers make informed choices about the products and ingredients they incorporate into their routines.

 With the holiday season approaching, the company's message is straightforward: balance, consistency, and realistic expectations can help make wellness a sustainable part of everyday life, during the holidays and throughout the year.
