# The Arts Council of Greater Los Angeles Relaunches with an Inaugural Event on Wednesday, 9/16 @ The Wende Museum, Culver City

The Arts Council of Greater Los Angeles launches as an independent nonprofit, marking a new era of artistic collaboration and cultural impact in LA.

 After 15 years with the Century City Chamber of Commerce, The Arts Council now independently aims to foster an inclusive arts community and position LA as a global cultural capital.

 As a standalone nonprofit, The Arts Council will expand its programming with a robust calendar of events designed to engage diverse audiences throughout Los Angeles. Its Inaugural event will take place on September 16 at the Wende Museum.

 What: LA in Motion Now & Next: Reimagining Art, Access and Policy Panel Discussion

 When: Wednesday, September 16, 2026

 Time: 6 - 9 PM

 Where: Wende Museum, Community Room10808 Culver Boulevard, Culver City, CA 90230

 Cost: Free Admission, Seating is limited

 RSVP: On Eventbrite: https://www.eventbrite.com/e/la-in-motion-now-and-next-reimagining-art-access-policy-discussion-tickets-1997639779355?aff=ebdssbdestsearch

 Social Reception Preceding, and Following, the Panel Discussion

 Panel Participants: * Maya Emsden, Executive Officer, Arts + Community Enrichment, LA Metro
* Sarah Cifarelli, LAX Art Program Director
* David Pagel, Claremont Graduate University Art Department
* Catherine Wagley, Moderator, Writer and Art Critic

 The Arts Council of Los Angeles will deepen its role as a collaboration hub, connecting artists with patrons and advancing arts access for all.

 “Our vision is to position Los Angeles not only as an entertainment capital, but as a true global arts capital,” said Agnes Penot, The Arts Council Chair.“We aim to celebrate creativity, preserve cultural heritage, and ensure the arts are accessible to everyone through innovative programs and partnerships.””
