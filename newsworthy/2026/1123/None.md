# Hawaii's Leading Realtors Achieve New Heights with Elite Luxury Certification

Heidi Dollinger and Mark Janes of Pacific Island Partners with Coldwell Banker Island Properties Global Luxury in Kapalua, Hawaii, completed the Luxury Home Marketing training offered by The Institute for Luxury Home Marketing, the premier independent authority in training and designation for real estate agents working in the luxury residential market. This training equips real estate professionals with the essential skills and expertise to meet the unique needs of affluent buyers and sellers, blending market insight with personalized concierge services for ultra-high-net-worth clients.

 The completion of this focused training also provides Pacific Island Partners founders Heidi & Mark, with membership into The Institute, which connects them to exclusive resources and benefits that their real estate team at Pacific Island Partners can use to successfully market their luxury residential listings, find qualified buyers for high-end property, and satisfy their clients’ needs in the West Maui community and throughout Hawaii.

 “Institute members represent the most qualified and skilled luxury real estate professionals in their respective markets,” said Kathleen Bibbins, Director of The Institute. “They understand the unique needs of high-net-worth individuals when it comes to listing and selling luxury estates and have access to an extensive network of successful luxury agents made possible by The Institute.”

 Heidi Dollinger has been a REALTOR® on Maui since 2014 and specializes in the West Maui real estate from Lahaina to Kaanapali and Kapalua, along with West Molokai estates in Maunaloa. In addition to her Luxury Property Specialist designation, Heidi holds a GREEN designation from the National Association of REALTORS®, a specialized credential that trains real estate agents to evaluate, market, and guide clients through energy efficient and sustainable housing, along with a designation as a Resort and Second Home Property Specialist. Heidi has been recognized by RealTrends as being among the top 1.5% of real estate agents nationwide, and has a strong client and broker referral network in Hawaii and beyond.

 For more than three decades, Mark Janes has developed luxury homes, managed construction projects, and advised clients on real estate investments. His experience as both a developer and a REALTOR® enable him to recognize not only what a property is today, but what it has the potential to become. Mark holds a GREEN designation from the National Association of REALTORS®, a specialized credential that trains real estate agents to evaluate, market, and guide clients through energy efficient and sustainable housing.

 Heidi Dollinger and Mark Janes co-founded their luxury real estate team, Pacific Island Partners, in 2025, combining their experience and expertise to serve high-net-worth clients seeking private retreats in Hawaii. Pacific Island Partners leverages market intelligence and innovative marketing to transcend traditional real estate, matching luxury homes with ideal buyers and coordinating tailored lifestyle services.

 “The Institute’s training provided me with insight about elevating our marketing and provided valuable networking contacts with other agents who specialize in luxury,” said Mark.

 As Luxury Property Specialists, Heidi and Mark with Pacific Island Partners are part of an exclusive group within the Coldwell Banker brand, making up the top tier of independent sales associates affiliated with the brand worldwide. The Coldwell Banker Global Luxury® program sets the gold standard for modern luxury real estate marketing.
