# Hegseth Pushes Live-Streamed Fort Hood Firing Squad as Hosts Dissect 'October Surpriselets'

Episode 1911 of No Agenda, titled 'Surpriselets,' hosted by Adam Curry and John C. Dvorak, published Sunday, October 11, 2026, brings listeners another sweeping Sunday media deconstruction from the heart of the Texas Hill Country. With Dvorak sidelined, Curry is joined by returning guest co-host Sir Rob Dew of Infowars for a nearly three-hour tour through what the hosts dub the Trump administration's cascade of pre-midterm 'surpriselets,' little October surprises stacked like stocking stuffers heading into the 2026 vote.

 The episode zeroes in on the news cycle's most provocative threads. Among the topics unpacked:

 * Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's announcement that convicted Fort Hood shooter Nidal Hasan will face a public, potentially live-streamed firing squad on December 3rd
* The bizarre FlyDubai axe attack by pilot Hammam al-Hammami and ex-CIA analyst Larry Johnson's theory tying it to Benjamin Netanyahu
* Trump's surprise diesel deal with Vladimir Putin and the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize going to Navi Pillay
* A suspected plague death at Russia's Anti-Plague Research Institute in Irkutsk
* Walmart's electronic shelf labels and dynamic pricing

 Curry and Dew press hard on the Hegseth announcement, noting Jake Tapper's point that the United States would join Afghanistan, Iran, North Korea, Somalia, and Yemen as the only nations conducting public executions. Dew offers a blunt reaction: 'I'm all for the execution. I don't know, the public execution, streaming it, you know, I'm kind of waffling. I definitely don't mind. I think it should have been done a long time ago.' They also replay Vice President JD Vance dodging a question about how the Pentagon plan squares with his Christian faith, and revisit the 1936 Owensboro, Kentucky hanging, America's last fully public execution.

 The deconstruction then widens to Marco Rubio's sweeping speech in Athens defending Western civilization and sanctioning the International Criminal Court, Barack Obama's new midterm hope-and-change pitch for Democrats, and Mike Tyson's surprise endorsement of Trump at a Syracuse rally. Curry flags Bret Weinstein's warning on DarkHorse that any plague outbreak traceable to the Irkutsk facility could involve gain-of-function engineering or a psyop exploiting IgG4 immune suppression in mRNA-vaccinated populations. The pair also eulogize Chicago Bears legend Mike Ditka, dissect Kimberly Guilfoyle's ethics troubles as ambassador to Greece, and salute Slovak MEP Milan Mazurek's fiery European Parliament speech on mass deportations.
