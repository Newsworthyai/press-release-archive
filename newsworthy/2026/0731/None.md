# DH Unplugged Bids Farewell to John C. Dvorak With Family Tribute and Fort Lauderdale Meetup

Episode 811 of DH Unplugged, titled Remembering JCD, published July 28, 2026, is a heartfelt tribute to co-host John C. Dvorak, who passed away peacefully at home the previous week while watching NCIS with his wife Mimi. Host Andrew Horowitz is joined by John's son, JC Dvorak, to reflect on 18-plus years of partnership behind the microphone, share family stories, and announce a public memorial meetup. The episode also returns to the show's core mission: unpacking the week's most consequential moves in financial markets.

 Listeners can expect a full hour that moves from remembrance to hard market analysis. Key threads include:

 * Andrew's on-air tribute to John and readings of condolence notes from listeners worldwide.
* JC Dvorak on growing up around figures like Larry Ellison, Kai Power Tools, and early CNET tech coverage.
* Nvidia's $250 billion OpenAI backstop, the Ohio data center buildout, and circular AI financing.
* Semiconductors entering a bear market, SK Hynix earnings, and the CXMT IPO surge in China.
* OpenAI's reported rogue model incident involving Hugging Face and the sandbox debate.

 The tone is by turns tender and unflinching. Reflecting on the loss, Horowitz says:

 He made our audiences smarter, and friendships richer. And Tuesday nights are never going to be the same, ever. JC Dvorak captures the family's private ritual, recalling standing weekly dinners: "For the past, you know, decade or more, we've been doing a dinner every Friday, and last Friday was really hard because it was, you know, we didn't have dinner." The hosts also confirm an in-person tribute and No Agenda / DH Unplugged meetup in Fort Lauderdale on 8/8 at 3:33 PM, coinciding with John's funeral earlier that day.

 

 On the market side, the discussion drills into what Horowitz calls a "rug pull" mood around SpaceX, Tesla robotaxi promises, and the Cathie Wood trillion-dollar thesis. The pair unpack a chart posted at dhunplugged.com showing Nvidia at the center of a circular financing loop involving Oracle, SoftBank, and major banks, drawing parallels to Lucent and Nortel's vendor financing during the 1999 to 2000 telecom bubble. JC Dvorak, speaking from inside the AI industry, details how DeepSeek and Moonshot's Kimi K3 are pressuring margins, describes a client migration from a $10 million Anthropic contract to a $100,000 on-prem Kimi deployment, and questions whether OpenAI's incident was incompetence dressed up as existential marketing. The Close to the Pin winner: Mark Pugner of Ireland, using John's old pseudonym, guessed SpaceX at $133 against a $123.99 close.

 About DH Unplugged DH Unplugged is a weekly investing and markets podcast hosted by Andrew Horowitz and John C. Dvorak that blends market commentary, economic news, business trends, and offbeat cultural observations in a candid, skeptical, and often humorous conversation. Episodes are available at dhunplugged.com, via RSS, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Amazon Music/Podcasts. Episode 811 is available now wherever podcasts are heard.
