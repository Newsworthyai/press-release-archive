# Fauci Pleads the Fifth 111 Times as Guest Hosts Fill In for No Agenda

Guest hosts Darren O'Neill and Larry Blythner step in for the July 30, 2026 broadcast of the long-running media deconstruction podcast, filling the chairs left open as Adam Curry takes bereavement time following the passing of co-host John C. Dvorak. Episode 1890, titled "Flock Off!" tackles Anthony Fauci's headline-grabbing Senate appearance, Elon Musk's latest predictions on artificial intelligence, and a growing bipartisan revolt against Flock Safety license plate cameras. The pair open with a candid explanation of how they were tapped to keep the twice-weekly show running without interruption.

 The episode dissects several stories dominating the news cycle with the show's trademark clip-driven analysis. Topics include:

 * Fauci invoking the Fifth Amendment 111 times before a Senate committee, and the diary entries that surfaced alongside it
* Elon Musk telling The Economist that AI will exceed human intelligence within five years, with a 10 to 20 percent chance of "killer robots"
* The ACLU joining right-leaning municipalities in Sedona, Arizona and elsewhere to cancel Flock Safety contracts
* A McLaughlin poll on the Iran conflict and Strait of Hormuz strikes
* A WNBA controversy involving the Seattle Storm owner confronting two teenage Sophie Cunningham fans

 The hosts read verbatim passages from Fauci's roughly 1,100-page diary, including entries about Julia Roberts, Barbra Streisand, and a Kennedy Center evening with Joan Baez. Blythner does not mince words about the spectacle. "This is the biggest, uh, comedic event in the history of United States government, no question about it," he says, arguing Fauci squandered a chance at a Caesar's Palace residency by listening to his six attorneys instead of answering Senator Josh Hawley's questions about the color of his tie and the carpet in front of him.

 The Flock Safety segment digs into CEO Garrett Langley's TED Talk and the company's 120,000 cameras across 49 states. O'Neill and Blythner reference the ACLU's Chad Marlowe, the dflock.org tracking site, and reporting from Sir Ryan Bemrose about antenna sensors capable of harvesting MAC addresses from passing phones. They contrast Langley's pitch that "safety should just be a right" with Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson's stance on incarceration, El Salvador's Bukele prison model, and Radley Balko's book "Rise of the Warrior Cop." A lengthy donation segment honors Dvorak with tributes from producers around the world, plus knightings and meetup announcements from Coeur d'Alene to Calgary.

 About No Agenda Show No Agenda is a long-running, listener-supported podcast hosted by Adam Curry and John C. Dvorak that takes a skeptical, independent look at mainstream media, politics, culture, and the forces shaping the daily news cycle. Known for sharp commentary, humor, and media deconstruction, the show questions how stories are framed, amplified, or ignored across news outlets and government messaging. Episode 1890 is available now wherever podcasts are heard.
