# Boerne, Texas Welcomes Autumn with Vibrant Fall Activities 

As the leaves begin to turn and cooler temperatures settle over the Texas Hill Country, Boerne is rolling out a season full of reasons to visit. From elevated dining and curated shopping events to handmade markets and farm-fresh experiences, this charming destination offers the perfect backdrop for a fall getaway along the historic Hill Country Mile.

 Whether planning a girlfriends' weekend, a family outing, or a relaxing retreat, Boerne's lineup of autumn activities makes it easy to slow down, explore, and soak up the season.

 Fall Shopping Events on the Hill Country Mile

 November is the perfect time to gather your girlfriends, make a little room in your holiday shopping list, and plan a day - or weekend - exploring Boerne's Hill Country Mile. With charming boutiques, locally owned shops, cozy cafés, and festive shopping events, downtown Boerne makes holiday shopping feel a lot more like a getaway and a lot less like a chore.

 From Diva Night to Pink Friday and the holiday magic of Dickens on Main, there are plenty of reasons to grab your favorite shopping buddies and head to downtown Boerne this November.

 Make It a Girls' Night at Diva Night | November 5

 Fall Diva Night returns to the Hill Country Mile for an evening of boutique shopping, dining, drinks, and quality time with friends. Whether you're getting a head start on holiday gifts or treating yourself to something special, Diva Night is the perfect excuse to explore downtown Boerne's one-of-a-kind shops and discover something new.

 Think Pink, Shop Small on Pink Friday | November 20

 Before the rush of Black Friday, celebrate Pink Friday in Boerne! This nationwide shop-small movement encourages shoppers to support locally owned businesses ahead of the busiest holiday shopping weekend. This year, 30 local businesses are joining the fun with special shopping activities and surprises, including the Pink Friday Duck Hunt - a playful scavenger hunt inviting shoppers to search for hidden pink ducks at participating businesses for a chance to earn prize entries. It's a fun way to explore new shops, discover unique gifts, and enjoy a little friendly competition with your girlfriends.

 Experience the Magic of Dickens on Main | November 27-28

 There's no better way to wrap up a month of shopping small than with Boerne's beloved Dickens on Main celebration. Thanksgiving weekend transforms the Hill Country Mile into a festive holiday destination, with Plaid Friday on November 27 and Small Business Saturday on November 28 offering a wonderful opportunity to support the locally owned shops that make Boerne such a special place. Between holiday shopping, seasonal dining, live entertainment, and the festive atmosphere, there's plenty of reason to gather your girlfriends or bring the whole family for a full weekend along the Hill Country Mile.

 Discover the Hill Country Mile Beyond the Events

 Of course, you don't need a special occasion to enjoy shopping in Boerne. The Hill Country Mile is home to a delightful mix of independent boutiques, specialty shops, galleries, restaurants, and inviting spots to take a break between purchases. Plan a leisurely lunch, stop for coffee or a glass of wine, and leave yourself plenty of time to explore. For an even more relaxed escape, book an overnight stay and turn your shopping trip into a Hill Country girlfriends' getaway.

 Boerne Handmade Market | November 6-7

 The largest handmade market of its kind in South Texas returns to Boerne for a two-day celebration of local artistry and craftsmanship. Featuring 75+ handmade-only vendors, the Boerne Handmade Market showcases an impressive selection of jewelry, apparel, leather goods, toys, home décor, art, handmade candles, specialty food, drinks, and more.

 * VIP Night (Friday): 6:30-9:30 p.m. | $25
* Saturday Market: 9 a.m.-4 p.m. | $5
* Location: 1910 Church, 130 Sisterdale Road, Boerne, Texas

 VIP tickets are expected to sell out quickly, so early purchase is encouraged. The event is family friendly and perfect for discovering one-of-a-kind holiday gifts from local makers.

 For tickets and more information, visit the Boerne Handmade Market event page or contact Email Contact | (210) 663-9046.

 Sunny's All Day Brunch - Over-the-Top Fall Menu

 Sunny's All Day Brunch brings seasonal indulgence with its special new signature Over the Top Fall Menu. Known for generous portions and creative dishes, Sunny's gives visitors yet another reason to plan a fall outing in downtown Boerne.

 Fall Workshops at The Downtown DIY

 For those looking to get creative this season, The Downtown DIY offers hands-on fall workshops where visitors can craft seasonal décor, personalized gifts, and more. It's a fun addition to any girls' trip or family visit to downtown Boerne.

 Farm-Fresh Finds at Boerne's Farmers Markets

 Boerne is home to two standout farmers markets that celebrate the region's agricultural roots and community spirit:

 The Farmers Market at the Herff Farm

 More than a market, this experience connects visitors with the land through educational programming and locally grown produce in a scenic Hill Country setting.

 Boerne Farmers Market

 Located along the Hill Country Mile, this vibrant market features local vendors, live music, and a Monthly Kids Market - making it a favorite for families and visitors looking to experience Boerne's community culture firsthand.

 Plan Your Fall Getaway to Boerne

 With so much to see, taste, shop, and experience, a single afternoon may not be enough. Visitors are encouraged to book an overnight stay and turn a day trip into a full Hill Country escape. From boutique lodging at The Kendall Hotel to pampering at Art Spa House and elevated dining at The Kendall Restaurant, Cypress Grille, and Valeria, Boerne offers every ingredient for the perfect autumn retreat.

 About Boerne, Texas

 Boerne is a charming Texas Hill Country destination located just 30 miles northwest of San Antonio along Interstate 10. Known for its historic Hill Country Mile lined with locally owned shops, galleries and acclaimed fine dining restaurants, Boerne blends small-town character with sophisticated culinary offerings that appeal to discerning travelers. Surrounded by the natural beauty of the Hill Country, rolling limestone hills, spring-fed creeks and towering cypress trees, Boerne has established itself as a premier food destination with elevated dining, wine experiences and year-round events that make it one of Texas's most sought-after weekend getaways.
