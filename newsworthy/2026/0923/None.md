# HR.com Recognizes Infor Talent Science for Advancing Smarter Hiring in Retail

HR.com has named Infor Talent Science the recipient of the Best HR Solution for Retail Business Award for 2026, recognizing its innovative approach to one of retail’s most pressing challenges: attracting, selecting, and retaining the right talent in a fast-changing, high-volume environment.

 The award highlights Infor Talent Science’s use of predictive analytics to help retailers make more informed talent decisions, identify candidates with a stronger potential for fit, and build teams positioned for long-term success. By turning workforce data into actionable insights, the platform enables HR teams to move beyond reactive hiring and take a more strategic approach to workforce planning and retention.

 “Congratulations to Infor on this well-deserved recognition,” said Debbie McGrath, CEO of HR.com. “Their innovative HR solutions help retail employers address workforce challenges, strengthen talent practices, and build stronger organizations and people.”

 Best HR Solution for Retail Business Award: Infor Talent Science

 Infor Talent Science is transforming retail hiring and talent management with predictive people analytics, helping organizations reduce frontline turnover, accelerate high-volume hiring, improve productivity, develop stronger leaders, and deliver more consistent customer experiences.
