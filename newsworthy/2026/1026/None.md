# WolfeBarron Law Announces New Name, Next Generation of Leadership, & Continuing Legacy of Excellence

The Law Office of Randall J. Wolfe, a respected Oregon personal injury law firm with decades of combined legal experience, is proud to announce its rebranding as WolfeBarron Law, along with its relocation to 3 Centerpointe Drive, Lake Oswego, OR 97035. The new name reflects an exciting transition in the firm's leadership as attorney Charis WolfeBarron, daughter of founder Randall J. Wolfe, carries forward the family's legacy of dedicated legal advocacy.

 Although the name and location are changing, WolfeBarron Law's core values, experienced legal team, and commitment to protecting the rights of injured Oregonians remain steadfast.

 A New Generation of Leadership, Built on a Strong Foundation For years, The Law Office of Randall J. Wolfe has earned a reputation for providing compassionate, results-driven legal representation to individuals and families throughout Oregon. Founded on principles of integrity, personalized attention, and determined advocacy, the firm has helped countless clients navigate some of life's most challenging circumstances.

 As the next generation of leadership, Charis WolfeBarron brings her own legal experience, perspective, and dedication to client service while preserving the standards that have defined the firm.

 The transition represents more than a change in name. It is a continuation of a family legacy, bringing together the foundation established by Randall J. Wolfe and the vision of a new generation of leadership.

 Randall J. Wolfe will continue his association with the firm in an Of Counsel capacity, allowing WolfeBarron Law to maintain the benefit of his extensive legal knowledge and experience as the firm moves forward.

 The Same Commitment to Excellence, Under a New Name As WolfeBarron Law, the firm remains dedicated to representing individuals and families affected by negligence, serious injuries, and wrongful death throughout Oregon.

 The firm's practice continues to encompass a broad range of personal injury matters, including motor vehicle collisions, commercial truck accidents, motorcycle accidents, catastrophic injuries, premises liability, and wrongful death claims.

 Clients can continue to expect the personalized attention, thorough case preparation, open communication, and determined legal advocacy that have long distinguished the firm.

 The name is new, yet our mission is unchanged: to deliver exceptional legal representation with compassion, respect, and individualized attention.

 This commitment to continuity is especially important for current clients, who can feel confident that the firm's dedication to their cases and pursuit of favorable outcomes remains unwavering throughout the transition.

 Honoring the Past While Embracing the Future The rebranding to WolfeBarron Law is both a tribute to the firm's history and a reflection of its future.

 By incorporating the next generation's name into the firm's identity, WolfeBarron Law recognizes Charis WolfeBarron's leadership while honoring the reputation and professional foundation established by her father.

 The firm also plans to introduce a new website in the future to reflect its updated identity and provide an enhanced online experience for current and prospective clients. In the meantime, visitors can continue to access information about the firm's attorneys, practice areas, and legal services through its existing website at https://www.attorneywolfe.com/.

 As WolfeBarron Law enters this new chapter, its focus remains where it has always been: standing beside injured individuals and families, protecting their rights, and pursuing justice through skilled, compassionate legal representation.

 About WolfeBarron Law WolfeBarron Law, formerly The Law Office of Randall J. Wolfe, is a Lake Oswego-based personal injury law firm serving clients throughout Oregon. With more than 45 years of combined legal experience, the firm represents individuals and families in personal injury, motor vehicle accident, catastrophic injury, premises liability, and wrongful death matters. Led by Managing Partner Charis WolfeBarron, with founder Randall J. Wolfe serving Of Counsel, WolfeBarron Law continues its tradition of dedicated advocacy, personalized service, and a commitment to excellence.
