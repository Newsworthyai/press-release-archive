# HARO Launches Self-Serve Advertising in the Journalist-Request Newsletter Read by PR Professionals

HARO (Help a Reporter Out), the journalist-request newsletter owned by Featured, today launched Advertise in HARO, a self-serve system for buying text advertising in HARO's three weekday editions. Advertisers pick a placement, choose editions and dates on a calendar that shows the live price for every open slot, write the ad in the browser, and pay through Stripe. HARO staff read every ad before it runs. Booking is open at helpareporter.com/advertise.

 HARO does not charge PR professionals to pitch journalists. The newsletter is free to receive and free to answer, and advertising is what pays for it.

 "Some platforms in this category charge PR people a monthly subscription for the right to pitch more often. We took the other route," said Brett Farmiloe, founder and CEO of Featured, the company that owns HARO. "HARO stays free for journalists and for the experts who answer them. The companies that pay are the ones that want to reach this audience, and now they can book that reach in a few minutes without a sales call."

 HARO sent more than 100 million emails to subscribers over the past year. Its readers are PR professionals, publicists, marketers, founders, and subject matter experts who answer journalist requests in categories including business, technology, health, travel, lifestyle, and entertainment. Advertise in HARO is built for companies that sell to that audience, such as PR and marketing software, agencies, media databases, research publishers, event organizers, course creators, and other newsletters.

 How to purchase a HARO ad Advertisers can book at helpareporter.com/advertise. The process has three steps and a review:

 1. Choose a placement, then an edition and date. The calendar shows the price for every open slot, up to 45 days ahead.
2. Write the ad copy and link text, and add one destination URL. Orders with several dates can carry different versions of the ad, assigned date by date.
3. Review the order and pay through Stripe. No HARO account is needed. The receipt and review decision go to the work email on the order.
4. HARO reviews the ad during business hours and confirms the scheduled edition by email.

 HARO requires at least one business day between booking and publication for review. Advertisers can request up to two revisions before the deadline stated in their review email. Ads that do not meet HARO's advertising guidelines are declined and refunded in full.

 Placement types HARO ads are text placements in HARO's free journalist-request newsletter, sold self-serve at published rates to companies that want to reach PR professionals, publicists, marketers, founders, and subject matter experts. Every HARO ad is plain text with one link. HARO does not accept images or HTML.

 Advertising standards HARO reviews every advertisement before it runs. Ads may not promise a story or editorial coverage, describe a placement as guaranteed press, sell links, or present a paid placement as news. Ads that do not meet the guidelines are declined and refunded.

 "We wanted buying an ad in HARO to feel the way submitting a query feels for a journalist. You see the rate and pick the date, and the ad runs once our team has read it," Farmiloe said. "That is the whole system."

 About HARO HARO (Help a Reporter Out) is a free newsletter that connects journalists with expert sources. Founded in 2008, HARO was shut down by its previous owner in December 2024 and relaunched by Featured in April 2025. HARO delivers journalist requests to subscribers in three weekday editions and sent more than 100 million emails in the past year. The newsletter is free for journalists and for the sources who answer them, and it is supported by advertising. Learn more at helpareporter.com.

 About Featured Featured is the AI co-pilot for PR. Founded in 2022 and headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona, Featured operates as Terkel, Inc. and owns HARO and Connectively. Learn more at featured.com.
