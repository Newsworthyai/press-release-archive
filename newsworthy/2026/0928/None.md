# North Austin Senior Care Leader Stacey Eisenberg Named Finalist for Austin Business Woman’s 2026 Health Business/Expert of the Year

Stacey Eisenberg, co-owner of A Place At Home - North Austin, has been named a finalist for Austin Business Woman’s 2026 Health Business/Expert of the Year award. The recognition highlights her leadership in non-medical home care and her significant contributions to Central Texas organizations serving older adults, caregivers, and vulnerable adults.

 Austin Business Woman says the category honors a woman-owned business making a significant impact in health and wellness through innovation, leadership and a commitment to community well-being. The winner will be announced at the 2026 Star Awards Gala on November 12, 2026, from 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. at The Norris Conference Centers in Austin.

 Eisenberg's lifelong commitment to senior care began in her childhood at the nursing home where her mother worked. She began working in senior care at 13 and has since worked across nursing homes, assisted living and memory care, hospitals, rehabilitation centers and independent living settings. As a trained recreation therapist, she brings decades of experience to families making difficult care decisions. 

 In 2023, Stacey and her husband, Bryan Eisenberg, founded A Place At Home - North Austin. Based in Round Rock, the agency helps older adults and other clients remain at home with non-medical personal care, companionship, dementia support, respite care, transportation and care coordination. It serves families across Williamson and Travis counties, including Round Rock, Georgetown, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Hutto, Leander and North Austin.

 Leadership throughout the community Stacey Eisenberg, from A Place At Home - North Austin, extends her influence beyond the agency as a Community Educator for the Alzheimer’s Association and supports the Williamson County Walk Committee. She also serves as Treasurer of the Adult Protective Services Advisory Board, Community Awareness Chair for the Aging Services Council, and Co-Facilitator of the Williamson County Health Resource networking group.

 She is also a member of the National Aging in Place Coalition, contributes to Age Friendly Austin initiatives, and participates in Healthy Williamson County, the Williamson Collab Action Group and the Regional Dementia Support Coalition for the Capital Area. Her volunteer work also includes serving as President of Round Rock High School’s Project Grad program and PTSA Spiritwear Chair.

 Recognition from clients, caregivers and neighbors In 2025, A Place At Home - North Austin received the Activated Insights Best of Home Care® Provider of Choice and Employer of Choice awards, reflecting feedback from clients and care professionals. The agency was also named Best Senior Care Service in the 2024 and 2025 Best of Round Rock awards. Eisenberg’s new finalist recognition highlights the broader community work behind the care families receive at home.

 About Stacey Eisenberg Stacey Eisenberg is a senior care expert and co-owner of A Place At Home - North Austin in Round Rock, Texas. Her experience spans residential care, dementia support, recreation therapy, care navigation and local advocacy for older and vulnerable adults. She serves in volunteer and leadership roles across Alzheimer’s education, adult protective services, aging services and community health.

 About A Place At Home - North Austin A Place At Home - North Austin provides non-medical in-home care and care coordination in Williamson and Travis counties. The Round Rock-based agency supports older adults, veterans, adults with disabilities and their families with personal care, companionship, dementia care, respite care, transportation and help navigating care needs. Learn more at aplaceathome.com/north-austin.
