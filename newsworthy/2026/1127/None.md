# Comedy Village NYC Announces $99 Black Friday Unlimited Comedy Pass for Two

Comedy Village, one of New York City's live stand-up comedy destinations, is excited to announce the launch of its Black Friday Unlimited Comedy Pass. Available for a limited time at just $99, this exclusive VIP membership offers unlimited admission for two people to standard comedy shows for an entire year at its Times Square location.

 Available for purchase through December 3, 2026, the Black Friday promotion delivers exceptional entertainment value for New Yorkers, comedy enthusiasts and visitors looking to experience Manhattan's vibrant nightlife without paying an admission fee each time they attend.

 Located at 352 West 44th Street, between Times Square, the Broadway Theater District and Hell's Kitchen, Comedy Village features professional stand-up comedians, nationally touring performers, special guests and emerging talent from New York City's legendary comedy scene, including comedians seen on Netflix, HBO and The Tonight Show.

 “Our Black Friday Unlimited Comedy Pass is the ultimate gift for comedy lovers,” said a PJ Landers, owner Comedy Village. “We want to make live entertainment more accessible and affordable while giving audiences the opportunity to discover incredible comedians throughout the year.”

 One Pass. Two People. One Year of Unlimited Laughter For a one-time payment of $99, pass holders can enjoy unlimited admission to standard comedy shows with no recurring membership fees or blackout dates on standard performances. A two-item food or beverage minimum applies per person, per show.

 Whether planning a Manhattan date night, looking for things to do in NYC after work, entertaining out-of-town guests or enjoying a weekend with friends, the Unlimited Comedy Pass provides an affordable alternative to traditional entertainment memberships.

 With regularly changing lineups and live performances seven nights a week, every visit offers something different.

 Give the Gift of Laughter This Holiday Season The Comedy Village Unlimited Comedy Pass also makes an ideal Christmas gift, holiday present or New York City experience gift for couples, friends, family members, students and coworkers.

 Rather than purchasing a traditional gift, customers can give someone an entire year of live entertainment in the heart of Midtown Manhattan.

 The limited-time Black Friday offer is available exclusively through December 3, 2026.

 Purchase the $99 Black Friday Unlimited Comedy Pass at: https://comedyvillage.com/black-friday-unlimited-comedy-pass/

 About Comedy Village Comedy Village is a Veteran owned New York City stand-up comedy club dedicated to showcasing established comedians and the next generation of comedic talent. Located in the heart of Manhattan's entertainment district, the club offers live comedy seven nights a week, providing an authentic NYC nightlife experience for residents, tourists and comedy fans from around the world.
