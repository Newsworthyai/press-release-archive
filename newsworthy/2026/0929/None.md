# Don't Miss Nathan Macintosh's Texas Debut at The Riot Conroe

Comedian Nathan Macintosh is returning to Texas for two nights at The Riot Conroe on Friday, October 9 and Saturday, October 10. Macintosh has performed several times at The Riot in Houston, but the October dates will mark his first appearance at the comedy club’s Conroe location.

 “I’ve done The Riot in Houston a few times, so I’m looking forward to checking out the Conroe room,” said Macintosh. “New room, new crowd. It should be a fun couple of nights.”

 The Friday show begins at 7:30 p.m., followed by a 7:00 p.m. performance Saturday. Macintosh is known for fast-paced stand-up focused on technology, money, relationships and the increasingly strange parts of modern life. His television appearances include CONAN, The Late Show with Stephen Colbert and The Tonight Show. Variety named him one of its “10 Comics to Watch” in 2024. With stand-up clips and specials like 'Money Never Wakes' and 'Down With Tech,' Macintosh has amassed a large online following.

 The Conroe dates add another Texas stop to Macintosh’s touring schedule and give audiences north of Houston a chance to see his live show closer to home.

 Event Details

 * Friday, October 9, 2026, 7:30 p.m.
* Saturday, October 10, 2026, 7:00 p.m.

 Tickets and additional information are available at RiotConroe.com.

 About Nathan Macintosh Nathan Macintosh is a stand-up comedian known for energetic performances and sharp commentary on technology, culture, relationships and everyday life. He has appeared on CONAN, The Late Show with Stephen Colbert and The Tonight Show. His stand-up specials include Money Never Wakes and Down With Tech. For more information and upcoming tour dates, visit NathanMacintosh.com.
