# ICS Legal Strengthens Client Protections with Human-Led Review of AI-Assisted Case Handling

ICS Legal, a UK immigration law firm regulated by the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA), today confirmed that all case work supported by artificial intelligence tools continues to be reviewed and signed off by a qualified member of its legal team before any advice, application, or decision reaches a client.

 As AI tools become more common across the legal and immigration advice sector - from document summarisation to application-checking software - ICS Legal has maintained a policy of keeping a qualified adviser accountable for every case outcome, regardless of which tools are used during preparation.

 "Technology can help us work through paperwork faster and catch inconsistencies earlier," said a spokesperson for ICS Legal. "But immigration cases affect people's lives, families, and futures. Every case that passes through our firm is reviewed by a person who understands the law and the client's circumstances, not just by a tool."

 The firm, which has advised on UK immigration, British nationality, and European law matters for individuals and businesses since 2006, said the approach reflects its broader commitment to transparency and accountability as the sector adapts to new technology.

 ICS Legal continues to provide advice across a range of areas, including skilled worker visas, sponsor licences, family and partner visas, settlement, and immigration appeals, from its offices in London.

 About ICS Legal ICS Legal is an IAA-regulated immigration law firm based in London, providing advice on UK immigration, British nationality, human rights, and European law to individuals and businesses.
