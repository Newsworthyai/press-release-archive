# Sharpen Your Memory in a Minute a Day: Resona Health Launches Memory Room, a Free Daily Memory Game

Resona Health today announced the launch of Memory Room, a free daily game designed to help players sharpen their memory and attention in about a minute a day. It puts one simple question to players: how much did you really notice? Players step into a room for just 20 seconds. Then the room disappears,

 and six quick questions ask what they actually saw. A new room opens every day at midnight Eastern, and the game is free to play now at memoryroomgame.com.

 The idea is easy to grasp and surprisingly hard to master. The clock starts the moment a player opens the door, and anything in the room might matter - the color of a lamp, the time on a clock, the number of books on a shelf. Some rooms add video and sound, so players have to listen as well as look.

 "We walk through rooms all day long and assume we're taking it all in," said Mark L Fox, founder of Resona Health and author of The Memory Room. "Then someone asks a simple question about what was on the table, and you realize you have no idea. Memory Room turns that moment into a game. It takes about a minute, it's a little humbling, and most people want to come back tomorrow to do better."

 How it works * Look: Open the door and study the room for 20 seconds.
* Answer: The room vanishes and six multiple-choice questions test what you noticed.
* Compare: Once enough people have played, see how everyone else answered the same room.
* Come back: A brand-new room arrives every day at midnight Eastern. Track your streak and personal best, and challenge friends with a shareable link.

 First-timers can warm up in a practice room that doesn't count toward their score. The game runs in any web browser on a phone, tablet or computer - there is nothing to download and no account is required to play.

 The game takes its name from Fox's book The Memory Room, an Amazon #1 Bestselling Book and #1 Hot New Release that ranked #1 in Brain Diseases, Nursing Home Care and Medical eBooks. The book is available free to readers, and the game carries forward its theme: the moments and details we hold onto, and the ones that slip past us.

 Memory Room is designed for anyone 13 and older who enjoys a quick daily challenge, from puzzle fans who already have a morning game routine to families looking for something to play and compare together.

 Availability Memory Room is free and available now at memoryroomgame.com.
