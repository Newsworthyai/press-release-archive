# Trinzik.ai partners with Houston RIA Symphony Financial, drives 300 percent gain in AI recommendation win rate

Trinzik.ai, one of the first AI visibility firms built specifically to serve compliance-heavy, regulated industries, today announced an expanded partnership with Symphony Financial, a registered investment advisory (RIA) firm based in Houston, following a 90-Day AI Visibility Sprint that grew the firm's head-to-head win rate against competing advisors by more than 300 percent.

 The win rate measures how often Symphony Financial is cited or recommended when prospective clients use AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity to research financial advisors, a discovery channel that traditional SEO and marketing strategies were not built to address.

 Building on those results, Trinzik is relaunching Symphony Financial's website on an agentic foundation designed to eliminate the manual bottlenecks that typically slow firms down on AI visibility, all operating within Trinzik's compliance guardrails. The new site is built to keep Symphony Financial's content current and discoverable across AI platforms without adding work for the firm's team or introducing compliance risk.

 “Financial advisory clients are already asking AI platforms who they should trust with their money,” said John Michaels, Co-Founder and Director of Sales at Trinzik.ai. “Firms that don’t understand how they show up in those conversations are leaving their reputation to chance. Symphony Financial’s results show what’s possible when a firm takes that channel seriously.”

 “We are excited about the opportunity to showcase our firm and leverage Trinzik.ai to improve our fingerprint as a top RIA,” said Amber Walker, COO & VP Operations of Symphony Financial, Ltd. Co.

 Unlike agency-driven marketing solutions, Trinzik works directly with firms like Symphony Financial, delivering a compliance-first approach built specifically for the regulatory realities of wealth management and RIA firms.

 About Symphony Financial Symphony Financial is a Houston, Texas-based registered investment advisory firm.

 About Trinzik.ai Trinzik.ai is one of the first AI visibility firms built specifically to serve compliance-heavy, regulated industries, including registered investment advisors and wealth management firms. Based in Austin, Texas, Trinzik works directly with clients to help them understand and improve how they are represented across AI-driven search and recommendation platforms. Trinzik's team brings decades of enterprise B2B experience to a compliance-first methodology designed for the way regulated firms actually operate.

 Media Contact: John Michaels, Co-Founder and Director of Sales, Trinzik.ai
