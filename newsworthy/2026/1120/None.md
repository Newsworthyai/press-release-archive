# Mitch Gould Recalls Business Partnership With Hulk Hogan

Mitch Gould, Founder and CEO of Nutritional Products International (NPI), is looking back on his business partnership and friendship with the late Hulk Hogan, sharing a personal recollection that offers a glimpse of the man behind one of professional wrestling's most recognizable personalities.

 In a recent interview published on YouTube video, Gould recalls how a business relationship involving Hogan-branded nutritional products eventually led the two entrepreneurs to form Sports Nutrition Innovations, a company that brought together Gould's experience in consumer product distribution and Hogan's celebrity appeal.

 The partnership was one of several high-profile ventures in Gould's decades-long career in sports nutrition, retail distribution, and entrepreneurship.

 "We made a deal to represent Hogan Granules. And from that point, it just kept exploding into more products, more products," Gould recalled in the video.

 The relationship eventually grew into a joint business venture, culminating in a launch celebration in Delray Beach, Florida, attended by approximately 500 guests, including the city's mayor.

 But for Gould, one of his most memorable experiences with Hogan had nothing to do with business.

 The Human Side of Hulk Hogan Gould recalled an evening when he and Hogan were having dinner at a friend's steakhouse in Delray Beach.

 During the meal, Hogan noticed a child with a disability dining outside with his parents. Rather than continuing his dinner, Hogan approached the family, lifted the child onto his shoulder, and posed for photographs.

 It was a spontaneous gesture that left a lasting impression on Gould.

 "That was Terry. Terry was a good man. He was the real thing. I really miss him," Gould said, referring to Hogan by his real name, Terry Bollea.

 For Gould, the encounter revealed a side of Hogan that was often overshadowed by the larger-than-life personality millions of wrestling fans knew from television.

 While Hogan was celebrated for his achievements in professional wrestling and entertainment, Gould remembers the friend and business partner who took time to make an ordinary evening special for a family.

 "I've learned so much from him that I really appreciate the opportunity to get to know that man," Gould said.

 From Celebrity Partnerships to Consumer Brand Distribution Gould's partnership with Hogan was part of a broader entrepreneurial career that included working with prominent personalities in sports and entertainment, including action film star Steven Seagal.

 These experiences helped Gould develop an understanding of how celebrity recognition, product development, marketing, and retail distribution intersect in the consumer marketplace.

 His work in sports nutrition also helped establish relationships and industry experience that would become central to his subsequent business ventures.

 Today, Gould leads Nutritional Products International, where he applies decades of experience helping domestic and international consumer brands navigate the complexities of entering the U.S. market.

 NPI's proprietary Evolution of Distribution® platform integrates logistics, regulatory compliance, product positioning, retail and e-commerce distribution, and post-placement marketing into a coordinated market-entry strategy.

 Throughout his career, Gould has emphasized the importance of personal relationships, trust, and the connections that develop through business partnerships.

 His memories of Hogan reflect that philosophy.

 Although their partnership began with nutritional products and business opportunities, Gould's lasting appreciation extends beyond their commercial accomplishments.

 For Gould, the most meaningful part of the experience was the opportunity to know Terry Bollea as a person.

 About [Mitch Gould](https://nutricompany.com/team/mitch-gould/) Mitch Gould is a third-generation retail distribution and manufacturing expert with more than 25 years of experience launching and scaling consumer products across sports nutrition, health and wellness, skincare, hardware, and beverages. He has worked with iconic consumer brands such as Igloo, Rubbermaid, Sunbeam, and Miracle-Gro, as well as high-profile celebrity brands including Steven Seagal, Hulk Hogan, Chuck Liddell, 8× Mr. Olympia Ronnie Coleman, and martial arts pioneer Bob Wall.

 Over the course of his career, Gould has played a key role in helping shape the growth of the U.S. sports nutrition category-supporting the expansion of performance-focused products across major retail and e-commerce platforms, including Amazon, Walmart, GNC, CVS, and Walgreens. His work has centered on bringing muscle-building, recovery, and performance-oriented products to a broader consumer audience.
