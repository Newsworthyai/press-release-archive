# Direct Reach Expands U.S. Education Audience Data

Direct Reach today announced an expansion of its U.S. education audience data solutions, providing marketers with additional audience segments designed for education, tutoring, test preparation, admissions, training and education-technology campaigns.

 The expanded portfolio brings several education-related audience categories together under Direct Reach’s broader Education Industry Email List offering.

 Organizations can use the available audience segmentation options to plan campaigns around specific education markets rather than relying exclusively on broad consumer or business databases.

 The expanded coverage includes audience categories associated with SAT and ACT preparation, Advanced Placement programs, MCAT preparation, USMLE preparation, dental admissions testing, pre-veterinary education, online K-12 tutoring, academic competitions and other specialized education markets.

 Direct Reach said the expansion is intended to support organizations such as test-preparation providers, tutoring businesses, EdTech companies, educational publishers, training organizations, higher-education marketers and other businesses seeking more focused education-sector outreach.

 Growing Demand for More Specific Education Audience Segmentation Education marketing has become increasingly specialized, with organizations frequently needing to distinguish between audiences based on academic stage, examination interest, subject area, professional education pathway or educational service.

 Direct Reach's expanded education portfolio is structured around those distinctions.

 Available audience categories include:

 * Education industry professionals and organizations
* SAT-related audiences
* ACT-related audiences
* AP-related audiences
* PSAT/NMSQT-related student and parent audiences
* Online K-12 tutoring audiences
* Olympiad and academic-competition parent audiences
* MCAT-related audiences
* USMLE-related audiences
* DAT-related student audiences
* Pre-veterinary student audiences
* Health-specialties education professionals

 Rather than treating education as one broad market, Direct Reach organizes these audience segments so marketers can select data more closely aligned with the campaign they are planning.

 Supporting Test Preparation and Education Marketing Campaigns The expanded portfolio may be relevant to businesses marketing services such as academic tutoring, examination preparation, online learning, educational software, admissions support, curriculum resources and professional education programs.

 For example, organizations concentrating on undergraduate admissions preparation can explore Direct Reach's SAT, ACT and PSAT-related audience options.

 Companies serving professional and healthcare education markets can instead evaluate specialized audience categories connected with the MCAT, USMLE, DAT, pre-veterinary education and health-specialties instruction.

 Academic enrichment organizations can also explore segments related to Olympiad families and online K-12 tutoring.

 “Our goal is to help education-focused organizations build more precise outreach campaigns by giving them access to clearly segmented audience options across test preparation, tutoring, higher education and specialized education markets,” said Steven Johnson of Direct Reach.

 Education Audience Data for 2026-2027 Planning Direct Reach expects the expanded education portfolio to support marketing teams planning outreach during the 2026-2027 academic and enrollment cycle.

 Depending on the selected audience and available records, data fields may include information such as contact names, email addresses, geographic information and other audience-specific attributes.

 Availability and fields vary by audience segment, and organizations can request information about the specific targeting criteria and available fields before selecting a database.

 Direct Reach encourages customers to use audience data responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy, email-marketing and data-protection requirements.

 The company is an independent data provider. References to examinations, education programs or organizations are descriptive of audience interests or market segments and do not imply affiliation with or endorsement by the organizations that administer those programs or examinations.

 About Direct Reach Direct Reach provides targeted audience and business data solutions for organizations seeking to reach defined professional, consumer and education-market segments across the United States.

 Its education data portfolio includes audiences related to test preparation, tutoring, student recruitment, academic programs and specialized professional education.
