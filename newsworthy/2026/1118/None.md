# TNT Highlights Growing Creatine Popularity Among Women

Creatine, once associated primarily with male bodybuilders and competitive athletes, is attracting a growing audience of women and everyday fitness enthusiasts. German sports nutrition company TNT (True Nutrition Technology) says the trend reflects a broader shift in the sports nutrition market as consumers become more interested in ingredient quality, product transparency, and fitness-focused nutrition.

 A November 16 Forbes article, "Inside the Creatine Boom: How Brands Are Developing Products for Women," highlights the changing market. Citing July 2026 data from market research firm SPINS, Forbes reported that U.S. retail creatine sales increased 56% year over year.

 The article also referenced research involving 2,904 creatine users that found women were more likely than men to identify healthy aging as a reason for using creatine, while men were more likely to emphasize athletic performance.

 For TNT, whose portfolio includes Creatine Monohydrate Creapure®, the expanding market presents an opportunity to introduce more consumers to the importance of ingredient sourcing, manufacturing standards, and product quality.

 Creatine Moves Beyond Traditional Bodybuilding Creatine has long been a familiar ingredient in sports nutrition, particularly among strength athletes and bodybuilders.

 Today, its audience is expanding to include recreational gym members, women participating in resistance training, and active adults interested in incorporating sports nutrition into their fitness routines.

 The trend also reflects broader changes in consumer purchasing behavior. Products once marketed almost exclusively to competitive athletes are increasingly being introduced to mainstream audiences.

 "Sports nutrition is no longer limited to competitive athletes or bodybuilders," said Markus Himmelstoss, Managing Director of TNT. "We're seeing growing interest from consumers with a wide range of fitness goals, including women who are incorporating strength training into their routines. That creates an opportunity to educate more people about ingredient quality and what they should look for when choosing sports nutrition products."

 Why Ingredient Quality Matters As demand for creatine grows, consumers are encountering an expanding selection of brands, formulations, and delivery formats.

 TNT encourages consumers to look beyond marketing claims and consider several factors when evaluating creatine products:

 * Ingredient sourcing: Understanding where ingredients originate and how they are manufactured.
* Purity and consistency: Looking for clearly identified ingredients produced under established quality controls.
* Independent testing: Considering whether manufacturers provide credible information about ingredient testing and quality verification.
* Transparent labeling: Reviewing product composition and ingredient information.
* Established formulations: Recognizing that creatine monohydrate is the most extensively researched form of creatine.

 TNT's Creatine Monohydrate Creapure® features Creapure®, a German-manufactured creatine monohydrate ingredient produced under controlled manufacturing conditions with established purity standards.

 The ingredient reflects TNT's broader approach to sports nutrition, emphasizing carefully selected raw materials, manufacturing quality, and transparency.

 "With so many new products entering the market, consumers should understand that ingredient sourcing and manufacturing standards matter," Himmelstoss said. "At TNT, our focus has always been on quality and transparency. We believe those principles are important whether someone is an experienced athlete or purchasing a sports nutrition product for the first time."

 German Sports Nutrition for a Growing U.S. Market Founded in Germany in 2012, TNT has developed a portfolio of sports nutrition products serving consumers with a variety of training and nutritional goals.

 Its lineup includes Creatine Monohydrate Creapure®, Synapsensause Pre-Workout, Whey Protein, and CLEAR WHEY Protein.

 As TNT continues expanding its presence in the United States, the company sees opportunities to reach a broader audience of fitness enthusiasts interested in established ingredients, clear product information, and manufacturing quality.

 The growing popularity of creatine among women illustrates how the sports nutrition category is evolving beyond its traditional customer base.

 For TNT, the company's long-term strategy centers on introducing American consumers to its German approach to sports nutrition while building awareness around ingredient quality and transparency.
