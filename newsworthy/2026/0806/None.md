# Noble Studios Co-Founder Reveals the Missed Tree Lighting That Rewired His Leadership

Episode 167 of The Proven Entrepreneur, titled 'From Architect to Agency Leader: A 22 Year Lesson in Delegation and Trust,' hosted by Don Williams, features a candid conversation with Jarrod Lopiccolo, co-founder of Noble Studios. Published August 4, 2026, the episode centers on the invisible weight seasoned founders carry and the specific moment that forced Lopiccolo to stop being the designer in the chair and start being the leader of a creative digital performance marketing agency his team could actually run.

 Across the hour, Williams and Lopiccolo dig into a series of specific topic threads pulled straight from 22 years of building Noble Studios with his wife Susan:

 * Serving travel and tourism clients (destination marketing organizations, cities, counties, national parks) alongside B2B fintech, healthtech and manufacturing tech.
* The founder transformation from tradesperson (architect, designer) to culture-first business leader.
* The pandemic stress test on culture, client relationships and the partner-versus-vendor line on a client's balance sheet.
* Why forcing functions, including a three-week sabbatical taken 16 years ago, build trustworthy teams.

 The emotional center of the episode arrives when Lopiccolo recounts December 2008: an Autodesk deadline at 5 p.m., a Carson City tree lighting ceremony, and Susan walking out with the kids as his heart sank. He describes his team swiveling their chairs and telling him they had it covered.

 "That was the moment that I realized I have to let go of these skill sets that once served me to get to where I am. And I've got to develop new ones. I've got to be able to delegate." - Jarrod Lopiccolo Lopiccolo, trained as an architect, argues that seeing interdependent systems (timelines, materials, people, processes) is an entrepreneur's real superpower, and Williams connects the point to the roughly 38 percent of the world's billionaires who come from engineering backgrounds. The two also trade road-tested lessons on trust: Williams recounts a three-week trip to Thailand and a five-week trip to China that proved his team could run without him, while Lopiccolo frames the pandemic as the ultimate forcing function, when Noble Studios earned pole position with clients by acting as strategic partners rather than vendors. Williams also references his book Romancing Your Customer, tying exceptional customer experience back to how leaders treat their teams and families.

 About The Proven Entrepreneur Hosted by Don Williams, The Proven Entrepreneur delivers unscripted conversations with founders and operators who have built real businesses, grown real sales, and led real teams. Williams never pre-briefs his guests, mining their lived experience for practical lessons on culture, delegation, customer experience and leadership.
