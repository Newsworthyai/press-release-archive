# George Salinas Injury Lawyers Joins Univision Contigo Health Fair on San Antonio's South Side

SAN ANTONIO, TX, September 24, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- George Salinas Injury Lawyers, a San Antonio-based personal injury law firm with 20 years of service to South Texas families, will join more than 50 organizations at the Univision Contigo Health Fair on Saturday, Sept. 26. The firm is the only legal practice participating in the event, which brings free health screenings, resources, and community support to thousands of residents on San Antonio's South Side.  The fair runs from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m at Rancho El Chema Event Center, 19935 U.S. Highway 281, San Antonio, Texas 78221. Organizers expect more than 2,500 attendees. Univision Contigo designed the event for families who face the greatest barriers to care, including low-income and Spanish-speaking households across the South Side.  A 20-Year Commitment to San Antonio Families  For two decades, George Salinas Injury Lawyers has stood alongside injured individuals and their families — in the courtroom and in the community. The firm's participation in the Contigo Health Fair is a natural extension of that mission: being present where neighbors gather, speaking plainly, and treating every family with respect.   "At George Salinas Injury Lawyers, we believe serving our community extends far beyond the courtroom," said George Salinas, founder and CEO. "The Univision Contigo Health Fair gives us an opportunity to connect families with valuable health and community resources, and we are honored to stand alongside organizations dedicated to improving the well-being of our neighbors across San Antonio."  "Our team is proud to volunteer at this event and support an effort that empowers families through education and access to services," Salinas added. "Strengthening our community as a trusted resource is one of the most meaningful ways to give back."  What Families Will Find at the Booth  Employee volunteers will staff the firm's booth throughout the day, greeting attendees, speaking with parents and children, and offering family-friendly activities. Giveaways are designed with practical household use in mind: 2027 wall calendars for tracking appointments, magnets that keep the firm's contact information close at home, and additional items for attending families.  As the only law firm at the event, George Salinas Injury Lawyers will stand alongside medical providers, nonprofits, and neighborhood organizations — ensuring that attendees have access to trusted legal guidance in the same place they come for health services and community support.  A Lasting Investment in This Community  Saturday's booth represents one day in a longer commitment to San Antonio. For 20 years, the firm has supported schools, first responders, and neighborhood events across the city. The Contigo Health Fair brings that work face-to-face with thousands of local residents, many from underserved households, reinforcing the firm's role as a trusted legal resource in the same places families already turn for education, services, and a better quality of life.  George Salinas Injury Lawyers remains deeply rooted in San Antonio and is committed to serving families both inside and outside the courtroom throughout South Texas.
