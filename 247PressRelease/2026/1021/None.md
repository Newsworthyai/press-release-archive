# EZ on Coat at Three-Day DIY Event Featuring the Property Brothers, Mr. Kate, Chelsea and Cole DeBoer and Leading DIY Innovators

DALLAS, TX, October 21, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- EZ on Coat®, a family-owned American manufacturer of roof coating, sealing, repair and maintenance products, will showcase its growing line of DIY roof protection products at DIY CON 2026, November 6–8 at Fair Park in Dallas.  EZ on Coat staff will be meeting with homeowners, RV owners, contractors and DIY enthusiasts at the EZ on Coat booth #INV20 and introducing attendees to products designed to help protect, repair and extend the useful life of roofing surfaces.  DIY CON will be held Friday, November 6; Saturday, November 7; and Sunday, November 8, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. each day at Fair Park, 1001 Washington Street in Dallas.  The inaugural event is designed for DIYers of all skill levels and will feature hands-on workshops, live demonstrations, product exhibits and appearances by some of the most recognizable names in home improvement and design. Featured celebrity programming includes Mr. Kate on Friday; Drew and Jonathan Scott, best known as the Property Brothers, on Saturday; and HGTV's Chelsea and Cole DeBoer of "Down Home Fab" on Sunday.  "DIY CON is a perfect opportunity for us to meet people who want to take a more active role in protecting and maintaining their homes and RVs," said Matt Cole, President for EZ on Coat. "Many roof maintenance projects don't have to be overwhelming. We look forward to showing attendees products and solutions that can help them take on projects themselves while protecting one of their most important investments."  At the EZ on Coat booth, attendees will have an opportunity to learn more about the company's product line and how preventive roof maintenance can help address problems before they become more expensive repairs.  EZ on Coat products include solutions for residential shingle roofs, RV roofs, roof repairs and other coating and maintenance applications. The company's focus on easy-to-use products makes its solutions particularly relevant to the growing DIY community, as well as contractors interested in providing roof maintenance and coating services to their customers.  DIY CON's exhibit floor is designed to give attendees the opportunity to discover and test products, watch demonstrations and connect directly with brands serving the home improvement and DIY marketplace. The event will also feature workshops and educational experiences covering a range of skill levels.  "Consumers are increasingly looking for ways to maintain what they already own, save money and complete projects themselves," Cole added. "EZ on Coat is excited to be part of an event that brings together homeowners, makers, contractors, creators and some of the biggest personalities in the DIY industry."  DIY CON 2026 EVENT INFORMATION Dates: November 6–8, 2026 Hours: 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. daily Location: Fair Park, 1001 Washington St., Dallas, TX 75210 Event Information: www.diycon.com EZ on Coat Products: www.EZonCoat.com Additional DIY CON programming, ticket information and event updates can be found at www.diycon.com.  About EZ on Coat® EZ on Coat® is a multigenerational, family-owned American manufacturer of RV Roof Coating and Shingle Sealer Coating products. Made in the USA, the company's environmentally responsible coating solutions are designed to help RV owners, homeowners, contractors and DIY consumers protect and extend the useful life of roofing surfaces.  EZ on Coat remains committed to American manufacturing, product education, quality, customer service and providing practical solutions that can help property owners protect their investments.  For product information, demonstrations and purchasing information, visit www.EZonCoat.com.
