# Morgan's Jewelers Announces Highly Anticipated Annual Watch Event in Torrance, California

TORRANCE, CA, October 13, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Morgan's Jewelers, South Bay's largest and most trusted jeweler for 80 years, is proud to host its premier Annual Watch Event on Friday, October 16, and Saturday, October 17, 2026. Taking place at their iconic showroom located at 22200 Hawthorne Blvd., Torrance, California, this exclusive two-day showcase will feature an unprecedented gathering of fine horology.  Guests will discover the region's largest selection of both new and pre-owned luxury timepieces. The event highlights an extraordinary curation of prestigious brands, including Rolex, Breitling, Cartier, Audemars Piguet, Patek Philippe, and many other world-renowned watchmakers. Whether acquiring a rare addition to a personal collection or seeking a timeless heirloom, attendees will benefit from the guidance of Morgan's GIA-trained professionals.  Event doors will be open on Friday, October 16, from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Saturday, October 17, from 10:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Collectors, enthusiasts, and first-time buyers alike are invited to experience personalized service, exceptional craftsmanship, and the refined elegance that has defined Morgan's Jewelers for eight decades.  For more information regarding the event, contact Morgan's Jewelers, and visit or call the Torrance showroom directly at (310) 375-4471.  About Morgan's Jewelers  In 1946, Morgan Varon opened the doors of Morgan's Jewelers, and in the seven decades since, the business has thrived by offering a personalized and professional shopping experience that sets Morgan's Jewelers apart. With fine designer jewelry, cutting-edge design and repair facilities, and a staff of GIA-trained professionals, Morgan's Jewelers is the place to go for any Swiss watch or jewelry need. No matter the need, Morgan's Jewelers has the experience to surpass expectations. For information on the products and services offered by Morgan's Jewelers, please visit morgansjewelers.com or call the Torrance, California showroom directly at (310) 375-4471!
