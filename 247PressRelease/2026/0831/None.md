# US Doors Direct Corporation Expands Roll-Up Door Operations in Florida, Georgia, and California

ST. CLOUD, FL, August 31, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- US Doors Direct Corporation has expanded its roll-up and rolling steel door sales and installation operations across Florida, Georgia, and California as the company continues to broaden its geographic footprint.  The family-owned company, headquartered in St. Cloud, Florida, currently provides installation services throughout Florida and Georgia and supports sales operations in Florida, Georgia and California. Customers in other states can access products through available pickup options and nationwide delivery.  The expansion builds on more than 30 years of industry experience serving residential, commercial and industrial projects, including work involving contractors, builders, developers, property owners and storage facilities.  "As we expand into additional markets, our focus is on building the infrastructure and service capabilities needed to support customers in each region," said Alycia Barnard, Vice President of US Doors Direct Corporation. "We have spent more than three decades in this industry, and we want to carry that experience forward as the company grows."  US Doors Direct Corporation supplies roll-up and rolling steel doors for applications that include commercial buildings, storage facilities, garages, workshops and other structures. The company offers 12 standard door colors without an additional color charge.  The company's current operating footprint includes:  Installation services: Florida and Georgia Sales operations: Florida, Georgia and California Nationwide service: Shipping and delivery throughout the United States Pickup: Available for qualifying products and locations Product options: 12 standard colors Industry experience: More than 30 years  The expansion into multiple states reflects the company's effort to serve a broader mix of residential, commercial and construction-related customers while developing a larger national distribution presence.  US Doors Direct Corporation said it plans to continue building its sales, installation and delivery capabilities as demand develops in existing and new markets.  About US Doors Direct Corporation  US Doors Direct Corporation is a family-owned and operated roll-up and rolling steel door company headquartered in St. Cloud, Florida. The company serves residential, commercial and industrial customers and provides door sales in Florida, Georgia and California, installation services in Florida and Georgia, and pickup and delivery options for customers throughout the United States.
