# Law Bear Establishes Headquarters in Phoenix, Arizona

PHOENIX, AZ, August 04, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- In conjunction with the firm's recent Supreme Court approval under Arizona's Alternative Business Structure program, Law Bear Injury Lawyers announced the establishment of its headquarters in Phoenix, Arizona. Located in the city's Camelback Corridor, the headquarters serves as the operational center for the firm's statewide personal injury practice and reinforces its long-term commitment to serving injured individuals and families throughout Arizona.  The headquarters is located at 2375 E. Camelback Rd., Suite 635, Phoenix, AZ 85016. From this location, Law Bear coordinates legal operations, case management, client intake, and administrative functions while supporting clients across the state, with a particular focus on the greater Phoenix metropolitan area.  Law Bear's headquarters supports an 11-person core team responsible for the firm's daily operations. The team includes a managing attorney, a senior case manager, intake professionals, operations leadership, and administrative staff who work together to provide responsive client service and efficient case management. Two members of the firm's six-person intake department are based at the Phoenix headquarters, while additional leadership personnel work remotely to support firm operations across Arizona.  As president of Law Bear Injury Lawyers, Sean Schmitt oversees the firm's operational strategy, client service standards, and long-term growth initiatives. The headquarters serves as the central hub for the firm's legal operations while providing a permanent Arizona presence for clients seeking representation after serious motor vehicle accidents and other personal injury incidents.  Law Bear's primary practice areas include car accidents, truck accidents, and wrongful death claims, as well as a range of other personal injury matters arising from another person's negligence.  "Too many injured people in Arizona get treated like a file number by firms they'll never actually meet," said Sean Schmitt, president of Law Bear Injury Lawyers. "We built Law Bear to be the opposite: a local team you can reach, that answers when you call, and that stays with you from the first conversation forward."  Schmitt said establishing a permanent headquarters in Phoenix reflects the firm's commitment to building lasting relationships within the communities it serves. Rather than operating solely through remote systems or referral networks, Law Bear has invested in local infrastructure and personnel to support clients throughout every stage of their legal matters.  The headquarters also serves as the operational foundation for Law Bear's Alternative Business Structure model. Arizona remains one of only a handful of states that permits approved law firms to operate under this regulatory framework, which combines innovation in legal services with oversight by the Arizona Supreme Court.   The establishment of the firm's headquarters marks another milestone in Law Bear's continued growth as it expands its presence in Arizona's legal community while serving clients who have been injured in car and truck accidents, as well as other serious incidents, throughout the state.  About Law Bear Injury Lawyers  Law Bear Injury Lawyers is a Phoenix-based law firm focused on helping individuals and families after serious motor vehicle collisions and other injury-causing incidents. Operating under Arizona's Alternative Business Structure program, the firm combines legal representation with modern operational systems designed to promote transparency, accountability, and client service. From its headquarters in Phoenix, Law Bear serves clients throughout Arizona and is committed to helping injured individuals understand their legal rights and pursue fair compensation.
