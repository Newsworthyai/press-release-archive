# Multi-Platinum, Chart-Topping Producer Giuseppe D. Releases ULTRAFOCUS; First Single On The Other Side Featuring LOLA XO Arrives September 25

WEST NEW YORK, NJ, September 23, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Giuseppe D., the multi-platinum, chart-topping producer, composer and remixer whose career has crossed pop, dance and Latin music for more than three decades, has released his new album ULTRAFOCUS through M4DE Projects.  Built for clubs, headphones and the moments when resolve matters most, ULTRAFOCUS is an adrenaline-fueled journey through movement, resilience, self-discovery and the connection between music and purpose. Its peak-time tracks carry the physical force of the dancefloor, while its more reflective moments explore ambition, perseverance, love and loss.  The album brings Giuseppe D.'s history into direct contact with the present. After decades of producing, remixing and contributing to releases connected with artists including Michael Jackson, Celine Dion, Whitney Houston, Janet Jackson, Lady Gaga, Gloria Estefan, Luis Fonsi, *NSYNC and Elvis Crespo, he channels that experience into electronic music with big hooks, modern production and the instincts of someone who has spent a lifetime making records move people.  "ULTRAFOCUS is about locking in on what truly matters and continuing forward through everything life puts in front of you," says Giuseppe D. "Every track represents another part of that movement - from honoring the people we love to finding clarity, pushing through setbacks and losing ourselves in the energy of the dancefloor."  Leading the campaign is the album's first single, "On The Other Side" featuring LOLA XO, arriving September 25. The track is a deeply reflective anthem about leaving the familiar in search of purpose, truth and something greater. Dedicated to Giuseppe D.'s 84-year-old mother, Rubi, it gives the record an intimate emotional center, with LOLA XO bringing a distinctive featured performance to the song.  From there, "Rock It Tonight" ignites the album with rebellious, chant-driven club energy, while "Get There Anyway" turns setbacks and self-doubt into a resilient statement of forward motion. "What's On My Mind" explores music as both escape and identity through a hypnotic groove, and the stripped-down "Move Like A Machine" locks the dancefloor into a relentless mechanical pulse.  Giuseppe D. also revisits the club classic "Derb," preserving the raw force of its iconic riff while rebuilding it with a modern, high-impact edge. "Runnin' On High" follows with clarity, confidence and euphoric lift before "Back And Forth" closes the album in motion, capturing the restless rhythm of a life lived between cities, studios and dancefloors.  Across its eight tracks, ULTRAFOCUS connects the music that shaped Giuseppe D. with the sound driving him now. The history is present, but the focus is firmly forward.  ULTRAFOCUS Track Listing 1. On The Other Side (feat. LOLA XO) 2. Rock It Tonight 3. Get There Anyway 4. What's On My Mind 5. Move Like A Machine 6. Derb 7. Runnin' On High 8. Back And Forth  About Giuseppe D. Giuseppe D. is an award-winning producer, composer and remixer whose career includes multiple RIAA-certified releases, chart-topping hits and credits alongside some of the biggest names in popular music. Across more than three decades, his work has crossed pop, dance and Latin music. Today, he brings that experience into a new era of electronic music driven by big hooks, club energy and a forward-looking creative vision.  About LOLA XO LOLA XO made her recording debut with a cover of Cher's "A Woman's Story," followed by "Running Wild," both produced by Giuseppe D. Their creative collaboration continues with "On The Other Side," where LOLA XO's expressive vocal presence adds another emotional dimension to the reflective first single from ULTRAFOCUS.
