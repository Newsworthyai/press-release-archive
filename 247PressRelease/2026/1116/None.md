# Tutorela Reaches 500 Tutors in the U.S., Combining Human Teaching With Personalized AI Learning

NEW YORK, NY, November 16, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Tutorela, an online tutoring marketplace that combines human tutors with AI-powered learning tools, has reached a milestone of 500 registered tutors in the United States, marking a significant step in the platform's expansion into the American education market.  Launched in the U.S. in August 2026, Tutorela connects students with tutors across a wide range of academic subjects, test preparation programs, professional skills, and personal interests. The platform aims to make personalized education more accessible while extending learning beyond traditional tutoring sessions through integrated artificial intelligence.  500 Tutors Across a Wide Range of Subjects and Learning Levels  Tutorela's growing network of 500 tutors covers subjects including mathematics, English, physics, chemistry, biology, statistics, economics, history, science, Spanish, computer programming, reading, writing, and music.  The platform also supports students preparing for standardized tests such as the SAT and ACT, alongside other academic assessments and examinations.  Tutoring is available for learners at different stages of life, including:  Preschool and early learners: Foundational skills, early literacy, basic mathematics, and school readiness.  Elementary school students: Mathematics, English, reading, writing, science, and other core subjects.  Middle and high school students: Advanced mathematics, algebra, geometry, physics, chemistry, biology, and exam preparation.  College and university students: Statistics, economics, mathematics, science, programming, and other academic subjects.  Adult learners: Language learning, professional development, academic support, and continuing education.  Enrichment and hobbies: Music, languages, coding, and other personal interests.  Students can search for tutors based on subject, location, availability, and price, with options for both online and in-person lessons.  Unlike services that require students to purchase lesson packages or pay before contacting a tutor, Tutorela allows students to explore tutor profiles and contact tutors without an upfront commitment.  Connecting Human Tutors With AI-Powered Learning  Beyond helping students find tutors, Tutorela introduces a learning model designed to connect human instruction with personalized AI support between lessons.  After a tutoring session, teachers can tell Tutorela's AI which topics or skills a student is struggling with. The AI then creates a personalized practice plan designed to strengthen those areas before the next lesson.  Students complete interactive exercises with real-time feedback, explanations, and assistance when they encounter difficulties. They can also independently request additional exercises from the AI, allowing them to practice at their own pace.  As students work through exercises, the AI analyzes their mistakes, identifies areas requiring additional attention, and automatically generates further practice tailored to their needs.  The system also provides progress reports for both students and tutors. Teachers receive updates about students' performance and difficulties, helping them prepare more focused lessons and adjust their teaching accordingly.  This approach is designed to create continuity between tutoring sessions, with human tutors guiding the learning process while AI provides additional opportunities for practice, feedback, and progress monitoring.  Built on More Than a Decade of Tutoring Experience  Tutorela was created by the founders of Limud Naim, one of Israel's leading private tutoring marketplaces.  Established more than a decade ago, Limud Naim has facilitated over 500,000 private lessons and collected more than 50,000 student reviews, providing extensive experience in connecting students with tutors and understanding what contributes to successful learning relationships.  Tutorela brings this marketplace experience to the U.S. while expanding the traditional tutoring model through AI-powered learning technology.  "Reaching 500 tutors is an important milestone for Tutorela, but our vision goes beyond helping students find the right teacher," said Roee Naim, Co-Founder and CEO of Tutorela.  "We believe the most effective learning experience combines the expertise and personal connection of a human tutor with the availability and adaptability of AI. A tutor understands the student, identifies where they need help, and guides their progress. AI can then help the student practice those exact skills between lessons, recognize mistakes, and keep the tutor informed. Together, they create a more continuous and personalized learning experience."  Expanding Access to Personalized Education  As Tutorela continues expanding its tutor network across the United States, the company is focused on helping learners find suitable tutors across academic subjects, test preparation, enrichment activities, and lifelong learning.  By combining flexible tutor selection with AI-supported practice, Tutorela aims to make personalized learning more accessible, continuous, and responsive to each student's individual needs.  About Tutorela  Tutorela is a U.S.-focused tutoring marketplace connecting students of all ages with private tutors for online and in-person lessons. The platform offers tutoring across mathematics, English, science, test preparation, languages, programming, music, and many other subjects.  Tutorela combines human instruction with AI-powered personalized practice, interactive exercises, real-time feedback, and progress tracking between tutoring sessions.  Founded by the team behind Limud Naim, an established Israeli tutoring marketplace with over 500,000 lessons delivered, Tutorela brings years of tutoring marketplace experience to the U.S. education market.
