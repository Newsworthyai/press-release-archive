# Infosearch BPO Gears Up for the 2026–27 Sports Season with Expanded Sports Annotation Services

CHENNAI, INDIA, August 19, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Infosearch BPO has a standout team of over 230 professional sports data annotators, ready to meet the increasing demand for high-quality sports data annotation by sports technology companies, analytics platforms, broadcasters, and AI solution providers across the globe.  The company's expertise extends across a wide range of sports, including Soccer, American Football, Ice Hockey, and Basketball, and provides it with accurate, scalable, and high-volume sports data that fuels next-generation sports analytics applications.  AI is being used with growing frequency in sports to analyse player movements, formations, game events, and performance metrics. Precise image, video, and object annotation services offered by Infosearch BPO enable machine learning models to create actionable insights for coaches, teams, broadcasters, and sports tech companies.  A spokesperson for Infosearch BPO said, "The use of high-quality annotated data has never been more critical with AI reshaping the sports landscape. We have more than 230 experienced annotators and expertise in various sports to support clients with scalable, accurate, and timely annotation services during the 2026-27 sports season."  Infosearch BPO's annotation team is well-trained to process complex sports data for player tracking, ball tracking, pose estimation, event tagging, object detection and video annotation. Its commitment to quality enables the delivery of reliable and consistent training data to AI programs for player performance analysis, officiating technologies, player development, injury prevention, fan engagement, and broadcast improvement.  Infosearch BPO is dedicated to developing and investing in skilled resources, quality control processes, and delivery capabilities that will meet growing global demand for AI-powered solutions in sports. The sports annotation service for the company aims to enable the client to speed up AI development while ensuring the accuracy and consistency of the sports information generated for mission-critical applications.  Infosearch BPO is an AI training data, Data annotation, Business process outsourcing (BPO), Call Centre and Data management solutions provider. The company's solutions provide organisations with the best possible annotation services across various industries, such as sports analytics, autonomous vehicles, healthcare, retail, geospatial intelligence, and computer vision, enabling them to create smarter and more accurate AI systems.
