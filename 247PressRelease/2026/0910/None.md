# Prime Finish Painters Provides Professional Painting Services Across Monmouth County, New Jersey

KEYPORT, NJ, September 10, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Prime Finish Painters, a residential and commercial painting company serving Monmouth, Ocean and Middlesex Counties, continues to provide professional interior painting, exterior painting, cabinet refinishing, drywall repair and commercial painting services to homeowners and businesses throughout Central New Jersey.  Founded in 2009, Prime Finish Painters focuses on detailed surface preparation, professional application and long-lasting finishes. The company serves communities throughout Monmouth County including Red Bank, Rumson, Fair Haven, Middletown, Holmdel and Colts Neck, as well as surrounding areas.  Homeowners searching for a painting company in Monmouth County, NJ can work with Prime Finish Painters for both individual rooms and complete residential painting projects.  Interior Painting Services  Prime Finish Painters provides professional interior painting in Monmouth County for walls, ceilings, trim and other interior surfaces. Each project begins with proper preparation designed to create a smooth, consistent finish.  Interior painting services are available for individual rooms as well as larger projects involving kitchens, living rooms, bedrooms, hallways and entire homes.  Exterior House Painting  The company also provides exterior painting services throughout Monmouth County, NJ. Exterior painting projects focus on both appearance and protection, using coatings appropriate for exterior surfaces and New Jersey weather conditions.  Prime Finish Painters works with homeowners looking to refresh existing exterior colors, restore aging painted surfaces or update the overall appearance of their homes.  Cabinet Painting and Refinishing  Kitchen renovation does not always require replacing existing cabinetry. Prime Finish Painters offers professional cabinet painting and cabinet refinishing in Monmouth County for homeowners seeking to update kitchens while retaining their existing cabinets.  The refinishing process is designed to provide a smooth, durable finish while giving kitchens a significantly updated appearance.  Drywall Repair and Surface Preparation  Painting results depend heavily on the condition of the underlying surface. Prime Finish Painters provides drywall repair and surface preparation as part of its painting services, including repairs to cracks, holes and damaged surfaces prior to painting.  Commercial Painting  Prime Finish Painters also works with commercial clients throughout Monmouth, Ocean and Middlesex Counties. Commercial painting services are designed to provide professional finishes while minimizing disruption to normal business operations.  Owner Michael Caruso said the company's approach is centered around preparation and workmanship rather than simply applying another coat of paint.  Prime Finish Painters reports more than 15 years of experience and more than 2,400 completed projects. The company states that its painting work is completed by its own team rather than subcontracted crews.  Prime Finish Painters currently serves homeowners and businesses throughout Red Bank, Rumson, Fair Haven, Middletown, Holmdel, Colts Neck and surrounding New Jersey communities.  Customers interested in interior painting, exterior painting, cabinet refinishing, drywall repair or commercial painting can learn more or request an estimate at https://primefinishpainter.com/.
