# NEW LOVELY HEART COLLECTION CAPTURES YOUNG READERS IMAGINATIONS

MARYLAND, November 13, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Just in time for spring reading, author Beti Gashaw Seid has released her Lovely Heart collection, and early readers are already embracing the adventures of Blossom, Curiosity, ZigZag, and the reformed shadow-character Blacky. Since its publication, the collection has been charming children with its blend of everyday situations and extraordinary transformations.  The seven-story collection takes young readers on journeys both familiar and fantastic. In one tale, a simple trip to the playground becomes a rescue mission when a child needs help. In another, a princess's practice day is interrupted by a sudden storm. The collection also explores what happens when a new princess arrives at the castle gates, wielding powerful magic and mysterious intentions.  Seid has built a world where transformation is more than just magical powers; it's about growing, learning, and choosing to be better. The collection is now available on Amazon.   Book Details: • Title: Lovely Heart Series • Author: Beti Gashaw Seid • Publisher: Heritage Book Publishing • Publication Date: 14 October 2026 • Availability: Paperback, and eBook available through all major online retailers, including Amazon and via the Heritage Book Publishing website.
