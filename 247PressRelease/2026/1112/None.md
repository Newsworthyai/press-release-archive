# 30A Media and Bizarre Bunny Partner to Expand Advertising Production and Television Content Distribution

SANTA ROSA BEACH, FL, November 12, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- 30A Media (www.30a.media) and Bizarre Bunny (www.bizarrebunny.com) are partnering to connect incredible AI-assisted creative production with television advertising, content distribution, and streaming app and channel development.   The collaboration aims to help businesses reach audiences through professionally produced advertising while opening distribution opportunities for Bizarre Bunny-produced series and other original programming.  Following a chance connection through Boardy.ai, the two companies discovered complementary strengths and shared goals, laying the groundwork for the partnership.   The partnership brings together Bizarre Bunny's capabilities in commercial production, animation, and episodic storytelling with 30A Media's experience in broadcasting, streaming television, and content delivery. Together, the companies will pursue opportunities spanning advertising production and placement, series distribution, and branded television apps and channels.  The collaboration includes the upcoming launch of connected TV apps designed to showcase Bizarre Bunny-produced content and commercials. The apps will provide a dedicated viewing destination for original programming and creative work, allowing audiences and prospective clients to experience the company's productions on television screens. Launch timing and platform availability will be announced separately.  For advertisers, the goal is to connect the development of commercials with suitable television and streaming distribution opportunities. For original programming, the companies will explore ways to introduce Bizarre Bunny-produced series to additional audiences through dedicated apps, relevant channels, and media partners.  "Great content needs a clear path to its audience, whether that's a commercial for a business or an original television series," said Paul Vizard, CEO of 30A Media. "This partnership brings production and distribution closer together. The upcoming connected streaming TV apps will give viewers a dedicated place to discover Bizarre Bunny's work and give businesses a way to see what's possible."  "We see an opportunity to connect what we create with how audiences discover and watch it," said Justin Bizarre Bunny VP of Bizarre Bunny Licensing. "Working with 30A Media allows us to bring our advertising work and original series to broadcast audiences, while bringing client projects from an initial idea through production and into distribution."  The companies will initially focus on the app launch, joint advertising and programming opportunities, and production referrals. Individual campaigns, series, and distribution arrangements will be developed on a project-by-project basis.  About 30A Media 30A Media is a media production, broadcasting, and streaming television company serving businesses, content owners, and independent producers. Its services include video production and hosting, television advertising, content licensing and distribution, and the development of streaming apps and 24/7 television channels. Through its 30ATV network and relationships with third-party platforms, the company helps connect entertainment, lifestyle programming, interviews, and other content with audiences across connected television and digital media.  About Bizarre Bunny Bizarre Bunny is a creative production company developing advertising, animation, and original episodic programming through AI-assisted production and human creative expertise. Its capabilities combine writing, visual development, 3D animation, facial capture, lip synchronization, editing, and human performance to support commercial and entertainment projects. The company's work spans business advertising and commissioned storytelling, with an emphasis on flexible production workflows that help bring ambitious creative ideas to the screen.
