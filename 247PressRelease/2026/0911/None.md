# Suspected Drunk Driver Causes Deadly Six-Vehicle Crash on Southern State Parkway

NEW CITY, NY, September 11, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- A recent June evening turned tragic on the Southern State Parkway, where a suspected drunk driver set off a six-vehicle crash that killed two people and hurt several others. New York State Police say the collision happened around 10:15 p.m. near Exit 17S in Hempstead, on Long Island, and shut the highway down for hours. If a reckless driver caused a wreck like this that impacted you or your family, a New City, NY car accident lawyer can explain what compensation you're entitled to.   According to the preliminary investigation, a 36-year-old driver from Oceanside was allegedly drunk and speeding when she lost control of her Cadillac Escalade after sideswiping a BMW in the left lane. Her SUV crossed the center median and struck oncoming traffic, hitting a Toyota Highlander head-on. Donald Maxwell, 82, and Liscent B. Maxwell, 88, who were riding in the Highlander, were pronounced dead at the scene. Ten people and six vehicles were involved in the wreck, and at least one other person was left in critical condition.  The driver was hospitalized and now faces charges that include aggravated vehicular homicide, vehicular manslaughter, and driving while intoxicated. State police have asked anyone with video or information about the crash to come forward while the investigation continues. The two people who died were in their 80s, and their family was left reeling after the loss.
