# PRPowered Announces Intent to Acquire Global Nonprofit PR Services Organization

TRINITY, FL, July 30, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- PRPowered today announced that it is actively seeking to acquire a global nonprofit public relations services organization as part of its long-term strategy to strengthen communications capabilities for nonprofit, charitable, advocacy, and community-focused organizations around the world.  The proposed acquisition would enable PRPowered to expand its reach, enhance service offerings, and provide greater access to professional public relations, media relations, digital communications, AI and emerging technologies for organizations dedicated to social impact.  "Effective communications can be transformative for nonprofits working to address critical challenges in communities across the globe," said Tim Nissen, Executive Director of PRPowered. "We are exploring acquisition opportunities that align with our mission of helping organizations amplify their voices, engage stakeholders, and increase their impact. It's time for us to further expand strong domestic success, and enhance international reach."  PRPowered is specifically interested in organizations that have established regional networks, nonprofit communications expertise, and a demonstrated commitment to supporting social causes, humanitarian initiatives, education, health, and community development.  PRPowered welcomes confidential discussions with nonprofit organizations, boards of directors, advisors, and stakeholders interested in exploring strategic acquisition opportunities. Contact information available at PRPowered.com.  About PRPowered  An entity adapting to ever-adjusting needs, with ever-evolving technologies, PRPowered is an affordable PR services provider for nonprofits, focusing on public relations benefitting 501(c)(3) peer mission-based entities. Enhancing abilities to reach people and organizations with shared interests and objectives is the foundational cornerstone.   PRPowered's mission is strengthening nonprofit brand-impact awareness, donor-engaging fundraising and volunteer-activating encounters through the Power of PR, by utilizing customized, targeted media, digital communications and events, adapting to ever-adjusting needs, with ever-evolving technologies.
