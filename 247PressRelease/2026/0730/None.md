# 20 DM Injury Law Attorneys Selected for 2026 Super Lawyers and Rising Stars Lists Across Missouri, Kansas, and Oklahoma

KANSAS CITY, MO, July 30, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- DM Injury Law has announced that 20 of its attorneys have been selected for inclusion in the 2026 Super Lawyers and Rising Stars lists across Missouri, Kansas, and Oklahoma. The annual selections recognize lawyers across the region in personal injury practice areas.  About the Super Lawyers Selection Methodology  Super Lawyers, a Thomson Reuters rating service, lists lawyers from more than 70 practice areas based on peer recognition and professional achievement. The selection methodology uses a multiphase process that incorporates statewide peer nominations, independent research evaluations, and practice area peer reviews.  Under the program's criteria, no more than 5% of lawyers in a state or region are named to the Super Lawyers list, and no more than 2.5% are selected to the Rising Stars list, which is designated for attorneys who are 40 years old or younger, or in practice for 10 years or less.  Candidates are evaluated using 12 indicators. These criteria include verdicts and settlements, representative clients, transactions, experience, pro bono and community service, scholarly lectures and writings, honors and awards, educational background, bar or professional activity, and special licenses or certifications.  Scope of the Regional Selection  The selection includes attorneys from DM Injury Law's offices in Missouri, Kansas, and Oklahoma. Because the evaluation process incorporates both peer evaluations and research, inclusion on the annual list reflects professional activity and case results within the region. Prior Recognitions and Practice Context  Attorneys at DM Injury Law have received Super Lawyers and Rising Stars selections in previous years, including 2025, 2024, 2023, and earlier.   DM Injury Law represents individuals in personal injury matters, including motor vehicle collisions, truck accidents, workplace injuries, premises liability, and wrongful death claims. The firm handles cases on a contingency fee structure, where legal fees depend on obtaining a recovery. Consultations are provided without cost.  About DM Injury Law  DM Injury Law is a personal injury law firm serving clients across the Midwest. Founded in Kansas City, the firm has expanded to multiple offices throughout Missouri, Kansas, and Oklahoma. DM Injury Law handles a wide range of civil litigation, including motor vehicle collisions, workplace accidents, premises liability, and wrongful death claims. To date, the firm's attorneys have recovered over $1 billion in compensation for clients. DM Injury Law operates on a contingency fee basis and offers free legal consultations.
