# Mathison Manufacturing Highlights Supply Chain Risk From Multiple Supplier Handoffs

WAUKESHA, WI, September 17, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Mathison Manufacturing, Inc. is highlighting how supplier handoffs can affect complex manufacturing projects. The U.S.-based contract manufacturer supports tight-tolerance metal fabrication and complex electromechanical assemblies.  Its work also includes project management and communication across key production stages. This approach can help manufacturers address supply chain risk while keeping project needs clear.  To learn more about supplier handoffs and manufacturing costs, visit https://mathisonmfg.com/the-hidden-cost-of-too-many-suppliers-how-handoffs-create-risk-in-manufacturing-projects/.  Complex manufacturing projects often depend on several linked processes. When those processes move between many suppliers, each handoff can add another point of risk. Details may be missed, schedules can shift, and quality issues may take longer to trace. Like links in a chain, each production step can affect the strength of the full manufacturing supply chain.  The number of suppliers can also influence manufacturing costs in ways that are not always easy to see. A quoted part price may represent only one piece of the total project cost. Freight, added inspections, schedule changes, rework, and extra coordination can affect the final result. According to the company, careful project management can help teams identify and solve these issues across production stages.  Reducing supplier handoffs may also make communication more direct. A contract manufacturer that handles several related processes can maintain a clearer view of project requirements. This can be useful when tight tolerances, assembly needs, and delivery schedules must stay aligned. From a practical standpoint, fewer communication gaps can make complex projects easier to manage.  Mathison Manufacturing, Inc. combines metal fabrication with complex electromechanical assembly capabilities for contract manufacturing projects. Its focus on responsive communication and problem solving supports customers dealing with detailed production requirements.
