# Oresky Associates Represents Family of Construction Worker Killed in Flatbush Building Collapse

BRONX, NY, November 20, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Oresky & Associates, PLLC represents the family of a construction worker who tragically lost his life in the November 11 building collapse in Flatbush, Brooklyn. The firm extends its sincere condolences to all those who lost loved ones or were otherwise affected by the devastating incident.  For the family Oresky & Associates represents, the collapse has brought unimaginable loss. They are mourning the death of a husband, father, and loved one while seeking to understand how this tragedy occurred and whether it could have been prevented.  "Our immediate focus is supporting the family during an incredibly difficult time," said Jacob Oresky, founder of Oresky & Associates. "They deserve answers, accountability, and the opportunity to grieve without additional burdens while those answers are pursued."  The firm has begun investigating the circumstances surrounding the collapse, including site safety practices, supervision of the work, compliance with applicable safety regulations, and the decisions made leading up to the incident. The investigation will examine whether proper safeguards were in place and whether any parties may bear responsibility for the conditions that contributed to the collapse.  Construction remains one of the most dangerous industries in New York City. Workers routinely face serious risks, many of which can be reduced or prevented through proper planning, oversight, training, and adherence to established safety requirements. When those protections fail, workers and their families often suffer the consequences.  "This family deserves to know what happened and whether steps could have been taken to prevent this loss," Oresky added. "We intend to thoroughly investigate the facts and pursue accountability wherever the evidence leads."  The firm respectfully requests that the family's privacy be honored during this difficult time.  About Oresky & Associates, PLLC  Oresky & Associates, PLLC is a New York personal injury law firm representing individuals and families harmed by construction accidents, workplace incidents, motor vehicle collisions, premises liability accidents, and wrongful death. The firm is committed to helping injured New Yorkers and grieving families obtain answers, accountability, and the compensation they deserve.
