# Main Event Celebrates Grand Opening of Brea Location in California

BREA, CA, November 09, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Main Event, the ultimate destination for family fun and entertainment, will open its newest location in Brea, California, on Monday, November 16, with a special ribbon-cutting ceremony at 10:45 a.m. to celebrate the grand opening. Doors will open at 11 a.m., and the first 200 guests in line will score free laser tag for a year.   Located at 245 W Birch St, Brea, CA. Main Event Brea brings nonstop entertainment with exciting experiences, including 18 bowling lanes (with 4 VIP lanes), immersive laser tag, 2 billiards tables, 1 shuffleboard table, and a state-of-the-art arcade game hall. Guests can also enjoy a full-service restaurant and bar, with crafted favorites for your whole crew, from shareable apps to epic desserts like Bone-In or Boneless Wings, the Smashed Burger Slider Trio, Big Super Fun Sundae, and specialty drinks like the famous Margarita Flight and Spiked Strawberry Lemonade.   Guests can take advantage of weekly specials and deals, including:   - Monday Night Madness: Monday is the new Friday! All-you-can-play activities or games are available for $12.99 per person after 4 p.m. every Monday.  - Food & Fun Combo: Pick from crowd favorites like a classic double cheeseburger, crispy chicken sandwich, hand-breaded chicken tenders, flatbread pizza, or wings, and pair it with game play for the ultimate combo deal.  - Happy Hour: Enjoy special deals and pricing during Happy Hour windows:  -  Sunday, Tuesday, & Thursday: 3:00 p.m. – 6:00 p.m. and 9:00 p.m. – 12:00 a.m.  - Monday: 3:00 p.m. – 6:00 p.m. and 9:00 p.m. – 1:00 a.m.   "Opening our doors in Brea marks an exhilarating new chapter for our brand, and our entire team is thrilled to become an active part of this vibrant local community," said Mark Medina, General Manager of Main Event Brea. "We have built an incredible, high-energy space packed with nonstop activities, great food, and unforgettable experiences, and we can't wait to welcome everyone in to share the fun."   In addition to endless entertainment, Main Event makes birthday parties a breeze. Every party includes a dedicated host to keep things running smoothly, plus customizable packages with activities for all ages, available seven days a week.   Main Event Brea hours of operation will be Sunday from 10 a.m.–12 a.m., Monday from 11 a.m.–1 a.m., Tuesday through Thursday from 11 a.m.–12 a.m., Friday from 11 a.m.–2 a.m., and Saturday from 9 a.m.–2 a.m.   Main Event Brea will also feature fully equipped private meeting spaces with state-of-the-art A/V technology, full-service catering, and everything needed to host social gatherings or corporate events of any size. For larger occasions, full venue rentals are also available.   For more information or to book an event, visit www.mainevent.com/locations/california/brea.  About Main Event Entertainment  Founded in 1998 and headquartered in Coppell, Texas, Main Event Entertainment, Inc. operates over 66 locations in 23 states across the country. Offerings include state-of-the-art bowling, laser tag, hundreds of games, virtual reality and an in-center restaurant Family Kitchen, making it the perfect place for families to connect and make memories.   Main Event is owned and operated by Dave & Buster's Entertainment, Inc., which offers premier entertainment and dining experiences to guests now through two distinct brands: Dave & Buster's and Main Event. Dave & Buster's has 182 stores in 45 states, Puerto Rico and Canada, and offers guests the opportunity to "Eat, Drink, Play and Watch," all in one location.   Each store offers a full menu of entrées and appetizers, a complete selection of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages and an extensive assortment of entertainment attractions centered around playing games and watching live sports and other televised events.    For more information about each brand, visit www.daveandbusters.com and www.mainevent.com.
