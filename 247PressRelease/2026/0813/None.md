# 14 Countries, One Vision: Season 41 of the Outstanding Photo Award

ALBERTA, CANADA, August 13, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Since its inception in 2014, the Outstanding Maternity Award and Outstanding Photo Award have built a remarkable trajectory in the visual universe.  Over a decade has been dedicated to telling deep and unforgettable stories through photography. As the first worldwide association and maternity photography award to adopt this format focused on family photography, the Outstanding Photo Award has established itself as a global benchmark of prestige and inspiration.  Photography, by its very nature, carries the power to evoke emotion. However, when an image wins an award, the impact goes beyond poetry: it brings renewed enthusiasm to the photographer and the certainty of being on the right track.  While talented artists already recognize the strength of their own work, international recognition amplifies that reach. For potential clients reviewing a portfolio in search of excellence, the seal from the Outstanding Maternity Award | Outstanding Photo Award reaffirms that trust and highlights their artistic credibility.  A Global Connection Without Borders  The 41st Season of the award reinforces the power of photography to connect diverse worldviews and cultures. In this edition, professionals from 14 countries stood out:  Austria Belgium Brazil Bulgaria Germany Latvia Luxembourg Portugal Russia Slovakia Switzerland Turkey United States Uruguay  It is with immense pride that we share the winning images of this Season, celebrating the talent and dedication of artists who transform moments into works of art.  Season 41  The Eye of the Evaluator: Season 41 Judges  To select the best images from hundreds of international submissions, Season 41 featured the rigorous curation of two major names in photography:  Yvonne C. Krystovsky (Germany)  An internationally acclaimed and multi-award-winning photographer, Yvonne works near Stuttgart, Germany, specializing in maternity, newborn, family, and fine art portraiture.  With an academic background in Interior Design and Architecture, she developed a unique visual language marked by minimalism, emotional depth, and a timeless aesthetic. Her work focused on authentic human connections and the "less is more" philosophy has been featured in global outlets such as The Times and My Modern Met, culminating in winning top honors at the Black & White Minimalist Photography Prize.  A speaker, educator, and co-founder of the Luminar Elite Akademie and the Luminar Award, Yvonne looks for originality, striking visual composition, technical excellence, and the ability to evoke a genuine connection in submitted images.  Wellington Fugisse (Brazil)  With over 15 years of experience and a portfolio of more than a thousand covered events, Wellington is one of the most prestigious names in Brazilian and international photography. Known for his sensitivity and creativity, his work encompasses weddings, family sessions, and maternity shoots, always focused on capturing real emotions.  In addition to his work in the field, he plays a fundamental role in mentoring and training new talents through workshops and lectures. Wellington is an ambassador for major industry brands such as photography festivals, software companies, and luxury photo album manufacturers and holds hundreds of international awards.  The Outstanding Maternity Award | Outstanding Photo Award remains firm in its mission: to shine a light on life's most beautiful narratives, promoting artistic excellence and bringing the global photography community together with every new season.
