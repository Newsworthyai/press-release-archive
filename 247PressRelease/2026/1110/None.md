# Attorney Brian White Personal Injury Lawyers Named to the Inc. 5000 List of America's Fastest-Growing Private Companies

HOUSTON, TX, November 10, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Attorney Brian White Personal Injury Lawyers is proud to announce that the firm has been named to the Inc. 5000, Inc.'s annual list recognizing the fastest-growing private companies in the United States.  The recognition marks an important milestone for Attorney Brian White Personal Injury Lawyers and reflects the firm's continued growth while serving individuals and families throughout the Houston area.  The Inc. 5000 recognizes privately held, independent U.S. companies based on percentage revenue growth over a three-year period. Earning a place on the list puts Attorney Brian White Personal Injury Lawyers among a group of growing businesses from industries across the country.  Growth Built Around Client Service  Attorney Brian White Personal Injury Lawyers represents people who have been injured due to the negligence or wrongdoing of others. The Houston-based firm handles personal injury matters involving car accidents, commercial truck accidents, workplace and industrial accidents, catastrophic injuries, wrongful death, and other serious injury claims.  As the firm has grown, it has continued to expand its ability to serve clients throughout the greater Houston area while maintaining a client-focused approach to personal injury representation.  For founder, Brian White, the Inc. 5000 recognition is not simply a measure of business growth. It represents the continued development of a team and infrastructure capable of helping more injured Texans while maintaining the level of service and advocacy upon which the firm has built its reputation.  A Growing Houston Personal Injury Law Firm  Brian White has represented injured clients in Texas for more than two decades and is Board Certified in Personal Injury Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization.  The firm's inclusion on the Inc. 5000 adds to its growing list of professional and community recognition while underscoring the expansion of its personal injury practice in the Houston market.  Despite that growth, the firm says its focus remains the same: providing injured clients with accessible, personalized legal representation and pursuing accountability from insurance companies and other responsible parties.  About Attorney Brian White Personal Injury Lawyers  Attorney Brian White Personal Injury Lawyers is a Houston-based personal injury law firm representing individuals and families who have been injured because of negligence or wrongdoing. Led by founding attorney Brian White, the firm handles cases involving car accidents, truck accidents, workplace and industrial accidents, catastrophic injuries, wrongful death, and other personal injury matters.  Brian White is Board Certified in Personal Injury Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization and has practiced law in Texas since 2002.
