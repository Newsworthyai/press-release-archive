# Calm Water Bath Honors Army Veteran Joe Casey with a Safer, More Accessible Bathroom This Veterans Day

CHERRY HILL, NJ, November 10, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- This Veterans Day, Calm Water Bath is honoring Army veteran Joe Casey through Baths for the Brave, the company's way of giving back to the veterans and service members of South Jersey. Calm Water Bath will help create a safer, more accessible bathroom that supports Joe's comfort and independence at home.  Joe served in the Army, with duties that included weapons, security and guard work. In the years that followed, he faced ongoing medical challenges, repeated hospital stays and, eventually, the loss of his leg. Today he lives with a prosthetic leg and remains actively involved in his community.  Faith is at the center of Joe's life. He met his wife, Cheryl, at Bethel Church, and his Christian faith inspires his work helping others. Through mission outreach in Philadelphia's Kensington neighborhood, Joe helps provide food and clothing to people experiencing homelessness, gathering and distributing more than 500 bags of clothing and food. For Joe, the chance to meet people, share his faith and help them feel supported is what makes this work so meaningful.  "Through Baths for the Brave, we are honored to recognize Joe's military service and the care he continues to show his neighbors today," said Briana Hetherington, owner of Calm Water Bath. "We look forward to helping create a safer, more accessible bathroom that supports his comfort and independence at home."  Baths for the Brave is a nomination-based program. Through community nominations, Calm Water Bath helps create a bathroom built around dignity, safety and ease for a veteran or service member who has given so much. Learn more at calmwaterbath.com/baths-for-the-brave.  About Calm Water Bath: Calm Water Bath is a women-owned, family-run bathroom remodeling company based in Cherry Hill, New Jersey, founded and led by Briana Hetherington. The company specializes in tub-to-shower conversions, walk-in showers, bathtub replacements and bathroom safety upgrades, and most projects are completed in as little as a single day. Calm Water Bath serves Atlantic, Burlington, Camden, Cape May, Cumberland, Gloucester and Salem counties and is a licensed New Jersey Home Improvement Contractor (#13VH14093900).
