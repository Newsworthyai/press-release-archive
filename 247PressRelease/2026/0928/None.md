# Tijuana Flats Brings Back the Cheeseburger Tacos Fans and Franchisees Have Been Demanding

MAITLAND, FL, September 28, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- For nearly a decade, the most requested return product by customers, franchisees, and Flatheads, is the Cheeseburger Taco. On Sept. 28, Tijuana Flats is answering the call, and making it even better by adding two new ways to get your cheeseburger fix (no bun needed).   "Our flatheads and our franchisees never stopped asking for Cheeseburger Tacos and as we move forward on our Bigger.Better.Bolder brand journey, we knew we had to listen to them and bring it back," said James O'Reilly, CEO of Latitude Food Group, parent company of Tijuana Flats. "When we heard how high the demand was, we realized we had something special. So now we have brought the Cheeseburger Tacos back, and we created two new ways to experience that cheeseburger flavor."  All three Cheeseburger items stack seasoned ground beef, queso, lettuce, tomatoes, onions, pickles, ketchup and mustard, without the bun this time:  Cheeseburger Tacos ($10.99): Two grilled flour tortillas served with fries. Cheeseburger Burrito ($11.99): Wrapped in a warm flour tortilla with seasoned fries inside, toasted until golden and served with fries on the side. Cheeseburger Loaded Fries ($9.99): Crispy fries loaded with all the cheeseburger goodness.  The cheeseburger lineup launches Sept. 28 at all Tijuana Flats locations across Florida, Alabama, North Carolina and Kentucky. Order dine in, carryout, or online at www.tijuanaflats.com.  Loyalty is always rewarded and celebrated at Tijuana Flats, and those who are part of the family are dubbed Flatheads. Flatheads earn one point for every dollar spent, which can be redeemed for exclusive rewards and deals, and they also gain access to bonus point opportunities and instant rewards just for signing up.  About Tijuana Flats  A fast-casual Mexican concept that's part of the newly formed Latitude Food Group, Tijuana Flats was founded in 1995 in Winter Park, Florida, and operates nearly 100 company-owned and franchised restaurants across Florida, Alabama, North Carolina and Kentucky. With bold flavors and colorful experiences piled high with big-hearted, over-the-top service, Tijuana Flats boasts a menu consisting of fresh, made-to-order tacos, burritos and other Mexican appetizers, entrées and desserts.
