# Friendly Recovery Center Releases New Educational Resource Exploring PTSD Nightmares and the Role of Intensive Outpatient Treatment

TUSTIN, CA, November 27, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Friendly Recovery Center, an outpatient mental health treatment provider serving Orange County, California, announces the publication of a new educational resource examining the connection between post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and recurring nightmares, including the potential role of intensive outpatient treatment in addressing trauma-related symptoms.  The publication is part of Friendly Recovery Center's ongoing efforts to increase mental health awareness and provide individuals and families with accessible information about psychological conditions, treatment options and professional support.  While nightmares are often associated with PTSD, their impact can extend well beyond nighttime discomfort. Persistent sleep disturbances can affect emotional well-being, concentration, interpersonal relationships and an individual's ability to manage everyday responsibilities.  The newly published resource explores how traumatic experiences can influence the body's response to perceived threats, even during sleep. It also addresses the relationship between sleep disruption and other symptoms of PTSD, helping readers better understand why treatment may need to address several interconnected concerns.  Another key focus of the resource is the importance of recognizing when additional professional support may be appropriate. For individuals experiencing persistent trauma-related symptoms that interfere with daily functioning, structured outpatient care may offer an alternative to traditional weekly therapy or residential treatment, depending on their clinical needs.  Through its Orange County Intensive Outpatient Program, Friendly Recovery Center provides structured mental health support that allows participants to receive treatment while continuing to live at home. Individualized treatment planning may incorporate therapeutic approaches, coping skills development and strategies for managing emotional distress.  The resource also emphasizes that treatment recommendations should consider each individual's circumstances rather than focusing exclusively on a single symptom. Professional assessment can help determine an appropriate level of care based on the severity of symptoms, personal history and overall treatment needs.  By expanding its educational resources, Friendly Recovery Center aims to help individuals and families develop a better understanding of trauma-related mental health challenges and make more informed decisions when exploring professional care.  About Friendly Recovery Center  Friendly Recovery Center is a mental health treatment provider located in Tustin, California, serving individuals throughout Orange County and surrounding communities. The center offers individualized, trauma-informed mental health services through outpatient programs, intensive outpatient programs (IOP) and partial hospitalization programs (PHP). Its approach incorporates evidence-based therapeutic practices and personalized treatment planning to support emotional well-being and long-term mental health management.
