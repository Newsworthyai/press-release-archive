# From China's Western Desert to Latin America: Xuemo Brings Chinese Literature to the 2026 Guadalajara International Book Fair

NEW YORK, NY, November 27, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- From the vast deserts of western China to the literary landscape of Latin America, Chinese author Xuemo is bringing stories rooted in the lives and experiences of western China to readers across the world.  From November 26 to 30, Xuemo will embark on a new literary exchange tour in Mexico, participating in literary presentations, author dialogues, media interviews, university events, and programs associated with the 2026 Guadalajara International Book Fair (FIL).  A highlight of the visit is the presentation of three Spanish-language editions of his works: Desert Rites, Desert Hunters, and Selected Stories by Xuemo. Translated by renowned Spanish-language Chinese literature scholars Liljana Arsovska and Pablo Rodríguez Durán, the books are published by Ruxue International Media Inc., Xuemo's works have also been translated into English, German, Hindi, Bengali, and other languages, reaching international readers through publishers in different countries.  Rooted deeply in the landscapes, communities, historical memories, and everyday lives of western China, Xuemo's literary work explores universal human themes, including love, suffering, freedom, dignity, human nature, and spiritual growth. His writing combines a distinctive regional perspective with questions that transcend geographical and linguistic boundaries.  For Xuemo, international literary exchange means more than translating and publishing books. It is an extension of a writer's responsibility. Through his writing, he gives voice to the land and its people; through travel and face-to-face engagement, he actively connects with readers, writers, scholars, and younger generations around the world.  On November 26, from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m., Xuemo will attend a literary presentation at the Rosario Castellanos Auditorium of the National Autonomous University of Mexico's (UNAM) School of Languages (ENALLT), joining translator Liljana Arsovska for a discussion on literary creation and international publishing. He will also be interviewed by journalist Carolina López Hidalgo.  On November 28, from noon to 1:00 p.m., Xuemo will meet Mexican writer Edgardo Bermejo at Casa Marie José y Octavio Paz in Mexico City for a literary dialogue hosted and interpreted by Arsovska and organized by Leticia Luna.  On November 30, Xuemo will become the first Chinese author invited to give a speech and participate in the 2026 Guadalajara International Book Fair's program. From 11:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., he will speak with students at the Juana C. de Tlajomulco campus in the El Salto region, sharing his literary journey and reflections on life.  Later that day, from 6:30 to 7:20 p.m., he will join the FIL international literary session, "Xuemo: A Journey Through Stories—From the Author's Imagination to the Reader's Eyes," alongside Liljana Arsovska and Emiliano Becerril. The discussion will explore the translation, publication, and international circulation of Chinese literature in Latin America.  From academic exchanges and literary conversations to school presentations and international publishing partnerships, Xuemo's Mexico visit demonstrates how a writer can build meaningful connections across borders through literature and direct engagement.  For Xuemo, a writer's responsibility extends beyond creating books. It also means bringing literature into society, reaching readers, encouraging reflection, and fostering mutual understanding. The value of literature lies not only in the written word, but also in the dialogue, connections, and contributions it creates.  From the deserts of western China to Latin America, Xuemo is building bridges through literature and action, bringing stories from one part of the world into a broader global conversation.
