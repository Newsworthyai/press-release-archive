# THE SLAM CREW ANNOUNCES THREE-YEAR NORTH AMERICAN ROAD SHOW

HOUSTON, TX, November 27, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- The Slam Crew, a premier basketball entertainment team based in Houston, Texas, specializing in school assemblies, educational programming, and live basketball entertainment, today announces an ambitious three-year North American touring initiative.  Over the next three years, The Slam Crew will bring its high-energy basketball entertainment experience to 39 U.S. states, Canada, and Mexico, delivering unforgettable performances for schools, families, communities, and basketball fans across the continent.  The tour will showcase The Slam Crew's signature combination of freestyle basketball, high-flying dunks, crowd interaction, athletic entertainment, and youth engagement. Through its dynamic performances, the organization aims to inspire audiences, promote positive messages, and create memorable experiences wherever the team travels.  This expansion marks a significant milestone for The Slam Crew as the organization continues to evolve from a regional basketball entertainment team into a national and international touring brand.  THREE YEARS ARE SCHEDULED IN  - 39 U.S. STATES - CANADA - MEXICO  From school assemblies and educational programs to community events, festivals, basketball camps, and special performances, The Slam Crew is committed to bringing the excitement of basketball directly to communities throughout North America.  With a focus on entertainment, education, and audience engagement, The Slam Crew continues to expand its reach while delivering the high-energy performances that have become the foundation of its brand.  As the organization prepares for this next chapter, The Slam Crew invites schools, event organizers, community leaders, and entertainment partners across North America to be part of the journey.  Energy. Excitement. Elevation.  ABOUT THE SLAM CREW  The Slam Crew is a premier basketball entertainment team based in Houston, Texas, specializing in school assemblies, educational programming, and live basketball entertainment shows. Through freestyle basketball tricks, high-flying dunks, interactive performances, and youth-focused programming, The Slam Crew delivers engaging experiences designed to entertain, inspire, and connect with audiences of all ages.  For booking inquiries, event information, and upcoming tour announcements, visit TheSlamCrew.com
