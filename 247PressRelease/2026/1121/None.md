# At 90, Montana Author Terry J. Atzen Publishes His First Novel

BIGFORK, MT, November 21, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Most people don't begin a new chapter at 90. Terry J. Atzen just did.  After writing practical books on investing, the Montana author has published his first novel, Midas' Touch: On the Gridiron. On Wall Street. And in Politics. Inspired by Atzen's own experiences, the story follows Terrance Jon "TJ" Nilsen from a tornado-struck Iowa farm to Stanford football, Navy service aboard the USS Saint Paul, a career at Merrill Lynch, and a new life in Montana's ranching and political circles.  The settings change, but the question at the heart of the novel remains: What is success worth when the things that matter most cannot be bought?  Atzen previously wrote K.I.S.S. Keep Investing Shamelessly Simple and De-Mystify Investing: Master the Teeter Totter Principle, drawing on his work as a financial coach and founder of The Investment Compass. With Midas' Touch, he shifts from explaining how people build wealth to examining what the pursuit of it can demand.  "I spent decades writing about how to build and protect wealth," Atzen said. "At ninety I finally wrote about what that pursuit costs a man—and what it leaves him. The most interesting chapter starts when most people think the story is over."  TJ's journey brings together ambition, loyalty, love, and responsibility. His relationship with Dr. Sarah Almquist offers a personal counterweight to the professional goals that repeatedly pull him in new directions. As the story moves to Montana, TJ must consider what he has gained—and what may have been left behind.  For Atzen, publishing a first novel at 90 is more than a milestone. It is a reminder that a new creative chapter can begin at any age.  Midas' Touch is available on Amazon: https://a.co/d/0dSHDQZS  About the Author Terry J. Atzen lives in Montana and is the author of K.I.S.S. Keep Investing Shamelessly Simple and De-Mystify Investing: Master the Teeter Totter Principle. He is also the founder of The Investment Compass. Midas' Touch is his first novel, blending a lifetime of experience with fictional storytelling to explore ambition, relationships, and the cost of success.
