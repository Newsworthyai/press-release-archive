# Sabbeth Law Launches Sober Holiday Rides Program for Halloween

BURLINGTON, VT, October 07, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- In an effort to promote safer roads during Halloween celebrations, Sabbeth Law is offering free ride reimbursement through its Sober Holiday Rides program on October 31, 2026.  The initiative encourages individuals celebrating the holiday to choose a taxi or rideshare instead of driving after drinking. Eligible participants can receive reimbursement of up to $50 (including a 10% tip) for a one-way ride home.  Halloween night brings a dangerous combination to the roads: costume parties and drinking on one side, and children and families walking door to door after dark on the other. This year, Halloween falls on a Saturday, meaning more celebrations and more people out late. As a law firm serving Vermont and New Hampshire, Sabbeth Law has seen firsthand the consequences of preventable drunk driving crashes.  "We want people to celebrate responsibly and get home safely," said Michael J. Sabbeth, Esquire. "If covering the cost of a ride removes one barrier to making a safe decision, it's worth it."  Program Details  - Eligible Ride Date: October 31, 2026 (rides that begin the night of October 31 and end after midnight are eligible) - Submission Window: November 1–November 8, 2026 - Reimbursement: Up to $50, including a 10% tip - Location: Vermont and New Hampshire  Eligibility Requirements  - Rider must be 21 or older - Ride must occur within Vermont or New Hampshire - One-way ride only - Rides taken on other days of Halloween weekend do not qualify - Receipt and valid identification must be submitted within 7 days  Participants can find full program details and submission instructions at: https://sabbethlaw.com/about/community/sober-holiday-rides-initiative/  A Commitment to Community Safety  Sober Holiday Rides is part of Sabbeth Law's broader commitment to injury prevention and community safety. The program is offered during select high-risk holidays throughout the year to help reduce impaired driving incidents across the region.  Community members are encouraged to plan ahead, save the program link before heading out on Halloween, and share it with friends.  "Getting home safely should never be an afterthought," Attorney Sabbeth added.  About Sabbeth Law  Sabbeth Law is a personal injury law firm headquartered in Burlington, Vermont, representing individuals and families throughout Vermont and New Hampshire. Led by attorney Michael J. Sabbeth, the firm focuses on serious injury cases, including car accidents, trucking accidents, catastrophic injuries, and wrongful death.
