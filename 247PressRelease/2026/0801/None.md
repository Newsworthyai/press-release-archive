# Custify adds integrations with Pylon, Maxio, Groove for better customer visibility

BUCHAREST, ROMANIA, August 01, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Custify, a top customer success platform (CSP) for SaaS, today announced four new native integrations with Pylon, Maxio, Groove and Granola. The integrations bring support tickets, billing data, revenue signals and AI meeting notes directly into the Customer 360 Profile, giving CSMs a fuller picture of account health.  Custify, recognized as a leader in customer success software by G2, said the integrations help CSMs catch support and billing risk earlier, before it turns into churn.  "Support and billing signals are some of the earliest warnings a CS team can get," said Philipp Wolf, CEO of Custify. "With Pylon, Maxio and Groove, that signal now shows up precisely where CSMs are already working, as part of customer sentiment and health scores."  The new Custify integrations allow SaaS companies to:  1. Track B2B support tickets from Slack, email and in-app channels  The new Pylon integration imports support tickets into the Customer 360 Profile, so CSMs can review ticket volume and status next to health scores and renewal data.  2. Sync subscription, billing and revenue data automatically  Through the Maxio integration, CSMs can track MRR, ARR, invoices and renewal dates, and trigger playbooks on billing events such as payment failures or downgrades.  3. Import email support tickets and monitor ticket volume  The Groove integration imports tickets and contacts, rolling ticket counts up to the company level so CSMs can spot accounts with rising support activity.  Custify also added a Granola integration, which enriches existing calendar meetings with AI-generated summaries and transcripts inside the Customer 360 Profile.  More information available at custify.com.  Custify is a customer success software company serving B2B SaaS companies. Its CSP helps teams centralize customer information, automate workflows, monitor health scores and improve customer retention and expansion. Custify is headquartered in Bucharest, Romania.
