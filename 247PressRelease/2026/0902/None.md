# Jones &amp; Swanson Welcomes Ella J. Mitchell as First Female Attorney in Firm History

MARIETTA, GA, September 02, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Jones & Swanson, a Marietta-based personal injury law firm with more than 50 years of combined legal experience serving Georgia communities, is proud to announce the addition of Ella J. Mitchell to its legal team as the firm's first female attorney in its history.  The addition represents an exciting new chapter for Jones & Swanson as the firm continues to grow while remaining committed to the values that have guided its work for decades: personalized client service, experienced advocacy, and a relentless commitment to helping injured people and their families.  "We are incredibly excited to welcome Ella to Jones & Swanson," said . "Having the first female attorney join our firm is an important milestone in the history of Jones & Swanson, but what makes this moment even more exciting is the person Ella is and the perspective she brings to our team. Having served as an insurance defense attorney, she has unique experience of what it's like on the other side of our cases."   Ella Mitchell: A Marietta Native With a Unique Perspective  Mitchell was born and raised in Marietta and has remained closely connected to the community throughout her life. She attended Mount Paran Christian School before earning two bachelor's degrees from the University of Georgia in Political Science and International Affairs. She went on to graduate cum laude from the University of Georgia School of Law in 2025.  During law school, Mitchell served on the editorial board of the Georgia Journal of International and Comparative Law, served as Treasurer of the Georgia Student Trial Lawyers Association, and participated in the Criminal Defense Practicum.  Her professional experience also gives her a particularly valuable perspective when representing people injured through the negligence of others. Before joining the plaintiff's side of personal injury law, Mitchell practiced insurance defense, giving her firsthand experience with how insurance companies and defendants evaluate and defend injury claims.  That experience now informs her approach to representing injured individuals and families.  "Personal injury law is about more than the legal process," Mitchell said. "It is about the people behind each case. I value getting to know my clients, understanding what they have been through, and helping them navigate what can be an overwhelming process."  Mitchell says one of the most rewarding aspects of her career is seeing a client achieve a positive result and knowing that she played a role in providing relief, closure, or peace of mind during a difficult period.  A New Generation of Leadership  Mitchell's addition comes as Jones & Swanson continues to build on its longstanding presence in the Georgia legal community. The firm has earned more than 500 Google reviews with a 5.0 rating and has reported millions of dollars recovered on behalf of clients, including significant results involving automobile accidents, motorcycle accidents, wrongful death, trucking accidents, and other serious injury claims.  For the firm, Mitchell's arrival is about more than adding another lawyer. It represents an opportunity to bring a new voice and perspective to a team that has spent decades advocating for people during some of the most difficult moments of their lives.  As a lifelong Marietta resident, Mitchell says she is grateful for the opportunity to serve the same community that helped shape her. "I am grateful to live and work in a community I have called home for so many years, and I am honored to have the opportunity to serve the people who make it such a special place," Mitchell said.  A Historic Addition to a Firm Rooted in the Community  Jones & Swanson has built its reputation by representing individuals and families throughout Georgia following serious accidents and injuries. The firm handles a wide range of personal injury matters, including car and truck accidents, motorcycle accidents, catastrophic injuries, pedestrian accidents, slip and fall cases, dog bites, wrongful death, and other injury claims.  The firm emphasizes individualized representation, taking the time to understand each client's circumstances rather than treating clients like case numbers. With offices in Marietta and Cartersville, the firm serves clients throughout the surrounding Georgia communities, including Kennesaw, Acworth, Woodstock, Mableton, Sandy Springs, Dallas, and Roswell.
