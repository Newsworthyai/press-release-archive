# Life Giving Store Introduces Pure USA Micellar Casein, a Single-Ingredient Protein Powder

TALLAHASSEE, FL, September 22, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Life Giving Store is adding Pure USA Micellar Casein Protein Powder to its single-ingredient lineup, giving customers a clean dairy protein option with no extra ingredients.  The product contains one ingredient: micellar casein (milk protein isolate). It is not calcium caseinate. Each 25-gram serving provides approximately 21.5 grams of protein and 482 mg of calcium, with 0.3 grams of total fat and 0.2 grams of total carbohydrate. The 680-gram (1.5-pound) container provides about 27 servings. Contains milk.  The micellar casein is sourced from USA dairy and manufactured in the United States. It is listed as Non-GMO, gluten-free, Kosher Dairy, and Halal certified. The powder contains no lecithin, soy, gums, anti-caking agents, or fillers.  Micellar casein is a slow-digesting milk protein. Because this formula has no added lecithin or gums, Life Giving Store recommends mixing one serving with 8–12 ounces of water, milk, or another beverage and shaking or blending thoroughly. It can also be used in overnight oats, yogurt, pancakes, smoothies, and similar recipes.
