# Faith Meant to Be Lived: Robert Canton's Newly Published Fundamentals of Power-Filled Living Brings God's Word Into Daily Practice

LOS ANGELES, CA, September 26, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Author and Catholic evangelist Robert "Bob" Canton has released Fundamentals of Power-Filled Living: How to Live a Power-Filled Life Through Daily Application of God's Word, a faith-centered guide that brings Scripture, Catholic teaching, prayer, ministry experience, and practical spiritual instruction together for readers seeking a more active daily relationship with God.   Built around three major areas Christian growth, spiritual warfare, and healing. The book moves from foundational questions of faith into subjects such as the Holy Spirit, trust, forgiveness, intercession, spiritual protection, praise and worship, divinity, and service. Its structure reflects Bob's central concern: that belief should shape the way Christians respond to hardship, temptation, uncertainty, and the responsibilities of everyday life.   Bob's years in the Catholic Charismatic Renewal Movement, including leading prayer meetings, giving spiritual talks, preaching as a Catholic evangelist, and conducting healing and deliverance services in the United States and abroad. Those experiences are paired with Scripture and teachings from the Catechism of the Catholic Church, giving the book both a devotional and instructional character.   Fundamentals of Power-Filled Living emphasizes practices readers can carry into daily life. Chapters explore walking by faith, trusting God, surrendering weakness, surviving trials, growing as a Christian, forgiving others, interceding in prayer, recognizing spiritual conflict, and approaching healing through prayer. Rather than treating spiritual life as an abstract subject, Bob repeatedly connects faith with choices, relationships, service, perseverance, and prayer.   Readers can also explore Bob's broader body of work through his author website, which features A Winnable War, focused on spiritual warfare and deliverance; Miracles Never Ending, centered on healing, prayer, and testimony of Awesome Power from On High, which examines the gifts of the Holy Spirit and their role in Christian service. Together, these titles reflect recurring themes in Bob's writing, including spiritual strength and life in the Holy Spirit.  The new release arrives as an invitation for readers at different stages of Christian faith to return to the fundamentals of prayer, Scripture, trust, service, and spiritual growth. The  Fundamentals of Power-Filled Living is available through Amazon and Barnes & Noble.
