# Systemic Silence Exposed: FRM² Demands Crisis-Care Legislative Action

KANSAS CITY, KS, September 14, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Families Rights Matter² (FRM²), founded by Leon Shelmire Jr., has published a new article examining how postpartum psychosis exposes critical, dangerous communication gaps in America's crisis-care system.  The piece, "Mind, Science, and Systemic Silence," highlights how rigid privacy laws lock families out during severe psychiatric emergencies—preventing them from alerting providers before it is too late. Drawing on neuroscience, policy research, and real-world cases, the article outlines why FRM² is advocating for a national crisis-time communication standard to give families a vital legal voice. FRM² advocates for reforms that ensure families are not silenced when critical safety information could prevent harm.
