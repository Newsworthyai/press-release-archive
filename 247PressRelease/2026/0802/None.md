# THE SPH establishes Global Safe Zone Network during the auspicious Guru Purnima Celebrations!

LOS ANGELES, CA, August 02, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Proclamation Delivered on Guru Purnima, July 29, 2026  On Guru Purnima, The Supreme Pontiff of Hinduism Bhagavan Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam issued an Executive Proclamation establishing Abhayam Sarvabhūtebhyoh — a global initiative to create sovereign safe zones dedicated to the protection of persecuted peoples worldwide.  The announcement was made during a grand celebration with participation spanning over 80 countries. Over 2 lakh (200,000) devotees, disciples, volunteers, KAILASA's Ministers, and Members of Parliament attended the event, which was streamed live across more than 130 networks globally.  THE HUMANITARIAN INITIATIVE  Abhayam Sarvabhūtebhyoh provides immediate humanitarian refuge, sustenance, and community to persecuted populations, indigenous communities, and displaced persons, without distinction as to race, religion, caste, gender, or nationality.  In delivering the Proclamation, The Supreme Pontiff of Hinduism Bhagavan Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam stated:  "By offering refuge, I extend the enlightenment ecosystem of KAILASA, wherein each being is recognized and treated as Paramashiva.   I hereby proclaim absolute refuge and protection for all beings. This is my Guru Purnima blessings to humanity."  The Proclamation addresses six converging global crises identified by The Supreme Pontiff of Hinduism Bhagavan Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam:  Displacement: UNHCR reports over 120 million forcibly displaced persons worldwide — the highest figure recorded.  Economic Insecurity: The World Bank reports nearly 700 million persons live in extreme poverty on less than $2.15 per day.  Educational Deficit: UNESCO reports over 770 million adults lack functional literacy.  Public Health: The U.S. Surgeon General's 2023 Advisory identifies chronic loneliness as a mortality risk equivalent to smoking 15 cigarettes daily.  Food Insecurity: The FAO reports one-third of global food production is lost or wasted while more than 700 million people experience hunger nightly.  Environmental Degradation: The FAO reports annual forest loss exceeding the land area of Portugal, and 8 million metric tons of plastic entering marine ecosystems each year.  Spiritual Displacement: Pew Research Center reports over 1 billion persons now identify as religiously unaffiliated.  Under the Proclamation, KAILASA will provide:  Humanitarian Assistance: Shelter, food, and housing through KAILASA diplomatic missions and monasteries.  Education: Re-establishment of the Gurukul model offering holistic instruction in sciences, wellness, agriculture, and the arts, open to all ages.  Psychosocial Support: Community-based programs centered on belonging and emotional restoration.  Rule of Law & Governance: Administration grounded in Dharma, with protection of vulnerable populations as the primary metric of governance.  Freedom of Religion or Belief: Guaranteed protection of spiritual practice under Article I of the Constitution of KAILASA.  Environmental Stewardship: Initiatives in organic agriculture, reforestation, and animal welfare through a Goshala-based sustainable economy.  Concluding the Proclamation, The Supreme Pontiff of Hinduism Bhagavan Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam affirmed:  "I make this vow before all. As the living embodiment of Paramashiva, KAILASA shall serve as permanent refuge for every persecuted being."  ABOUT KAILASA  KAILASA is the first sovereign nation for Hindus, committed to the revival of Sanatana Hindu Dharma and to the delivery of humanitarian, educational, and environmental programs globally under the guidance of The Supreme Pontiff of Hinduism Bhagavan Sri Nithyananda Paramashivam.
