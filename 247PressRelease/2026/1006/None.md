# Smoking Pig BBQ Las Vegas Celebrates Grand Re-Opening with Huge Community Party October 10

LAS VEGAS, NV, October 06, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Smoking Pig BBQ Las Vegas is firing up the smokers and throwing a Grand Re-Opening Celebration on Saturday, October 10, 2026, from 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM at 4379 N. Las Vegas Blvd., Las Vegas, NV.  Following a remodel and now under new management, Smoking Pig BBQ is inviting the Las Vegas community to come see the changes, meet the team and celebrate with an event that will take over both the restaurant and the entire parking lot.  Whether you bring the whole family, round up your friends or come by yourself, everyone is welcome.  The celebration will feature a Ribbon Cutting with the Chamber of Commerce, with artist Klypso DJing the event and music throughout the afternoon. Guests can enjoy giveaways every hour, a selfie booth, face painting, a character sketch artist, Smoking Pig merch, sports playing on all TVs and a full bar, all alongside the restaurant's signature low & slow smoked barbecue.  Food creators, influencers and local foodies are especially invited to come out, experience Smoking Pig BBQ and capture the Grand Re-Opening celebration.  "We've remodeled, we're under new management, and we're excited for everyone to come see what's new, have some great BBQ, enjoy the party and get sauced with us." said the Smoking Pig BBQ team.   The Grand Re-Opening gives both longtime customers and first-time guests a chance to experience the newly refreshed location while enjoying an afternoon packed with entertainment.
