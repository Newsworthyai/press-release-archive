# Nugent Bryant Personal Injury Lawyers Named Top-Ranked Personal Injury Attorney in North Haven for 2026

NORTH HAVEN, CT, September 09, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Nugent & Bryant Personal Injury Lawyers has been recognized as the Ranked #1 Personal Injury Attorney in North Haven for 2026 by BusinessRate, a review-analytics platform that certifies local business rankings using verified Google Reviews data. The recognition places Nugent & Bryant at the top of its category among personal injury firms serving the North Haven area.  BusinessRate's rankings are generated from a business's aggregate customer feedback rather than through an application or nomination process. The firm's BusinessRate Score indicates the volume, consistency, and content of client reviews left on Google, offering a data-driven snapshot of how the firm is regarded by the real people it has represented.  "We're proud of this recognition, especially because it comes directly from the people we've represented," said James J. Nugent, one of the lead attorneys at the firm. "At the end of the day, our goal is for every client to feel heard and taken care of during one of the hardest periods of their lives, and this ranking tells us that work is resonating."   Nugent & Bryant has built its practice around representing injured plaintiffs across Connecticut in cases involving automobile accidents, brain injuries, wrongful death, sexual abuse and trafficking, and other personal injury and criminal justice matters. The firm's case results and client testimonials reflect a satisfied client base that spans Hartford, Bridgeport, New Haven, Milford, and surrounding Connecticut communities.  As Nugent & Bryant continues taking on new cases across Connecticut, the firm has said this recognition reinforces its approach to client communication and case preparation, particularly as more complex litigation matters make up a growing share of its caseload. The firm plans to build on this year's ranking with continued outreach and support to injury victims throughout the state.  About the firm:  Nugent & Bryant Personal Injury Lawyers is a Connecticut-based plaintiff-side law firm representing injury victims and their families throughout the state, with a focus on car and truck accidents, catastrophic injuries, wrongful death, and sexual abuse and trafficking claims. The firm operates on a contingency-fee basis. Anyone in the area who's been hurt in an accident is welcome to request a free consultation with the firm and explore their legal options for pursuing compensation.
