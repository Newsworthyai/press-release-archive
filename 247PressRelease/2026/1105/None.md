# Because Life's Hot Enough: Arctic Undies Launches Kickstarter to Bring Cooling Innovation to Everyday Underwear

RALEIGH, NC, November 05, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- For anyone who's experienced overheating, uncomfortable moisture, cling, or the constant need to adjust their underwear, the struggle is all too familiar. Yet while innovation has transformed everything from athletic apparel to sleep products, everyday underwear remains an overlooked opportunity for better comfort and design.  Arctic Undies, an emerging apparel brand founded by entrepreneur and author Ruth Danoff, is setting out to change that.  Guided by its tagline, "Because life's hot enough," Arctic Undies is developing a new generation of elevated cooling essentials designed for people who run hot. The brand has launched a $10,000 Kickstarter campaign to help bring its first collection to life, inviting early supporters to be part of rethinking one of the most essential and often overlooked items in our wardrobes.  A Personal Frustration Inspires a New Approach to Everyday Comfort  For founder Ruth Danoff, the inspiration was personal. Like so many people navigating everyday heat sensitivity, whether from hormonal changes, warm environments, active lifestyles, or simply running hot, she found herself frustrated by underwear that wasn't designed with these challenges in mind.  "People spend so much time finding comfortable shoes, the right mattress, and clothing that fits," said Danoff. "But underwear? We often just put up with it. I started Arctic Undies because I believe something we wear so close to our bodies deserves more thought. We should be able to feel comfortable and confident in our underwear throughout the day, not just when we first put it on."  Rather than approaching cooling underwear as a niche or clinical solution, Danoff envisioned something different: thoughtfully designed, elevated everyday essentials that bring together comfort, functionality, and a modern aesthetic.  Rethinking Underwear from the Fabric Up  Arctic Undies is taking a design-led approach to everyday comfort, exploring how advanced textiles and thoughtful garment construction can work together to address the challenges of heat, moisture, breathability, and fit.  The brand's development is centered around four key pillars:  - Targeted Cooling & Thermal Management: Performance-driven textile development focused on managing heat and improving comfort during everyday wear.  - Moisture Management & Dryness: Fabric technologies selected to help move moisture away from the body and support a more comfortable wearing experience.  - Engineered Breathability: Strategically designed ventilation, including a proposed laser-perforated gusset, to support airflow where it's needed most.  - Stay-Put Comfort: Thoughtful construction intended to minimize shifting, friction, and the need for constant adjustment.  Unlike products that rely solely on an initial cool-to-the-touch sensation, Arctic Undies is focused on developing comfort that considers the full experience of wearing underwear throughout the day.  The initial collection is expected to include briefs and boy shorts, with development focused on a female-forward launch while embracing the broader community of people who run hot, including men.  Final materials, garment specifications, and performance characteristics will be determined through ongoing product development and evaluation. The brand is not making medical claims or promising a measured temperature reduction.  Building the First Collection Together Through Kickstarter  Arctic Undies has launched a $10,000, all-or-nothing Kickstarter campaign, running through November 29, 2026, to support the next stage of product development and help bring its first collection to market.  The campaign invites early supporters to get behind a new approach to everyday comfort, with planned single-pair and multi-pair rewards and opportunities to follow the brand's development journey.  "Comfort shouldn't come with a category or an age limit," added Danoff. "Whether you're navigating hormonal changes, dealing with a hot workday, or you've simply always run warm, you deserve underwear that considers your needs. We're building Arctic Undies for all of those moments, and we're excited to invite people into the journey from the very beginning."  As a Kickstarter project, Arctic Undies remains in development. Final product specifications, timelines, and fulfillment details are subject to the risks and updates outlined on the campaign page.  Join the Movement for Cooler Everyday Comfort  The Arctic Undies Kickstarter campaign is open through November 29, 2026. Early supporters are invited to back the project, explore campaign rewards, and help bring the brand's first collection to life.  Explore and support the campaign: https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/articundies/arctic-undies  About Arctic Undies  Arctic Undies is an emerging apparel brand founded by entrepreneur and author Ruth Danoff in Raleigh, North Carolina. Built around the belief that everyday comfort deserves better innovation, Arctic Undies is developing elevated cooling essentials for people who run hot. Combining performance-driven textile development, engineered breathability, moisture management, and thoughtful fit, the brand is reimagining underwear for real life.  Because life's hot enough.
