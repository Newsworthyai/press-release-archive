# Tinnitus Hearing Center of Arizona Introduces Oticon Reveal™ Hearing Aids: World's First Dual AI Technology Delivers Natural Hearing Experience

TEMPE, AZ, August 27, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Dr. Rohe at the Tinnitus & Hearing Center of Arizona is pleased to announce the availability of the Oticon Reveal™, a groundbreaking miniRITE hearing aid that represents the most significant advancement in hearing care technology to date. Launched globally in August 2026, the Reveal is the world's first hearing aid powered by Dual AI, delivering crystal-clear speech and meaningful contextual sounds in optimal balance for a more natural hearing experience.  Dual AI Sound Processing: Speech and Context in Perfect Balance  The Oticon Reveal features Oticon's revolutionary Dual AI system, powered by the new Reveal AI technology platform. Unlike single-AI systems, Reveal uses two dedicated AI processors running simultaneously in real time.  Speech AI: Continuously identifies and enhances speech from people around the user with industry-leading access to speech clarity.  Context AI: Recognizes and balances surrounding contextual sounds, preserving the environmental information your brain needs to understand what's happening around you.  This dual-processing approach delivers a 60% stronger brain response than the previous Oticon Intent platform, creating clearer and more natural hearing without compromising battery life, comfort, size, or discretion.  Advanced Features for Real-World Performance  The Reveal AI platform includes several breakthrough technologies:  Reveal Optimizer: Provides up to 91% predicted speech access—10% higher than closest competitors—with 30% faster feedback prevention and four times the fitting precision of previous Oticon technology.  Wind & Handling Stabilizer 2.0: Delivers up to 40 dB attenuation of wind and handling noise—up to 10 dB more than first-generation technology.  4D Sensor Technology: Continuously monitors head movements and environmental changes to optimize sound processing in real time.  Voice Clarifier: Enhanced speech enhancement for challenging listening situations.  All-Day Power with Enhanced Connectivity  The Oticon Reveal features improved rechargeable lithium-ion batteries with enhanced power management:  - Full-day battery life with overnight charging - Approximately 22 hours of runtime with a 2-hour charge. - Streaming Comfort feature for optimized music, podcast, and phone call streaming.  The device introduces a new connectivity platform with enhanced signal strength, providing more stable streaming and longer range for hands-free calls and audio. As a Made for iPhone and optimized for Android hearing aid, it supports.  - Bluetooth® LE Audio for seamless smartphone integration - Auracast™ broadcast technology for public venue audio - Improved streaming stability and range - New Google Fast Pair Technology  About the Oticon Reveal  The Oticon Reveal represents Oticon's commitment to bringing hearing aid users closer to natural hearing through Dual AI technology. Built on Oticon's latest BrainHearing™ insights, the Reveal is available in four technology levels (Platinum, Gold, Silver, and Bronze) across the miniRITE rechargeable form factor, making advanced hearing care accessible to a wider range of patients.  Available in the U.S. market starting August 24, 2026, the Oticon Reveal marks a new era in hearing aid technology—offering the natural sound quality patients need with the advanced AI features that make everyday listening easier.  Availability  The Oticon Reveal is now available for fitting at The Tinnitus & Hearing Center of Arizona. Due to high demand and the phased rollout schedule, we recommend scheduling a consultation early to ensure timely availability.  For more information or to schedule a hearing assessment and fitting consultation, patients can visit www.tinnitusaz.com.  About Tinnitus & Hearing Center of Arizona: Tinnitus & Hearing Center of Arizona is a leading hearing care provider specializing in comprehensive tinnitus management and hearing aid fitting. With a commitment to providing patients with the latest advancements in hearing technology and evidence-based tinnitus treatment, Audiologists Allen and Amy Rohe are now accepting new patients.
