# Nancy Volante Helps People Hear the Quiet Voice They've Been Missing Through "The Whispering Soul Podcast"

RIDGEFIELD, CT, August 11, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- In a world filled with constant distractions and outside expectations, wellness educator, speaker and founder of The Whispering Soul, Nancy Volante believes there is a quieter voice that never disappears.  People do not lose it. They simply stop hearing it.  For more than three decades, Nancy Volante has dedicated her life to helping people reconnect with that inner wisdom. Whether teaching students, guiding private clients or speaking to listeners through The Whispering Soul Podcast, her work has remained centered on one belief: every person carries an authentic voice that deserves to be heard.  "My hope is that people remember they have a voice," Volante says. "Whether someone is experiencing joy, uncertainty or a difficult season of life, what they are feeling matters. When people are truly heard, something begins to change."  Most people won't remember every detail of a conversation years later. What they will remember is how it made them feel. For many who work with Volante, what stays with them is not simply an interpretation of a birth chart. It is the experience of being deeply heard, accepted without judgment and reminded that the wisdom they have been searching for may already be within them.  "I want people to connect with their soul self," adds Volante. "I want them to align with their North Star, whatever that means for them. That may be creativity. It may be self-expression. It may simply be learning how to feel good within themselves again, regardless of what their past has been."  Volante's philosophy was shaped by her own journey. As a teenager struggling with anxiety, she discovered yoga while searching for peace. That search led her to spend fifteen years as a teaching artist in the New York City public school system before founding Imagine Wellness, a yoga studio she owned and operated for eight years.  Volante admits, "There have been low periods in my own life. And there have also been times of rising again. Those experiences taught me that the struggles we go through often hold the seeds of transformation."  From those experiences, The Whispering Soul was born, rooted in the belief that every person already possesses the inner wisdom to move forward and sometimes simply needs someone to help them hear it again.  Released each Wednesday, The Whispering Soul Podcast explores creativity, intuition, emotional well-being and personal growth through thoughtful conversations that encourage listeners to reconnect with themselves.  "I want people to feel good about who they are," Volante explains. "No matter what they have experienced, they still have a voice, and that voice deserves to be heard."  Alongside the podcast, Volante offers private Astrology Wellness Coaching that integrates humanistic and intuitive astrology with mindfulness, movement and wellness practices. Rather than telling clients who they should become, she helps them better understand who they already are.  "I think it's very important that people allow themselves to fully experience what they're feeling," Volante says. "When they leave a session, I hope they feel empowered because they have reconnected with themselves."  Nancy Volante does not measure success by audience size alone.  She adds: "If my podcast reaches two people and those two people receive something meaningful from it, then it has already done what I hoped it would do. Of course I hope it reaches many more, but I will continue because it gives me great pleasure to give in that way."  As The Whispering Soul continues to grow, her hope is simple: that people slow down long enough to reconnect with themselves and remember that beneath life's distractions, disappointments and doubts, their authentic voice is still there, waiting to be heard.  "There is still a direction that belongs to you," Volante continues. "Sometimes the first step is becoming quiet enough to listen."  In a world that often encourages people to look everywhere else for answers, Nancy Volante offers a different invitation: beneath the noise of everyday life, the quiet voice pointing us back toward who we truly are has been there all along, patiently waiting to be heard.  To learn more about The Whispering Soul Podcast, Nancy Volante's Astrology Wellness Coaching practice, or her philosophy of helping people reconnect with their authentic selves, visit her official website.  About The Whispering Soul  The Whispering Soul is a wellness practice founded by Nancy Volante that helps individuals reconnect with their authentic selves through humanistic and intuitive astrology, wellness coaching, mindfulness and movement. Drawing on more than three decades of experience as an educator, wellness practitioner and coach, Volante creates supportive spaces where people feel deeply heard, gain greater clarity and develop a stronger relationship with themselves. Through private coaching, public speaking and The Whispering Soul Podcast, Volante continues encouraging people to trust that the wisdom, resilience and direction they seek are often already within them.
