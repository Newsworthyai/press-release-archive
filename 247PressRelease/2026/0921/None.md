# Masha Kova Launches New Era With "Red Light Green Light," VEVO Music Video Premiere and Miami Performance Weekend

MIAMI, FL, September 21, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Masha Kova is stepping into a new era with the release of "Red Light Green Light," the Miami Beach-based pop and R&B artist's newest single, now available across major music streaming platforms.  Released Friday, September 18, "Red Light Green Light" introduces a confident, flirtatious sound driven by polished pop production, house influences and the push-and-pull energy at the center of the record.  The release was accompanied by the premiere of the official "Red Light Green Light" music video on VEVO, extending the new era visually while setting the stage for a full weekend of live performances and experiences in Miami.  Kova celebrated release night with an intimate private yacht event in Miami Beach aboard XOXO Yachts, bringing together invited media, creatives, tastemakers, and members of her growing community for an evening centered around the new single.  The celebration continued Saturday when Kova debuted "Red Light Green Light" live as part of a 10-minute performance set at Open Sessions, an event powered by Soul Era Records and The Lipstickroyalty Agency at Arlo Wynwood. Joined by backup dancers, Kova performed both "Red Light Green Light" and previously released music as she introduced audiences to the performance direction behind her evolving artist brand.  On Sunday, Kova continued the momentum with another live performance of "Red Light Green Light" at the Pretty Hustlaz LinkUp, completing a three-day Miami release weekend built around music, performance, visual storytelling, and community.  The release marks the latest chapter in an active year for Kova as she continues expanding her presence across music, media, and live entertainment.  Earlier this year, Kova released "Bye Bye Bye," featuring and produced by legendary producer Teddy Riley. The project introduced a broader audience to her sound while setting up the evolution now continuing through "Red Light Green Light."  Her latest release blends pop and R&B with house-driven elements, creating a record built equally for headphones, nightlife and live performance.  For Kova and her team, the rollout surrounding "Red Light Green Light" was intentionally designed to extend beyond release day. From the music video and visual campaign to the private launch experience and multiple live performances, the weekend gave audiences several entry points into the record and the artist behind it.  "Red Light Green Light" is available now on major streaming platforms. The official music video is available on VEVO.  For interviews, media opportunities, appearances, and additional press assets, contact The Lipstickroyalty Agency.  Masha Kova is a Miami Beach-based pop and R&B recording artist blending contemporary pop, R&B and dance influences with a confident, performance-driven approach to music. A student and emerging recording artist, Kova continues to build her catalog through new music, visual storytelling and live performances. Her recent releases include "Bye Bye Bye," featuring and produced by Teddy Riley, and her latest single, "Red Light Green Light." Follow Masha Kova across social platforms and listen to "Red Light Green Light," available now on major streaming platforms.
