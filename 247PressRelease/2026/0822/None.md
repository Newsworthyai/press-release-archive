# South Dakota Pioneer Audrey Saylor Launches National Manufacturing Scholarship to Fuel Industrial Innovation

DE SMET, SD, August 22, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- A new national academic initiative launched in South Dakota aims to inspire the next generation of industrial leaders. The Audrey Saylor Scholarship for Manufacturing opens its application cycle to undergraduate students across the United States who demonstrate a drive for creative problem-solving and technological advancement in the modern manufacturing sector.  Founded by South Dakota business leader Audrey Saylor, the scholarship addresses the growing need for forward-thinking talent within complex supply chains, engineering discipline, and industrial operations. As the manufacturing landscape evolves alongside global market demands, this initiative provides financial support and national recognition to college students preparing to shape the future of American industry.  The scholarship program reflects the career journey of Audrey Saylor, who transitioned from a background in education to co-found American Engineered Products (AEP) in 2004. Over two decades as Vice President and business owner, Audrey Saylor guided AEP to become a global pioneer in specialized metal manufacturing for the casino and video lottery markets. Today, Audrey Saylor channels that extensive background in business development and industrial design into supporting emerging scholars nationwide.  "Modern manufacturing relies on continuous adaptation, precision engineering, and bold ideas," states Audrey Saylor. "This scholarship exists to support students who possess the vision and determination to solve real-world industrial challenges."  Eligibility and Application Requirements The Audrey Saylor Scholarship for Manufacturing is open to undergraduate students nationwide. Applicants do not need to reside in South Dakota to apply, as the program supports students enrolled at any accredited two-year or four-year college or university across the United States.  To qualify for consideration, candidates must meet the following criteria: • Academic Enrollment: Currently enrolled as an undergraduate student at an accredited higher education institution within the United States. • Relevant Field of Study: Pursuing a degree or career path aligned with modern manufacturing, including Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Design, Supply Chain Management, Materials Science, or Business Operations. • Essay Submission: Submitting an original 500-to-700-word essay addressing the official prompt regarding industrial innovation. • Adherence to Deadlines: Completing all application requirements before the official cutoff date.  Essay Prompt and Focus Areas Applicants must submit an original essay addressing the following prompt: "The manufacturing industry thrives on forward-thinking innovation and adapting to global market demands. Drawing inspiration from Audrey Saylor's 20-year history of innovation with metal bases in the casino and lottery industries, write a 500-to-700-word essay explaining how you plan to bring fresh, creative problem-solving or technological innovation to your chosen field within modern manufacturing."  Submissions undergo review based on original research, clarity of thought, and practical application to modern industrial challenges.  Important Dates and Award Details The program awards a one-time grant of $1,000 to the selected recipient to assist with higher education expenses. Key dates for the current application cycle include: • Application Deadline: March 15, 2027 • Winner Announcement: April 15, 2027  Students across all fifty states are encouraged to submit their applications online before the deadline.  About the Audrey Saylor Scholarship for Manufacturing Based in De Smet, South Dakota, the Audrey Saylor Scholarship for Manufacturing supports undergraduate students pursuing careers in engineering, design, operations, and industrial technology. Founded by longtime manufacturing executive Audrey Saylor, the program fosters academic excellence and creative leadership across the American manufacturing landscape.
