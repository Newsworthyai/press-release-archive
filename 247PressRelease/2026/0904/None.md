# Mizrahi Kroub LLP Continues Fight Against Discrimination Through Employment and ADA Litigation

NEW YORK, NY, September 04, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Mizrahi Kroub LLP, a New York City-based plaintiff-side law firm, continues its mission of combating discrimination and advocating for individuals whose rights have been violated in the workplace and beyond. With a focused practice in Employment Law and Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) litigation, the firm is dedicated to helping employees and disabled individuals pursue justice.  Founded in 2021, the firm was built on the belief that individuals facing discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or accessibility barriers deserve strong legal representation and a trusted advocate. Mizrahi Kroub LLP represents employees in matters involving age, race, disability, and pregnancy discrimination, as well as sexual harassment, sexual assault, hostile work environments, wrongful termination, retaliation, wage theft, unpaid overtime, Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) violations, and whistleblower claims.  "Our purpose is to stand with people who have been wronged and help them hold powerful organizations accountable," said Joseph H. Mizrahi. "We believe every client deserves respect, empathy, and dedicated representation, regardless of the size or complexity of their case."  Serving clients throughout New York City and Suffolk County, the firm leverages its experience with employment and civil rights laws, including protections provided under the New York City Human Rights Law. In addition to representing workers, the firm advocates for individuals whose rights have been impacted by accessibility barriers and other forms of disability discrimination under the ADA.  Mizrahi Kroub LLP has developed a reputation for combining aggressive advocacy with personalized client service. Every client is supported by a dedicated client relations contact who helps ensure clear communication and guidance throughout the legal process. This commitment to accessibility and responsiveness reflects the firm's belief that effective representation extends beyond the courtroom.  The firm also handles qualifying matters on a contingency fee basis, meaning clients pay no out-of-pocket legal fees and owe nothing unless compensation is recovered on their behalf. This approach helps make legal representation accessible to individuals who may otherwise be unable to pursue their claims.  Driven by a team of fierce advocates, Mizrahi Kroub LLP remains committed to protecting employee rights, combating discrimination, and helping individuals seek accountability when they have been treated unfairly. Through strategic litigation, client-focused service, and a passion for justice, the firm continues to make a meaningful impact on the lives of those it represents.  About Mizrahi Kroub LLP  Mizrahi Kroub LLP is a full-service, plaintiff-side law firm based in New York City's Financial District, dedicated to protecting the rights of employees, consumers, and disabled individuals. The firm has successfully litigated large class-action lawsuits against some of the largest corporations in the United States, while remaining equally committed to representing everyday people in individual cases of any size. Mizrahi Kroub's employment law team focuses on holding employers accountable for workplace discrimination, sexual harassment, retaliation, unpaid wages, wrongful termination, and FMLA violations. For more information, visit www.mizrahikroub.com.
