# DR. AMAR SURDHAR ANNOUNCES SUPPORT OF THE HAS FOUNDATION TO ADVANCE NEUROLOGICAL HEALTH, INNOVATION AND COMPASSIONATE CARE

NEW YORK, NY, September 25, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Esteemed psychiatrist Dr. Amar Surdhar, M.D., founder of Clarion Psychiatry, has announced his support of the HAS Foundation, contributing to an organization dedicated to advancing neurological health through science, innovation, compassionate care and interdisciplinary collaboration.   The HAS Foundation works to improve the lives of individuals and families affected by neurological conditions and brain trauma while helping bridge the gap between scientific discovery and meaningful patient impact. Its work addresses complex neurological and neurodegenerative conditions, including ALS, Alzheimer's disease, dementia and Parkinson's disease, while encouraging collaboration among scientists, physicians, researchers, engineers, innovators and healthcare professionals.   The Foundation's approach extends beyond symptom management, lacing emphasis on research, rehabilitation, cognition, therapeutic innovation and whole-person care. It also seeks to connect emerging scientific discoveries with clinical applications and innovative solutions that can improve neurological health and quality of life. For Dr. Surdhar, supporting the HAS Foundation reflects a shared belief that medicine must address not only illness, but also the dignity, humanity and overall well-being of every patient.   "As a psychiatrist, I have seen how profoundly conditions affecting the brain can impact not only an individual, but an entire family," said Dr. Amar Surdhar. "I chose to support the HAS Foundation because its mission recognizes that meaningful progress requires science, innovation and compassion working together. Our work in psychiatry is deeply connected to understanding the brain, human behavior and quality of life. Supporting an organization committed to advancing neurological health, recovery and innovative solutions is a natural extension of that commitment. Every breakthrough brings us closer to giving patients and families greater hope, dignity and possibility."  The HAS Foundation's mission is particularly significant as neurological and neurodegenerative diseases continue to present complex challenges for patients, caregivers and the medical community. By creating opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration and connecting research, technology and clinical practice, the Foundation seeks to accelerate the translation of scientific discoveries into solutions that can make a tangible difference in patients' lives.  Dr. Surdhar's contribution also highlights the important role physicians, researchers, philanthropists and community leaders can play in supporting organizations working at the intersection of brain health, innovation, healing and human-centered care. Through his support, Dr. Surdhar joins the HAS Foundation in championing a future in which individuals confronting neurological disease and brain trauma are met not only with better science and treatment possibilities, but also with compassion, resources and hope.  About Dr. Amar Surdhar  Dr. Amar Surdhar, M.D. is a psychiatrist and founder of Clarion Psychiatry, a New York-based psychiatric practice focused on thoughtful, individualized mental health care. His approach emphasizes understanding the whole patient while combining clinical expertise with compassionate, personalized care. Clarion Psychiatry PLLC is dedicated to providing discreet, high-quality psychiatric care for executives and high‑net‑worth individuals. Dr. Amar and his team offer personalized evaluations, medication management, and telepsychiatry, ensuring compassionate, confidential care in Midtown Manhattan.  About the HAS Foundation The HAS Foundation supports efforts focused on neurological health, scientific research, innovation, rehabilitation and collaboration across disciplines. Its mission centers on improving outcomes and quality of life for individuals and families affected by neurological disease and brain trauma.
