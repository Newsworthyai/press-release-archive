# Tijuana Flats Is Bringing the Beef with New Barbacoa Chimirrito

MAITLAND, FL, November 17, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Barbacoa is back on the menu at Tijuana Flats, and this time it's making a big entrance with the all-new Barbacoa Chimirrito. The new creation gives the slow-cooked favorite the full Flats treatment, packing tender beef and melty cheese in a giant tortilla fried until golden and crispy. It's big. It's bold. It's got attitude. It's everything Flats guests expect, turned all the way up.  Priced at $9.99, the Barbacoa Chimirrito starts with slow-cooked beef barbacoa, layered with choice of rice, diced onions, cilantro, garlic lime sauce and cheddar jack cheese. It's all wrapped in a 13-inch flour tortilla and fried until golden and crispy, then served with fresh tortilla chips and Smack Habanero sauce for dipping. One bite brings together savory barbacoa, creamy garlic lime sauce and plenty of crunch, with a little extra heat on the side.  "We didn't bring barbacoa back just to give guests another protein option," said Julia Simon, VP of Marketing at Tijuana Flats. "We took our slow cooked beef, rolled it up big and fried it crispy, because that's how we do things at Tijuana Flats. The Barbacoa Chimirrito is loud, it's messy in the best way and it's worth every napkin. We'd love to invite guests in to grab one before it's gone."  Guests can also add barbacoa to any Tijuana Flats entrée for just $1.50. Pile it into tacos, roll it into a burrito or build it into a bowl. The juicy, tender beef brings bold garlic and chili flavor to any menu item. The Barbacoa Chimirrito and barbacoa add-on will be available at participating Tijuana Flats locations through Jan. 3, 2027, while supplies last.   The bold flavors don't stop there. Tijuana Flats is bringing back Georgia Peach & Vidalia Onion Sauce, a guest favorite that's earning a permanent spot on the Hot Sauce Bar. Its sweet and savory flavor gives guests one more reason to come back and one more way to turn up the flavor.  Hungry yet? For the nearest Tijuana Flats at www.tijuanaflats.com or follow Tijuana Flats on social media for the latest.  About Tijuana Flats  A fast-casual Mexican concept that's part of the newly formed Latitude Food Group, Tijuana Flats was founded in 1995 in Winter Park, Florida, and operates nearly 100 company-owned and franchised restaurants across Florida, Alabama, North Carolina and Kentucky. With bold flavors and colorful experiences piled high with big-hearted, over-the-top service, Tijuana Flats boasts a menu consisting of fresh, made-to-order tacos, burritos and other Mexican appetizers, entrées and desserts.
