# A Murder Inside a New York Hospital Sets the Stage for a Gripping Tale of Revenge, Secrets, and Justice in A New York Collage

NEW YORK, NY, November 17, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- A mysterious murder inside a New York hospital sets the stage for A New York Collage, a suspenseful crime novel by Virginia E. Escandor. When Anne Marie Danbury, a nurse manager in the radiology department at St. Thomas More Hospital, is found murdered in an MRI room, what begins as a shocking discovery quickly becomes a complex criminal investigation. Lieutenant Tim Reilly and Sergeant Ron Timmons of the NYPD are assigned to the case, tasked with uncovering the circumstances surrounding her death and identifying whoever is responsible. As they begin their work, the possibility that the killing was motivated by revenge becomes increasingly difficult to ignore.  For Reilly and Timmons, solving the case means looking beyond the crime scene and examining the life Anne Marie led, the people she encountered, and the relationships that may have influenced the events leading up to her murder. Their investigation takes them into the hospital environment, where professional connections and personal histories may hold important clues. As they question those connected to the victim and begin piecing together the details, the detectives discover that the situation is not as straightforward as it first appears. What may seem like an isolated act of violence gradually reveals a wider web of circumstances, raising questions about loyalty, resentment, and the motives people may conceal from those closest to them.  As new clues come to light, the investigation grows more complicated. The detectives must distinguish between what people are willing to reveal and what they may be trying to keep hidden. Every lead has the potential to change their understanding of the case, while the possibility of further violence adds urgency to their search for answers. The apparent connection to revenge also forces them to consider whether the murder is rooted in a recent conflict or in events that have remained unresolved for years.  The story reaches beyond the immediate circumstances of Anne Marie's death, drawing attention to the lasting influence of the past. Earlier experiences, personal grievances, and relationships formed over time become relevant as the investigation unfolds. The novel brings together different lives and histories, showing how events that once seemed unrelated can connect through a single act of violence. As Reilly and Timmons follow the evidence, the consequences of vengeance extend beyond the original victim, putting others in danger and threatening to disrupt the lives of those in its path.  In A New York Collage, Escandor explores the complicated nature of human relationships and the ways unresolved conflicts can shape people's decisions. The novel considers the fine line between seeking justice and pursuing revenge, while examining how anger, betrayal, and past experiences can have consequences far beyond their original circumstances. Against the backdrop of a murder investigation, the story builds suspense through a series of revelations that challenge assumptions and keep the truth just out of reach.  Blending crime, mystery, and suspense, A New York Collage invites readers to follow two detectives as they work to uncover the truth behind a killing that may have deeper roots than anyone initially suspects. With a growing number of questions and the threat of further harm hanging over the investigation, Reilly and Timmons must determine how the pieces fit together before vengeance claims another victim.  About the Author  Virginia E. Escandor was born and raised in the Philippines and is based in New York, New York. She worked as a nurse for 30 years, an experience that gave her firsthand familiarity with the healthcare environment that features in A New York Collage. Her writing has appeared in magazines in both the Philippines and the United States. In 2012, she published her first book of poetry, I Quote Myself. With A New York Collage, Escandor brings readers a work of crime fiction shaped by her experiences and observations of people and relationships.
