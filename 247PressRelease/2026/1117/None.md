# John J. Scura III to Lecture at NJ Association of Justice Seminar on Bankruptcy and Employment Law Ethics

WAYNE, NJ, November 17, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Scura, Wigfield, Heyer, Cammarota & Gonzalez, LLP is pleased to announce that firm partner John J. Scura III will serve as a lecturer at the New Jersey Association for Justice's (NJAJ) Meadowlands Seminar® 2026: Employment Law on Thursday, November 19, 2026.  As part of the employment law program, Scura will present a lecture entitled "Ethics: Media, AI and Bankruptcy Considerations for Employment Practitioners." His presentation will address ethical issues involving media, artificial intelligence (AI), and bankruptcy considerations relevant to employment law practitioners.  The Meadowlands Seminar® 2026 is a three-day legal education event taking place November 18–20, 2026, at the World of Blue Hotel (formerly the Hilton Meadowlands) in East Rutherford, New Jersey. Hosted by the New Jersey Association for Justice, the seminar brings together legal professionals for educational programming and discussions on developments and issues affecting their areas of practice.  Scura's participation reflects his commitment to continuing legal education and engagement with the broader legal community. Through his presentation, he will share his experience and perspective on the ethical responsibilities and professional considerations attorneys face when navigating complex legal matters.  As a partner at Scura, Wigfield, Heyer, Cammarota & Gonzalez, LLP, Scura brings his legal experience and professional knowledge to the firm's practice and its ongoing involvement in the legal community. His participation in the seminar underscores the firm's commitment to professional development, legal education, and the responsible practice of law.  About Scura, Wigfield, Heyer, Cammarota & Gonzalez, LLP  Scura, Wigfield, Heyer, Cammarota & Gonzalez, LLP is a New Jersey-based law firm with more than 50 years of experience providing legal representation to individuals, families, and businesses. With five office locations throughout New Jersey, the firm handles matters involving personal injury, bankruptcy, employment law, business and civil litigation, estate planning, elder law, and real estate. The firm's attorneys are committed to providing knowledgeable counsel and dedicated advocacy tailored to each client's needs.
