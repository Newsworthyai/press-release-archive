# Avident Health and Unite for HER Awarded Quality Improvement Grant from Pfizer and the National Comprehensive Cancer Network® (NCCN®) to Support Metastatic Breast Cancer Patients

BALTIMORE, MD and MALVERN, PA, October 08, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Avident Health LLC, a pioneer in digital oncology navigation and caregiver-inclusive care coordination, and Unite for HER, a national non-profit organization providing integrative therapies and supportive services to breast and ovarian cancer patients, today announced that they have been awarded an independent quality improvement (QI) grant funded collaboratively by Pfizer Inc. and the National Comprehensive Cancer Network® (NCCN®).  The collaborative project will deploy Avident Health's proprietary MyNavigator™ digital platform to enhance care delivery for patients diagnosed with metastatic breast cancer (mBC). The initiative aims to bridge critical communication gaps between clinic visits by delivering personalized, patient-friendly education aligned with NCCN Guidelines for Patients®, driving active enrollment and utilization of Unite for HER's free supportive services, and equipping informal family caregivers to foster shared decision-making, treatment adherence, and enhanced quality of life.  Living with metastatic breast cancer requires complex, ongoing treatment management where adherence, symptom monitoring, and whole-person supportive care are paramount. However, patients and their loved ones often struggle to navigate complex clinical guidelines and access community-based supportive resources outside traditional clinical appointments.  Through this grant-funded initiative, the MyNavigator platform will serve as an interactive, home-based companion that:  • Delivers Tailored NCCN Guideline Education: Translates complex clinical pathways into accessible, bite-sized modules tailored to each patient's specific treatment journey, empowering patients to participate actively in informed clinical decision-making.  • Streamlines Access to Integrative Supportive Care: Directly integrates Unite for HER's comprehensive suite of free services—including professional nutrition counseling, fresh food delivery, mental health counseling, and evidence-based integrative therapies—directly into daily navigation prompts.  • Engages the Informal Caregiver: Uniquely includes family members and daily care partners in a secure, proactive communication loop, ensuring they have the context, education, and early symptom alerting needed to support treatment adherence at home.  • Enables Proactive Care Team Interventions: Captures patient-reported check-ins and symptom trends to alert clinical teams early, preventing avoidable complications and reducing acute care utilization.  "Navigating metastatic breast cancer is a daily reality that extends far beyond the four walls of the clinic," said Dr. Maen Farha, Avident Health LLC. "We are honored to receive this support from Pfizer and NCCN. By combining the proactive digital coordination of MyNavigator with Unite for HER's exceptional integrative services and NCCN's gold-standard guidelines, we are creating a continuous circle of support that empowers patients, supports clinical teams, and actively engages the family caregivers who are vital to treatment success."  "Unite for HER is dedicated to provide a supportive community and transformative integrative care directly to metastatic breast cancer patients," said Sue Weldon, Founder and CEO of Unite for HER. "Collaborating with Avident Health on the MyNavigator platform enables us to reach patients and their families directly at the exact moment they need guidance, removing barriers to our integrative nutrition, wellness, and emotional support resources."  The project will measure key quality improvement endpoints over the grant period, evaluating improvements in patient knowledge concordance with NCCN Guidelines, rates of supportive service utilization, patient-reported decisional conflict, and therapy adherence metrics.  About Avident Health LLC Avident Health LLC is a digital health company dedicated to transforming cancer care coordination and treatment adherence. Avident's flagship platform, MyNavigator™, connects patients, informal cancer caregivers, and oncology clinical teams into a unified, proactive digital loop. By integrating personalized education, symptom tracking, and proactive navigation alerts, Avident Health empowers patients and reduces administrative burden for clinical navigators across health systems and community practices. For more information, visit www.avidenthealth.com or follow Avident Health on LinkedIn.  About Unite for HER Unite for HER (UFH) is a national profit organization that supports breast and ovarian cancer patients nationwide. UFH provides free integrative therapies, services, support, and education directly to patients at no personal cost. To learn more, visit www.uniteforher.org.  About the National Comprehensive Cancer Network® (NCCN®) The National Comprehensive Cancer Network® (NCCN®) is a not-for-profit alliance of leading cancer centers dedicated to patient care, research, and education. NCCN is dedicated to improving and facilitating quality, effective, equitable, and accessible cancer care so all patients can live better lives. For more information, visit www.nccn.org.
