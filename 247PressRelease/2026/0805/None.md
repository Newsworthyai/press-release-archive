# Smith Brothers Farms Celebrates "National Dog Month" One Doorstep at a Time

KENT, WA, August 05, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- August is National Dog Month, and Smith Brothers is celebrating the four-legged family members who eagerly welcome our Milkmen and Milkwomen at doorsteps throughout the Seattle, Tacoma, Vancouver, Wash., and Portland, Ore. metro areas. It's also a great opportunity to remind everyone about how to keep our furry friends safe when temperatures rise.  Throughout August, Smith Brothers Farms is teaming up with Blue Dog Bakery to celebrate National Dog Month by giving away one prize pack each week. Lucky winners will receive a Blue Dog Bakery Prize Pack plus a $50 Smith Brothers grocery delivery credit to stock up on fresh local favorites delivered right to their doorstep.   "Leaving treats for our four-legged friends is a tradition we're proud to carry on all year long," said Peter Olson, Senior Marketing Manager. "Our Milkmen and Milkwomen keep complimentary dog treats on their trucks and love rewarding the pups who eagerly greet them at the door. It's a small thing that helps make delivery day a little more special."  Keep Your Best Friend Safe This Summer Please keep these warm-weather safety reminders in mind from The Humane Society:  • Never leave pets in a parked car. Even with cracked windows, temperatures inside a vehicle can quickly become dangerously hot. • Protect sensitive paws. Test pavement with the back of your hand before walks, and aim for early morning or evening walks when surfaces are cooler. • Keep fresh water nearby. Make sure pets always have access to cool drinking water at home and bring water along for walks and adventures. • Create cool spaces. Provide plenty of shade and cool resting areas, and consider a shallow kiddie pool to help pets beat the heat. • Watch for signs of overheating. Heavy panting, excessive drooling, lethargy, vomiting, diarrhea, rapid heartbeat, or difficulty walking may signal heat-related illness.  • Take it easy outdoors. Especially during wildfire season, shorten walks and vigorous play during the hottest parts of the day, and pay attention to your pet's comfort and energy level.
