# My Inspired Space Serving Homeowners as New Vancouver-Area Home Storage System Designer and Installer

VANCOUVER, BC, September 01, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- My Inspired Space is a new home storage and organization design provider in the Greater Vancouver area. Owner and main designer Michael Pachal has over 15 years of experience in custom storage unit design and cabinetry.  It is common for homeowners to have less floor space in their homes than they might like. For many owners there is a need to be creative to best utilize available space. Smartly designed modular storage space units like the ones designed and installed by My Inspired Space are ideal for doing that.   In 2010 Michael started out with Tailored Living and Premier Garage franchises and with these two ventures he started to build on his design skills and craftsmanship. Tailored Living completed more than 3,000 custom storage unit designs and installations between then and 2025. This speaks to the business' success and the quality of the work, along with reinforcing that there is an ongoing demand for these types of home storage and organization solutions.   After 15 years Michael came to the realization that he would be better off working independently rather than as a franchisee for a U.S.-based company. Serving homeowner and builder clients with a new independent business was the better choice, and this lead to the start of My Inspired Space in 2025.   No longer being a franchisee means My Inspired Space will be more free to work with local B.C. vendors. They prefer to be channeling business to those vendors rather than ones outside the Province or Canada, and to better support the local and Provincial economy. Moving on and starting My Inspired Space will allow for that.   My Inspired Space designs and installs custom storage units that match client needs and have the right mix of functionality and design aesthetic. They do complete custom fabrication and can create designs for any space with no limits to size or materials. There is a lifetime structure warranty provided for wood products and no-charge initial consultations are available for interested homeowners or builders. Installation schedules can be flexible based on the owner or builder's needs.   Custom closets, walk-in closets, home spaces, Bedroom Cabinets and mud-room cabinets, garage spaces, Murphy beds, entertainment centers, and living room and home office storage are among the many design types available to customers. Playroom storage design is a popular choice for families with young children, and they also do commercial storage unit design for business storefronts in the Vancouver area.   Homeowners and builders who would like to see these designs can do so by viewing the design gallery and different room-type pages at myinspiredspace.ca.  My Inspired Space is a custom home storage design and installation provider in the Pitt Meadows region of Greater Vancouver. They serve homeowners and builders across the entire Lower Mainland and the Fraser Valley. The company grew out of the owner's previous success with Tailored Living and Premier Garage, two similar ventures in home storage and organization design and custom cabinetry. He has over 15 years of experience in the business.  Homeowners in Greater Vancouver and the Fraser Valley who wish to maximize their use of space in their home for better item storage and room organization have a quality local provider available to them with My Inspired Space. They do complete custom home storage unit fabrication and installation and can create units of any size, including custom closets and cabinets, wall units, Murphy beds, Kitchen pantries, and garage storage unit design.
