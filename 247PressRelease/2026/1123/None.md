# Transworld Business Advisors Opens New Office in Northeast Wisconsin to Help Local Entrepreneurs Buy and Sell Businesses

KIMBERLY, WI, November 23, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Transworld Business Advisors®, the world's largest business brokerage franchise, has opened a new office in Kimberly, bringing expert business brokerage, franchise consulting, and mergers and acquisitions services to local entrepreneurs in Northeast Wisconsin. The office is owned by Aaron Johnson and his wife, Amanda, whose firsthand experience as entrepreneurs and business owners enables them to guide clients through every stage of buying, selling, and growing a business.  "I have personally purchased businesses, and I want to help Main Street business owners successfully transition to the next chapter of their lives. Transworld has a great reputation and the support system to help them do just that," said Johnson. "I know what it takes to buy, grow, and lead a company, and I want to help business owners navigate their next chapter."  Transworld Business Advisors specializes in helping business owners confidentially sell their businesses, connecting qualified buyers with business opportunities, and helping entrepreneurs explore franchise development opportunities. Backed by a global network and decades of experience, the brand offers the expertise, resources, and support needed to guide clients through every stage of the transaction process.  "When I sit across the table from a business owner thinking about selling or a buyer trying to make the leap into ownership, I'm not speaking from theory. I've lived both sides of the deal, and that perspective helps me guide clients through one of the biggest financial decisions of their lives with confidence and empathy," added Johnson.  The new office will serve entrepreneurs throughout Northeast Wisconsin, including Oshkosh, Neenah, Appleton, Green Bay, and Door County, providing personalized guidance to business owners looking to transition ownership, expand through acquisition, or explore franchise opportunities.  About Transworld Business Advisors Transworld Business Advisors® (Transworld) is among the United Franchise Group™ (UFG) family of affiliated brands. With over 40 years of business brokerage experience, Transworld Business Advisors is in the business of helping buyers and sellers connect, conducting franchise consultations, and supporting franchise development. The company represents acquisition-minded corporations or individuals interested in owning their own company or franchise. From business brokerages to mergers and acquisitions, Transworld Business Advisors are business sales specialists that represent numerous listings across multiple industries. For more information about Transworld visit www.tworld.com and for information on owning a Transworld franchise, visit www.tworldfranchise.com.
