# Water View Vacations Highlights Two South Florida Holiday Boat Parades for December 2026

POMPANO BEACH, FL, October 16, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- South Florida's holiday season will shine a little brighter this December as two of the region's most celebrated holiday boat parades take place on consecutive evenings. Water View Vacations, an owner-managed waterfront vacation rental company in Pompano Beach, is highlighting the December 11–12 festivities and helping visitors discover the area's holiday traditions.  On Friday, December 11, the 64th Annual Greater Pompano Beach Holiday Boat Parade will bring illuminated vessels, festive decorations, and music to the Intracoastal Waterway. Organized by the Greater Pompano Beach Chamber of Commerce, this year's celebration features the theme "A Party in the U.S.A.," recognizing America's 250th anniversary.  The festivities continue Saturday, December 12, with the 55th Annual Seminole Hard Rock Winterfest Boat Parade in Fort Lauderdale. The spectacular procession features decorated boats and yachts traveling approximately 12 miles from downtown Fort Lauderdale along the New River and Intracoastal Waterway toward Pompano Beach.  Together, the two events offer visitors an opportunity to experience South Florida's boating culture, waterfront restaurants, beautiful beaches, and seasonal celebrations—all during one December weekend.  For travelers considering a holiday getaway, Water View Vacations offers privately managed waterfront accommodations in Pompano Beach's desirable Harbor Village neighborhood.  Bella Acqua is a three-bedroom waterfront vacation home featuring a private heated saltwater pool, boat dock, and comfortable outdoor living areas.  Oltre Mare provides another waterfront retreat with two bedrooms, a private heated saltwater pool, and convenient access to Pompano Beach and Fort Lauderdale attractions.  Both homes offer visitors a relaxing alternative to traditional hotel accommodations, with the privacy and convenience of an entire waterfront residence.  Visitors should plan ahead for event viewing locations, parking, and potential bridge closures during parade festivities. Event schedules and routes are subject to change.
