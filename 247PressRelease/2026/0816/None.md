# Bestselling Author Gina Iafrate Announces No Charge Download Of Ebook Version Of Her Award-Winning Novel, The English Professor

WILMINGTON, NC, August 16, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Award winning author Gina Iafrate has announced that the ebook version of her award-winning novel, The English Professor, will be available at no charge August 18 and 19, 2026 at Amazon at https://www.amazon.com/stores/Gina-Iafrate/author/B00MRZ8Y9A.  In this book, Andrew Robertson, an introverted, middle-aged professor leaves his unremarkable London life to embark on a journey to Sicily where strangers welcome him kindly. In this sensuous Mediterranean setting, he develops a burning infatuation for another man's wife. Falling into each other's arms, Andrew and Francesca both satisfy and heighten the emotional and physical longing they have felt all their adult lives. Their union results in a child. But Andrew must go back to London and Francesca must protect herself, Andrew and their child from the violent reach of her husband.  The Professor's Daughter is the sequel to The English Professor. Set in Italy, this romantic fiction novel allows readers to immerse themselves in the life of Gracy and her adventures in love and the trials and tribulations she faces.  In The Professor's Daughter, Gracy Robertson is the love child of an intense love affair.  At birth she was taken from her dying mother and left in the care of the devastated Professor, her dad. Living in hiding for years, they managed to avoid the long reach of the corrupt mobsters who had taken her mother's life.  Once the threat was gone, they believed that peace could return to their lives at the place where the professor's passion and love for her mother had resulted in Gracy's conception.  Once there, just as a peaceful life and a sense of normalcy seem long lasting, the professor's life journey comes to an end. Gracy, grief stricken by the loss of her dad's love and his devotion, is challenged to overcome the heartache and the fogginess of her empty world.  Her destiny once more takes her across the ocean where she finds love and affection. However, fate is not done with her, and it strikes with both people and difficult situations that threaten her peace of mind, her heart, and her life. These threats and dangers are appeased only when her heart finds its home in her denied lover's devotion.  Love and Betrayal is the third in the series.  Gracy thought she had found her true eternal love. This manifested deep in her faithful heart.  Unfortunately, later in her married life, this happiness turned disenchanting. Alvaro's promises of his vows, for better or for worse, in happiness and sorrow, until death do us part, had all changed. Fate delivered his unfaithfulness with pain and suffering. His behaviour brought catastrophic consequences along the way, influenced by someone else's greed, jealousy, and revenge. Gracy's emotional journey of survival turned challenging.  Later in life, in solitude and despair. the universal bliss tested her with waves of courage. With the intervention of caring people, she finally found, once more, love, peace, and support. Her heart finally rejoiced in comfort once more until her terrestrial journey came to an end.  Gina Iafrate writes sweeping family sagas that allow readers to travel the globe without leaving home. Like fine pieces of music, her books are layered, providing readers with intricately formed mental images, punctuated by dramatic movements. In the tradition of writers like Nicholas Sparks and John Grisham, Gina's extraordinary story lines reveal the richness of the human experience told through the eyes of finely drawn characters. Nearly all of her story lines involve rich and tactile descriptions of exotic places, placing the reader in the very midst of those locales.  Other books in Gina Iafrate's Strong Women series features a number of romantic fiction novels that feature characters who have managed to overcome staggering odds and yet emerge from overwhelming circumstances to build lives that can serve as models for women everywhere. Books in the Strong Women series also include:  The Girl From The Cornfield  It is 1950 and Giuliana Ferrante is growing up in a sleepy town deep in the Italian mountains.  In this coming-of-age saga, her young life is rocked to its core by a vicious attack that shatters the peaceful existence that she and many around her have always taken for granted.  Giuliana, determined to make a future for herself, flourishes by working tirelessly toward her education, securing a legal degree and ultimately a successful position in the business world.  She conquers all with kindness and love, managing to rise above the painful memories of her youth.  This sweeping, inspirational tale hurtles across time, countries, and characters as it explores how love can heal, reconcile, and restore.  Bestowed By Love And Splendour:  A breath-taking story of a broken-hearted wife and mother who unexpectedly finds the universal bliss takes her away from a tormented life, far across the ocean. On the exotic island of Sardinia, she becomes hypnotized by nature's beauty; the cloudless sky, the emerald-blue hues of the sea and the gentleness of the people.  But fate strikes once more. Destiny takes over and transports her to a world beyond her imagination . . . allowing her to find peace, love, and splendour.
