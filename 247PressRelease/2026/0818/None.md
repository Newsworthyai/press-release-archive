# Huuso Bio Puts More Trained Technicians on the Road Across Chicagoland, Cutting Response Times for Families in Crisis Needing Crime Scene Cleanup and Biohazard Cleanup

CHICAGO, IL, August 18, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- When somebody calls Huuso Bio, it is usually the worst day of their life. A parent has died alone. A crime happened in the living room. Nobody teaches you what to do next, and the last thing a grieving family should worry about is whether help is three hours away.  That is why Huuso Bio has grown its roster of trained, IICRC-certified technicians across the Chicago area. More local techs means faster arrivals in Chicago, Naperville, Joliet, and Aurora, plus Elgin, Hoffman Estates, Lake Forest, Highland Park, and every community in between. And because the crews are already here, there are no travel fees anywhere in the Chicagoland service area. Ever.  "Nobody plans for the day they need us," said Kevin Reifsteck, co-founder of Huuso Bio. "When that phone rings, someone on the other end is hurting. They don't need a call center reading a script, and they don't need a truck coming from another state with a travel charge tacked on. They need a neighbor who knows exactly what to do. That's who we hire and that's who we train."  The Family That Wrote the Book on This Work  Huuso Bio isn't new to hard days. The Reifsteck brothers who founded the company have spent decades in trauma and crime scene cleanup, and the protocols they developed helped shape the ANSI/IICRC S540 Standard, the national standard the entire industry follows today. Every Huuso Bio technician trains through the company's own Huuso Bio Academy, including its "I Care" program, which teaches techs how to communicate with families in the middle of a traumatic event, not just how to disinfect a room.  That matters, because this work is about more than cleaning. It is blood cleanup, unattended death cleanup, suicide and homicide cleanup, hoarding, and infectious disease disinfection, done to OSHA, CDC, EPA, and Illinois Department of Public Health requirements. But it is also sitting with a widow at the kitchen table and explaining, in plain English, what happens next.  One Call, Handled Start to Finish For Fast Friendly Crime Scene and Biohazard Cleanup  Faster response is only half of it. Huuso Bio built its whole process so that a single phone call takes the entire problem off the customer's plate:  - A live team available 24/7/365, every day of the year - Direct work with homeowners and property insurance carriers, so in most cases customers pay nothing out of pocket at the time of service - Full documentation, Proof of Disinfection, and a 100 percent satisfaction guarantee - Discreet crews who protect the family's privacy from arrival to final walkthrough  Residents can learn more about service in their community on the pages below:  Chicago: https://www.huusobio.com/service-areas/chicago-il-death-cleanup-company/ Naperville: https://www.huusobio.com/service-areas/naperville-il-crime-scene-cleanup-company/ Joliet: https://www.huusobio.com/service-areas/joliet-il-crime-scene-cleanup-services/ All Chicagoland communities: https://www.huusobio.com/service-areas/  Homeowners, landlords, and property managers can also call (630) 200-1436 any hour of the day.  Frequently Asked Questions  Does Huuso Bio charge travel fees in the Chicago area? No. Huuso Bio never charges travel fees anywhere in its Chicagoland service area, including Chicago, Naperville, Joliet, Aurora, Elgin, Hoffman Estates, Lake Forest, and Highland Park.  Who pays for biohazard or crime scene cleanup? In most situations, homeowners or property insurance covers it. Huuso Bio works directly with the carrier and adjuster, handles the paperwork, and typically collects nothing from the customer at the time of service.  How fast can Huuso Bio respond? Crews live and work throughout Chicagoland, and the phone is answered around the clock. Dispatch starts on the first call, so help is coming from down the road, not across the state.  Huuso Bio is a family- and veteran-owned crime scene and biohazard remediation company based in Plainfield, Illinois, serving communities throughout Chicagoland and the state. Founded by the Reifsteck brothers, pioneers whose protocols helped shape the ANSI/IICRC S540 Standard for trauma and crime scene cleanup, Huuso Bio crime scene cleanup pairs certified technical work with something rarer: genuine care for people on the hardest day of their lives. Learn more at https://www.huusobio.com
