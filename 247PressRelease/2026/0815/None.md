# Law Bear Auto Accident Attorneys Rebrands to Law Bear Injury Lawyers, Expanding Beyond Traffic Wrecks

PHOENIX, AZ, August 15, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Law Bear, a personal injury law firm headquartered in Phoenix, today announced an official rebranding from Law Bear Auto Accident Attorneys to Law Bear Injury Lawyers. The updated name reflects the firm's expanded case criteria, allowing its team of dedicated, Arizona-licensed attorneys to serve a wider population of injured victims across the state.  While motor vehicle accidents, including car, commercial truck, motorcycle, rideshare, and pedestrian collisions, remain a core focus of the firm, Law Bear Injury Lawyers is now officially accepting cases in several expanded practice areas, including nursing home neglect and abuse, premises liability (slip-and-fall accidents and hazardous property claims), and general personal injury.   Evolving to Meet the Community's Legal Needs  Since its founding, Law Bear built its reputation as an advocate for individuals facing tough legal battles against insurance companies. Managing Attorney Jason Ruen founded the firm with a personal mission after watching a commercial truck injure his father and seeing how insurance adjusters treated his family. He dedicated his career to ensuring no injured victim had to face an insurance company alone.  As the firm grew, client demand for Law Bear's representation expanded beyond the highway. Victims of elder neglect in care facilities, individuals injured on unsafe commercial property, and those suffering from personal injuries routinely sought out Law Bear for legal help.  Expanding practice criteria to handle nursing home neglect, premises liability, and general injury claims allows the firm to protect more families across Arizona when they need legal assistance.  Expanded Practice Areas at a Glance  Under its new name, Law Bear Injury Lawyers will accept cases across an expanded spectrum of personal injury law:  Motor Vehicle & Traffic Accidents: Continuing representation for car crashes, commercial trucking accidents, motorcycle collisions, rideshare (Uber/Lyft) accidents, bicycle, and pedestrian injuries. Nursing Home Neglect & Abuse: Holding long-term care facilities, assisted living centers, and nursing homes accountable for bedsores, medication errors, falls, malnutrition, and physical or emotional mistreatment of elderly residents. Premises Liability: Representing victims injured due to hazardous conditions on commercial, residential, or public properties, including slip-and-falls, walkway hazards, negligent security, and dog bites. General Personal Injury & Wrongful Death: Fighting for individuals and families facing life-altering injuries or loss caused by third-party negligence.  The Law Bear Promise Remains Unchanged  Despite the brand evolution and expanded practice criteria, Law Bear Injury Lawyers maintains its client-first commitment: "No Win. No Fee. No Risk to You."  Clients pay zero out-of-pocket costs, and legal fees are only collected if the firm recovers financial compensation on the client's behalf. Furthermore, every case will continue to be directed by experienced, Arizona-licensed attorneys who understand local injury statutes, adjuster tactics, and courtroom strategies.  While the firm's name is changing to better capture the full range of services provided, the core mission remains unchanged: bringing dedicated legal advocacy to nursing home neglect claims, premises liability cases, and highway collisions alike.  Law Bear Injury Lawyers is an Arizona-licensed personal injury law firm headquartered in Phoenix, AZ. Founded on the principle that injured victims deserve supportive service and firm legal representation, Law Bear handles car accidents, truck wrecks, nursing home neglect, premises liability, and general personal injury claims throughout Arizona, including Phoenix, Tucson, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Scottsdale, and Tempe. Licensed by the Arizona Supreme Court as an Alternative Business Structure (ABS), Law Bear provides immediate legal guidance backed by advocacy against insurance companies.  To learn more or request a free, no-obligation case review, visit lawbear.com.
