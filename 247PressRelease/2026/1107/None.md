# MachineTranslation.com Rolls Out Team Plan as Translation Becomes a Shared Workflow

BEAVERTON, OR, November 07, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Translation used to be a task one person handled. For growing businesses, it is now work that passes through marketing, legal, operations and leadership, often in the same week.  MachineTranslation.com, the AI translation platform built by Tomedes, today launched the Team Plan to match that shift. The plan offers one shared account, member invites, per-seat pricing and a single invoice, so every person who touches a translation works from the same place.  The launch follows months of requests from clients of Tomedes, a professional translation company with nearly 20 years in the language industry. Many had outgrown individual accounts but did not need an enterprise contract.  Why individual accounts stopped working  Individual accounts were never designed for teams. As volume grows, colleagues share login credentials, translation histories scatter across accounts, and billing splits into separate charges. Holding a consistent standard gets harder with every new hire.  "We talk to our clients constantly," says Ofer Tirosh, CEO of Tomedes, developer of MachineTranslation.com. "For months, the same request came up: teams that had outgrown individual accounts but did not need an enterprise contract. Businesses are growing fast and they need their translation workflow to grow with them. The Team Plan exists because they asked for it. That is the only reason it needed to exist."  What a team gets with the plan  A new member is invited and set up in minutes. Everyone shares one account and one quality standard, and every seat uses the same platform, where multiple leading AI models translate each text independently and the output the majority agrees on is what reaches the team.  Billing becomes one line item. Growing the team means adding a seat, not opening another account.  "Translation quality should not vary depending on who on the team is logged in," says Rachelle Garcia, AI Lead at Tomedes. "Team members work from the same translation environment and quality standard. That is what the Team Plan makes possible."  The Team Plan is available now at machinetranslation.com.  MachineTranslation.com is a professional AI translation platform built by Tomedes, a professional translation company with nearly 20 years of experience in the language industry. It takes a multi-model approach: leading AI models, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepL, Google and others, produce independent translations of the same text, and the platform surfaces the one the majority agreed on, designed to reduce translation errors by cross-checking outputs across multiple AI models, across 330+ languages.
