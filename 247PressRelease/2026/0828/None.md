# Three Partners at Rosenthal, Levy, Simon Sosa Injury Lawyers Honored in the 2027 Edition of The Best Lawyers in America©

WEST PALM BEACH, FL, August 28, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Rosenthal, Levy, Simon & Sosa Injury Lawyers is proud to announce that partners Jonathan Levy, Steve Simon, and Luis Sosa have been officially recognized in the 2027 edition of The Best Lawyers in America© for their exceptional work in Personal Injury Litigation – Plaintiffs.  Inclusion in Best Lawyers is regarded as a significant honor within the legal profession because it is compiled entirely through a rigorous and confidential peer-review evaluation process. The 2027 listing directly reflects the profound respect, integrity, and professional standing these attorneys have earned among their peers within the legal community.  This milestone reflects their unwavering dedication to fighting for the injured for over 40 years and their continuous commitment to legal excellence.  About Rosenthal, Levy, Simon & Sosa Injury Lawyers  For over 40 years, Rosenthal, Levy, Simon & Sosa have been dedicated to advocating for the rights of individuals who have been injured. With offices in West Palm Beach and Port St. Lucie, our attorneys have earned a reputation for prioritizing clients and delivering justice for those affected by personal injury. Boasting more than 150 years of combined legal experience, our firm specializes in handling auto, truck and motorcycle accidents, slip-and-falls, workers' compensation, and more.
