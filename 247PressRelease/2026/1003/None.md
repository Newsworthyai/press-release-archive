# Author Van Norman Announces the Launch of Rocky's Forever Home: Learning to Love Again

DALLAS, TX, October 03, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Van Norman, a Dallas-based children's author with decades of hands-on experience working alongside children and young adults, has officially announced the release of his most heartfelt work to date Rocky's Forever Home: Learning to Love Again. Available now in paperback and hardcover through major book retailers, the book is an emotionally rich, beautifully illustrated story that speaks as powerfully to the adults in the room as it does to the children they are reading it with.  Rocky is a German Shepherd who has spent his life on the streets and cycling in and out of shelters and who, after too many disappointments to count, has decided that love is something that happens to other dogs. He is guarded, sarcastic, and quietly heartbroken, and he has convinced himself that keeping everyone at arm's length is the only way to survive. When Rocky and his companion Dottie an optimistic, open-hearted Dalmatian who grew up in the alley but never stopped believing in something better arrive together at a new home, the tension between their two worldviews sets the stage for a story that is funny, tender, and quietly profound.  The household they step into is alive with personality. Molly, the graceful Sheltie who has known only the warmth of a forever home, welcomes them with patient wisdom. Bandit, the spinning, laughing Beagle next door, fills every scene he enters with joy. Paris the Yorkshire Terrier rules her corner of the house with a sassy authority that nobody quite knows how to argue with, and Sam the Basset Hound bellows his grievances from room to room in a voice that shakes the walls. Each character is drawn from a real dog Norman has known, and it shows — the whole neighborhood breathes.  But the story's emotional center belongs to Dottie. When she slips back through the fence and makes her way alone through the city to the alley where she grew up past the graffiti walls, the rusted truck, the upended trash can, the old friends sleeping under moving blankets she carries with her a mission that is both brave and selfless. What she brings back is not just news, but proof: proof that love endures across distance and time and all the walls we build to keep it out. For Rocky, witnessing what unfolds in the aftermath is the moment that challenges everything and it is the moment the story has been building toward all along.  "This story was written because I believe every child deserves to feel loved, seen, and at home in their own life. Rocky's journey from fear and self-protection to openness and trust mirrors something deeply universal. I hope readers of every age find a piece of themselves in him and find the courage to believe again." — Van Norman, Author  Norman writes without condescension and without false comfort. His prose trusts young readers with real emotion — the fear of being sent back, the longing for a brother left behind, the complicated ache of wanting to love but being too afraid to try and gives them the language to sit with it, process it, and ultimately move through it. That emotional precision, worn lightly and carried with warmth and humor, is what makes this book more than an illustrated story. It is a tool, and a gift, and in the right hands it will open conversations that last far beyond story time.  Rocky's Forever Home: Learning to Love Again is available now in paperback (ISBN: 9798250492591) and hardcover (ISBN: 9798248659159). The book is dedicated to Norman's grandson, Layne Kale Ichiro Lewis Norman, and was made possible by the steadfast support of his wife Tina and the editorial guidance of his Aunt Beth. It features vibrant full-color illustrations, interactive coloring pages, and a reader reflection section designed to encourage discussion and emotional engagement. Educators, librarians, school counselors, and parents will find it a natural and powerful addition to any collection built around social-emotional learning, themes of adoption and belonging, or simply the enduring belief that every reader deserves a forever home.  For media inquiries, review copy requests, school visit bookings, or signing event scheduling, reach out directly or visit Norman's official website at vannormanauthor.com.  About Van Norman Van Norman is a Dallas, Texas-based children's author whose writing is rooted in a lifelong passion for the emotional lives of children and young adults. His work focuses on themes of love, belonging, trust, and the courage to begin again — values he has championed through decades of direct, hands-on work with children and youth. Norman writes with the deep conviction that every child deserves to feel seen, valued, and at home in their own story. Rocky's Forever Home: Learning to Love Again is his latest release.
