# High Rise Financial Expands With New Locations in Minneapolis and Columbus

LOS ANGELES, CA, November 19, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- High Rise Financial LLC has expanded its Midwest presence with new locations in Minneapolis, Minnesota, and Columbus, Ohio. The two locations give people with pending personal injury claims a local point of contact for questions about pre-settlement funding, including whether they may qualify and what information they need to apply.  - Minneapolis — 331 2nd Ave. S., #459, Minneapolis, MN 55401 - Columbus — 7965 N. High St., Suite 350-32, Columbus, OH 43235  The two-city announcement builds on the Los Angeles-based company's existing Midwest presence in Chicago. It is part of High Rise Financial's broader effort to make funding more accessible to people who need help covering expenses before a personal injury claim is resolved.  "After an injury, missing even one paycheck can make it hard to keep up with rent or groceries. A personal injury claim can take months to resolve, but those bills are due now," said a spokesperson for High Rise Financial. "With our Minneapolis and Columbus locations, we want more people to know what funding options they may have while their case is pending."  High Rise Financial reviews applications from people represented by an attorney in a pending personal injury claim, including claims stemming from car crashes and slip-and-fall injuries. The company works with the applicant's attorney to evaluate the claim. Eligible plaintiffs can use approved funding for everyday expenses while their cases are pending.  The funding is non-recourse. Repayment comes from a settlement or verdict under the terms of the funding agreement. If the plaintiff does not recover compensation, they do not repay the funding.  People in Minneapolis and Columbus can learn more or start an application through the High Rise Financial website.  About High Rise Financial LLC  Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Los Angeles, High Rise Financial LLC provides pre-settlement funding to eligible plaintiffs with pending personal injury and other civil claims. The company works with applicants and their attorneys to help address financial needs that arise before a claim is resolved.  Original Source
