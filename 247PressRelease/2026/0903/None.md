# Aaron Warner and Elissa Fitzmartin Named to 2026 Boca Raton Observer Top Lawyers List

LAKE WORTH, FL, September 03, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Aaron Warner and Elissa Fitzmartin, founding partners of Warner & Fitzmartin, have been named to The Boca Raton Observer's 2026 Top Lawyers list.  The list is compiled by DataJoe Research through an online peer-nomination process and cannot be purchased or influenced by advertising. It will publish in the Observer's September issue and at bocaratontoplawyers.com.  Warner, who grew up in South Florida, said the recognition ties back to where he chose to build his career. "I grew up in South Florida and I knew I needed to get back to Palm Beach, because of my connections, growing up here, knowing doctors, knowing so many people. There's no place I'd rather be building this firm," he said.  Fitzmartin pointed to what personal injury law lets her offer clients. "I really feel like with personal injury I'm giving access to the courts to all types of people, even people that wouldn't normally be able to afford hiring a lawyer. You get the same quality of attorney, maybe even better, for zero cost to you," she said.  Warner and Fitzmartin founded their Lake Worth firm to give South Florida injury victims direct, hands-on legal representation. The firm handles car accidents, truck accidents, motorcycle accidents, bicycle accidents, slip and falls, negligent security, and wrongful death claims throughout Palm Beach County, Broward County, and South Florida.  About Warner & Fitzmartin   Warner & Fitzmartin is a personal injury law firm based in Lake Worth, Florida, serving clients throughout Palm Beach County, Broward County, and South Florida.  For more information, visit warnerfitzmartin.com or call 561-803-0167.
