# Leave Timeshare Named U.S. Business of the Year 2027 and Honored With Multiple National and Global Awards

IRVINE, CA, October 22, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Leave Timeshare, an Irvine-based advocacy company that helps timeshare owners exit unwanted contracts, has been recognized with a series of awards across 2026 and 2027, including the American Business Vanguard 2027 award naming it U.S. Business of the Year 2027. Leave Timeshare has received industry or business recognition every year since 2023.  Recent recognition includes:  American Business Vanguard 2027: Named U.S. Business of the Year 2027 Global 100 2027: Winner, for helping owners exit unwanted timeshares with transparency and support IE100 Awards 2026: Winner Best in Business 2026 (BNS): Winner Consumer Ratings Institute (New York) 2026: Awarded for Business Excellence World's Best Employer – United States 2025 (USIQ): Recognized for workplace culture AI Business Excellence Awards 2025: Timeshare Contract Termination Specialists of the Year, USA Acquisition International Business Excellence Awards 2024: Best Timeshare Contract Termination Specialists, USA Top Workplaces Orange County 2023 (Orange County Register): Recognized as a Top Workplace Snoball Giving Back Award 2023: Recognized for community giving, including its partnership with One Tree Planted  "These awards reflect the work our team does every day for owners who feel they were misled into a timeshare," said Jordan Snow, Office Manager at Leave Timeshare. "Our focus is honest communication, ethical practices, and real resolutions for our clients. We're grateful for the recognition and proud of the people behind it."  Founded in 2018 by professionals with prior experience in the timeshare industry, Leave Timeshare assists owners who believe they were subjected to unfair or deceptive sales practices. The company reviews each client's documentation to build an exit plan. Leave Timeshare earned BBB4Good verification in 2023 and partnered with One Tree Planted that same year to support reforestation in California, helping communities recover from the effects of devastating wildfires. A complete list of awards is available at www.leavetimeshare.com.  Leave Timeshare is a timeshare contract termination advocacy company based in Irvine, California, founded in 2018. It has been recognized with business excellence awards every year since 2023. Its services require documented evidence of deceptive or misleading sales practices. Learn more at www.leavetimeshare.com.
