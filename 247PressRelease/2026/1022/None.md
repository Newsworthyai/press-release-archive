# Genovation to Showcase K–12 School Cafeteria POS Keypads at the CSNA Annual Conference

IRVINE, CA, October 22, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Genovation, a designer and manufacturer of purpose-built computer keypads, keyboards, specialty point-of-sale (POS) terminals, and custom electronic products since 1989, will showcase its school lunch solutions at the California School Nutrition Association (CSNA) 74th Annual Conference, November 11–14, 2026, at the Long Beach Convention Center in Long Beach, California.  School nutrition directors, district administrators, cafeteria managers, and IT professionals visiting Genovation at Booth #112 can explore student identification keypads designed to support everyday cafeteria transactions, with configurable connectivity and optional integrated scanning to accommodate different school lunch operations.  CSNA Annual Conference — Exhibit Information  • Conference dates: November 11–14, 2026 • Location: Long Beach Convention Center, Long Beach, California • Genovation booth: #112 • Exhibit Day 1: Friday, November 13: 10:30 a.m.–3:00 p.m. • Exhibit Day 2: Saturday, November 14: 10:00 a.m.–1:30 p.m.  Keypad Solutions for School Cafeterias  Genovation offers K–12 school districts flexible keypad configurations for student identification at cafeteria POS stations.  All Genovation keypad models can be ordered with or without an integrated barcode scanner. Scanner-equipped configurations can read standard barcodes and QR codes from printed materials and digital displays, including codes shown on smartphone screens.  School lunch solutions include:  • Student PIN pads: Keypads for entering student PINs or identification numbers during cafeteria transactions. • Integrated barcode and QR code scanning: Optional built-in scanners for reading printed student ID cards, standard barcodes, and QR codes displayed on mobile devices. • RFID options: Contactless identification using compatible RFID-enabled cards, where supported by the selected model. • Custom keypad configurations: Options for specialized cafeteria workflows and equipment requirements.  • Warranty: 3-year warranty on POS PIN pads, and extended warranties are available for many models.  Flexible Connectivity for School Lunch POS Systems  Genovation keypads offer configurable interfaces to help schools evaluate hardware for their existing cafeteria software and infrastructure.  Depending on the model and configuration, options include:  • USB HID keyboard: Windows recognizes compatible keypads as standard keyboard devices. • Virtual serial COM port: An alternative interface for software that supports serial input. • Bluetooth: Wireless connectivity on supported configurations. • Wi-Fi: Wireless networking options for supported models and applications. • Power over Ethernet (PoE): Combined power and network connectivity for supported configurations.  These options give school districts flexibility when selecting equipment for cafeteria workstation layouts, network infrastructure, and software input requirements.  School Lunch POS Software Compatibility Reference  Genovation keypads are used with a range of K–12 school lunch POS software systems.
