# From Page to Screen: Xuemo's "The Undying Diamond Heart" and Personal Documentary Preview Debut at the 2026 Frankfurt Book Fair

FRANKFURT, GERMANY, October 12, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- A renowned author Xuemo brings his literary world to international audiences at the 2026 Frankfurt Book Fair with the release of the English edition of "The Undying Diamond Heart "and the unveiling of the trailer for In Conversation with Xuemo: A Personal Documentary.  The documentary traces Xuemo's life, literary journey, and cultural exploration, drawing on the vast desert landscapes and rich cultural heritage of northwestern China. It explores how his writing engages with nature, life, human nature, and contemporary society, while examining broader questions at the intersection of literature, technology, civilization, and humanity's future.  Published by Ruxue International Media, the English edition of "The Undying Diamond Heart" is translated by acclaimed American sinologist and translator Howard Goldblatt and Sylvia Li-Chunlin. Xuemo describes the novel as a work capable of profound personal transformation: "Other novels by Xuemo may influence you and change you, but "The Undying Diamond Heart "can help you become who you are meant to be."  A reviewer for Booklife Prize praised the book's originality and distinctive fusion of Eastern mysticism and deeply felt inner conflict, noting that such an approach is rare among comparable works. The review highlights the novel's immersive narrative, which blurs the boundaries between history and divine revelation, weaving together curses, pilgrimage, karmic trials, and dreamlike logic.  The reviewer further describes the book as a work of spiritual power and magical realism that moves freely between the physical world and transcendent realms, using bold symbolism to portray the protagonist's inner journey.  Xuemo's works have been translated into 31 languages and published internationally. His work has been showcased at the Frankfurt Book Fair for eight consecutive years. According to the event organizers, he is the only Chinese author to have both an independent exhibition booth and an invitation to attend the fair's opening ceremony.  During the 2022 Frankfurt Book Fair, Xuemo ranked first in an international media-attention ranking covering media outside Germany, surpassing Spain, the fair's Guest of Honour that year. In 2025, the inaugural "Xuemo World Literature Forum " was successfully held at the Frankfurt Book Fair, creating another platform for international literary exchange.  From the publication of an English-language novel to the screen adaptation of a personal documentary, Xuemo's presence at Frankfurt 2026 represents a broader approach to international literary engagement—one that extends beyond individual books to encompass an author's life experience, intellectual and spiritual journey, and wider literary vision.  About Author Xuemo  Xuemo is a contemporary Chinese author whose works explore human nature, cultural traditions, spirituality, and the relationship between individuals and society. Through international publishing, literary events, and cross-cultural dialogue, his writing continues to reach readers around the world.
