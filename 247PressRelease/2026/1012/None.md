# Reno's Jasmine Wells Named 2026 Beauty &amp; Aesthetics Businesswoman of the Year by the International Elite 100 Awards

RENO, NV, October 12, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Jasmine Wells, owner and director of operations of Aesthetics Medical Spa, has been named Beauty & Aesthetics Businesswoman of the Year - Nevada, USA, in the 2026 International Elite 100 Awards. The honor marks the second consecutive year Wells has received the award in her category.  The International Elite 100 Awards recognize exceptional talent across all areas of business through a merit-driven selection process, honoring leadership, innovation, and excellence internationally. The North America feature profiles Aesthetics Medical Spa as "a defining force in the evolution of modern beauty, wellness, and medical aesthetics."  Jasmine Wells brings 26 years of aesthetic expertise as a Master Aesthetics Educator and has led Aesthetics Medical Spa for 16 years, building the practice into one of Nevada's most trusted destinations for transformative aesthetic care. Under her direction, the clinic has expanded from a focused injectables practice into a comprehensive medical spa treating fine lines, rosacea, enlarged pores, cellulite, and volume loss, with services spanning skin rejuvenation, dermal fillers, PDO thread lifts, laser hair removal, body contouring, PRF hair restoration, and medically supervised weight loss.  "This recognition belongs to an extraordinary team and to the clients who trust us with their care," said Jasmine Wells, owner and director of operations of Aesthetics Medical Spa. "In sixteen years, one standard has never changed: every treatment is personalized, medically sound, and delivered with genuine care. Being recognized internationally is meaningful, but the real measure is the confidence a client carries out our door."  The IE 100 editorial credits the clinic's growth to what it describes as "a commitment to results without compromise," noting that clients "arrive seeking confidence, clarity, and change; they leave with visible improvements and a renewed sense of self."  Wells' work extends beyond the treatment room. She also directs Aesthetics Medical Training, which educates aesthetic practitioners across the region, and developed Merci4, an in-house professional-grade product line that allows clients to maintain their results at home. Aesthetics Medical Spa has been recognized by the Reno Award Program with the Best of Reno Awards for 16 consecutive years and multiple inductions into the Reno Business Hall of Fame.  Previous Awards & Recognitions - Reno Award Program – Reno's Best Medspa, 16 consecutive years - International Elite 100 Awards - Beauty & Aesthetics Businesswoman of the Year 2025 - GHP's Best Medical Aesthetics Clinic – Nevada 2025 - Influential Businesswoman Award 2024 - Reno Award Program's 2024 induction into the Reno Business Hall of Fame - Three Best Rated® 2023 - LUX Life's Health Beauty and Wellness Award 2022 - GHP's Best Health & Nutrition Medispa Nevada 2021 - LuxLife Magazine Best Aging Gracefully Aesthetics Medispa - Nevada 2021 - Business Hall of Fame Reno 2020 - GHP Best Aesthetics Spa Service Provider - Nevada 2020 - Employer Support of the Guard and Reserve's Seven Seals Award  The practice holds a 5.0-star average across 167 Google reviews. The clinic serves the greater Reno area from its 6295 Sharlands Avenue, Reno, NV location and offers flexible payment plans through CareCredit.  Founded in 2010, Aesthetics Medical Spa (Aesthetics MedSpa) is an award-winning medical spa in Reno, Nevada, offering cosmetic, medical, and wellness treatments designed to enhance natural beauty and confidence. Services include injectables, dermal fillers, PDO thread lifts, laser treatments, skin rejuvenation, body contouring, PRF and PRP therapies, IV and vitamin therapy, and medical weight loss programs. The practice combines advanced technology with personalized care in a professional, relaxing environment. Owner Jasmine Wells also leads Aesthetics Medical Training and the Merci4 professional skincare line. For more information, visit https://aestheticsmedspa.com.
