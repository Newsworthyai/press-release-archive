# Bliss Animal Hospital Voted Top 3 Veterinary Hospital in Orange County Register's Best of OC 2026

LAKE FOREST, CA, September 27, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Bliss Animal Hospital has been voted among the Top 3 in the Best Pet Clinic/Veterinary Hospital category in the Orange County Register's Best of Orange County 2026 readers' poll.  The annual program invites Orange County Register readers to select their favorite local businesses and services. This year's recognition places Bliss among the community's top choices for veterinary care.  Founded in 2024 by veterinarians Dr. Nayara Pataro, known to clients as Dr. Naya, and Dr. Sam Amirshahi, Bliss Animal Hospital is an independently owned practice in Lake Forest serving families throughout Orange County.  The Orange County Register's feature highlights the hospital's emphasis on relationships, listening to families, and helping pet owners understand their pets' care. These principles guide the team's approach, from routine preventive visits to more complex medical decisions.  Bliss offers wellness exams, vaccinations, in-house laboratory and imaging diagnostics, dentistry, surgery, laser therapy, and urgent-care support. A dedicated cat exam room helps provide a calmer experience for feline patients.  The hospital extends its appreciation to the clients, neighbors, and pet families whose support made this recognition possible. For the Bliss team, the award is an opportunity to thank the community and reaffirm its commitment to compassionate care, clear communication, and personalized medicine.  Bliss serves Lake Forest, Foothill Ranch, Irvine, Mission Viejo, Rancho Santa Margarita, and surrounding Orange County communities.  Read the Orange County Register's full feature: https://www.ocregister.com/2026/09/16/best-of-orange-county-2026-services-best-pet-clinic-veterinary-hospital/  For more information about Bliss Animal Hospital, visit https://blissanimalhospital.com/ or call 949-354-5201.  Bliss Animal Hospital is an independently owned veterinary practice located at 26741 Rancho Parkway, Suite 105-D, Lake Forest, California. Founded in 2024 by Dr. Nayara Pataro and Dr. Sam Amirshahi, the hospital provides preventive, diagnostic, dental, surgical, and urgent veterinary care for cats and dogs. Guided by its motto, "Better care. Better medicine.", Bliss combines personalized care with a commitment to lasting relationships with pets and their families. Learn more at https://blissanimalhospital.com/.
