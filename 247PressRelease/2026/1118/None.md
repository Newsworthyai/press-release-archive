# Bayuk Pratt Lawyer Secures Verdict of Over $31 Million for Two Severely Injured Florida Women in Lawsuit Against Motorcycle Business

ATLANTA, GA, November 18, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Attorney Christopher Lambden of the personal injury firm Bayuk Pratt has secured a verdict of more than $31 million for two severely injured women in a Florida lawsuit against a motorcycle business, the Atlanta-based law firm announced today.  In an order dated November 11, 2026, Circuit Judge Donna Goerner of the Seventeenth Judicial Circuit in and for Seminole County entered a final judgment in favor of the plaintiffs against Power Play Motor Sports, LLC d/b/a Prime Motorcycles. The judgment came after a non-jury trial on August 25, 2026.  Judge Goerner awarded one plaintiff $22,998,255.94 in economic and non-economic damages stemming from a de-gloving injury to her right forearm that required five surgeries and physical therapy.   The judge awarded a second plaintiff $8,657,402.53 in economic and non-economic damages for her injuries, including a broken sternum, a lower right leg injury, and injuries to her neck, back, and pelvis.   The court found the defendant, Power Play Motor Sports, LLC d/b/a Prime Motorcycles, liable for the plaintiffs' injuries based on the legal theories of strict liability and negligence when granting summary judgment in favor of the plaintiffs in May 2026. The issue of damages was reserved for the August trial.  The consolidated case is Daniela Fernandez and Jannie Beverley v. Power Play Motor Sports, LLC d/b/a Prime Motorcycles, Case No. 2023CA001779.
