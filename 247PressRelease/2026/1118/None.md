# DPL Financial Partners names growth-minded CFO

LOUISVILLE, KY, November 18, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- DPL Financial Partners, the leading platform for commission-free annuities, today announced that Ron Novak has joined the firm's senior leadership team as chief financial officer. A seasoned financial executive, Novak has more than 25 years of experience helping fintech and insurance technology companies scale through pivotal periods of growth.  "Ron brings a strategic mindset and a deep understanding of financial technology and the insurance industry," said DPL Founder and CEO David Lau. "His experience building finance and operations infrastructures designed for long-term, sustainable growth will be invaluable as we scale our business to serve an expanding network of advisors, their clients, and consumers."   Novak will oversee DPL's finance and operations functions. Most recently, Novak served as vice president of portfolio operations at Andromeda Software, an operating group of Constellation Software Inc. He built and deployed AI-enabled best practices across Constellation's vertical-market software businesses.  Before that, Novak spent more than a decade at Fidelity National Information Services (FIS), a global leader in financial technology and payments. He rose to head of finance transformation, where he architected AI-powered forecasting across the enterprise. He also served as group CFO of IT and Operations and led the financial workstream for FIS's $43 billion acquisition of Worldpay, helping realize $400 million in annual synergies.  Earlier, Novak was the first CFO of LOGiQ3, a technology, consulting, and outsourcing provider to the life insurance and reinsurance industry. There he built the finance function from the ground up and positioned the company for acquisition by RGAx. He began his career at KPMG in audit, then moved into M&A advisory with KPMG Transaction Services, where he led more than 100 financial due-diligence engagements.   "DPL is bringing to annuities the same transparency and efficiency that technology has brought to payments and wealth management, and that creates a significant opportunity for advisors and their clients," said Novak. "This is an exciting time to join DPL, and I'm looking forward to supporting the continued growth of this platform."   To learn more about DPL, its platform, Avenew®, and commission-free annuities, visit dplfp.com.  About DPL Financial Partners DPL Financial Partners is modernizing annuities and insurance to meet the needs of RIAs, their clients, and individual investors. As the pioneer of commission-free annuities, DPL is removing legacy barriers and delivering a wide range of low-cost solutions from the nation's leading carriers. Through Avenew®, its integrated platform, DPL offers a transparent, competitively priced marketplace—empowering advisors and investors with powerful tools to evaluate, compare, and seamlessly implement solutions designed for today's financial planning needs. DPL supports more than 8,500 RIA firms directly and through integrations with leading wealth management platforms including Orion and Black Diamond.
