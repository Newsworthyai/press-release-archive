# Best Viking Agent Spotlight: Heather Viking of Club Cruise Honored with Top Agent Award for Service and Viking Production

SAN FRANCISCO, CA, July 28, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Heather Viking, Director of the Viking Division at Club Cruise™, has received a Top Agent Award recognizing outstanding client service and Viking cruise production.  The "World Viking Club Cruise Award" honor reflects Heather's specialization as Diamond Star TLN agent for Viking River, Ocean and Expedition voyages and her growing reputation among travelers seeking expert guidance before or after booking. Her work includes itinerary and stateroom selection, Viking airfare planning, My Viking Journey support, pre- and post-cruise arrangements, and reviews of Viking promotions and discount codes.  "Heather's success comes from her genuine commitment to every traveler," said Amanda Huber, CEO of Club Cruise. "She combines exceptional Viking knowledge with personal service, attention to detail and a determination to help every client maximize the value and enjoyment of their journey."  Mario Herrera, Viking's West Coast Business Development Manager and 2026 Best Rep Waves Award winner,, has recognized Heather as a "Viking Rock Star" and "The Viking Queen," reflecting her dedication, specialization and support of Viking travelers.  The award comes as Heather and Club Cruise expand their complimentary Viking Promo Codes and Booking Review and prepares for more Viking shows. The service helps travelers compare promotional offers, airfare options, booking benefits and existing reservations before important deadlines.  Travelers who recently booked directly with Viking may also request a review to determine whether their reservation may qualify for additional agency support or available benefits. Guests will enjoy complimentary wine and light appetizers, exclusive event offers and expert guidance on Viking Ocean destinations, included experiences, current promotions, Viking Air Plus and insider booking strategies. She assists travelers with itinerary selection, promotional and discount-code reviews, airfare, hotels, extensions, insurance guidance, My Viking Journey support and complete travel planning before, during and after each voyage.
