# Hair By Taylor Now Open and Accepting New Clients at the Salon and Spa Galleria Azle in Azle, Texas

AZLE, TX, July 28, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- Licensed cosmetologist Taylor Farkas proudly announces the grand opening of Hair By Taylor, located at the Salon and Spa Galleria Azle in Azle, Texas. Farkas offers a variety of services, including cuts for men, women, and children. She specializes in color, full and partial highlights, balayage, root touchup, blowouts, and updos and styles for special events.  With 11 years of experience working in the industry, Farkas learned her skills at Duvall's School of Cosmetology. She has always been passionate about the artistry of cosmetology, and that passion is what inspired her to pursue this career. And once she started working behind the chair, Farkas said she quickly realized it was more than just creating beautiful hair.  "I learned that the best part was helping people feel confident, refreshed, and empowered," Farkas said. "I've been dedicated to providing personalized hair services, building lasting relationships with my clients, and creating looks that enhance each person's unique beauty. I love it when my clients leave my chair feeling beautiful and excited about their hair," she added.  Whether you're looking for a fresh haircut, dimensional color, highlights, or styling for a special occasion, Hair By Taylor will provide that…and more. Farkas also offers specialized hair treatments that include deep conditioning, hair glaze, and split end treatment. Her Brazilian blowout is a semi-permanent technique that helps smooth and straighten hair.  Farkas is delighted to welcome clients into her new shop, and to celebrate her grand opening, she is offering a $20 discount for new clients.  "My goal is to make every appointment a relaxing, enjoyable experience where you feel heard, cared for, and confident," Farkas said. "Nothing brings me more joy than seeing my clients leave my chair feeling refreshed, beautiful, and excited about their hair. I can't wait to help you achieve the look you've been dreaming of," she added.  When Farkas isn't working, she's enjoying time with her husband Chris, and their two children, Beau and Opal Anne. According to Farkas, her family is her greatest joy and the inspiration behind all she does.  Hair By Taylor is currently accepting new clients by appointment only. Hours are from 10 a.m.-3 p.m. Tuesday, from 10 a.m.-8 p.m. on Wednesday, and from 7 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday. To schedule an appointment, visit Farkas' booking site.   In close proximity to several large businesses and shopping areas, Hair By Taylor at the Salon and Spa Galleria Azle in Azle, Texas is in a convenient location for clients. Several major interchanges and roadways are nearby, allowing easy access from any direction. There is plenty of free parking, and the building is handicap accessible, with wide hallways and a comfortable waiting area.  About Salon and Spa Galleria Salon and Spa Galleria offers salon suites designed for independent beauty and wellness professionals. With 23 locations in the United States, Salon and Spa Galleria is one of the fastest-growing, privately held salon studio for lease salons. To inquire about space for rent in the Salon and Spa Galleria Azle, visit the salon chain's website.
